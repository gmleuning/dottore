# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The community of need and provision is what the relation of master and bondsman becomes because the bondsman, as the means of mastery, must be kept alive. In place of the brute destruction of the immediate object there come acquiring, preserving and forming it, as the middle in which the independent and the dependent extremes join. The satisfaction of need takes the form of universality: a lasting means and a provision that cares for and secures the future.
