# 1095 Rhymed Accentual Verse
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › iii. Unification of Verse and Rhyme
G: Der gereimte akzentuierende Vers
Gloss: Modern verse that unites accentual rhythm with rhyme.
HGloss: The rhymed accentual verse of the modern languages, which joins the rhythm of stressed syllables with the echo of rhyme.

N:
  rhymed accentual verse; iambic pentameter, heroic couplet, tetrameter couplet
  rhymed iambics, the alexandrine, the stanza, rhymed verse

Adj:
  accentual-syllabic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rhymed accentual verse combines rhythm and rhyme to a restricted extent. Word accent alone does not occupy the ear enough, so that sounding syllables are needed as supplement, and against rhyme's strength the metre must offer a counterweight, making the beat prominent, as in German rhymed iambics and trochees; rhyme suits only simple metres, not imitated classical stanzas.
