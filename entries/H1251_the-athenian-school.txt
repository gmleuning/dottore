# 1251 The Athenian School
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › iii. Neoplatonism
G: Die athenische Schule
Gloss: The last school of Athens, Syrianus, Proclus and Damascius, which built Neoplatonism into a vast system of triads.
HGloss: The Athenian school, Proclus above all, in whom the Neoplatonic triad is most fully developed; it ends with the closing of the schools in 529.
