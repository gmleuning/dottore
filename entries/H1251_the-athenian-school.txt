# 1251 The Athenian School
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › iii. Neoplatonism
G: Die athenische Schule
Gloss: The last school of Athens, Syrianus, Proclus and Damascius, which built Neoplatonism into a vast system of triads.
HGloss: The Athenian school, Proclus above all, in whom the Neoplatonic triad is most fully developed; it ends with the closing of the schools in 529.

N:
  the Athenian school
  Proclus, Damascius, Syrianus, Plutarch of Athens, Marinus, Simplicius, Olympiodorus, the Elements of Theology, the last pagans, the closing of the Academy
  the Platonic Theology, the triads, the henads, Justinian's edict

Adj:
  Athenian, Proclean

Phr:
  the last of the pagans
  remaining, procession, return, monē, proodos, epistrophē [Gr.] [Proclus]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Proclus, who taught as head of the school in Athens, is much more definite than Plotinus and goes further; his work contains what is most highly developed in the Neoplatonists, the triad of the One, the unlimited, and the limit. In 529 the Emperor Justinian had the school closed and banished the pagan philosophers.
