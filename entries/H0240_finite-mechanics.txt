# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Finite mechanics treats matter and motion as external to each other. Matter repels and attracts, inseparably both, and as their negative unity is singularity seeking an ideal centre in gravity. Taken as separate masses, bodies are inert and receive motion from outside, communicate it by impact, and in falling show, in a relatively free motion, their own weight.
