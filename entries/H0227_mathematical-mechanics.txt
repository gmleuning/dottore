# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mathematical mechanics considers space and time, self-externality in complete abstraction, before any matter fills them. Space, the abstract continuity of asunderness, carries negation within it as point, line and plane; negation posited for itself is time, the truth of space; and their unity is place and motion, which collapses into matter.
