# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The vertebrates, mammals, birds, fish and amphibians, have a backbone, and with it a greater development, an equilibrium of inner and outer, where both sides form one unity. Their further division therefore turns on the element for which each is destined, land, water or air, though nature also produces transitional mixtures.
