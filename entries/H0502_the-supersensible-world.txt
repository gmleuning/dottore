# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The supersensible world is the enduring beyond which, over against the vanishing world of the senses, is disclosed to the understanding as the true world. At first it is empty, only the nothingness of appearance and the simple universal, and consciousness does not find itself in it. But it comes forth out of appearance, which is its mediation, essence and fulfilment: the supersensible is the sensuous and perceived posited as they are in truth, as appearance. It is therefore appearance as appearance, and in it appearance's flux is held as the motionless difference of law.
