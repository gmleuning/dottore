# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea is nothing but the concept, the real existence of the concept and the unity of the two. The concept alone is not yet the idea, which it is only when present in its reality and one with it. This unity is no neutralization in which each loses its qualities, as acid and alkali form a salt: the concept predominates, generating its reality out of itself, and so remains one with itself in its objectivity.
