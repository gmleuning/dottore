# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Intuitive practical feelings are those whose content comes from intuition or representation, and so are more determinate than the pleasant and unpleasant: delight, joy, hope, fear, anguish and pain. In each the self feels the harmony or discord between itself and some event, thing or person, the content entering from outside. A sensible person of great character may find, or suffer, something without giving way to the feeling.
