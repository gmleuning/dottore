# 1092 Rhyme Proper
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › ii. Rhyme
G: Der eigentliche Reim
Gloss: Full end-rhyme, and the stanza forms built on it.
HGloss: Rhyme proper, the full echo of like sounds at the line's end, in which the modern inward self hears itself; with it the rhymed stanza forms.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rhyme proper brings to maturity what alliteration and assonance achieve imperfectly: a complete accord between whole stems apart from initial letters, with one, two or three syllables. Its place is the line's end, in simple return or artful interlacings that satisfy, tease and content the ear, and lyric, loving it for its subjectivity, develops it into stanza systems.
