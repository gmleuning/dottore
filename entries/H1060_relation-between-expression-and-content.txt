# 1060 Relation Between Expression and Content

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of music's means of expression to content arises with melody, the sphere of free invention. Music may accompany a content already put into words, or tear itself free to be independent, a difference marked broadly by vocal and instrumental music. As accompaniment it must make its expression adequate to the text, yet it is no servant: the text serves the music.
