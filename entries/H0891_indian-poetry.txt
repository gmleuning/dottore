# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Indian poetry offers the first example of pantheistic poetry. Its supreme divinity is the most abstract unity, specified in particular gods who revert into Brahma, the one permanent foundation of everything. In the Bhagavad Gita Krishna declares himself the origin and destruction of the whole world, the taste in water and the splendour of sun and moon, immanent in all things and yet transcending each.
