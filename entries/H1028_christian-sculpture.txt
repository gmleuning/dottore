# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Christian sculpture has from the start a principle that does not cohere directly with sculpture's material: romantic art concerns inward life withdrawn from the external, and grief, torture, death, resurrection and love find no adequate form in three-dimensional shape. So it yields to painting and music and serves chiefly as architecture's adornment, acquiring a pictorial principle.
