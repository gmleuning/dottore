# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Positing reflection is reflection as the movement of nothing to nothing, negation coinciding with itself. Its self-coincidence is immediacy, but only as the turning back of the negative into itself, which is positedness. There is nothing beforehand from which or to which it would return: only in this turning back is there that which starts out or returns. Because it produces immediacy by sublating the negative, it at the same time presupposes, and in sublating what it posits, passes into external reflection.
