# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Coercion belongs to the life-and-death struggle and the force that ends it. Each self-consciousness puts the other's life in danger and exposes its own, bent on compelling the other to recognize it. Such combat in its extreme form occurs only in the state of nature, not in civil society and the state, where recognition is already present; and although a state may arise by force, it does not rest on force, which only brings into existence what is justified in itself.
