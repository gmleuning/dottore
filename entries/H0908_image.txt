G: Das Bild
Gloss: A fuller picture drawn from another sphere and set out for itself to show the meaning.
HGloss: Das Bild: a developed metaphor, in which a whole situation from another sphere is portrayed to express a meaning that is not itself named.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: An image stands between metaphor and simile, a metaphor extended in which the meaning is not separately set against the object compared. It joins two independent situations so that one supplies the meaning made intelligible by the picture of the other, as Goethe's poem on Mahomet portrays the prophet's rise through a spring swelling into a mighty river, only the title showing the meaning.
