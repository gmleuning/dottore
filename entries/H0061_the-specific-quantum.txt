# 61 The Specific Quantum
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › α. Specific Quantity
G: Das spezifische Quantum
Gloss: A definite amount bound up with a particular quality; the size at which a thing is what it is.
HGloss: A quantum that is the existence of a quality, so that the thing is what it is only within this amount.

N:
  the specific quantum; proper amount, right size, due measure, natural size, characteristic magnitude; norm, optimum, just right, sweet spot; quota, allotment, dose; fitting proportion, appropriate scale, human scale
  measure, quantum, ratio, proportion
  the specific amount, the proper size, the qualitative quantum, the determinate size, the natural measure

V:
  fit, suit, befit; be the right size, be just right
  specify, fix, be of a size

Adj:
  proper, appropriate, fitting, due, right-sized, optimal, characteristic, specific
  definite, determinate

Adv:
  just right, just so, to a nicety, to a T. at its proper size, specifically

Phr:
  Goldilocks's porridge: not too hot, not too cold
