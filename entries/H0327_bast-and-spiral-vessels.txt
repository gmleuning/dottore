# 327 Bast and Spiral Vessels
# Nature › 3. Organics › II. Plant Nature › α. The Plant's Process of Formation › a. Formation as Such › ii. Cellular Tissue and Vessels
G: Bast und Spiralgefäße
Gloss: The plant's long fibres and coiled conducting vessels.
HGloss: The vessels into which the cellular tissue draws itself out: the fibrous bast and the spiral vessels, the plant's lines of inner motion.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Bast and spiral vessels arise as the cellular tissue is differentiated. Bast is the fibrous, tight cellular tissue proper, with long narrow cells; the spiral vessels are what the plant's second, inner division produces together with the vital vessels. Hegel sees in cells, fibres and spiral vessels the antithesis of node and line, with the vessels striving toward roundness.
