# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The situation is the particular state of affairs that opens the general condition of the world into collisions and complications, giving individuals the chance to show what they are in a determinate shape. The general state of the world is still unmoved and only the possibility of individual configuration; art must go beyond it to pictures of definite characters and actions, where the universal powers still hold sway.
