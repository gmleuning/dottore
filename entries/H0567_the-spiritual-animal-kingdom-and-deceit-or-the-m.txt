# 567 The Spiritual Animal Kingdom and Deceit, or the Matter in Hand Itself
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › a. The Certainty and Truth of Reason › iv. Individuality Real In and For Itself

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The spiritual animal kingdom and deception, or the matter in hand itself, is the shape in which individuals, each with an original determinate nature, express themselves in their works and take this to be absolute reality. The work proves transient, exposed to the interests of others, and consciousness holds instead to the matter in hand, the unity of actuality and individuality. Each then claims to care for the matter itself while caring for its own activity, deceiving others and being deceived, until the matter becomes the work of each and all.
