# 370 Spirit
# (top of the system)
G: Geist
Gloss: Mind in the broadest sense: the self-aware activity that knows itself and acts freely, and the human world it creates.
HGloss: The idea returned to itself out of nature's externality: spirit, whose essence is freedom, the concept knowing itself; nature's truth and its absolute prius.
