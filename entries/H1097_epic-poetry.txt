G: Epische Poesie
Gloss: Narrative poetry recounting the deeds of a people or a hero.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Epic, whose name means word or saga, tells what a matter is that has been turned into word: an inherently independent content, brought before the mind in all its relations and events, in the whole sweep of circumstances and development, the topic in the entirety of its existence. It is considered in its general character, its principal particulars and its historical development.
