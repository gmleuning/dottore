# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Physiognomy is bodily expression as it concerns character and is therefore permanent, whereas pathognomic expression relates to passing passions; a passion that holds sway lastingly ingrains itself in the face. Everyone has a physiognomic aspect on which others pass a first, instinctive judgement, but since this immediate exterior corresponds only imperfectly to the mind, the judgement may well be false. A person is known far less by outward appearance than by actions, and to raise physiognomy to a science was vacuous.
