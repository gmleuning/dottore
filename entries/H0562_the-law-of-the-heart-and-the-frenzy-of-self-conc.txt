# 562 The Law of the Heart and the Frenzy of Self-Conceit
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › a. The Certainty and Truth of Reason › iii. Self-Actualization of Reason
G: Das Gesetz des Herzens und der Wahnsinn des Eigendünkels
Gloss: The demand that the world conform to one's own heart, which ends in fury at a world that will not.
HGloss: The heart that wants its law to be the world's, and that rages against the world as a perversion of its law.

N:
  the law of the heart and the frenzy of self-conceit
  the law of the heart

F:
  German: Schwärmerei [enthusiasm, fanaticism], Eigendünkel

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The law of the heart and the frenzy of self-conceit is the shape in which self-consciousness knows that it has the universal, the law, immediately within itself, and wills to realize it for the welfare of all. Once realized, the law ceases to be the law of its heart and becomes an alien order, and other hearts reject it as merely another's. The concern for humanity then turns into a mad self-conceit that denounces the universal order as a perversion made by others, though the inversion lies in the heart itself.
