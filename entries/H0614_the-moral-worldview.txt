# 614 The Moral Worldview
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › b. Spirit as a World › iii. Spirit Certain of Itself: Morality
G: Die moralische Weltanschauung
Gloss: The view that duty and the world stand opposed, so that virtue and happiness can be reconciled only in a beyond.
HGloss: The moral worldview, which postulates the harmony of duty and nature in a beyond that is never reached.

  the moral view of the world, the moral outlook, the postulates, the harmony of morality and nature, the beyond, pure duty, the holy lawgiver

V:
  postulate, posit, hope, strive endlessly, ought
  moralize, oppose, defer, harmonize

Adj:

  Kantian, otherworldly
