# 614 The Moral Worldview
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › b. Spirit as a World › iii. Spirit Certain of Itself: Morality
G: Die moralische Weltanschauung
Gloss: The view that duty and the world stand opposed, so that virtue and happiness can be reconciled only in a beyond.
HGloss: The moral worldview, which postulates the harmony of duty and nature in a beyond that is never reached.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The moral worldview is the stance of a self-consciousness that knows duty as its sole essential purpose and is bound by nothing else. Because it is essentially mediation, it is still related to an otherness, which is for it a meaningless actuality: a nature complete in itself, with laws of its own, as unconcerned with moral self-consciousness as that consciousness is with nature. From this relation of mutual indifference and essential claim arise the postulates that would harmonize morality and nature.
