# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Pure being is the beginning of the Logic: being without any further determination. It is immediacy with nothing indeterminate about it left over to be fixed, equal only to itself and not even unequal to anything else, with no difference inside it or outward. Any content would take away its purity. There is therefore nothing in it to intuit or to think; it is only empty intuiting or empty thinking itself. Precisely as this indeterminate immediacy, being is in fact nothing, neither more nor less.
