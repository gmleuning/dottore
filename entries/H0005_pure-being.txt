# 5 Pure Being
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being
G: Reines Sein
Gloss: Existence with no further content at all; "is" without any "what." So empty that nothing definite can be thought in it.
HGloss: Being, pure being, without any further determination: indeterminate immediacy, equal only to itself, with nothing to be intuited or thought in it.

N:
  pure being; being as such, being qua being; mere being, bare being, naked being, sheer existence; indeterminate immediacy; the empty, the void &c H0006, emptiness, vacancy, blankness, vacuity, inanity; abstraction, the abstract, highest genus; the One of Parmenides, the Eleatic One
  [Modern] being in general, Being
  sheer being, indeterminate being, the empty is

V:
  be simply, be purely, merely be; abstract from everything, prescind, strip away, empty out; be equal only to oneself
  be

Adj:
  pure, sheer, mere, bare, naked, simple, unalloyed, unqualified, unconditioned; indeterminate, undetermined, undifferentiated, indistinct, featureless, characterless, contentless, empty, void, vacant, blank, vacuous, inane; abstract, general; self-same, self-equal; ineffable, unsayable, unthinkable
  empty &c H0227, null
  homogeneous

Adv:
  purely, simply, merely, barely; as such, qua being

F:
  German: unbestimmte Unmittelbarkeit, Sein überhaupt, das reine Sein
  Latin: esse purum, esse simpliciter, ens qua ens, summum genus, ens generalissimum, ens commune [scholastic], simpliciter, tabula rasa
  Greek: on hē on
  Sanskrit: sat, sanmātra [pure being], nirguṇa [without qualities]
  Chinese: wuji [the limitless]
  Korean: sunsu jonjae [pure being]
