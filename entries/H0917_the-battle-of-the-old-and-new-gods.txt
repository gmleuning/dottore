# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The battle of the old and new gods is the higher stage of the debasement of the natural: the genuine gods of classical art have free self-consciousness as individual spiritual powers, with knowledge and will, so that the human shape belongs to their very meaning. Mere personification of natural powers such as sun, sea and earth is insufficient, and the old nature gods must be overcome by the new spiritual ones.
