# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Frenzy is what the insane person's feeling of inner disruption can become. It may remain a quiet pain, but it can also rise to a rage of reason against unreason and of unreason against reason. The unhappy feeling is easily joined by suspicion and malice and by fury at the obstacle offered by surrounding actuality and by those who limit the sufferer's will; and so too the wilfulness of a spoilt person turns to insanity when the rational will sets up a dam that it cannot overleap or break through.
