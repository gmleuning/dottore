# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Religious love is the one form that meets the demand that absolute spirit, reconciled with itself, be felt and contemplated in art: the depth of feeling of a spirit free and satisfied in itself. Its content is the return out of another into self; its essence is to give up self-consciousness, forget oneself in another self, and in that surrender possess oneself.
