# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The historical development of painting must be considered because the art rests wholly on individual characters, situations and colouring, and demands sight of the works themselves. Only a historical arrangement orders their medley and shows painting's inner progress: from traditional static types to growing life, expression and individual character, to dramatic action, grouping and the full magic of colour, and the differences of schools.
