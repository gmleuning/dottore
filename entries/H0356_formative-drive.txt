# 356 Formative Drive (Nisus Formativus)
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › β. The Animal's Process of Assimilation
G: Bildungstrieb
Gloss: The animal's instinct to produce forms outside itself, as in building nests, webs and hives.
HGloss: The animal's instinctive art, its drive to give an outward form to what it needs: nests, lairs, webs, hives.
