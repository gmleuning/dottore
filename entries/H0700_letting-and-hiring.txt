# 700 Letting and Hiring

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Letting and hiring is the alienation of the temporary use of a property in return for rent, while the owner keeps ownership. It divides into the letting of a specific thing, such as a house, and the letting of a universal thing, money, where the lender remains owner only of its value.
