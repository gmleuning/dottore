# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mania or insanity is the third main form of derangement, and only in it is the deranged subject aware of being torn apart into two contradictory modes of consciousness. The sufferer vividly feels the contradiction between their merely subjective representation and what is objective, yet cannot give the representation up and insists on making it actual or on destroying what is actual. It need not spring from an empty illusion: great misfortune, the collapse of one's individual world or an upheaval of the state of the world can bring it about.
