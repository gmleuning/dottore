# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The measureless is that into which something based on a measure is driven when the mere change of its magnitude carries it beyond itself and it collapses. Abstractly it is quantum without inner order; in the nodal line each new measure is measureless in relation to the one before, yet is itself a quality existing for itself. In this alternation without end, both the specific relations and the merely quantitative progression are negated, and there emerges the infinite for itself: one persisting substrate beneath its changing measures.
