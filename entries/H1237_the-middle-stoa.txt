# 1237 The Middle Stoa
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 1. Dogmatism › a. Stoic Philosophy
G: Die mittlere Stoa
Gloss: The Stoics of Rhodes and Rome, Panaetius and Posidonius, who softened the doctrine and carried it to the Roman world.
HGloss: The middle Stoa of Panaetius and Posidonius, which moderated Stoic rigour and taught Cicero and the Roman nobility.

Def: Between the founders of the Stoa and the Roman Stoics the lectures name only briefly the later members of the school who became prominent, among them Diogenes of Seleucia and Panaetius, the teacher of Cicero.
