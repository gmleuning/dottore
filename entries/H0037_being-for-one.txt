# 37 Being-for-One
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self › a. Being-for-Self as Such
G: Sein-für-Eines
Gloss: The standing of whatever falls within a self-contained thing: it is there only as an aspect for that thing, not as an independent other; ideality.
HGloss: The ideality of the finite: what is in being-for-self has no being of its own, but is only for the one.
