# 37 Being-for-One

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Being-for-one expresses how the finite is in its unity with the infinite, as idealized. In being-for-itself negation is no limit and refers to no other existence, so that although something is called being-for-one there is nothing other for which it could be. That for which it is and that which is for it are not yet distinct: what is for-one refers in its other only to itself. Thus the I, spirit, or God are idealizations because they are infinite, and God is for himself in so far as he is himself that which is for him.
