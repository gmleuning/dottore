# 167 First Figure
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › a. Syllogism of Existence (Qualitative Syllogism) [Tetrad]
G: Erste Figur
Gloss: The form of inference that joins an individual to a general idea through a specific one: Socrates is a man, men are mortal, so Socrates is mortal.
HGloss: Singular joined to universal through the particular: S–P–U, the scheme of the understanding's reasoning.

N:
  first figure; S–P–U, singular–particular–universal; perfect syllogism
  S-P-U, the particular as middle, the perfect figure, the major and minor premise

V:
  subsume, apply, bring under
  mediate, conclude

Adj:
  perfect, primary, paradigmatic, standard
  first-figure, subsumptive

Adv:
  in the first figure
  through the particular

F:
  Latin: Barbara, Celarent, Darii, Ferio [mnemonic moods], figura prima
  Greek: prōton schēma, teleios syllogismos

Phr:
  all men are mortal
  Socrates is a man
  therefore Socrates is mortal
  all men are mortal, Socrates is a man
