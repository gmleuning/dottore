# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Beat and metre concern how single time relations are ranged into lines governed by rules for number and kind of feet and for beginning, middle and end, and then into stanzas. The great difference from music is the beat: poetry, which has its stability in the ideas it expresses, keeps a time measure but need not submit to a fixed beat.
