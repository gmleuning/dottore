# 956 Indian Subterranean Buildings
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › a. Architecture › i. Symbolic Architecture › 3. Transition to Classical Architecture › a. Indian and Egyptian Subterranean Buildings
G: Indische unterirdische Bauten
Gloss: The temples cut into living rock in India, at Ellora, Elephanta and Ajanta.
HGloss: The Indian cave temples, huge halls hewn out of the rock with columns and sculptured walls, the earth hollowed out rather than built upon.

Adv:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Indian subterranean buildings, as at Salsette and Ellora, are marvellous excavations that surpass anything built on the surface. Constructed for religious wonder and assembly, they have colonnades, elephants and colossal idols hewn out of the rock and left growing from the unworked mass, as the columns were left standing; some open entirely at the front, others are dim and torchlit.
