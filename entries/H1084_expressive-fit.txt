# 1084 Expressive Fit
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse › 2. Accent
G: Übereinstimmung von Bewegung und Inhalt
Gloss: The fit between the movement of the verse and what it says.
HGloss: The correspondence of the rhythmic movement with the content and its feeling, in which the verse's tempo and accent express the matter.

N:
  expressive fit; the fit of movement to content, sound echoing sense, expressive rhythm, mimetic rhythm, onomatopoeia

Phr:
  the sound must seem an echo to the sense [Pope]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Expressive fit is the correspondence that versification, especially rhythmic movement, must have with its subject matter and above all with a particular movement of feeling. The calmly rolling hexameter suits epic narrative, and with the pentameter the elegy; the rushing iambus fits dramatic dialogue; the anapaest suggests something spirited and joyful.
