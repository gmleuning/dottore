# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Instinct as a real process individualizes inorganic things and consumes them: the process with air in breathing and through the skin, with water in thirst, and with individualized earth in hunger. It is first a process with the elements, since the outer world is at first universal; the plant stops there, while the animal goes further.
