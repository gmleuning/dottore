# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Actuality is the unity of essence and concrete existence, of the inner and the outer, in which shapeless essence and unstable appearance have their truth. It is first the absolute as such, in which form has sublated itself; then actuality proper, whose formal moments are actuality, possibility and necessity; and last the unity of the absolute and its reflection, the absolute relation, or substance. It stands higher than being and concrete existence, as what manifests itself.
