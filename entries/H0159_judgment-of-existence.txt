# 159 Judgment of Existence (Qualitative Judgment)

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The judgement of existence, or qualitative judgement, is judgement in its immediacy, before any reflection or movement of its terms. Judgement should be truth, the agreement of concept and reality, but at first it is not so. It is also the judgement of inherence: the subject, as immediate, is the first and essential term, and the predicate is something without subsistence of its own that has its foundation in the subject. It runs from positive through negative to infinite judgement.
