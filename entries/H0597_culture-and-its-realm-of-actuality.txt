# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Culture and its realm of actuality is the formation by which the individual becomes what it is in itself and gains actuality and power in the measure that it is cultivated. Its actuality consists in sublating the natural self, so that original nature is reduced to a difference of greater or lesser energy of will. Here actuality is won only by what empties itself and becomes universal, and every fixed distinction of power and wealth, good and bad, noble and base, is found to turn into its contrary.
