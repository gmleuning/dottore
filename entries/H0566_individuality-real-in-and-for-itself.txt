# 566 Individuality Real In and For Itself

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Individuality that is real in and for itself is self-consciousness that has grasped its own concept, being certain that in itself it is all reality, and whose purpose is now the self-moving permeation of the universal, its gifts and abilities, with individuality. It no longer sets a purpose against an existing actuality that it must overcome: doing is its own truth and actuality, and the expression of individuality is its end. It appears as the spiritual animal kingdom, then as reason giving laws, and testing them.
