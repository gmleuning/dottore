# 566 Individuality Real In and For Itself
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › a. The Certainty and Truth of Reason
G: Die Individualität, welche sich an und für sich reell ist
Gloss: Individuality confident that its own activity is what is real.
HGloss: Individuality that knows its action to be the real, the interpenetration of self and being.

  self-realizing individuality, the real individual, the individual and its work, the interpenetration of individuality and universality
