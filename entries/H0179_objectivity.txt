# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept
G: Objektivität
Gloss: Thought realized as a world of self-standing objects; the kinds of order (mechanical, chemical and purposive) that belong to objects themselves.

N:
  objectivity; object, world of objects, objective world; externality, outwardness, thinghood, thing-world, the objective; realization of the concept; immediacy regained
  the realized concept, objecthood

V:
  objectify, objectivize, externalize, realize, embody, reify

Adj:
  objective, external, outward, real, realized, thingly

Adv:
  objectively, externally, in reality, out there
  in the object
