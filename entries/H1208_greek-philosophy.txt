# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Philosophy in the proper sense begins only with the Greeks, among whom educated Europeans feel at home. The standpoint of the Greek world is the spirit of freedom, a cheerful serenity of spirit, in which the subject is for itself. The freedom of the subject is the principle of Greek philosophy: the I that knows itself as infinite, and so thinking, in which the universal is active.
