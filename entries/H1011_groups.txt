# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Groups arise as sculpture proceeds to situations in movement, conflict and action, expanding a more concrete liveliness into opposition and reaction among several figures. Peaceful juxtapositions come first, then groups displaying discord and grief, which the Greeks bound to architecture, as in pediments. The Laocoon keeps nobility and beauty amid profound grief, yet belongs to a later age.
