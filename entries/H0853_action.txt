# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Action is the ideal moved out of the inactive repose of bliss into development, which involves one-sidedness, separation and opposition, so that even the gods must struggle and submit to fate. Greatness and force are measured by the opposition out of which spirit brings itself back to unity. Action requires a general state of the world, a determinate situation that brings strain into it, and the subject's response.
