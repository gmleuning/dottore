# 466 Objectified Delusions
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › ii. Derangement › 2. Madness Proper › c. Fixations
G: Ein Gerstenkorn oder ein Hund zu sein vermeinen
Gloss: The delusion that one is a thing or an animal: a barleycorn, a dog, a carriage.
HGloss: Madness whose content is something contingent, an object rather than a person: those who think they are a barleycorn or a dog, or that they have a carriage in their body.

N:
  objectified delusions; believing oneself a barleycorn, believing oneself a dog, a carriage in the body, believing oneself made of glass, zoanthropy, cynanthropy, boanthropy, clinical lycanthropy
  lycanthropy, transformation delusion, glass delusion

Adj:
  lycanthropic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Objectified delusion is madness whose content is determined contingently, by something other than the passion that gave rise to the disease: sufferers suppose themselves to be a barleycorn or a dog, or to have a carriage inside their body. The mind sticks fast in this merely subjective representation and regards it as objective, without any determinate awareness that the one contradicts the other.
