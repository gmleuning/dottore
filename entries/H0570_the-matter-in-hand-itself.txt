# 570 The Matter in Hand Itself

G: Die Sache selbst
Gloss: The thing that matters for its own sake, the shared cause in which every individual's work becomes everyone's concern.
HGloss: Die Sache selbst: the true work, the unity of doing and being, of individuality and objectivity, which becomes the concern of all and passes over into ethical substance.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The matter in hand itself, die Sache selbst, is the unity in which actuality and individuality permeate one another: the doing of this individual and pure doing as such, purpose and its passage into actuality. Its moments are sublated as valid on their own and count only as universal moments, and in it consciousness's certainty of itself becomes an objective essence. It is an object born of self-consciousness as its own, without ceasing to be free-standing and genuine, which distinguishes it from an ordinary thing.
