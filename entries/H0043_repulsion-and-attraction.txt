# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Repulsion and attraction are the two ways in which the difference of the one and the many refers them to each other. Repulsion is the one's excluding of the many from itself; attraction is the self-positing of the many ones in a one. Each first appears on its own, outside the other, but neither can be had apart. Repulsion, though negative, is essentially connection, since what is fled from still stands in relation, so attraction is within repulsion itself; and attraction presupposes repulsion, which supplies its material. In their equilibrium quality passes over into quantity.
