# 43 Repulsion and Attraction
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self
G: Repulsion und Attraktion
Gloss: The two relations among many units: pushing apart, which keeps them separate, and drawing together, which unites them.
HGloss: Repulsion, through which the one posits the many, and attraction, through which the many are one; each presupposes the other.
