# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The principle of Socrates is that human beings have to discover and learn from themselves what their vocation and final goal are, and what is true in and for itself; they must attain truth by and through themselves. It is the return of consciousness into itself, away from merely particular subjectivity. By irony and questioning he elicited consciousness of the universal, the good, the true, and the beautiful.
