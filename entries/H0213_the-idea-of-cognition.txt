# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea of cognition is the idea in its judgement: the concept exists for itself and has the concept as its object, its reality liberated into universality, whereas life was the concept still sunk in objectivity. Thought, spirit and self-consciousness are determinations of this idea. Hegel rejects the old metaphysics of the soul, with its substance, simplicity and immateriality drawn from an empirical subject, and Kant's critique of it.
