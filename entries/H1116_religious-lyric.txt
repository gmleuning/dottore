# 1116 Religious Lyric
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper
G: Religiöse Lyrik
Gloss: The lyric that lifts the soul to God.
HGloss: The religious lyric, in which the self raises itself to the divine and loses itself in it.

N:
  religious lyric; sacred poetry, devotional verse, religious poetry

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Religious lyric is the aspect in which the poet rises above the private character of personal ideas and feelings and sinks into universal contemplation of God or the gods, whose greatness and power pervade the whole inner life and make individuality disappear. Hymns, dithyrambs, paeans and psalms belong to it, developed differently by different peoples.
