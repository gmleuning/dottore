# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Necessity is what the intellect reaches when it takes the object as appearance with an interior that is for itself. That interior is a simple inner difference which stays equal to itself in the change of appearances, the realm of laws, and the essence of law is an inseparable, necessary inner connection of distinct determinations, as of crime with punishment. Speculative reason alone comprehends this unity, but the intellect already discovers it among phenomena and, finding in laws its own nature, becomes an object to itself.
