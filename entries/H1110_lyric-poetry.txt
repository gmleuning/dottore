# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Lyric poetry gives not the external thing but the poet's inner vision and feeling of it, the subjective side of creation predominating. Spirit turns from the objectivity of the matter into its own consciousness to display its presence in the heart's experience and imagination, in genuine feelings of universal validity, liberating the spirit not from feeling but in it.
