# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Painting is the art that first opens the way to finite and inherently infinite subjectivity, the principle of our own life, so that in pictures we see what is active in ourselves. Ancient sculpture leaves us somewhat cold, its sightless figures unable to reveal the life of the heart, whereas in painting God appears as a living spiritual person who gives each individual community and reconciliation.
