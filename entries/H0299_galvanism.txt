# 299 Galvanism
# Nature › 2. Physics › III. Physics of Total Individuality › γ. Chemical Process › b. Real Process (Combination)
G: Galvanismus
Gloss: The first stage of chemical process: two different metals joined through water act on each other and break the water down; the voltaic cell, understood as chemical action rather than as mere electric current.
HGloss: The process of metals: different metals in contact through a neutral medium, which is the first form of chemical process.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Galvanism begins the chemical process with metals, immediate and indifferent bodies distinguished only by specific gravity, which impart to one another their difference and enter into a still electrical tension. In the neutral medium of water, combined with air, their difference is actualized: a real activity arises between metal and water, passing from electrical to chemical process.
