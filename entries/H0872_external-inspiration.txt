# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External inspiration comes from an outside occasion, such as a commission, an event, or old sagas and chronicles, to which the artist of natural talent is solicited to give form. Pindar's odes were often commissioned, and buildings and paintings frequently prescribed. All that is required is that the artist grasp an essential interest and make the subject live in itself; then inspiration arises of its own accord.
