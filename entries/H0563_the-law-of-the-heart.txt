# 563 The Law of the Heart
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › a. The Certainty and Truth of Reason › iii. Self-Actualization of Reason › 2. The Law of the Heart and the Frenzy of Self-Conceit
G: Das Gesetz des Herzens
Gloss: The individual's demand that the world obey the law of their own heart, for the welfare of mankind.
HGloss: Das Gesetz des Herzens: the heart that holds its law immediately within itself and sets out to realize it against a world that oppresses it.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The law of the heart is the universal that a self-consciousness finds immediately in its own being-for-itself and holds to be necessary and valid for all, richer than the mere singular pursuit of pleasure. When the individual carries it out, the law passes into being: it becomes a universal order and a power indifferent to that heart. The individual no longer finds in it what was its own, and is entangled in an order not only alien but hostile, which its very deed has acknowledged as real.
