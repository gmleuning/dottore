G: Anaxagoras
Gloss: The philosopher who brought philosophy to Athens and made mind the cause that orders all things.
HGloss: Anaxagoras, with whom the understanding, Nous, is first recognized as the principle, the self-determining universal.

Adj:
  Anaxagorean

Phr:
  nous orders all things [Anaxagoras]
  like a sober man among random talkers [Aristotle, on Anaxagoras]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: With Anaxagoras the universal, objective element is for the first time said to be thought in and for itself: understanding, or Nous, is the orderer and cause of the world. Being, becoming, and the one are thoughts, nothing sensuous. The universal is defined as self-determining activity, which still has to develop itself purely on its own account.
