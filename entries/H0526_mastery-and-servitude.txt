# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mastery and servitude is the unequal recognition that results from the struggle: one consciousness, the master, is for itself and only recognized; the other, the servant, has life or being for another as its essence and only recognizes. The master relates to the thing through the servant and merely enjoys it. But because the recognition is one-sided, the master's truth turns out to be the servile consciousness, and servitude, forced back into itself, becomes through fear, service and work the opposite of what it immediately is.
