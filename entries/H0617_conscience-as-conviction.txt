# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conscience as conviction is the self-consciousness for which the essence of action lies in its own conviction of duty. Conscience keeps pure duty as the common element in which self-consciousnesses recognize one another, so that what is done from conviction is at once recognized and therefore actual. It knows that it cannot grasp all the circumstances of a case, yet counts its incomplete knowing, because it is its own, as sufficient.
