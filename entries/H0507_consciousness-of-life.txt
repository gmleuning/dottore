# 507 Consciousness of Life

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Consciousness of life is the threshold of self-consciousness. While it knows only laws, the intellect finds in its object a counterpart of the I but one still dead and unlike the I's activity. In beholding the living unity of what is distinct, where the differences are posited and sublated in the object itself, self-consciousness is kindled: the awareness of the simple ideal being that is its own object and is differentiated within itself, the I as the truth of what is natural.
