# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The quantitative infinite progress expresses the contradiction in quantum without resolving it. Quantum has a beyond of itself to which it passes; since it is continuous with that beyond, the beyond is again a quantum, which flees beyond itself in turn. The progress is the task of reaching the infinite without reaching it, a perpetual generating of the infinite that never gets past quantum and never has the infinite positively present, though the continuity of the terms gives it the appearance of a resolution.
