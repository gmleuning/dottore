# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of classical architecture is that spiritual meaning does not reside in the building itself but has attained free existence outside it, in sculpture or in living human activity, which architecture serves. The purpose then dominates the whole work and fixes its basic shape, so that neither materials nor the architect's caprice go their own way, and the architect's genius lies in fulfilling the purpose.
