# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Entoptic colours arise within a transparent body whose darkening principle is brittleness: brittle glass, though perfectly transparent, is their condition. Brittleness is here an immaterial punctiform determinateness, active only as a force, which becomes outwardly existent as opacity when a transparent crystal is reduced to powder, and so is the inner beginning of darkening.
