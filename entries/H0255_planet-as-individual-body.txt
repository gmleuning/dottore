# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The planet is the body of individual totality, in which rigidity opens into real differences held together by a self-like point of unity. Its rotation joined to its motion round the sun expresses its livingness. The planet is the true prius, the subject in which the differences are only ideal moments and life first has real existence; sun, moon and comets are its conditions.
