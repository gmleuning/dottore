# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first period of the German world: the barbarian migrations; Mahometanism, which sets against them the abstract worship of the One, pursued with an enthusiasm that is fanaticism; and the empire of Charlemagne, which gives the Frank dominion a systematic organization and settled institutions, developing arrangements partly already in existence.
