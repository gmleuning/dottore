# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Time is negativity posited for itself and indifferent to the inert side-by-side of space. As the negative unity of self-externality it is an abstract ideal being which, in being, is not, and in not being, is: becoming directly intuited, in which differences immediately sublate themselves. It is the truth of space and, like space, a pure form of intuition.
