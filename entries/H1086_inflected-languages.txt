# 1086 Inflected Languages
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse › 3. Sound of Syllables
G: Flektierende Sprachen
Gloss: Languages like Greek and Latin whose endings and long syllables carry the rhythm.
HGloss: The ancient languages, in which inflectional endings and natural length give the syllables a quantity independent of meaning.

N:
  inflected languages; inflection, endings, terminations, case endings, quantitative languages, the classical languages, Greek and Latin

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inflected languages such as Greek and Latin develop the root by declension and conjugation into many sounding syllables, which only modify it, so that the chief meaning and the sounding accent separate, as in amaverunt. The ear is then free to follow the sounds and shape their natural longs and shorts rhythmically.
