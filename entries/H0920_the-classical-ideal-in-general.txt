# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The classical ideal in general arises through the free transformation of earlier material: it is generated by spirit in the inward being of poets and artists, who took tradition as ingredients and drew the proper content and form from their own spirit. Its gods are serene and blessed, yet the depth of that serenity holds the negative, and they seem to mourn over their blessedness and bodily form, where their coming fate can be read.
