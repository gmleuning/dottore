# 413 Waking and Sleeping
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › α. Natural Soul › b. Natural Changes

Gloss: The alternation of the soul between conscious activity and rest.
HGloss: The soul's dividing itself from its natural immediacy: waking, its being-for-self, against sleep, its retreat into undivided substance.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Waking and sleeping are the alternation in which the individual soul distinguishes its being for itself from its mere being. Waking is the soul's own judgement, setting itself against its self-absorbed natural life as one natural state against another. Sleep is not a merely negative rest but an invigoration of waking activity. Unlike the passing differences of the ages of life and the fixed difference of the sexes, this opposition is an enduring alternation that produces itself in one and the same individual, and its empty repetition points on to sensation.
