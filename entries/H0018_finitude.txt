# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Finitude is the section of existence in which the negation present in something is developed. Something stands over against an other; it has a determination, a constitution and a limit; and as it carries that limit within itself as its own contradiction it is finite. Finite things are not merely limited: non-being constitutes their nature. Referring to themselves, they refer to themselves negatively and are driven beyond their own being. Their truth is their end, and their perishing is no mere possibility.
