# 736 Roman World (Abstract Legal Personhood)
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › γ. The State › c. World History [Tetrad]
G: Die römische Welt
Gloss: The world of Rome, in which, as in Greece, only some are free, and the individual counts as an abstract legal person under an impersonal state.
HGloss: The Roman world of abstract universality, where the person counts as a holder of rights and the state is a hard, alien power.
