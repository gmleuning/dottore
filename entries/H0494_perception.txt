# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Perception takes what exists for it as a universal, and its object is the thing with many properties. The thing is at once an Also, a passive medium in which indifferent properties permeate one another, and a One that excludes. Taking the object to be true and self-equal, consciousness knows that it can be deceived and puts every contradiction down to its own apprehending, then tries to correct itself. It learns that its perceiving is a turning back into itself out of the true, and that the dissolution of this perceiving is its result and truth.
