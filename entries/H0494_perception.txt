# 494 Perception (the Thing and Deception)
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › α. Consciousness as Such
G: Wahrnehmung
Gloss: Awareness that takes its object as a thing with many properties, and struggles to hold its unity and its many features together, at the risk of error.
HGloss: Consciousness that takes the object as a thing with many properties, and, failing to hold them together, deceives itself.
