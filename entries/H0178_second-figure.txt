# 178 Second Figure

G: Zweite Figur

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The second figure, particular, singular, universal, expresses the truth of the first: that something is joined to a universal quality not in and for itself but by means of a contingency, in a singular, which is therefore the true middle. Since a conclusion is mediated, it contains negation. One premise is already mediated by the first syllogism, so that the two presuppose each other, and the terms exchange places.
