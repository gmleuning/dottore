# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Greek profile joins forehead and nose in an almost straight or gently curved unbroken line, at a right angle to a line from the nose's root to the ear. In animals the mouth dominates as the organ of need; in the human face a second centre in brow and eye expresses spirit's theoretical relation to things, and the nose mediates between that and the organs of nourishment.
