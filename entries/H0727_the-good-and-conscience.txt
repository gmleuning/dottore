# 727 The Good and Conscience
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality
G: Das Gute und das Gewissen
Gloss: The universal good, and the inner certainty that knows it.
HGloss: The good, the idea as the unity of the will's concept and the particular will, and conscience, the subjectivity which determines it.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The good and conscience form the third moment of morality. The good is the Idea, the unity of the concept of the will with the particular will, in which right and welfare are superseded and preserved. Conscience is the subject's absolute inward certainty of itself, which determines what is good; the two still stand apart.
