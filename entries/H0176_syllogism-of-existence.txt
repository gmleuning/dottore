# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The syllogism of existence is the syllogism in its immediate form, whose terms are the abstract determinations of the concept not yet developed through mediation. Singularity and universality are its extremes and particularity stands between them as middle, uniting them immediately. It is the strictly formal syllogism, and formalism consists in stopping there. Its dialectical movement consists in positing the moments of mediation, through its figures.
