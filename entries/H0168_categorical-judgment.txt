# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The categorical judgement is the first, immediate judgement of necessity: its predicate is the universality in which the subject has its immanent nature, as in saying that the rose is a plant or that this ring is gold. What is necessary is the substantial identity of subject and predicate, so that it differs wholly from predicating an accidental property. Yet the subject's singularity still belongs to the immediacy of external existence.
