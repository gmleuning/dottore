# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Science of Logic is Hegel's exposition of pure thought, whose introduction declares that no science needs more to begin with the matter itself, without preliminary reflections. Its subject matter and method are not given in advance but generated in the course of the work, and what precedes it can only make its standpoint more intuitable. To study it, to dwell and labour in this realm of shadows, the world of simple essentialities freed from all sensuous concretion, is the absolute discipline of consciousness.
