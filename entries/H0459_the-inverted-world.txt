# 459 The Inverted World
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › α. Consciousness as Such › c. Force and the Understanding › ii. Laws
G: Die verkehrte Welt
Gloss: The topsy-turvy world: the world behind appearance turns out to be the reverse of the apparent one, where what is sweet here is sour there and punishment is really honour. Each side is the other's opposite.

  the inverted world; topsy-turvy world, world turned upside down, the upside-down world, inversion, reversal, mirror world, looking-glass world, antipodes, carnival, saturnalia, the world of opposites, Opposite Land, Bizarro world; the punishment that is honour, the sweet that is sour
  opposition, polarity, inversion &c H0218, counterpart
  eversion, subversion, reversion, contrariety &c H0092
  the world upside down, the second supersensible world

  invert, reverse, turn upside down, turn inside out, flip, overturn, stand on its head
  turn the tables, turn topsy-turvy
  subvert, turn topsy turvy, transpose, put the cart before the horse

  wrong side out, wrong side up
  opposite, inverse
