# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subjectivity is the concept in its beginning, formal or immediate. First the differences within its immediate unity are only a shine, so that they are the concept as such. Second, as absolute negativity it divides itself, and the moments, indifferent to each other, are the judgement. Third, the unity lost in the judgement is posited again through its movement as syllogism, the fully posited concept, whose completeness passes over from subjectivity into objectivity.
