# 34 The One and the Void
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self › b. The One and the Many
G: Das Eins und das Leere
Gloss: The unit and the empty space that separates it from others; the void as the nothing between units, as in ancient atomism.
HGloss: The one and its non-being outside it, the void, as the atomists set atom and void side by side.
