# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Pyramids belong to housing for the dead, the passage to building that serves an end beyond itself. Egypt first separates the living from the dead and preserves individuality, so that the spiritual is set apart as inner meaning and enclosed in a purely architectural shell. The pyramids, the oldest grandiose mausoleums, astonish by their size, their simple regular form concealing closed chambers.
