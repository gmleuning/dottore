# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ideal as such is beautiful individuality, gathered into itself as total subjective unity and independence out of the divisions of finite life. Among its basic characteristics are serene peace and bliss, self-enjoyment in its own achievement: the ideal work confronts us like a blessed god. This serenity is no easy contentment but the triumph of concrete freedom, kept even in the seriousness of grief.
