G: Die Metapher
Gloss: A comparison compressed into a single word or phrase, the image put in place of the thing itself.
HGloss: Die Metapher: an abbreviated comparison, in which image and meaning are not set apart but only the image is given and the meaning is read from it.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Metaphor is implicitly already a simile, an entirely compressed and abbreviated comparison that does not set image and meaning against each other but presents the image alone, extinguishing its literal sense and letting the intended meaning be recognized at once from the context, as in a sea of tears. Its meaning thus has only incidental artistic standing, as an adornment.
