# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The single statue has the proper function of sculpture itself, to provide images in temples, where its surroundings bear upon it. Here sculpture keeps its most perfect purity, setting the gods in beautiful, simple, inactive repose without a definite situation, or in free, naive ones. It departs from this when the position hints at an action's beginning or end.
