# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Vaisheshika of Kanada is a philosophy of the particular. It begins from substance, enumerating nine substances, from earth and water to soul and understanding, whose basic elements are atoms, and goes on to twenty-four kinds of quality and the further categories. Kanada's philosophy is thus atomistic.
