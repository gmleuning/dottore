# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Poetry as distinct from prose is not defined by collecting features from single works but derived from the concept of poetry, which coincides in general with that of artistic beauty. The poetic imagination, unrestricted by the material that binds the visual arts and music, answers only to the essential demands of ideal, artistically adequate representation, and differs from prose in treatment and in works.
