# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Action and reaction arise because causality presupposes an other on which to act: a passive substance, which is only in itself what the active substance is. The cause, acting on it, acts on itself as on another, sublating its otherness and positing the effect in it. Since the passive substance receives this as its own positing and reacts, becoming cause in turn, action is turned back on its originator, and the endless progress of causes bends into reciprocity.
