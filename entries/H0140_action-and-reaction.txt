# 140 Action and Reaction
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › γ. Absolute Relation › b. Relation of Causality
G: Wirkung und Gegenwirkung
Gloss: The relation in which an effect acts back upon its cause.
HGloss: The effect reacting on the cause, so that each passive substance becomes active; the cause is effect of its own effect.
