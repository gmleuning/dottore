# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Specifying measure proper is the determining of an external, indifferent magnitude by the qualitative side of the measuring something. A something with its own inner measure receives an alteration of magnitude from outside, but does not take it on as an arithmetical sum: it reacts against it as an intensive measure, assimilates it in its own manner and turns it into something else. The specified amount still varies with the external one, but in a constant way, so that measure exists as a ratio whose exponent expresses its specific character.
