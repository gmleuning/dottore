# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The totality of the solar system is matter's substance, gravity, developed into the totality of form, so that its self-externality is determined only by that totality. Matter thereby gains within itself the centre it only sought, determinateness of form, and becomes qualified matter, the sphere of physics: the solar system as a whole is what matter is now to be in detail.
