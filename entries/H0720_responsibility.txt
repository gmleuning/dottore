# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Responsibility (Schuld) is the imputation of a deed to the will as its own. Since the will acts on an external object in manifold circumstances, it is responsible in general for what it has altered insofar as the alteration is its own; this extends to damage done by things that are its property.
