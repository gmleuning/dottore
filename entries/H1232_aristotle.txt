# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In Aristotle the idea appears in its efficacy: what Plato's universal lacks, the principle of vitality or subjectivity, the activity of actualization, comes forward as energy and entelechy over against potentiality. At the summit stands Nous, the thinking of thinking, which of itself constitutes the nature of absolute spirit.
