# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The romantic subject matter of painting is the concentration of spirit in itself, the full range of deep feeling expressed in the external, which sculpture must renounce and music cannot show. Its centre is religious love without desire, reconciled and blessed, above all the Madonna's love for her child, and it reaches to deep oneness with everyday surroundings and the joy of independent existence.
