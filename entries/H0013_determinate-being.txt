# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Existence, or determinate being (Dasein), is the result of becoming: the simple oneness of being and nothing, which has the form of something immediate because its mediation lies behind it. It is not mere being, but being with a non-being taken up into it, so that the whole has determinateness. Through its quality, existent determinateness, something is opposed to an other; it is alterable and finite, and its negation, first opposed to the finite something, turns into the infinite and into being-for-itself.
