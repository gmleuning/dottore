# 569 Deceit

G: Der Betrug
Gloss: The game in which each pretends to serve the common cause while serving themselves, and so deceives and is deceived.
HGloss: Der Betrug: each claims the matter in hand as a universal concern while the others find they are dealing with someone's private affair; all deceive one another and themselves.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Deceit arises among individualities who each claim to be concerned with the matter in hand itself. One acts, and others suppose that it is the matter that interests this one and offer their help or announce that they have done it already; then they find that what was meant was only that individual's own activity, and feel cheated. Yet their eagerness was itself a wish to display their own doing, so that they meant to deceive in the same way. Action brings what is one's own into the light, where it becomes everyone's concern.
