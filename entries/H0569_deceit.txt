G: Der Betrug
Gloss: The game in which each pretends to serve the common cause while serving themselves, and so deceives and is deceived.
HGloss: Der Betrug: each claims the matter in hand as a universal concern while the others find they are dealing with someone's private affair; all deceive one another and themselves.
