# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ought is the in-itself of something in its negative reference to its own limit, now posited as restriction. It is a determination that has a being-in-itself over against negation, and at the same time a non-being that is distinct from that determination. What ought to be is and is not: if it were, it would not merely ought to be. The ought therefore has a restriction essentially, and the finite as ought transcends its restriction while remaining bound to it.
