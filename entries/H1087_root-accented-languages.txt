# 1087 Root-Accented Languages
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse › 3. Sound of Syllables
G: Stammbetonte Sprachen
Gloss: Languages like German in which stress falls on the root syllable that carries the meaning.
HGloss: The modern languages, in which the accent falls on the root syllable of meaning, so that sense rather than natural length governs the stress.

N:
  root-accented languages; root accent, root syllable, stem stress, accentual languages, stress-timed languages, the modern languages, the Germanic languages

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Root-accented languages such as modern German split what the ancients inflected into separate words, leaving the root a simple whole whose weighty meaning draws the accent to itself. Since stress then marks sense, it drowns the natural length of other syllables, and a rhythm independent of the root and its meaning can no longer exist.
