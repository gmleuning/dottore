# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The transition into essence is the self-sublation of indifference. As the totality in which all the determinations of being are contained and sublated, but at first only as a substrate in which differences are external, indifference is a contradiction. Posited as what it is, it is simple and infinitely negative self-reference, repelling itself from itself, in which determining is neither a passing over nor an external change. Its determinations no longer stand on their own but are only posited. Being which through the sublation of being is simple being with itself is essence.
