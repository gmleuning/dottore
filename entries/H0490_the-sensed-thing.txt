# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sensed thing is the object of sensory consciousness, which is aware of it only as a being, a something, an existing thing, an individual. It appears to be the richest in content but is the poorest in thoughts, its wealth consisting of determinations of feeling which the I separates from itself and first determines as being. The manifold content of sensations is gathered into a unit outside me, known immediately and individually, which comes into consciousness and vanishes again.
