# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Cynics made freedom and indifference, in thought and in actual living, the principle of the human vocation, over against all particular ends, needs, and enjoyments. Culture was to lead not only to inward independence from such things but expressly to dispensing with them, confining one's needs to what nature immediately requires: a flight from enjoyment.
