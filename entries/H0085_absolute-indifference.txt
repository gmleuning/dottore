# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute indifference is being become indifferent through the negation of every determinateness of quality, of quantity and of their immediate unity, measure, and thereby mediated with itself into a simple unity. Abstract indifference, such as pure quantity, merely lets determinations be external to it. Here determinateness remains only a state, something qualitative and external that has the indifference as its substrate; yet as this empty differentiation vanishes it proves to lie within the indifference itself, which thus ceases to be a mere substrate.
