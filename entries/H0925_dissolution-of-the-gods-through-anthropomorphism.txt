# 925 Dissolution of the Gods through Anthropomorphism

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dissolution of the gods through anthropomorphism follows because absolute necessity is no attribute of theirs: their particular side is given free play and runs into the externalities and finitude of human life, which pervert them into the reverse of the divine. The mind can no longer rest in them, since they lack infinite subjectivity, inwardness that knows itself, which true totality requires.
