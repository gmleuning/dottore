# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of independent measures concerns measures that have become self-subsisting because within themselves they are specified relations, so that they are things, physical and at first material. At first the two sides subsist in things external to each other and are combined externally. Yet these material beings are what they are qualitatively only through their quantitative determination as measures, and through it they are related to others in a series, their affinity, until one relation cuts itself off exclusively as elective affinity.
