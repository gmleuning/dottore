G: Formelle Kausalität
Gloss: Cause and effect taken in their bare form: the cause is a cause only in producing its effect and is spent in it, so that neither has meaning without the other.
HGloss: Cause and effect as the same content in different form: the cause is a cause only in its effect.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Formal causality is the relation of cause and effect taken first by itself. Cause is originative as against effect: substance, determining itself, posits what is determined and returns into itself, and this absolute activity is cause, the power of substance manifested, its effect being what is posited. The effect is substantial only by vanishing, and the cause is actual only in its effect, so that there is nothing in the one that is not in the other.
