# 148 Formal Causality
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › γ. Absolute Relation › b. Relation of Causality
G: Formelle Kausalität
Gloss: Cause and effect taken in their bare form: the cause is a cause only in producing its effect and is spent in it, so that neither has meaning without the other.
HGloss: Cause and effect as the same content in different form: the cause is a cause only in its effect.
