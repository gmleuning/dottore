# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sensuous certainty is the immediate knowing of what immediately is, in which consciousness receives the object without altering it or conceptualizing it. It appears as the richest and truest knowledge, but in fact yields the most abstract and poorest truth: it says of its object only that it is, as a pure this, to a pure I. Experience shows that neither the object nor the I is immediate. What is true of the this, the now and the here is the universal, and language, which says only the universal, cannot say what is merely meant.
