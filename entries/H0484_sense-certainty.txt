# 484 Sense-Certainty (Sensuous Consciousness)
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › α. Consciousness as Such
G: Sinnliche Gewissheit
Gloss: Awareness that takes its object as a bare "this, here, now," known directly through the senses; it claims the richest knowledge but can say the least.
HGloss: The immediate certainty of a this, here and now, the richest knowledge in appearance and in truth the poorest.

N:
  sense-certainty; sensuous consciousness; immediate knowledge, raw experience, bare acquaintance, knowledge by acquaintance, the given, the myth of the given, sense data, the this, the here, the now, this-here-now, deixis, ostension, pointing, the indexical, the ineffable particular; haecceity
  sensuous certainty, immediate knowing, the here and now, the meant

V:
  point, indicate, mean, intend, gesture at, sense, behold

Adj:
  immediate, sensuous, raw, unmediated, indexical, deictic, ostensive, ineffable, particular
  obvious, palpable, plain to see, staring one in the face, as plain as the nose on one's face

Adv:
  here, now, this, immediately, at first hand
  here and now
  firsthand
