# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inheritance is the transfer, upon the family's natural dissolution through the death of the parents, of what are in themselves common resources to individual ownership. Hegel rejects the unrestricted right to make a will, since the arbitrary will of the deceased deserves no more respect than the substantial right of the family.
