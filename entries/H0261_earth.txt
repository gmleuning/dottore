# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Earth, the element of developed difference and its individual determination, is distinct from the other moments at first as mere earthiness, still indeterminate. But as the totality which holds the different moments together in individual unity, it is the power that kindles them into a process and also sustains that process, the meteorological process of the earth.
