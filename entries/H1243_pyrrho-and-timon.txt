# 1243 Pyrrho and Timon
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 2. Skepticism
G: Pyrrhon und Timon
Gloss: Pyrrho of Elis and his disciple Timon, who taught suspension of judgment and calm indifference.
HGloss: Pyrrho and Timon, the older skepticism, which withholds assent and seeks imperturbability in indifference to all things.

N:
  Pyrrho and Timon
  Pyrrho, Timon of Phlius, Pyrrhonist, suspension of judgment, withholding of assent, non-assertion
  Elis, the Silloi, indifference

V:
  suspend judgment

Adj:
  Pyrrhonian, Pyrrhonic

Phr:
  no more this than that, ou mallon [Gr.]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Pyrrho counts as the founder of Skepticism, whose principle, the negative aspect of all things, is of great antiquity. He seems not to have taught publicly or left any school beyond a few friends he had educated. His follower Timon of Phlius, author of satirical poems, is of little importance.
