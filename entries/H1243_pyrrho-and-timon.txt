G: Pyrrhon und Timon
Gloss: Pyrrho of Elis and his disciple Timon, who taught suspension of judgment and calm indifference.
HGloss: Pyrrho and Timon, the older skepticism, which withholds assent and seeks imperturbability in indifference to all things.
