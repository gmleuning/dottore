G: Empirismus
Gloss: Philosophy that takes experience through the senses as the source of knowledge.
HGloss: Empiricism, which takes experience as the source of knowledge.

N:
  empiricism; British empiricism, experience as source of knowledge, ideas of sensation and reflection, induction, a posteriori knowledge, sensationalism, primary qualities, secondary qualities

V:
  observe, induce, derive from experience

Adj:
  empiricist, empirical, inductive, a posteriori

Adv:
  empirically

F:
  Latin: tabula rasa, a posteriori

Phr:
  nihil est in intellectu quod non prius fuerit in sensu [Lat.]
  nothing in the intellect that was not first in the senses
  experience is the teacher
