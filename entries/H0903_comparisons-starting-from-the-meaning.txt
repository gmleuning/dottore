# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Comparisons that start from the meaning take general ideas, reflections, feelings or maxims and find in the concrete world a picture that makes the abstract content perceptible. Because the two sides are indifferent to each other, their link is subjectively made and shows itself as such, so that the art of the poet as maker stands out; in genuine works such images should be only accessories.
