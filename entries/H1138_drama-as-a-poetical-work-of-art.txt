# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Drama as a poetic work, considered apart from staging, concerns its general principle, its particular characteristics and its relation to the public. It is the product of a developed and organized national life, presupposing epic and lyric as past, since both are combined in it and it is satisfied in neither alone, uniting them in a reconciling whole.
