# 149 The Determinate Relation of Causality
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › γ. Absolute Relation › b. Relation of Causality
G: Das bestimmte Kausalitätsverhältnis
Gloss: Causality among limited things with a definite content: the same content appears once as cause and once as effect, as rain and wetness, and each cause is itself an effect, giving an endless chain.
HGloss: Causality between finite substances: the cause is itself an effect of another, the spurious infinite of causes.
