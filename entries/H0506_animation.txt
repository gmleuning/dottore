# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Animation is life as consciousness meets it in the living creature: an interior that does not remain abstract but enters entirely into its expression, something mediated by the negation of what is external which turns that mediation back into immediacy. It is an external, sensory existence that is at the same time simply internal, a material being whose parts have lost their separateness, so that each individual is a member of the whole. Life must be conceived as an end in itself.
