# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The mysteries and bacchic enthusiasm are the cult in which the self-conscious people receives its god not as an empty depth but as an essence at one with the self. What is betrayed to consciousness there is still only immediate spirit, the spirit of nature: its life is the mystery of bread and wine, of Ceres and Bacchus, not that of the higher gods and not yet of flesh and blood.
