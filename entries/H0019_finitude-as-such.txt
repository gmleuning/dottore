# 19 Finitude as Such
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude
G: Endlichkeit als solche
Gloss: Limitedness taken seriously: a thing whose limit is part of its very nature, so that it is bound to pass away; mortality, transience.

  finite things &c H0016
  finiteness, limitedness, perishableness
