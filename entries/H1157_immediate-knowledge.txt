# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Immediate knowledge is the certainty that God, whom I represent to myself, also is, independently of me and of my knowing. Certainty is the immediate relation in which the content, though independent, is inseparably within me. Its first particular form is faith, certainty as it exists in feeling. Faith is commonly set against knowledge, but the opposition is empty, because what I believe I also know.
