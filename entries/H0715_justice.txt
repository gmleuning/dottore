# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Justice is right restored through the annulment of crime, as retribution: the injury of the injury. Since crime has a determinate extent, its negation is similarly determinate, but the equality it requires is one of value, not of specific kind. Where it takes the form of a particular will, as in revenge, it remains contingent.
