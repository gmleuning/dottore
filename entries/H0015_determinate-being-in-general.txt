# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Existence in general is what first proceeds from becoming: the simple oneness of being and nothing, which has the form of an immediate because the mediation that produced it lies behind it. It is being with a non-being, and the non-being is taken up into simple unity with being, so that the concrete whole has the form of being. This is determinateness as such. The word Dasein names being that is there, but the representation of space does not belong to the thought.
