# 15 Determinate Being in General
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › a. Determinate Being as Such
G: Dasein überhaupt
Gloss: Existence that carries a negation within it, since to be definitely one thing is not to be others; being and not-being held together in the form of simple being.
HGloss: Being with non-being taken up into its simple unity, so that its determinateness has the form of being.

N:
  determinate being in general; existence with a limit, being with negation; being and nonbeing united; finite existence; something rather than nothing; definite being, specific being; negation within being; inner limit, inherent bound; the being of a thing as distinct from other things; this rather than that
  being-there in general, determinacy
  quale
