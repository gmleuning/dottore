# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The theorem is the properly synthetic element of synthetic cognition, presenting the subject matter in its reality, in the conditions and forms of real existence, where the relations of its determinations are necessary. Because it joins diverse determinations whose unity is not yet posited, it needs proof. A first difficulty is to distinguish what belongs in the definition from what belongs in theorems, for which no principle lies ready, as Euclid's axiom of parallels shows.
