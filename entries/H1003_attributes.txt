# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Attributes proper in sculpture lose their independent and symbolic character and appear only beside the figure as external signs related to some aspect of a particular god, often animals, as Zeus's eagle or Juno's peacock, or tools such as the thyrsus of Bacchus. Antiquaries have pursued them pedantically, and since several gods share some, they do not always identify one alone.
