# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Science, the concept grasping itself, is spirit that has won the pure element of its existence. The differences of consciousness that burdened its shapes are closed: the moments no longer appear as shapes of consciousness but as determinate concepts and their organic, self-grounded movement, in each of which truth and self-knowing are immediately one. Time, the concept merely intuited, is erased when the concept grasps itself. To each abstract moment of science there corresponds a shape of appearing spirit.
