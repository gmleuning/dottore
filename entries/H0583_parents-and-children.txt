G: Eltern und Kinder
Gloss: The piety of parents toward children and of children toward parents, each seeing in the other its own passing.
HGloss: Eltern und Kinder: the parents' piety, moved by seeing their own actuality in another that grows as they fade, and the children's, who owe their being to a source that vanishes.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Parents and children are bound by a piety on each side that is affected by passing over into another. Parents are moved by the awareness that they have their actuality in the child and see it come to an independence of its own which they cannot take back. Children are moved by coming to be through an other who is vanishing, and by reaching their own independence and self-consciousness only through separation from their origin, a separation in which that origin recedes.

    — The Phenomenology of Spirit ¶455 (tr. Pinkard)
