G: Eltern und Kinder
Gloss: The piety of parents toward children and of children toward parents, each seeing in the other its own passing.
HGloss: Eltern und Kinder: the parents' piety, moved by seeing their own actuality in another that grows as they fade, and the children's, who owe their being to a source that vanishes.
