# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of outer and inner is the unity in which the difference of force and expression has become transparent. The inner is the form of reflected immediacy or essence, the outer the form of being, but the two are only one identity: one content, replete, which as inner is equally outer, and one fact that is indifferent to these determinations. What is only inner is only outer, and what is only outer is only inner. Their complete identity is actuality.
