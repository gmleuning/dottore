# 135 Relation of Outer and Inner
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › γ. Essential Relation
G: Verhältnis des Äußern und Innern
Gloss: The relation of inside to outside, in which what a thing is inwardly is just what it shows outwardly.
HGloss: The inner and outer as one content, so that what something is inwardly it is outwardly, and nothing more.
