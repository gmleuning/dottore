# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Originality is not merely following rules of style but inspiration that grasps a rational material and shapes it from within, in keeping with its kind of art and the ideal. It is identical with true objectivity, at once the artist's most personal inner life and the nature of the object, and must be distinguished from caprice, witty humour and irony. Having no manner has always been the one grand manner.
