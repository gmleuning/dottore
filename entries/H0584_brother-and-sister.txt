# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Brother and sister stand in the unmixed relationship within the family. They are of the same blood, which in them has come to rest and equilibrium; they neither desire each other nor owe their independence to each other, but are free individualities to one another, so that each can be recognized without the admixture of natural relation. This relation is also the limit at which the self-enclosed family dissolves: the brother passes out into the conscious ethical life of the polity, while the sister remains guardian of the divine law, and the two divide ethical substance between them.
