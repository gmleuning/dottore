G: Die Sestine
Gloss: The poem of six stanzas whose six end-words return in shifting order.
HGloss: Die Sestine: an artful form of the troubadours and Petrarch, in which the same end-words come back in changing order.

N:
  sestina
  end-word repetition, six stanzas, envoi, tornada, Arnaut Daniel

F:
  Italian: sestina

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sestina belongs with the sonnet to cultivated reflective poetry, brilliantly practised by the Italians. Since neither song's simplicity nor the ode's elevation is permitted in it, singability disappears, and in compensation the language itself, in its sounds and ingenious rhymes, becomes the notes of a spoken melody.
