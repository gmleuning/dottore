# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Hegel's system is the science of the Idea or the Absolute. Since what is concretely true is so only in unfolding itself and holding itself together in unity, that science must be a system. A system in this sense is not a philosophy resting on a restricted principle that shuts out others: it contains all particular principles within itself. Each part of philosophy is a circle closing upon itself, and the whole is a circle of circles.
