# 842 External Beauty
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › b. Natural Beauty
G: Die äußere Schönheit
Gloss: Beauty in the outward shape of a natural thing.
HGloss: The outward beauty of natural shapes, their abstract form and the unity of their sensuous material.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External beauty is the beauty of natural reality whose inner side remains indeterminate and abstract instead of attaining the concrete inwardness of soul. Its unity therefore appears as one determining the object from outside, and form and sensuous material fall apart. It is considered first as abstract form, in regularity, symmetry, conformity to law and harmony, and then as the simple unity of the sensuous material, its purity.
