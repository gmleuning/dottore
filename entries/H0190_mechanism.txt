# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mechanism is objectivity in its immediacy: the differentiated moments are complete, self-standing objects which, even in connection, relate as each on its own and remain external in every combination. Whatever connects them is alien to their nature, mere composition, mixture or aggregate. There is spiritual mechanism too: a mechanical memory, habit or mode of acting lacks the pervading presence of spirit in what it grasps or does.
