# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The substance of ethical life is what universal self-consciousness is the form of consciousness for. This mirroring, in which the concept knows itself in its objectivity as a subjectivity identical with itself, belongs to every essential spirituality: the family, the fatherland and the state. The unity of selves that are free and yet identical is present in the family, in love within its form of particularity, and in patriotism, the willing of the universal aims and interests of the state.
