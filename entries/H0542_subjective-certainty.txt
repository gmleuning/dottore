# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subjective certainty is reason as my singularity, no immediate singularity but a universal one, which determines itself in and for itself and so constitutes certainty. As the pure singularity of subjectivity, reason is certain that the determinations of self-consciousness hold objectively: that what I think in my own thoughts belongs equally to the essence of things. In nature the concept is neither certainty nor knowledge; only here does it have the form of reason.
