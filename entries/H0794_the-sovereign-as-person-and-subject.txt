# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sovereign as person and subject is the monarch, in whom the state's sovereignty exists as subjectivity certain of itself: the will's ungrounded self-determination, in which the finality of decision is rooted. It is this individual aspect that makes the state one; the monarch decides by saying 'I will'.
