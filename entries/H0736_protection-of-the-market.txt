# 736 Protection of the Market
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › c. Public Authority and Corporation › i. Public Authority (Police) › 2. Common Utility
G: Schutz des Marktes
Gloss: Oversight of the market for the buyer's sake: fixed prices for necessities, inspection of goods, honest weights and measures.
HGloss: Because goods of everyday use are offered to the public as a whole, the public authority may inspect them and fix the price of the commonest necessities (§236).
