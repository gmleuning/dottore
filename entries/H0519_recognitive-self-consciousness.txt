# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Recognitive self-consciousness is self-consciousness that is in and for itself only in being so for another: it exists only as recognized. For it there is another self-consciousness, so that it is outside itself, losing itself in the other and also seeing itself there. What either does to the other it does to itself, and a one-sided doing would be useless. In the pure concept of recognition each mediates itself with itself through the other, and they recognize themselves as mutually recognizing each other.
