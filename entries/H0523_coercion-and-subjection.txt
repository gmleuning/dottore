# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Coercion and subjection are how the combat for recognition ends. Since life is as essential as freedom, one combatant prefers life, keeps himself as a single self-consciousness and gives up the claim to recognition, while the other holds to relating to himself and is recognized by the first as subjected. Force is the basis in this appearance but not the basis of right: it is the outward beginning of states, not their substantial principle, and a necessary passage to universal self-consciousness.
