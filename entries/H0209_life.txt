# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Life is the immediate idea. It belongs in logic because the subject matter of logic is absolute truth, truth lies essentially in cognition, and cognition presupposes the idea in its immediacy, which is life. Logical life is to be distinguished from natural life, treated in the philosophy of nature, and from life as bound to spirit: it is life as idea, apprehended in pure thought, and has arisen through the concept's own necessity.
