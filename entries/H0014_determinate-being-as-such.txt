# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Existence as such is existence taken first by itself, before it stands in relation to an other. It emerges from becoming as simple immediacy, being with a non-being taken up into it, which constitutes determinateness. This determinateness is first distinguished as quality, which counts both as reality and as negation, and then is reflected into itself, so that existence is posited as something, an existent. Determinate being is concrete from the start, so that several determinations and relations of its moments at once emerge in it.
