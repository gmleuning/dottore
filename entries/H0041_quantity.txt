# 41 Quantity
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude (Quantity)
G: Quantität
Gloss: Being considered as indifferent to its limits, so that it can be increased or decreased without becoming something else; the unity of the continuous and the divided.
HGloss: Being-for-self become self-identical, indifferent to its limit; the unity of continuity and discreteness.
