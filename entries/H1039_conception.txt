# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conception, the painter's grasp of a subject, starts close to sculpture and architecture in isolated figures at rest and advances to specific situations. Since painting cannot show a succession of changes and can seize only one moment, that moment must show the whole situation in its bloom, concentrating what preceded and what follows, as in the moment of victory in a battle.
