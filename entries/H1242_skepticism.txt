# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism
G: Der Skeptizismus
Gloss: The philosophy that finds an equal argument against every claim and so suspends judgment, seeking peace in holding no opinion.
HGloss: The skeptics, the New Academy and Pyrrhonism, who suspend judgment by setting argument against argument.
