G: Der Skeptizismus
Gloss: The philosophy that finds an equal argument against every claim and so suspends judgment, seeking peace in holding no opinion.
HGloss: The skeptics, the New Academy and Pyrrhonism, who suspend judgment by setting argument against argument.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Skepticism has the reputation of being the most formidable adversary of philosophy, the art of dissolving everything determinate. It is the dialectic of everything determinate, and philosophical knowledge contains the same negativity; the difference is that Skepticism stands pat with the negative as its result, saying that what has an internal contradiction dissolves itself and is not.
