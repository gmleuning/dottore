# 809 Observation
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › a. Fancy
G: Beobachtung
Gloss: The artist's gift for seeing the shapes of the world and keeping them in memory.
HGloss: The first side of Phantasie: the gift and sense for grasping actuality, which sees much, attends closely and holds the many images of the world in a retentive memory.
