# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Constitution is the determinateness that belongs to something as external existence: what it has in it, but not as part of its being-in-itself. Constituted in this or that way, something is caught up in external influences and relationships, which appear accidental, although it is the quality of something to be given over to them. When something alters, the alteration falls on its constitution, the unstable surface of its otherness, while the something preserves itself and its determination is left untouched, until the two pass into one another.
