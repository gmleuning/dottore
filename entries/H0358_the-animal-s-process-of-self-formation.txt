# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In its process of self-formation the living structure converts its own members into a non-organic nature and means, lives on itself and produces itself, each member being both end and means. Nothing in it endures and everything is reproduced; each organ feeds on and gives itself up to the others, and the result is the simple feeling of self.
