# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The empire with which continuous history begins, its people being the first that is historical, subject to development and revolution and in the end to passing away. Its principle is light, a pure unity that governs individuals while leaving them free to develop their own character, so that many peoples of distinct customs are held together without losing their individuality.
