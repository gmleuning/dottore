# 1240 The Garden
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 1. Dogmatism › b. Epicurean Philosophy
G: Der Garten
Gloss: Epicurus and his school in their garden at Athens, a community of friends living quietly apart.
HGloss: The school of Epicurus at Athens, whose canon makes sensation the criterion and whose ethics seeks tranquillity in friendship and withdrawal.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Epicurus returned to Athens, bought a garden of his own, and taught there, living a regulated and very frugal life. No teacher was so loved and revered by his students, who lived in oneness and intimacy and stayed wholly faithful to his teachings, so that his school resembled a walled-in state that failed to develop.
