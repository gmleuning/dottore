# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Skepticism is the realization of that of which stoicism is only the concept, the actual experience of what freedom of thought is. It is thought which annihilates the being of the variously determined world, so that every determinate difference proves to be vanishing. Through this self-conscious negation it produces for itself the certainty of its freedom and an unchangeable self-certainty, ataraxia. Yet it is itself an absolute dialectical unrest, a medley of sensuous and thought representations, and this contradiction, once joined in a single consciousness, is the unhappy consciousness.
