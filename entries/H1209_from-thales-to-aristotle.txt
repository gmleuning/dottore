# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The period from Thales to Aristotle falls into three divisions. From Thales to Anaxagoras the absolute is first determined, ending in objective thought that determines itself. The Sophists, Socrates, and the Socratics bring in the principle of subjectivity. In Plato and Aristotle objective thought shapes itself into a whole, in Aristotle as the idea in its efficacy.
