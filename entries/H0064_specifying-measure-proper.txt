# 64 Specifying Measure Proper
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › α. Specific Quantity › b. Specifying Measure
G: Eigentlich spezifizierendes Maß
Gloss: Measure that modifies what it measures according to its own nature, as a body takes up heat according to its own capacity.

  multiplier
  the specifying measure in the strict sense, the modifying measure
