# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sensuous material of painting in its particularity includes linear perspective, which on a single surface renders distance by foreground, background and diminishing size; drawing, which gives objects distance and shape, its sculptural element; and above all colour, which makes a painter a painter, in chiaroscuro, where Leonardo and Correggio excelled, and in a play of reflections passing toward music.
