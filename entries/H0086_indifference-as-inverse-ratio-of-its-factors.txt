# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Indifference as inverse ratio of its factors is absolute indifference with its determinations posited in it. The substrate is one and indivisible, wholly present in its differences, which at first are only quantitative: two sides that make up a fixed sum, so that one grows as the other diminishes, each side containing both qualities with a more of one and a less of the other. But as qualitatively bound, each quality is only in so far as the other is, and reaches as far as it; every supposed excess of one factor vanishes, and the relation becomes a contradiction.
