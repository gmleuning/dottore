# 41 The One and the Void

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The one and the void are the first existence of being-for-itself. The one is the void as the abstract self-reference of negation, yet the void, as nothing, is absolutely diverse from the affirmative being of the one, and so stands outside it. Because both stand in one reference to the one, their difference is posited, and in this opposition of the one and the void being-for-itself has again acquired an existence, while its unity withdraws to one side.
