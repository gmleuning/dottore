# 618 The Beautiful Soul
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › b. Spirit as a World › iii. Spirit Certain of Itself: Morality › 3. Conscience, the Beautiful Soul, Evil and Its Forgiveness
G: Die schöne Seele
Gloss: A moral self that refuses to act lest it stain its purity, and wastes away in its own inwardness.
HGloss: The beautiful soul, which, lest it soil its purity by action, wastes away in yearning.

  the fair soul, moral purity, inwardness, moral fastidiousness, self-withdrawal, yearning, evaporation
