# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The beautiful soul is conscience that, raised above every determinate law, intuits its own divinity within itself and will not entrust itself to existence. Its certainty turns into a fading tone whose echo is all that returns to it. Lacking the force to relinquish itself and to make itself into a thing, fearing to stain its purity by action, it persists in powerlessness, a yearning that finds itself only as lost, until it dissolves like a shapeless vapour.
