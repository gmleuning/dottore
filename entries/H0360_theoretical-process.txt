# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The theoretical process is sensibility as outward process, a determinate feeling differentiated into manifold senses toward non-organic nature, free and disinterested, leaving the external object's existence untouched. The senses are feeling, for the mechanical sphere; smell and taste; and sight and hearing, in which the physical self is manifested in space and in time.
