# 1064 Epic
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › ii. Music › 3. Relation Between Expression and Content › c. The Execution of Musical Works of Art
G: Der epische Vortrag
Gloss: Performance that gives the work faithfully, the performer receding behind it as an epic rhapsode does.
HGloss: The first kind of execution, which gives itself wholly to the work and reproduces only what is there, like the epic reciter who disappears behind his matter.

N:
  epic; faithful performance, objective rendering, self-effacing performance, fidelity to the score, straight reading, Werktreue
  reproduction, delivery

F:

Phr:
  faithful to the score

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The epic manner of execution gives itself wholly to the work, as the rhapsodist lets personality retire behind the events reported, the more effaced the better. Where a composition has objective solidity, the performer must add nothing and be an obedient instrument, though not a mechanical barrel-organ player: life and soul must be given in the composer's sense, with technical freedom.
