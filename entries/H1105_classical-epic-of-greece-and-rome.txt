# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Classical epic takes us first into the truly epic world of art, above all in Homer, whose poems hover beautifully between the universal ethical order and individual character, spirit and nature, intention and outcome. Later Greek epic lost its naive freshness, and Rome, lacking an epic bible, produced the artistic epic, finest in the Aeneid, with historical and didactic poetry.
