# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ceasing-to-be is that direction of becoming in which being is the immediate and passes over into nothing. It is not an external destruction that comes upon being: being sublates itself and is the passing-over into nothing. Nothing, in turn, is just as much the opposite of itself, a passing into being, so ceasing-to-be is at the same time coming-to-be, and the two paralyze and interpenetrate one another.
