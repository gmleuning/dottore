# 21 Determination

G: Bestimmung
Gloss: What something is in itself and is meant to be; its vocation, kept up in its relation to others.
HGloss: Bestimmung, the in-itself of something filled with content and maintained against its relation to other.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Determination is affirmative determinateness: the in-itself by which something abides in its existence while involved with an other that would determine it, preserving its self-equality and holding on to it in its being-for-other. Something fulfils its determination in so far as the further determinateness that comes to it through its relation to the other becomes its filling. So what something is in itself is also present in it. The determination, or vocation, of the human being is rational thought, which is at first only in itself, as an ought.
