# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Tragedy's proper theme is the divine as it enters the world as concrete ethical order. Its powers, differentiated in action, collide: each side is justified yet can carry out its aim only by violating the other, and so incurs guilt. Eternal justice resolves the conflict by restoring ethical unity through the downfall or surrender of the one-sided individual, giving a sense of reconciliation.
