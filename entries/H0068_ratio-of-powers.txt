# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ratio of powers is the ratio in which quantum, self-identical in its otherness, determines its own passing beyond itself. The amount is determined only by the unit: it is the unit itself, so that a power is an aggregate of units each of which is that aggregate. The exponent is of a wholly qualitative nature. Here quantum fully realizes its concept; it has returned into itself and become quality again, and the truth of quantum is measure.
