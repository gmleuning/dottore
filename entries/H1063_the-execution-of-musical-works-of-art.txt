# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The execution of musical works is needed because, unlike a statue or picture, the musical work is presented only by a performer's action, as in drama the whole person comes alive on the stage. Corresponding to music's two directions there are two chief ways of performing: immersion in the given work, rendering nothing beyond it, and interpretation drawn chiefly from the performer's own resources.
