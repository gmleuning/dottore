# 863 Experience
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › a. Fancy
G: Empfindung
Gloss: The depth of feeling and lived experience through which the artist makes the material their own.
HGloss: The depth of heart (Gemüt) and of animating feeling (Empfindung), which pervades and quickens the whole and holds the content in subjective unity with the artist's inmost self.

N:
  experience; Empfindung, feeling, depth of feeling, Gemüt, heart, inwardness, lived experience, experience of life, a rich inner life, felt life, soulfulness, the animating feeling, concentration of heart

V:
  feel, live through, take to heart, put one's heart into

Adj:
  heartfelt, deeply felt, soulful, felt, lived, experienced

Adv:
  feelingly, from the heart

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Experience here is the concentration and depth of the artist's emotional life, through which a feeling penetrating the whole makes the material one's own inmost self. The artist must not only have looked about in the world but have drawn much that is great into their soul, been deeply gripped and lived through much; so genius may burst forth in youth, but maturity of the work comes only later.
