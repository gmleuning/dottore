# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Stoicism and Epicureanism, with Skepticism as their contrary, seek a criterion, a universal principle by which everything is to be determined and judged, and set up the figure of the sage who conforms to it. Their shared goal is freedom and independence of spirit, an imperturbability that has ties to nothing and is affected by nothing.
