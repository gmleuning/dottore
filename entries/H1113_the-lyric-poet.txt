# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The lyric poet, a poetic concrete person, is the centre and proper content of lyric, the inner link joining universal reflections and particular feelings into living individuality. The poet does not act or enter dramatic conflict but lends words to inner life so as to awaken the same spirit in hearers, and even a commissioned bard such as Pindar chiefly portrays himself.
