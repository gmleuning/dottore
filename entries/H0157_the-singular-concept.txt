# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Singularity is the concept reflected out of its determinateness into itself, the determinate determinate, in which it restores itself as self-equal in absolute negativity. Universality and particularity are in it what they are in and for themselves. Abstraction, which climbs from the singular to ever higher genera, goes astray: its highest universal is an empty surface, while the singularity it scorns is the depth in which the concept grasps itself and is posited as concept.
