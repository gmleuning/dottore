# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Clairvoyance is the immediate awareness in which, without any specific sense or any part of the body taking its place, an indeterminate sensation gives rise to a vision of something not sensibly near but distant in space or time. The soul in it is aware of its own world without having yet set that world outside itself as an object. Its phenomena all relate to the individual world of the feeling soul, and because its content is not set out in an intelligible connection, it is at the mercy of every contingency of feeling and imagining.
