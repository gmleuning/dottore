# ---- Hegel ----
Def: For Spinoza only absolute substance truly is, the unity of thinking and being, and the particular is something altogether limited. The great merit of this way of thinking, which must be the foundation of every authentic view, is its renunciation of everything determinate for the One; what is unsatisfying is that the One is fixed and immobile, and the free subject a mere transient modification.
