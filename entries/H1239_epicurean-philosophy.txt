# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Epicurus treats first the criterion of truth, then nature, then morals. His logic, the canon, makes sensation the first moment of the criterion, with anticipation and opinion; sensation by itself cannot be refuted. In morals the aim of all we do is to avoid pain and disturbance and to attain peace and equanimity, so that not every pleasure is chosen.
