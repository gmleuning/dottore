G: Der Takt
Gloss: The bar, a fixed group of beats with its own stress, repeated through the music.
HGloss: Der Takt: the regular repetition of equal units of time, given a definite unity by stress, which orders the uniformity of time-measure into a rule.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The bar establishes a definite unit of time as the measure and rule of the succession and of the notes' arbitrary durations, and renews it in uniform repetition, as regular columns do in architecture. In this uniformity self-consciousness finds itself again, the more fully since the unity belongs neither to time nor to the notes but is put into time by the self.
