# 177 First Figure
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › a. Syllogism of Existence (Qualitative Syllogism)
G: Erste Figur
Gloss: The form of inference that joins an individual to a general idea through a specific one: Socrates is a man, men are mortal, so Socrates is mortal.
HGloss: Singular joined to universal through the particular: S–P–U, the scheme of the understanding's reasoning.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first figure, singular, particular, universal, is the general schema of the determinate syllogism: the singular is connected with the universal not immediately but through particularity, and the universal descends to the singular through it. Its meaning is that the singular, otherwise only inward, emerges through particularity into existence. But the connection is still subjective, and its premises, themselves unmediated, demand proof, which leads to the further figures.
