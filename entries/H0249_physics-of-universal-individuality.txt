# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The physics of universal individuality treats matter as qualified in its universal physical forms: the free physical bodies, which are the heavenly bodies now physically determined, then the physical elements, related to the unity of their totality, and the meteorological process that engenders what is individual in them. Matter here differentiates and determines itself, and exists only through its qualities.
