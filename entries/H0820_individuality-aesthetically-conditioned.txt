# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The three forms in which beautiful individuality realizes itself, all of them works of art: the culture of the human being, the shaping of the world of gods, and the constitution of the state. In each the natural is only the starting point and material that spirit works into its own expression, the state uniting the subjective and the objective work as a living, self-conscious universal.
