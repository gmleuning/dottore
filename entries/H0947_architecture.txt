# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Architecture is treated first among the particular arts, as art's beginning in concept and in existence. That beginning is not to be found in historical conjectures or charming tales about a cavern or a tree trunk, which make origins seem intelligible without deriving them from the concept. It must be established by deriving it from the very concept of art, whose content now emerges into real, external existence.
