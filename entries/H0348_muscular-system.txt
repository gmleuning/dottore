# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The muscular system arises as the sensibility of the outer organism passes into irritability and opposition. The muscle's activity is the real, material, mechanical relation to inorganic nature: organic elasticity, which when stimulated withdraws into itself, then negates this yielding and restores itself, pushing against itself in linear movement, in contraction and extension.
