# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ganglionic system, especially the sympathetic nerves, is sensibility withdrawn into itself, a sphere of dull, indeterminate self-feeling without will, not developed into specific senses. Its ganglia are like little brains in the abdomen, not wholly independent of brain and spinal cord, yet distinct in function and structure, withdrawing the internal organs from the will.
