# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Roman architecture generally differs wholly in range and character from the Greek: the Romans were skilful in the mechanics of building and made richer, more magnificent works with less nobility and grace. New purposes arose, theatres, amphitheatres and the luxurious villas and baths of private life, whereas the Greeks lavished beauty only on public buildings. It later served Italians and French as a model.
