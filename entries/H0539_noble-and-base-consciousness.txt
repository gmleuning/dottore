# 539 Noble and Base Consciousness

G: Edelmütiges und niederträchtiges Bewusstsein
Gloss: The attitude that respects and serves state and wealth, and the attitude that resents and despises them.
HGloss: The noble consciousness which finds itself in state power and wealth, and the base which sees in them only oppression.
