# 596 The World of Self-Alienated Spirit

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The world of self-alienated spirit is the real world in which the individual has validity and actuality only through cultural formation, by relinquishing natural being. Its powers are state power and wealth, judged as good and bad, with a noble and a base consciousness relating to them; its mediation runs through service, counsel and language. Each moment inverts itself into its opposite, so that this world ends in disrupted consciousness and its vanity, and over against it there stand faith and pure insight.
