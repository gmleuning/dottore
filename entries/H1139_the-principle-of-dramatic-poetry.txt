# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The principle of dramatic poetry is the presentation of actual human actions by persons expressing them in words. Dramatic action is no undisturbed carrying out of an aim but rests entirely on collisions of circumstances, passions and characters, leading to actions and reactions that require resolution, and ends in a peaceful resolution that arises from the whole play of will.
