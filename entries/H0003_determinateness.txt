# 3 Determinateness (Quality)
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being
G: Qualität
Gloss: The character that makes a thing what it is, so that if the character changes the thing becomes something else; the most basic kind of definiteness.
HGloss: Determinateness immediately one with being, so that a thing ceases to be what it is when its quality changes.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Determinateness, or quality, is the first section of the doctrine of being. Being as the indeterminate immediate is free of determinateness, being without quality; yet its indeterminateness belongs to it only in opposition to what is determinate, and so itself constitutes its quality. The first being thus shows itself determinate in itself and passes into existence; existence, as finite, sublates itself into the infinite reference of being to itself, and passes into being-for-itself.
