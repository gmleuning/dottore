# 3 Determinateness (Quality)
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being
G: Qualität
Gloss: The character that makes a thing what it is, so that if the character changes the thing becomes something else; the most basic kind of definiteness.
HGloss: Determinateness immediately one with being, so that a thing ceases to be what it is when its quality changes.
