# 758 Immediate Seizure
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › a. The System of Needs
G: Unmittelbare Besitznahme
Gloss: Taking what nature offers without working it: gathering, foraging, hunting, scavenging.
HGloss: The satisfaction of need by direct seizure of what nature gives; Hegel notes how little such material there is, since even air must be warmed and only water is drunk as found (§196 Zusatz).
