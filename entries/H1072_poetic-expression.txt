# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Poetic expression covers the poetic way of imagining, diction and the music of words. Words are neither symbols nor spatial embodiments but signs, which in poetry must be made an end for contemplation. The origin of poetic speech lies not in the choice of words or in sound but in the kind of imagining, though it exists only in words that are spoken.
