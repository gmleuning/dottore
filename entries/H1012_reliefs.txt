# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reliefs are the mode in which sculpture takes a long step toward painting: figures stand on one surface and sculpture's third dimension begins to vanish, though ancient relief keeps to the plane without perspective, favouring figures in profile and actions that proceed along a single line, such as processions. Reliefs adorn walls and vessels in the greatest variety.
