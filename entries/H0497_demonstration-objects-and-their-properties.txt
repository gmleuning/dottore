# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Demonstration, objects and their properties belong to perception, which begins to show things as true by exhibiting how they hang together. Because the individual remains the underlying being over against the universal to which it is only related, the connection is a mixture and a many-sided contradiction: between the single things of sense that are to ground universal experience and the universal that is to be their essence, and between the independent individual and its many properties, which are free of it and of one another.
