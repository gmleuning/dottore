# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sculpture is the art in which spirit itself enters as content, withdrawing from the inorganic matter that architecture could only make reflect it. Spirit here grasps itself by expressing itself in bodily form and shapes spiritual individuality in solid matter, stone, wood, metal or clay, in all three spatial dimensions, in the shape appropriate to spirit, which is its own body.
