# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit is reason whose certainty of being all reality has been raised to truth, conscious of itself as its world and of the world as itself. It is ethical actuality, the self of actual consciousness confronting itself as an objective world no longer alien to it. It is the unshakable ground and the purpose of the doing of each and all, and also the universal work that arises through that doing. Its shapes are those of a world: true ethical spirit, self-alienated culture, and spirit certain of itself in morality.
