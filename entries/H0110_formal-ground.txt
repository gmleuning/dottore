# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Formal ground is the ground-connection in which ground and grounded have one and the same content, considered once as ground and once as grounded. The content is indifferent to these two forms and is simply one determination in each, so that the difference between the sides is one of form alone. Each passes over into the other and posits it: the mediation can begin equally from either. To explain something by such a ground is to say the same thing twice.
