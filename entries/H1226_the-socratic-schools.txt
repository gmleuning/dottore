# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Socratics are those disciples who remained relatively close to Socrates in their way of philosophizing. Leaving Plato aside, they offer only a more abstract grasp of the Socratic mode, more definite and therefore more one-sided, in a diversity of types. That conflicting philosophies issued from his teaching follows from the abstractness and indefiniteness of his principle.
