# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Enlightenment is the diffusion of pure insight, which, born of substance, knows the pure self of consciousness as absolute and takes it up into the consciousness of all actuality. At first without content, it gives itself one by its negative movement against faith. It strips faith of its content and leaves it an empty longing, and divides itself between pure thought and pure matter, finding its positive truth in utility, out of which absolute freedom arises.
