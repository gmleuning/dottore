# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Consciousness as such is the first series of shapes in which consciousness takes its object to be what is essential, something other than itself that is there whether or not it is known. It begins as sensuous certainty, which holds to the immediate this; learns that its truth is universal and so becomes perception, which takes the thing with its properties; and passes into the understanding, which seeks the inner truth behind appearance in force and law, until in that inner it comes upon itself.
