# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The painter's craft is the manner in which subject matter is conceived and executed in painting's material. It divides into the general modes of conception, advancing toward ever richer liveliness; composition, the pictorial motives of the chosen situation and grouping; and characterization, which proceeds from differences in the objects portrayed and in the way they are conceived.
