# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Doric belongs to the stage where security of the building is the chief concern, with heavy forms in which load-bearing material and gravity dominate. Its columns are the broadest and lowest, giving an impression of simple, serious, unadorned masculinity; standing mostly without bases, with the simplest capitals, it remains nearest to timber building, as triglyphs and metopes show.
