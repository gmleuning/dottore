# 559 The Being of Spirit Is a Bone

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The proposition that the being of spirit is a bone is where observing reason ends: the certainty of reason seeking itself as an objective actuality says that spirit is, that it is a thing. Its true meaning is the infinite judgement that the self is a thing, a judgement which cancels itself, since the thing is thereby shown to be the unity of the I and being. Held fast as an enduring proposition by representational thought, which fixes self and thing apart, it is reason grasped irrationally.
