G: Der Apolog
Gloss: A short tale used as an example to teach a moral, where the meaning is told openly.
HGloss: Der Apolog: a parable in which a single case is presented as an example of a general meaning that it does not merely signify but contains.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: An apologue, or moral fable, may be seen as a parable that does not use the individual case merely as a simile to illustrate a universal meaning but expresses the maxim within that cloak, the maxim being contained in the case told as an example. Goethe's God and the Bayadere is one, and the narrative may end by giving its lesson without comparison.
