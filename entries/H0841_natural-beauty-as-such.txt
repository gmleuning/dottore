# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Natural beauty as such is the idea in its immediate existence as life. Where the concept sinks wholly into matter, as in a metal, no ideal unity appears; in a system such as the solar system the bodies hang together yet the unity stays implicit; only in the living organism do the distinct members exist as moments of one animating subjective unity. Beauty in nature is this life as it appears to a beholder.
