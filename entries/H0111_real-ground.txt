# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Real ground is ground in which ground and grounded have different contents, so that the connection ceases to be a tautology: whoever asks for a ground now demands another determination of content than the one whose ground is sought. The grounded contains the content of the ground and also a content of its own. Since the two determinations are indifferent to each other, their unity is only an empty linking by a something that holds them together from outside, and so the connection is no true mediation.
