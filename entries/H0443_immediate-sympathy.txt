# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Immediate sympathy is the highest degree of the rapport between subjects, in which the clairvoyant knows, sees and feels not merely about another but in another. Without attending to the other individual, the subject immediately shares that person's sensations of everything that happens to them, and has the sensations of the other's individuality as their own. The two spheres of life have become one, a substantial identity of which the soul, as truly immaterial, is capable.
