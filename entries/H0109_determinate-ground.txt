# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Determinate ground is ground that has a content. A content, indifferent to the form of the connection, serves as the substrate in which ground and grounded are one, and the determinateness of that content gives the ground its definiteness. It is first formal ground, where the same content stands on both sides; then real ground, where the two sides differ in content; and finally complete ground, which holds both in one and passes into condition.
