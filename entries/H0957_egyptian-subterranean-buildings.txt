# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Egyptian subterranean buildings, with those of India, are excavations in upper Egypt and Nubia that rival and surpass surface works. Because excavation starts from what is already there and leaves the main mass alone, it lacks the freedom of building above ground, so that the enormous erections on the surface may be regarded as imitations and blossomings of the subterranean.
