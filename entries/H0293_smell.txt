# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Smell is the body's specific individuality as a simple theoretical process with air, the invisible destroyer: the combustible principle of the body relates to air and is silently volatilized in it as odour. Conceived as a separate odorous matter this property is oil, which burns as flame; as a mere property it exists in the smell of metals.
