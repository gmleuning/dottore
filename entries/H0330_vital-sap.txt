# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Vital sap is the sap now assimilated and fully organized, whose movement Hegel counts an original and important discovery by Schultz, though it is hard to observe in every plant. It circulates in a closed system through all the external parts, and by this circulation the many individuals that make up a plant are combined into a single individual.
