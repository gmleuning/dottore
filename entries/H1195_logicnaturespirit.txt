# 1195 Logic–Nature–Spirit
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › α. The Concept of Philosophy (the Three Syllogisms)
G: Logik–Natur–Geist
Gloss: The order of the system that runs from pure thought through nature to mind: thought expresses itself in the world and returns to itself in mind.
HGloss: The first syllogism, logic–nature–spirit, which has nature as the middle.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the first syllogism the logical is the ground and starting point, and nature the middle that joins spirit with the logical: the logical becomes nature and nature becomes spirit. Nature does not separate the extremes, being essentially a point of transit within the idea; but the mediation has the external form of transition, so that science takes the form of a necessary progression, and freedom appears only in one extreme.
