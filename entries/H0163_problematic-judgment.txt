# 163 Problematic Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment [Tetrad] › d. Judgment of the Concept
G: Problematisches Urteil
Gloss: A judgment of value stated as uncertain: "this house may be good."
HGloss: The subject judged according to whether it corresponds to its concept, so the judgment is only possibly true.
