G: Der Rhythmus
Gloss: Rhythm, the living play of stresses and durations within and against the bar.
HGloss: Der Rhythmus: the animation of the bar by accents, the free movement that enlivens the abstract rule of the measure.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rhythm alone properly animates time and bar, asserting the essential divisions within abundant variation. Accent laid on certain parts of the bar gives each kind its own rhythm; in accompaniment it must not clash with poetic metre; and the melody's animated rhythm is free from the bar's strict return, its sharpest counter-thrust in syncopation, so that it avoids pedantry and monotony.
