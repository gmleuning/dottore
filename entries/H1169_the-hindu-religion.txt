# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the Hindu religion the one substance is represented in and through the whole external world, in which everything particular is merely accidental. The substance particularizes itself into many powers, spiritual and natural, which appear independent and then vanish back into the One. This formless unity is Brahman; distinguished within itself it is three in one, and the deities also appear in many incarnations.
