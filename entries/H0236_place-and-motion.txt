# 236 Place and Motion (Union of Space and Time)

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Place and motion are the unity of space and time. Each collapses into the other, for space is a contradiction of indifferent asunderness and continuity and passes into time, and time falls back into space. The point made concrete through time is place, the here that is also a now; and place passing into another place, with time positing itself spatially, is motion.
