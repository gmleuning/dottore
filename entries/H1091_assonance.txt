# 1091 Assonance
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › ii. Rhyme
G: Assonanz
Gloss: The rhyme of vowels alone, as in Spanish romances.
HGloss: Assonance, the echo of vowels, especially in Spanish verse, between alliteration and full rhyme.

N:
  assonance; vowel rhyme, near rhyme
  assonance, consonance, half-rhyme

V:
  assonate

Adj:
  assonant

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Assonance stands between alliteration and full rhyme: it repeats the same letters, chiefly vowels, in the middle or at the end of different words, not initial letters. It need not close a line, though final syllables especially enter into it, and it flourishes richest among Latin peoples, above all the Spaniards with their sonorous language.
