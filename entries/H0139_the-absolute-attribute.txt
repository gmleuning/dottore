# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The absolute attribute is the relative absolute, the absolute in a determination of form. Since the form is the absolute's own, the attribute contains the whole content of the absolute, a totality that earlier appeared as a world or as one side of the essential relation, now its single true subsistence. But the determination that makes it an attribute is posited as unessential, a mere reflective shine; so the attribute is the absolute only in the determination of identity.
