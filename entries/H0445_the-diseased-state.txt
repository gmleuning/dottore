# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The diseased state of the soul is the separation of its soul side from mental consciousness. As bodily illness consists in one organ or system fixing itself against the harmony of the whole, so the soul falls ill when its soul side, independent of the mind's power, usurps the mind's function, and the mind sinks into that form and gives up its mediated relation to the actual world. The soul side then takes on the semblance of being what the mind truly is. This illness is more or less bound up with bodily illness.
