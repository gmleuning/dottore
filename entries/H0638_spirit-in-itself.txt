# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit in itself, represented in the element of pure thinking, is the simple eternal self-equal essence, which is absolute spirit. Were it to remain a simple essence it would be spirit only in empty words, for spirit is to be actual. As abstraction it is negativity in itself, absolute difference from itself, pure becoming other. Representation tells this necessity of the concept as an event: the eternal essence creates an other and in that otherness immediately returns to itself.
