# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Marble, with its soft purity, whiteness, absence of colour and delicate sheen, harmonizes most directly with sculpture's aim, and its grain and gentle infusion of light surpass gypsum. The Greeks used it above all in the age of Praxiteles and Scopas, when art passed into softer grace, since its translucency favours soft outlines and shows fine shadows.
