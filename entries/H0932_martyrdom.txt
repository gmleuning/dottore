G: Die Martyrien
Gloss: Suffering endured for the faith, in which the body is given up to prove the spirit.
HGloss: Die Martyrien: the endurance of pain and torment for faith, the negation of the natural self carried out in the flesh, which in art can turn to the cruel and merely horrible.
