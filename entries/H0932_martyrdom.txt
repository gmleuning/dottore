# 932 Martyrdom

G: Die Martyrien
Gloss: Suffering endured for the faith, in which the body is given up to prove the spirit.
HGloss: Die Martyrien: the endurance of pain and torment for faith, the negation of the natural self carried out in the flesh, which in art can turn to the cruel and merely horrible.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Martyrdom is the endurance of cruelty and freely willed renunciation, sacrifice and privation, through which spirit is to be transfigured, grief being an end in itself and the glory measured by the horror suffered. Physical torture inflicted by persecutors or self-imposed provides its chief topic, so that beauty is easily damaged, such repugnant externals being too far from it for healthy art to choose them.
