# 1029 Romantic Arts
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts
G: Die romantischen Künste
Gloss: The arts that express the inner life rather than the body.
HGloss: The romantic arts, painting, music and poetry, in which the inner life of the subject is expressed.

N:
  romantic arts; painting, music, and poetry, the arts of inwardness, the subjective arts
  the arts of subjectivity, painting music and poetry, the art of the soul, inner life, interiority, feeling, the heart

V:

Adj:

Adv:
  inwardly

Phr:
  the arts of the soul

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The romantic arts, painting, music and poetry, arise from the principle of subjectivity, spirit withdrawing out of the external world into an inner existence, so that it no longer coincides indissolubly with its body. The unity achieved by sculpture dissolves, its factors becoming independent: the divine grasped as subject and personality, and the human subject unfolding the whole heart, reunited through the common principle of subjectivity.
