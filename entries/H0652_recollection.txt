# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Recollection is the first and most abstract stage of representation. Intelligence places the content of intuition in its own inner space and time, making it an image; the image is preserved unconsciously; and at last it is related to a present intuition of the same content and recognized as one's own. By being summoned up often it grows vivid enough to need no outer intuition, and so children pass from intuiting to recollecting.
