# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The real mechanical process takes up the result of the formal one: the objects and the process now stand in a more determinate relation, differing definitely from one another. The opposition is distributed among objects as self-standing singularity and dependent universality, the weaker yielding to the stronger only so far as it accepts it and forms one sphere with it. Communication is followed by resistance, and the weaker is overcome by violence.
