# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Roman religion of expediency or purposiveness unites the religions of beauty and of sublimity, so that both lose their one-sidedness and also their higher content. The gods are reduced to means, and the One gives way to an empirical, universal aim. That aim is Rome itself: Jupiter Capitolinus is the god who makes Rome dominant, an abstract power and not the genuine spiritual One.
