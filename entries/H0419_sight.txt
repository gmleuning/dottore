# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sight is the sense of the physical ideal that we call light, which is, as it were, space become physical, weightless and without inwardness, whose essence is to manifest its other. It is concerned only with this ideal element and with its darkening, that is, with colour. It lets things subsist calmly and relates itself only to their ideal side, so that its relation to them is theoretical and not yet practical, and for this it may be called the noblest sense. It is also very imperfect, since it gives a body only as surface and not at once as a spatial whole.
