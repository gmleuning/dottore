# 419 Sight
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › α. Natural Soul › c. Sensation › i. The Senses › 1. Sight and Hearing
G: Gesicht
Gloss: The sense of light and colour, which takes in its objects at a distance and leaves them as they are.
HGloss: Gesicht, the sense of physical ideality, related to light, the ideal physical element; it lets its object be.
