# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Beauty of abstract form is natural form that is determinate, and so restricted, yet holds a unity and abstract relation to itself. It regulates the external manifold by that determinacy, which never becomes an immanent inwardness or soul that bears the shape, but remains an external determinacy imposed on it. Its kinds are regularity and symmetry, then conformity to law, and finally harmony.
