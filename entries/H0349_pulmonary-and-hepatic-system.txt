# 349 Pulmonary and Hepatic System
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › α. The Animal's Formation (Shape) › b. Systems of Shape › ii. Blood System
G: Lungen- und Lebersystem
Gloss: The lungs and liver, organs that refine and process the blood.
HGloss: The lungs and liver, the blood's process with air and the inward digestive fire.

N:
  pulmonary and hepatic system
  the pulmonary organs, the hepatic organs

Adv:
  by lung and liver

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Lungs and liver are the two systems into which the blood's circulation divides. The lung is the animal leaf, related to the atmosphere in breathing out and in; the liver is the return into being-for-self, a heat that rages against otherness and consumes it. The two are intimately connected, each tempering or vivifying the other.
