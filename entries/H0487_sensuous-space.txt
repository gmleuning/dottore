# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The here is the spatial form of the sensuous this. A here, for instance a tree, vanishes as soon as one turns around and is replaced by a house, while the here itself endures, indifferent to what fills it, as a mediated simplicity, a universality. Even the here that is pointed out is not a simple point but an in front and a behind, an above and a below, a right and a left, each passing into another. What endures is a negative this, a simple gathering of many heres, a universal here.
