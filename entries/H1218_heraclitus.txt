# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Where the Eleatics said that only being is, Heraclitus says that everything flows and nothing is fixed: becoming is the principle. Being no more is than non-being, and becoming contains the identity of the two. By becoming is understood arising and perishing, neither of which is on its own account.
