# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Objectivity is the concept that has sublated the mediation in its self-determination and raised it to immediate self-reference: being in and for itself, pervaded throughout by the concept. Hegel compares the transition into it with the ontological proof. It is not mere sensuous existence but is free of accidentality. It runs through mechanism, where objects stand indifferent to one another; chemism, where they are specifically related; and teleology, where the concept is posited as purpose.
