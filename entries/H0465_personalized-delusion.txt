# 465 Personalized Delusion

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Personalized delusion is madness whose fixed content comes from a particular passion, such as vanity or pride, and concerns who the sufferer takes themself to be: God, Christ or a king. The soul that has shut itself up in its subjectivity creates this content out of itself and holds it to be objective. The one so afflicted feels no contradiction between the idea and actuality, since only others perceive it.
