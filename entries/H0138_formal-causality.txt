# 138 Formal Causality
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › γ. Absolute Relation › b. Relation of Causality
G: Formelle Kausalität
Gloss: Cause and effect taken in their bare form: the cause is a cause only in producing its effect and is spent in it, so that neither has meaning without the other.

N:
  formal causality; identity of cause and effect, tautological causation, sameness of content in cause and effect; rain and wetness; transfer of content
  causality, causation, origination, production &c H0137
  formal cause-effect identity, the tautology of causation, the same content

V:
  pass over, transfer, carry over, communicate, impart
  cause, effect, repeat

Adj:
  formal, tautological, identical in content, equivalent
  identical

Adv:
  formally, in form only
  identically

F:
  Latin: causa aequat effectum [the cause equals the effect]
  Sanskrit: satkāryavāda [doctrine that the effect pre-exists in the cause]

Phr:
  causa aequat effectum [Lat.]
  the effect is contained in the cause
