# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third stage, that of the concrete, opens a new epoch, the shape of philosophy most closely connected with Christianity. What is authentically concrete is the universal that particularizes itself within itself and remains infinitely present to itself in this particularity, unlike a pantheism or emanation in which substance degrades itself without returning.
