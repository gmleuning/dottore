# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Elective affinity is the exclusive connection by which one self-subsisting measure, which has affinities with a whole series of others, prefers one of them. The term is chemical, but the same holds of a musical note, whose meaning depends on its combinations with others. Merely quantitative differences of affinity cannot by themselves yield exclusion; since the exponent is a determination of measure, however, it is also exclusive. What results is a sudden conversion of indifferent quantitative relating into qualitative, and back again.
