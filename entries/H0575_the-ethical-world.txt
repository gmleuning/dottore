# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ethical world is ethical substance in its ordered subsistence, absolute spirit realized in a plurality of conscious beings. It exists in two ways, each containing the whole: as the polity, the people and its citizens, under the human law known and publicly valid, and as the family, the immediate, natural ethical community under the divine law. Each has levels and differences within it, and their examination shows how the two engage one another, connect and pass into each other.
