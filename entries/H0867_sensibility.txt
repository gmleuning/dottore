# 867 Sensibility (Facility)
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › b. Genius
G: Leichtigkeit
Gloss: The ease with which the gifted artist executes the work, so that it comes almost into their fingers.
HGloss: Leichtigkeit, the ease of inner production and of outer technical skill: what lives in the artist's Phantasie comes as it were into his fingers, as our thoughts come into our mouths.

N:
  sensibility; facility, ease, fluency, dexterity, readiness, deftness, felicity, the knack, the touch, a sure hand, technical skill, mastery of the medium, sprezzatura, practical feeling

Adj:
  facile, fluent, deft, effortless, ready, dexterous, sure-handed

Adv:
  effortlessly, with ease, fluently

F:
  Italian: sprezzatura

Phr:
  was in seiner Phantasie lebt, kommt ihm dadurch gleichsam in die Finger [Ger.] [Hegel, Aesthetics]
