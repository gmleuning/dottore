G: Zeit
Gloss: Succession; not a container in which things come to be and pass away, but that coming-to-be and passing-away itself; change itself, perceived.
HGloss: The negativity of space posited for itself: being that, in being, is not, and in not being, is; intuited becoming. All things are in time, not time in them.
