G: Die Skulptur der Römer
Gloss: The sculpture of Rome, given to portraits and the record of public life.
HGloss: Die Skulptur der Römer, which keeps the Greek forms but turns to portrait and likeness, where the ideal begins to dissolve.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Roman sculpture begins the dissolution of classical sculpture: conception and execution no longer rest on the ideal proper, and the poetry of spiritual animation and inner nobility peculiar to Greek art gives way to a preference for portraiture and truth to nature. Even so it stands high, inferior to Greek essentially in lacking that poetry.
