G: Der subjektive Humor
Gloss: Humour in which the artist's own wit and whims dissolve every fixed content.
HGloss: Der subjektive Humor: the artist's subjectivity making itself the content, breaking up every objective shape by wit and the play of fancies, as in Jean Paul; Sterne and Hippel show its truer form.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subjective humour brings the artist's own person onto the stage: instead of shaping a topic objectively, the humourist enters the material and by flashes of wit and striking turns dissolves whatever would make itself objective or hold firm shape. It is only play with topics, a rambling of subjective views, easily growing flat; Jean Paul is the favourite example, astonishing in jumbling the remotest things.
