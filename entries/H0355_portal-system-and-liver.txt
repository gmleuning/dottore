# 355 Portal System and Liver
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › α. The Animal's Formation (Shape) › b. Systems of Shape › iii. Digestive System
G: Pfortadersystem und Leber
Gloss: The portal vein and the liver, with the stomach's and pancreas's juices.
HGloss: The second moment of the circuit: the portal system and the liver, where the blood, with the gastric and pancreatic juices, turns against the food it has to master.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the second moment of the circuit the blood's centre is the portal system, where its subjectivity becomes active, a consuming volcano. Active in the liver, it operates on the chyme of the stomach: after mastication and saliva, the gastric and pancreatic juices act as solvent acids inducing fermentation, a chemical and organic moment.
