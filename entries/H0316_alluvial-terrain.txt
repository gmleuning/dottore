# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Alluvial terrain is the last moment of the geological advance, the decay into indifferent existence: the transition from stratified formations to alluvial land is a mixture, an abstract layering of clay, sand, lime and marl, completely formless minerals. Primitive rock thus develops until it loses its mineral state and joins a vegetable one.
