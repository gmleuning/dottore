# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The completion of a contract by pledge secures its performance where ownership and possession are separated, as in letting or in an undertaking not completed at once. The pledge puts or keeps me in actual possession of the value that is, or is to become, my property, though not of the specific thing itself.
