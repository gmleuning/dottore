# 1083 Sense-Accent
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse › 2. Accent
G: Sinnakzent
Gloss: The stress that falls by meaning on the important word, which may cross the metrical accent.
HGloss: The sense-accent, which the meaning lays on the significant word and which may agree or contend with the metrical accent.

N:
  sense-accent; logical stress, rhetorical accent, sense stress, meaning-stress, word accent

Adj:
  emphatic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sense accent is the emphasis syllables and words receive from the poetic idea they signify, which hovers over their metrical value, stressing some and setting back others as insignificant, so that spiritual life is breathed into the verse. Poetry must not yield to it so far as directly to oppose the rules of metre.
