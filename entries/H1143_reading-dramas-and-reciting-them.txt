# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reading and reciting dramas runs against drama's basic character, since its material is the whole acting person, not the voice and word alone. It is a common error to treat arrangement for the stage as superfluous: a drama's inner dramatic worth comes only from a treatment that makes it excellent on stage, as the Greek tragedies written purely for it prove.
