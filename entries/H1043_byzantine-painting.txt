# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Byzantine painting kept the practice of the Greeks, and with it a good technique in postures and drapery, but wholly abandoned nature and life: traditional facial expression, typical and stiff figures, architectural arrangement, no landscape, little modelling or perspective. Tied to settled types, painting and mosaic often sank into mere craft, though similar work spread to Italy and stirred an urge beyond it.
