# 1070 Poetic and Prosaic Works of Art

G: Das poetische und das prosaische Kunstwerk
Gloss: The poetic work, a free and self-contained whole, as against prose works such as history-writing and oratory, which serve a purpose outside themselves.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The poetic work of art, unlike prose works of history and oratory, must be formed into an organic whole. Its dominant subject, an action or a feeling, must have unity, with everything related to it concretely and freely; the universal and the individuals in whom it appears must stay vitally interwoven, as the Iliad is sustained by the wrath of Achilles.
