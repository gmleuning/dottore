# 50 Continuous and Discrete Magnitude
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › α. Quantity
G: Kontinuierliche und diskrete Größe
Gloss: Quantity taken either as continuous, an unbroken whole like a line, or as discrete, a collection of separate units like a heap.
HGloss: Quantity posited as continuity, an unbroken self-equality, and as discreteness, a plurality of ones; each contains the other.

N:
  continuous and discrete magnitude; the digital and the analogue; the continuous and the discontinuous

F:
  German: Kontinuität, Diskretion
  Latin: gradatim
  Greek: synecheia

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Continuous and discrete magnitude are quantity posited in each of its two moments. Continuity is brought to completion only with discreteness, and quantity is concrete only as the unity of the two, each of which, in its unity with the other, remains the whole. Posited in the determination of continuity, quantity is continuous magnitude; posited in the determinateness immanent to it, the one, it is discrete magnitude. Neither is a separate kind that falls outside the other.
