# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Government is actual spirit reflected into itself, the simple self of the whole ethical substance, in which the polity has its living individuality. It lets the whole spread out into its members and the self-sufficient systems of property, right, acquisition and consumption, and it gathers these into a negative unity. So that they do not harden in isolation and let the spirit of the whole fade, government must from time to time shake them by war, making individuals feel the power of their master, death.
