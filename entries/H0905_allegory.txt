# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Allegory is the opposite of the riddle: it brings the qualities of a universal idea before our view through sensuous likenesses, aiming at complete clarity. It personifies abstractions such as justice, war or spring, but the subject remains an abstract idea with the empty form of subjectivity, without the individuality of a Greek god or a saint, so that it is rightly called frosty and cold.
