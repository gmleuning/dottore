# 79 Essence as Reflection Within Itself
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence
G: Wesen als Reflexion in ihm selbst
Gloss: The underlying nature of things understood as a depth that shows itself only through surface appearance. Reflection here means a turning back, as light is returned from a mirror.
HGloss: Essence as the reflection in itself of being: an inward movement that posits its own appearing.

N:
  essence as reflection within itself; self-reflection, reflexivity, reflexion, turning back, recoil, return, reflux; mirror, looking glass, reflecting surface; self-mirroring, self-reference; mediation; inwardness, interiority; negativity, self-negation; depth that shows itself
  reaction, retroaction, repercussion, springing back
  essence as reflection into itself, inward reflection, the depth of things, shine reflection and its determinations

V:
  reflect, mirror, return, recoil, bounce back, turn back upon oneself; mediate; show itself, manifest itself
  react, rebound, reverberate
  reflect into itself, shine within itself

Adj:

  inward, essential

Adv:

  within itself, reflectively
