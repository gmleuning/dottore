# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Modes of portrayal distinguish sculptures independent of anything else, for which surroundings are merely a prepared place, from those that decorate spaces in or on buildings, whose relation to the building determines their form and largely their content. In the main single statues exist on their own account, while groups and above all reliefs give up independence to serve architecture.
