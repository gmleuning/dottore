# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The hepatic system is the blood's return from its atmospheric process into being-for-self, with its centre in the portal system, where the blood's subjectivity becomes movement and an active process, a consuming volcano. Active in the liver, it works on the chyme prepared in the stomach, seeking a centre and the heat that consumes otherness.
