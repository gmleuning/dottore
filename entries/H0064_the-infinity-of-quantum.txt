# 64 The Infinity of Quantum
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum › c. Quantitative Infinity
G: Unendlichkeit des Quantums
Gloss: The recognition that quantity's endless going-beyond is its own nature; quantity understood as wholly relational.
HGloss: The quantum known as essentially self-external, so that its truth lies in its relation to another quantum: the ratio.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The infinity of quantum is what the infinite progress contains when its moments are taken together. The infinitely great and infinitely small are insubstantial images, each at once a quantum and its non-being. In the progress quantum is sublated, and so is its beyond, and the truth is the unity of these two negations. Quantum is precisely through its externality itself, with itself in its negation, so that the progress posits the concept of quantum and restores magnitude as a limit that is indifferent or external, now with a qualitative character.
