# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The external determinacy of the ideal is the outer reality into which it passes through action. A human being has a surrounding world, as a god's statue has a temple: nature, with its place, time and climate; the use made of it in tools, housing and comforts; and the concrete world of family, possession, worship, war and political life. Art must configure this prose of life in a way fit for it, not cut itself off from it.
