# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The determinacy of the ideal arises because artistic beauty, as idea, cannot stop at its universal concept but must go out into particularity, externality and finitude, and still maintain itself there. This determinacy is considered first as such; then as it develops into difference and its resolution, which is action; and finally in its external setting.
