# 16 Finitude
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being
G: Endlichkeit
Gloss: The condition of being limited; having an end that belongs to one's own nature.
HGloss: The determinate being of something, which has its end in itself; to be finite is to carry the seed of one's passing away.

  limit &c H0018; end, term, terminus, boundary, horizon

  limit, bounds, confine, bourn
  termination, finis, last, omega
  aught
