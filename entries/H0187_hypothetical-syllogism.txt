# 187 Hypothetical Syllogism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › c. Syllogism of Necessity
G: Hypothetischer Schluss
Gloss: Inference whose connecting term is a condition: if A then B; A; therefore B.
HGloss: The middle is the being of the condition, which is the being of the conditioned.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The hypothetical syllogism adds to the hypothetical judgement, "if A is, so is B", the immediacy of being: but A is, therefore B is. A is then no mere immediacy but essentially the middle term, an immediate being that is also contingent and sublates itself. The two sides are not independent beings but beings held in necessity, such as condition and conditioned, and what is posited is their necessary connection as an accomplished mediating unity.
