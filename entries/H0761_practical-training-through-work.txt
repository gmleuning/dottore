# 761 Practical Training through Work
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › a. The System of Needs › ii. Abstraction of Needs and Means
G: Die praktische Bildung durch die Arbeit
Gloss: The training that work gives: the habit of being occupied, of fitting one's activity to the material and to other people's wishes, and the skill this brings.
HGloss: Practical Bildung through labour: the need for and habit of occupation, the restriction of one's activity by the material and by the will of others, and the general skills won by this discipline (§197).
