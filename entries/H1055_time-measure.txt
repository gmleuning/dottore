# 1055 Time-Measure
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › ii. Music › 2. Particular Characteristics of Music › a. Time, Bar, Rhythm
G: Das Zeitmaß
Gloss: The regular division of time into equal units that music lays over the flow of sound.
HGloss: Das Zeitmaß: the uniform measure of time, which the self lays on the indeterminate flow of time to find itself again in it.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Time-measure is the rule music must give to time, which of itself is a uniform stream and undifferentiated duration. Because time shares the principle active in the self, whose inner life music objectifies, the self needs time determined: by gathering its experiences it interrupts the empty succession, and the beginning and ending of notes gives that flow definite divisions.
