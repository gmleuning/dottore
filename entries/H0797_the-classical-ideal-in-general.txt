# 797 The Classical Ideal in General
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › b. Classical Art › ii. The Ideal of Classical Art
G: Das klassische Ideal überhaupt
Gloss: The classical ideal in its general nature: serene, self-contained, perfect.

N:
  the classical ideal in general; ideal beauty, classical beauty, repose, serenity, blessed self-containment, a breath of mournfulness, idealized humanity
  the nature of the classical ideal, self-containedness, blessedness, cheerfulness, perfection, completeness, calm grandeur, noble simplicity [Winckelmann]

V:
  idealize, repose
  rest, perfect, complete, harmonize

Adj:
  ideal, serene, self-contained, tranquil, melancholy
  reposeful, calm, blessed, harmonious

Adv:
  serenely
  perfectly, in repose, in general

Phr:
  the breath of mourning that hovers over the blessed gods [Hegel, paraphrased]
