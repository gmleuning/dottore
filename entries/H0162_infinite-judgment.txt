# 162 Infinite Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment › a. Judgment of Existence (Qualitative Judgment)
G: Unendliches Urteil
Gloss: A judgment in which subject and predicate have nothing to do with each other, as "the mind is not an elephant"; or one that denies a whole kind of a thing, as crime denies right.
HGloss: The positive infinite judgment, the singular is singular, and the negative, in which subject and predicate do not fit at all: crime.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The infinite judgement negates not only a particular predicate but its whole universal sphere, so that the very form of judgement is cancelled. Its negative examples, that spirit is not red, that the understanding is not a table, are correct but nonsensical; a real instance is crime, which negates not a particular right but right as such. Its positive side is the negation of negation, singularity reflected into itself: the singular is singular.
