# 518 Identification
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness › a. Immediate Self-Consciousness (Desire) › iii. Satisfaction and Identification
G: Das Zusammenschließen des Ich mit sich selbst
Gloss: The self finding itself again in its satisfied desire, and so needing another self.
HGloss: The product of the process: the I closes together with itself and is for itself; its satisfaction shows that it needs another self-consciousness.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Identification is the result in which self-consciousness, having negated its own immediacy and the external object, raises itself above the self-centredness of destructive desire. It divides itself into a consciousness of a free object, in which the I knows itself as I but which is still outside it. It has filled the other with the I, turned it from something selfless into another self, and so brought itself face to face with itself as a distinct I.
