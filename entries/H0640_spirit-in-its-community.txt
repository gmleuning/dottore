# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit in its community is reached through the death of the mediator. The divine essence relinquishes itself, goes to its death and is reconciled with itself, so that this death is its resurrection as spirit. What representation holds as something that happened to a particular being is shifted into self-consciousness, whose particularity dies in its universality. The painful feeling that God himself is dead is the loss of substance as object and the certainty that substance becomes subject, universal self-consciousness.
