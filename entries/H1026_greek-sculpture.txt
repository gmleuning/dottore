# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Greek sculpture brings the sense of self fully to life, though even within it the ideal must strip off early defects. Its beginnings, as in Aeginetan works, remain tied to the typical and to merely natural life; the summit comes when artistic freedom works universal meaning wholly into individual shape, giving a spiritually animated beauty.
