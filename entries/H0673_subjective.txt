# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In practical feeling as subjective there is an ought: the will's own self-determination is set against an individuality that counts only so far as it is adequate to it. The feeling will compares how it is determined from outside with what its own nature determines it to be, and demands that the affection agree. Both sides being immediate, the judgement of pleasant and unpleasant that results is utterly subjective and superficial.
