# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Divorce is the dissolution of a marriage. Since marriage has its actuality only in subjective feeling and disposition, no one can be compelled to marry, and no merely legal bond can hold the partners together once their dispositions have become hostile. But marriage is ethical, so divorce must be made difficult and decided by a third, ethical authority.
