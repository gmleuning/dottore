# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The effects of magnetizing begin with the sinking of the magnetized person into the shrouded, undifferentiated natural life of sleep, though sleep is not strictly necessary. Bodily activity passes from the outward organs to the inner. The soul, immersed in its inwardness, intuits its individual world not outside but within itself, and sleep may pass over into clairvoyance. What results is a state of passivity in which the patient falls into the power of the magnetizer and remains in it afterwards.
