# 814 The Persian Empire and Its Parts

G: Das persische Reich und seine Bestandteile
Gloss: The provinces and peoples under Persian rule.
HGloss: The Persian empire, which let its peoples keep their own natures: Syria with its trade, Judaea with its one God, Egypt with its riddle.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: An empire in the modern sense, composed of dependent states that kept their own individuality, customs and laws, and in some cases their own kings, under a rule that protected their differences as light lets each object show its own colour. Within it the elements of upland, river valley and sea coast coexist, and its Syrian, Jewish and Egyptian parts set the problem that Egypt takes up.
