# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Language is the actuality of culture, the mediating middle in which the two extremes, state power and the noble consciousness, each existing for itself, pass into one another. Obedience subverts state power into the abstract universal called the common good and turns the noble consciousness into honour with a will still lying in wait. Only in language does spirit enter existence, knowing the pure self to be an actuality immediately in force, and so come to exist as spirituality.
