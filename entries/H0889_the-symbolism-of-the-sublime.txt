# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The symbolism of the sublime lifts the absolute above every immediate existent and so first purifies meaning from the sensuous present, a liberation that is abstract yet founds the life of spirit. The meaning is the one substance, grasped only by pure thought, which cannot find adequate shape in anything external. Hegel takes up Kant, for whom the sublime lies in the mind and not in any sensuous form.
