# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inert matter is matter as simply universal and immediate, differing at first only in quantity and divided into masses or bodies. A body is indifferent to space and time and so to their relation, motion, which is external to it, as rest is too: it is moved or brought to rest only by an outside cause. This inadequacy to its concept is its finitude.
