# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The one and the many is the development in which the one, the simple reference of being-for-itself to itself, shows that its immediacy cannot hold. The moments that had fallen together in it are now there as existents. As self-reference of the negative the one is a determining, and what is distinct from it is its own self-determining, demoted to reference. As negative unity it is therefore negation of itself as other, excluding the one from itself, so that the one passes into the void and into many ones.
