# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Formation as such begins with the germ, whose development is at first mere growth and increase. The germ is already the whole plant or tree in miniature, its parts fully formed and receiving only enlargement, formal repetition and hardening, so that becoming is a superficial movement; yet it is no less a qualitative articulation and shaping, and so an essential process.
