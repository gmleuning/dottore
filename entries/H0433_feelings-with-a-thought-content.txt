# 433 Feelings with a Thought Content
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › i. Particular Feelings
G: Empfindungen mit Gedankeninhalt
Gloss: Feelings whose content is a thought: shame, remorse, the feeling of right and of religion.
HGloss: Feelings with a spiritual content, a thought of right, duty or the divine held in the form of feeling: shame, remorse, reverence, the moral and religious feelings. They are taken up again as practical feeling (&c H0628).
