# 819 Artistic Intention
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › c. Enthusiasm › i. Origins of Enthusiasm
G: Absicht zur Produktion
Gloss: Setting out to be inspired by an act of will; Hegel says this too fails without a living content.
HGloss: Begeisterung cannot be called up by the bare spiritual intention to produce: whoever merely resolves to be inspired in order to make a poem, with no content already alive in him, will make no fine work.

N:
  artistic intention; resolve, intention, the will to create, deliberate effort, design, ambition, forced inspiration, contrivance, laboured effort

V:
  set out, intend, resolve, contrive, force it, strain after effect

Adj:
  deliberate, intentional, contrived, forced, laboured

Phr:
  weder jene nur sinnliche Anregung noch der bloße Wille und Entschluß [Ger.] [Hegel, Aesthetics]
