# 943 Imitation of Nature
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › c. Romantic Art › iii. The Formal Independence of Individual Characteristics › 3. The Dissolution of Romantic Art
G: Die subjektive Kunstnachahmung des Vorhandenen
Gloss: Art that copies the ordinary world as it is, in genre scenes and still lifes.
HGloss: Die subjektive Kunstnachahmung des Vorhandenen: faithful portrayal of everyday reality, as in Dutch painting, whose interest lies in the artist's skill and the life of the passing moment.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The imitation of nature takes contingent daily reality, domestic and civil life, as subject, and art dissolves into portraiture, painting and poetry especially. Measured by the ideal such products fall short, yet they are works of art through the artist's conception and execution, fidelity and marvellous skill, which make significant what has no significance in itself, and the vivacity with which life is breathed into it.
