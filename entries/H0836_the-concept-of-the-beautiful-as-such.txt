# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The beautiful as such is the idea in a determinate form: the unity of concept and reality as it appears to sense. The understanding cannot comprehend it, because it clings to the separation of ideal and real, sensuous and conceptual, objective and subjective, and so stays in the finite. The beautiful is infinite and free: in it the concept ensouls the existence that embodies it and is at home there.
