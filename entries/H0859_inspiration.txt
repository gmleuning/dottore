# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Imagination, genius and inspiration make up the artist's creative activity. Imagination, unlike passive fancy, is creative: it grasps reality and gives its rationality concrete shape. Genius is the general ability, and energy, to produce a true work of art, including a natural element; inspiration is that activity as the artist's state, being wholly filled with the theme and not resting until it has received artistic form.
