# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The centre is the empty manifold of objects gathered into objective singularity, the self-determining middle point that is their objective universality, their pervading immanent essence. In nature it is the central body, genus of the single bodies, toward which they strive and in union with which they rest, though this unity remains an ought. In spiritual things the centre takes higher forms, but the unity of the concept with its reality must there too be fundamental.
