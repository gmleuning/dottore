# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The kinds of lyric proper differ by the attitude the poet consciously takes to the subject. In one the poet's individuality vanishes into contemplation of God or the gods, as in hymns, dithyrambs and psalms; in odes the poet's own subjective personality is foremost; and in song the endless variety of lyric moods and reflections is spread out.
