# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The content of the trance states is the individual's own actuality, the whole circle of interests and relationships which has coalesced with that individuality and which healthy consciousness knows as an intelligibly connected external world. In somnambulism the individual is a monad inwardly aware of this actuality, intuiting it immediately without the series of mediations that waking consciousness must pass through. Only what is individually determined enters in; scientific discoveries and universal truths need the ground of thought developed to free consciousness.
