# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Chivalric love is the surrender of the person to another, the sacrifice of independent consciousness so as to have knowledge of oneself only in the other, whereas honour keeps personality in absolute independence. Yet love also realizes honour's need for recognition. It arises in feeling, with a basis in spiritualized nature, and its infinity lies in losing self-consciousness in another and there finding and enjoying oneself.
