# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The absolutely unconditioned is the one whole that condition and ground prove to be. At first each shines in the other while keeping a content of its own. But the forms of condition are not external to immediate existence: it is the nature of being to sublate its immediacy and go to ground, and so to be the becoming of essence. Condition is thus the whole form of the ground-connection, which both founders to the ground and is ground, one and the same movement.
