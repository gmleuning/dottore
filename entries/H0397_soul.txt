# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The soul, the object of Anthropology, is mind as it first comes out of nature as nature's truth: not an individual thing set against the body, but the universal immateriality of nature, its simple ideal life. It is the substance and absolute foundation in which mind finds all the stuff of its determination, though in this abstract form it is only the sleep of mind. It is first the natural soul that merely is, then the feeling soul that enters into relationship with its immediate being, and finally the actual soul that has moulded its bodiliness into itself.
