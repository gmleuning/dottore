# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Immediate religion, or nature religion, is the first form that religion necessarily takes. The name does not here mean what reason can know of God unaided, nor an original and perfect revelation given to humanity in paradise, of which later religions would be a decline. Hegel examines that widespread picture of an original state of innocence and true knowledge before turning to the actual forms of nature religion.
