# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Comparisons originating from the external object begin with something perceived and draw a meaning from it. They comprise fable, then parable, apologue and proverb, and finally metamorphoses, subordinate and hybrid kinds that are hard to arrange into definite species. Generally imperfect, a mere search for true art, they are treated only in relation to the general forms of art and not as kinds of poetry.
