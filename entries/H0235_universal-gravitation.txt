# Nature › 1. Mechanics › III. Absolute Mechanics (Astronomy)
G: Allgemeine Gravitation
Gloss: The striving of all matter toward a common centre, which binds the heavenly bodies into one system; commonly stated as the attraction of every body for every other.
HGloss: Gravity as the true concept of matter, its striving toward a centre outside each body, which forms the system of the heavens.
