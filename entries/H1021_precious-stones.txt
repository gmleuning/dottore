# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Precious stones were cut as gems, in which the finest figures show supreme beauty like organic works of nature and bear magnification without losing purity of line. Here technique approaches feeling, since the carver holds the stone against small wheels and must have the design in the sense of touch; cameos, by contrast, emboss figures from layered onyx.
