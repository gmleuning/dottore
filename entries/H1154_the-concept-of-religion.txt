# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The concept of religion is religion in its general nature, with which the philosophy of religion begins, as a result of the whole of philosophy. Its first moment is God, the universal which is the truth of all things. Its second is the distinction by which consciousness knows God. Its third is the cultus, the practical relationship in which the subject brings about its own union with God.
