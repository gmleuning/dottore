# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Complete ground contains formal and real ground together. In real ground the connection of the two contents is external and only posited, and so it has in turn another ground. This new ground has the same content as the one it grounds, and is also the immanent reflection of the link, relating the two determinations of content to each other. With it the identity of ground and grounded returns, and the contents that confronted each other immediately are mediated, each ground-connection resting on another.
