# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Certainty is reason as self-consciousness first has it: the certainty that its determinations are objective, determinations of the essence of things just as much as its own thoughts. Reason is truth in and for itself, the simple identity of the subjectivity of the concept with its objectivity and universality, so that the object once merely given to consciousness now permeates the I, and the I overarches the object.
