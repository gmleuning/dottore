# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third moment of the circuit is in the duodenum, where complete mastery over the food is achieved by fire, by the bile produced from the venous blood of the portal vein. The outward process still active in the lymph becomes being-for-self and an animal self, and the chyle returns into blood, so that blood has produced itself.
