# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Form and matter arise when the reflection of essence relates itself to essence as to something formless and indeterminate. Matter is simple identity without distinction, the other of form and its substrate, what is left when every form of a thing is abstracted away, and so is never seen or felt except as determinate matter. Neither exists of itself or is eternal: form presupposes matter and is presupposed by it. Matter is indifferent and passive, form active, self-referring negativity and therefore contradiction.
