# 334 The Plant's Process of Assimilation
# Nature › 3. Organics › II. Plant Nature
G: Assimilationsprozess der Pflanze
Gloss: The plant's taking in of what it needs from its surroundings.
HGloss: The plant's process with the elements, its turning outward toward light, air and water.

N:
  the plant's process of assimilation; assimilation, nutrition, absorption, uptake, photosynthesis, transpiration, respiration
  plant assimilation, intake, nourishment from light air and water

V:

Adj:

  absorbent

Adv:
  by assimilation
  from its surroundings

Phr:
  the plant drinks the sun
  to draw nourishment
  to drink in

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The plant's assimilation is its self-specifying process outward, directly bound up with formation: the seed germinates only when stimulated from outside, and root and leaf are turned toward earth and water, light and air. Its outer nature is not individualized things but the elements, and its return into itself yields no self-feeling.
