Def: Portrayal of the individual gods shows how the Greeks, despite their gods' universality and ideality, kept them individual and distinct without fixed portrait types, invention being subtlest where the substantial type remained its basis. Phidias gave Zeus supremacy, cheerful and benign; Neptune is wilder, Pluto darker; Juno has the greatest majesty among the goddesses.
