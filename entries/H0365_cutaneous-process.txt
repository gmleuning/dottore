# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The cutaneous process is an advancing vegetative process that breaks out into hair and feathers. The human skin is less hairy than the animal's, while the plumage of birds especially takes vegetable life up into animal life: feathers branch and subdivide in a way that everywhere recalls the plant.
