# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The public authority (police) is the universal power that secures welfare in the sphere of contingency. Since the particular will still governs the choice of ends, this authority is restricted to contingencies and is an external organization; its aim is to actualize and maintain the universal contained within the particularity of civil society.
