# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The natural soul is the soul that simply is, with natural determinacies in it. The universal soul is no world-soul standing as a subject apart: it is only universal substance, which has its actual truth as individuality. So it exists as an individual soul, but at first one whose determinations are natural qualities that it has in itself, not objects it confronts. These qualities, the alterations the individual undergoes, and sensation make up the stages by which the soul moves from universal naturalness toward individuality that is for itself.
