# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mortgage, like surety, is a particular form of pledge. The pledge is a specific thing that is my property only to the extent of the value I have handed over or am owed; its specific character and any excess value remain the property of the one who gave it. A pledge is not itself a contract, only a stipulation completing one.
