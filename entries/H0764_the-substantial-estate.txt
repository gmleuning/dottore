# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The substantial or agricultural estate has its resources in the natural products of the soil it cultivates. Since its work is bound to the seasons and dependent on nature, its disposition is that of an immediate ethical life, resting on the family relationship and on trust; with marriage, agriculture is the real beginning of states.
