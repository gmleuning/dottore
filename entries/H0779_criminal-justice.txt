# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Criminal justice here is the public authority's task of preventing crime or bringing it to justice, crime being contingency as the arbitrary willing of evil. Beyond crime, lawful actions and the private use of property also affect others, so the authority must oversee the possibility of injury, which has no intrinsic boundary.
