# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The letting of a thing is letting in the proper sense: the use of a specific thing, such as a flat, furniture or a house, is alienated for a time in return for rent, while the lessor remains its owner. The empirical character of the thing gives rise only to unimportant subdivisions.
