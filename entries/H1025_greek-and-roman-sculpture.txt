# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Greek and Roman sculpture shows sense of self fully alive, removing the Egyptian defects, though without violent leap: Aeginetan and Etruscan works are preliminaries, the ideal arrives when artistic freedom replaces the typical and traditional, and in Roman art classical sculpture begins to dissolve as spiritual animation yields to portraiture.
