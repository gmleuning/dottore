# 782 Regulation of Commerce
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › c. Public Authority and Corporation › i. Public Authority (Police) › 2. Common Utility
G: Die mit Bewußtsein vorgenommene Regulierung
Gloss: Public oversight of trade and industry as a whole, between free trade and regulation.
HGloss: The conscious regulation that stands above producers and consumers when their interests collide, and that brings the great branches of industry back to the general interest (§236).

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The regulation of commerce is the conscious control that stands above producers and consumers when their differing interests collide. Though the right relation between them comes about on the whole automatically, its adjustment also requires a control consciously undertaken, especially where large branches of industry depend on distant circumstances.
