# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Unconscious symbolism is the first shape of the symbolic, not yet known as mere image and simile. It rests on an immediate unification of universal spiritual meaning with a sensuous shape that is as adequate as inadequate, with no consciousness yet of their incongruity, although the link must already be formed by imagination and art. Its first presupposition is the immediate unity of the absolute with its existence in nature and human activity.
