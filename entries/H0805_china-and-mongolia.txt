# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The oldest empire, where history begins, built on the family relation extended to the state. The Emperor governs paternally as father of a people held to be in a condition of nonage; all are equal, but without freedom, so that despotism is the necessary form of government. Morality is fixed by law from above, and what belongs to inward subjectivity is wanting.
