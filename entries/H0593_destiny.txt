G: Das Schicksal
Gloss: The fate that overtakes the deed, in which both laws and both characters go down together.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Destiny is the downfall of ethical substance in which both powers are overtaken. The conflict appears as that of the two brothers who have an equal right to the state and destroy each other, after which government honours the one and denies burial to the other. The cause of the collapse lies in the immediacy of ethical life, through which nature enters its action: a people determined by nature is a restricted individuality, its living substance is lost, and its individuals are shattered into many separate points.
