# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Statue and temple are the first, objective work of art, in which the indwelling god, once the black stone, is drawn out of its animal covering and penetrated by the light of consciousness. The human shape strips off the animal, which remains only as a disguise or sign, and the shape of the god casts off the neediness of natural life. The old gods of nature are banished, and clear ethical spirits of self-conscious peoples take their place.
