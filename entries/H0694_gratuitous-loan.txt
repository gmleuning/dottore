# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The gratuitous loan is the gift of part of a thing, namely a restricted use and enjoyment of it, without interest. The lender remains the owner; the thing lent may be a specific thing, or something counted as universal, such as money. Where interest is charged, the loan passes over into the contract of letting.
