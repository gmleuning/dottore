# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Secular architecture of the Middle Ages runs parallel to religious building, repeating and modifying its character, but with less room for art since narrow aims and varied needs must be met, and beauty enters only as decoration. Its prominent type is the stronghold of fortified dwellings, in the country and in cities, with strength, security, magnificence and individual forms.
