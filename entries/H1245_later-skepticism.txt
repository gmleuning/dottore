G: Der spätere Skeptizismus
Gloss: The skepticism of Aenesidemus, Agrippa and Sextus Empiricus, with its modes for setting argument against argument.
HGloss: Der spätere Skeptizismus: the ten tropes of Aenesidemus and the five of Agrippa, collected by Sextus, which turn dialectic against every determinate claim.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Later Skepticism, expounded by Sextus Empiricus, works through tropes, procedures of argument against whatever is asserted. Beside the earlier ones, five further tropes pertain to thinking, to the dialectic of the concept, and belong to a more cultivated standpoint of reflective thought; the first of them points to the diversity of opinion about any object.
