# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Egyptian temple precincts are huge open constructions, without roofs, of gates, partitions, porticos and forests of columns, which astonish by colossal mass and variety. They do not house a god or a congregation but are symbols of universal meanings, even substitutes for books through their hieroglyphs: long avenues of sphinxes, pylons and courts end in a small shrine. Building was itself a cult uniting king and people.
