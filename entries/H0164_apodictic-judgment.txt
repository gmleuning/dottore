# 164 Apodictic Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment [Tetrad] › d. Judgment of the Concept
G: Apodiktisches Urteil
Gloss: A judgment of value stated with its reason, and so as necessary: "this house, being built thus, is good."
HGloss: The subject's constitution named as the ground of its agreement with its concept: the house, so constituted, is good.
