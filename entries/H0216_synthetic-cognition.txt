# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Synthetic cognition aims to comprehend what is, grasping the manifold determinations in their unity, and so seeks necessity; it is the second premise, connecting the diverse as such. Its identity is still inner, its determinations external to one another, and the singular content is something it finds. Though it transforms the objective world into concepts, it gives it only the form, and proves necessity as that of its own cognition, not from the concept.
