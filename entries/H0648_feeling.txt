# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feeling is the form in which intelligence first occurs, finding itself determined and weaving dimly within itself, with the whole material of its knowledge in the simple shape of individual, subjective mind. A feeling is a determinate affection whose determinacy is simple, so that however pure and true its content may be it has the form of contingent particularity, and its content may equally be meagre and untrue. All rational content enters into it, but this is the lowest form.
