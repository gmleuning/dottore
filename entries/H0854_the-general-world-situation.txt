# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general state of the world is the way in which the substantial element of spiritual reality, ethics, law and justice, holds together its manifestations and provides the ground on which individuals act. For the ideal it must take the form of independence, in which universal and individual are not yet torn apart, such as the heroic age, whose figures are themselves the bearers of right and custom.
