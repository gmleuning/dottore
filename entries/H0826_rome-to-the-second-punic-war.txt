# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first period of Rome, which begins as an artificial and violent foundation of robber-herdsmen and a gathering of all sorts of people, and not from natural patriarchal bonds. Under kings and then under consuls the struggle of patricians and plebeians develops; once it is settled by concessions to the plebeians, the state gains the internal strength to defeat the people that preceded it in world history.
