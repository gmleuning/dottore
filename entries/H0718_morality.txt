# 718 Morality
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit
G: Moralität
Gloss: The sphere of the will that answers to itself; inner moral judgment.
HGloss: The will reflected into itself, which is infinite within itself: subjectivity, which recognizes only what is its own.

Phr:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Morality is the standpoint of the will that is infinite not only in itself but for itself: the will reflected into itself, which makes the person a subject. Its right is to recognize as its own only what it knows and wills. It moves through purpose and responsibility, intention and welfare, to the good and conscience.
