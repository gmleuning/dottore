# 60 Alteration of Quantum
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum › b. Extensive and Intensive Quantum (Degree)
G: Veränderung des Quantums
Gloss: The change of one amount into another, to which every amount is open, since nothing in it fixes it at the size it is.
HGloss: The quantum's continual going beyond itself, since its limit is indifferent to it.
