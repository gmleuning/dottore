# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Modal actuality is actuality proper, the absolute as the manifestation that has no content save to manifest itself, absolute form. It stands higher than being, which is not yet actual, and higher than concrete existence, which is not yet the posited unity of reflection and immediacy. Its moments are the modal ones: actuality, possibility and necessity, developed first formally, as contingency, then really, as relative necessity, and finally as absolute necessity.
