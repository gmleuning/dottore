# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Existence is preserved in being-for-itself, but only as a moment. Being-for-itself is infinity that has sunk into simple being, so that negation of negation is present in it only as infinite qualitative determinateness. Since being is still distinguished from that determinateness, existence belongs to it, for being-for-itself contains being affected by negation. The determinateness which in existence was an other, and a being-for-other, is bent back into the infinite unity of being-for-itself, and there existence is present as being-for-one.
