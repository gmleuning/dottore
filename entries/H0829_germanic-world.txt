# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The last stage of world history, whose bearers are the German peoples and whose principle is Christian spiritual freedom. Its aim is to realize truth as the unlimited self-determination of freedom, producing that freedom from the peoples' own self-consciousness. They begin by overrunning older civilizations and develop by taking up a foreign culture, religion and law and fusing these with their own life.
