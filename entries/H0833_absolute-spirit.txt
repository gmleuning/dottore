# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute spirit is mind in which the subjective and objective are one: an identity eternally within itself and returning into itself, the single universal substance as spiritual, and a knowing for which that substance is as such. Its reality is knowledge of the absolute idea, and its sphere, generally called religion, issues from the subject and equally from absolute spirit in its community. It unfolds as art, revealed religion and philosophy.
