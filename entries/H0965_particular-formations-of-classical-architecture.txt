# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular determinants of architectural forms are the material, wood or stone, the specific forms of the temple, and the temple as a whole. Purpose dominates, and the basic form of the house is developed into beauty, so that wooden construction may be taken as earlier; the chief point in the god's house is not enclosure but beams that carry and what they carry.
