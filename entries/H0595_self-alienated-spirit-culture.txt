# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Self-alienated spirit, culture, is the world in which substance is spirit as the unity of self and essence, but each means to the other an alienation. Consciousness faces an objective actuality that exists freely on its own, which actual self-consciousness enters by relinquishing itself, and over against this actual world stands pure consciousness and a beyond. It unfolds as the world of culture and the realm of faith and pure insight, then as Enlightenment, and ends in absolute freedom.
