# 72 Nodal Line of Measure Relations
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › β. Real Measure
G: Knotenlinie von Maßverhältnissen
Gloss: A scale of gradual change in amount, marked at certain points by sudden changes in kind, as water freezes or boils; a leap.
HGloss: A line of gradual quantitative alteration interrupted by qualitative leaps, as at the freezing and boiling of water.
