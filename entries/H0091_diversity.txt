# 91 Diversity
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › I. Essence as Reflection Within Itself › β. Determinations of Reflection › b. Difference
G: Verschiedenheit
Gloss: Mere variety; things simply unlike one another, compared by an outside observer in respects that do not matter to them.
HGloss: Difference falling apart into indifferent differents, compared by a third through likeness and unlikeness.
