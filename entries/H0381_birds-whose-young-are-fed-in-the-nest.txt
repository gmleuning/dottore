# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The last four of Lamarck's orders, pigeons, perching birds, birds of prey and climbers, have young that can neither walk nor feed themselves when hatched. Hegel suggests that this very circumstance could justify placing them ahead of the others. Among birds are those that not only have voice but sing.
