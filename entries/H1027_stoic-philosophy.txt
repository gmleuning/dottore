# 1027 Stoic Philosophy
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy [Tetrad] › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism
G: Die stoische Philosophie
Gloss: The philosophy that grounds truth in the assent of reason and places the good in a will in harmony with the rational order of the world; freedom as inner self-command.
HGloss: The Stoics, for whom the criterion is the thought that grasps, the logos.
