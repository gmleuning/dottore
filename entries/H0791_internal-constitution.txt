# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The internal constitution is the state's articulation into distinct powers according to the nature of the concept, each of which is itself the totality. The state is divided into the legislative power, which determines the universal; the executive, which subsumes the particular under it; and the crown, the power of ultimate decision.
