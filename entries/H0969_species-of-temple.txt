# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The specific forms of the temple develop the house as its model, with load-bearing masses, what they carry, and the enclosure in three dimensions, whose differences must appear and fit together on mathematical principles. The Greek peculiarity is to shape supporting as such, making the column fundamental: unlike a wall it does not enclose but stands free, and unlike a mere post has proportion of its own.
