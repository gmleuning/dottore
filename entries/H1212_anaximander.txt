# 1212 Anaximander
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Anaximander
Gloss: The Milesian who made the boundless the source from which all things arise and to which they return.
HGloss: Anaximander, whose principle is the infinite (to apeiron), an indeterminate matter from which the opposites separate out.

N:
  Anaximander
  the boundless, the unlimited
  Anaximander of Miletus, the indefinite, the separating out of opposites, the gnomon, the first map

F:
  Greek: to apeiron [the boundless]

Phr:
  they pay penalty to one another for their injustice [Anaximander]
