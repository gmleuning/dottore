# 1135 Schillerian Reflection
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › c. Song
G: Schillers Gedankenlyrik
Gloss: The lyric of ideas, as in Schiller's philosophical poems.
HGloss: Schiller's reflective lyric, in which great thoughts of life and the ideal are carried in passionate verse.

N:
  Schillerian reflection; reflective lyric, philosophical poem, poem of ideas, Gedankenlyrik

F:
  German: Gedankenlyrik

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Schiller's reflective lyric, in poems such as The Ideal and Life, is neither song, ode, hymn nor elegy in the classical sense; what is remarkable is its grand fundamental thought, which does not drive the poet into dithyramb or struggle. Mastering it fully, the poet develops it with fiery feeling and breadth, a bard reciting what is dignified to the best people.
