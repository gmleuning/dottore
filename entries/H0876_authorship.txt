# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Authorship, in the section on manner, style and originality, concerns the identity of the artist's subjectivity with the true objectivity of the work. Having wholly identified with the topic and shaped it from inner heart and imagination, the artist unites what had been separated as genius and objectivity. This unity is the essence of genuine originality, to be reached by overcoming the one-sidedness of subjective manner and of style.
