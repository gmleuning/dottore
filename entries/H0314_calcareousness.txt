# 314 Calcareousness
# Nature › 3. Organics › I. Terrestrial Organism › β. Geology (Oryctognosy) › a. Primitive Rocks
G: Kalkartigkeit
Gloss: The character of rocks rich in lime, such as limestone and chalk.
HGloss: The calcareous moment, the earth of neutrality, limestone and chalk, which links to the organic.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The calcareous side is total neutrality, its modifications concerning outer formation more than inwardly specifying difference; primitive limestone is the antithesis of granite. Limestone is at first granular marble, purely mineral, but in later formations tends toward the bony nature of the animal, so that the calcareous formation represents the transition to organic being.
