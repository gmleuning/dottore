# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mohammedan poetry, above all Persian, develops oriental pantheism more freely and subjectively. The poet who longs to see the divine in everything sacrifices personality, and in the enlarged inner life grows serene, free and blissful, intimate with natural things, the beloved and the tavern. Jalal-ed-Din Rumi is praised above all, and this happy depth of feeling contrasts with the wistful inwardness of the northern romantic.
