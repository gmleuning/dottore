# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Wood sap is, in Schultz's account which Hegel reports, the plant's still imperfectly assimilated food, taken up by wood, cells and spiral vessels, which absorb water through the root and air above ground. It is only faintly sweet and not yet worked into the plant's peculiar character of smell and taste; capillary action does not explain its uptake.
