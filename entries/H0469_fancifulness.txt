# 469 Fancifulness

G: Eine von Einbildungen und Grillen gefolterte Stimmung
Gloss: A whimsical, suspicious and malicious turn of mind; hypochondria.
HGloss: The hypochondriacal mood tormented by fancies and whims, and the suspicious, false, jealous, mischievous and malicious disposition, the dark subterranean powers of the heart.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fancifulness belongs to insanity as the hypochondriac mood tormented by fancies and whims that easily accompanies the unhappy feeling of disruption, together with a suspicious, false, jealous, mischievous and malicious disposition. Traces of malice are in everyone, but ethical or prudent people know how to suppress them. In insanity, where a particular representation usurps power over the rational mind, the subject's particularity comes out without restraint, and the dark subterranean powers of the heart are set free.
