# 558 Phrenological Observation

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Phrenological observation takes as the outer of self-consciousness no speaking sign, like gesture or the face, but an entirely motionless actuality which, separated from self-conscious movement, presents itself as a mere thing: the skull. Observation turns from changeable expression back to hard and fast being, treating externality as the immediate actuality of spirit in the form of a dead thing, so as either to make spirit into a thing or to give dead being the significance of spirit.
