# 1127 Folk Song
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › c. Song › i. Song Proper
G: Volkslied
Gloss: The songs of a people.
HGloss: The folk song, in which a people's feeling finds its voice anonymously.

N:
  folk song; Volkslied, folk ballad, the song of the people, work song
  folk song, ballad, lullaby, roundelay

Adj:
  balladic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Folk songs, because of their immediacy, generally remain at the level of song and usually demand a tune. Their subject is national deeds and events in which a nation feels its very life and which live in its memory, or else the feelings and situations of the classes, sympathy with nature and intimate human relations, in every note of joy and grief.
