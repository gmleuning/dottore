# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular characteristics of painting concern its subject matter, its sensuous material and the artistic treatment of both. Its content corresponds to that of romantic art, but one must ask which spheres of it best suit painting; its material, colour on a plane surface, must be specified in the forms it can express; and differences of treatment answering to content and material yield particular genres.
