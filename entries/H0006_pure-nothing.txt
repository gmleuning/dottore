# 6 Pure Nothing
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being
G: Reines Nichts
Gloss: The complete absence of anything; emptiness taken as the opposite of being, yet just as blank and indefinite.
HGloss: Nothing, pure nothing: simple equality with itself, complete emptiness, absence of all determination; the same as pure being.
