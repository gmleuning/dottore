# 1015 Ivory, Gold, Bronze, Marble
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › iii. Different Kinds of Sculpture › 2. Materials for Sculpture
G: Elfenbein, Gold, Erz, Marmor
Gloss: Sculpture in ivory, gold, bronze and marble.
HGloss: Ivory, gold, bronze and marble, the materials of the classical ideal.

F:
  French: cire perdue

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ivory, gold, bronze and marble are the materials of first importance. Phidias used ivory and gold for his Zeus and Athene; bronze was the most widespread material of antiquity, cast by the Greeks with supreme mastery; and stone, above all marble, with its permanence, purity, soft sheen and absence of colour, harmonizes most directly with sculpture's aim.
