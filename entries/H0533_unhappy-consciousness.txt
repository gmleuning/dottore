# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The unhappy consciousness is consciousness of itself as doubled and self-contradicting within one consciousness. It takes the simple unchangeable to be its essence and the manifoldly changeable to be inessential, places itself on the side of the changeable, and must strive to free itself from itself. Its struggle is with an enemy against whom victory means defeat, so that life and action bring only sorrow. Through the surrender of its own will, mediated by a third, there arises for it the representation of reason, the certainty of being all reality.
