# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Universal self-consciousness is the affirmative awareness of oneself in another self. Each, as free individuality, is wholly independent, yet by negating its immediacy does not distinguish itself from the other; it has real universality as reciprocity, knowing itself recognized in the free other in so far as it recognizes that other and knows it to be free. It is the result of the struggle for recognition, and its truth, the unity of consciousness and self-consciousness, is reason.
