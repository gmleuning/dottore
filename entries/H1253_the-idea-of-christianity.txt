# 1253 The Idea of Christianity
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › c. Medieval Philosophy
G: Die Idee des Christentums
Gloss: The Christian teaching that God has become human, which gives infinite worth to the individual soul and sets philosophy a new task.
HGloss: The idea of Christianity, that God is spirit and has become man.

N:
  the idea of Christianity, Christianity, the Christian principle, infinite worth of the individual, the infinite value of the soul, God become man, the Incarnation, the God-man, the Word made flesh, the Logos, the Mediator, reconciliation, redemption, revelation, the Trinity as speculative truth, the unity of divine and human nature, the principle of subjectivity, inwardness, the kingdom of God, the absolute religion, freedom of the spirit

V:
  incarnate, reveal, become flesh, become man, take flesh, reconcile, redeem, mediate, manifest, spiritualize

Adj:
  Christian, speculative, incarnational, trinitarian, revealed, reconciling, redemptive, theanthropic, divine-human, spiritual

Adv:
  in the Christian sense, in spirit and in truth, in Christ, incarnately, christianly

F:
  German: Menschwerdung, Gottmensch, Versöhnung
  Latin: verbum caro factum est, Deus homo
  Greek: theanthrōpos, logos

Phr:
  the infinite worth of the individual
  God has become man
