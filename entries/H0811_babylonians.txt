# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The people of the great city on the Euphrates, set in a fertile plain suited to agriculture, crossed by canals and served by river trade, with the temple of Bel on one bank and royal palaces on the other. Their accumulated wealth and commerce, an organized government with its wise men and Chaldeans, and a manner of life denounced by the prophets mark them out.
