# 129 The Absolute Attribute
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › α. The Absolute
G: Das absolute Attribut
Gloss: A single basic aspect under which the all-inclusive whole is understood, such as thought or physical extension.
HGloss: The absolute as it is for reflection, the whole absolute under one determination, as Spinoza's thought and extension.
