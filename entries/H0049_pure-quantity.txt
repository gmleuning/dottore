# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Pure quantity is quantity with as yet no determinateness or limit in it, a compact, infinite unity that continues itself into itself. It is attraction as the moment of continuity: simple self-same reference to itself, unbroken by any limit or exclusion, though not an immediate unity but the unity of ones that are for themselves. Plurality is still in it, without distinctions, so that it immediately possesses the moment of discreteness. It is repulsion flowing creatively away from itself into what is the same, and therefore unbroken.
