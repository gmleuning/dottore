# 870 Boredom

Gloss: Waiting for inspiration to arrive by itself, through wine or idleness; Hegel says it does not come so.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The belief that sensuous stimulus produces inspiration fails: heated blood achieves nothing by itself and champagne yields no poetry, as Marmontel found surrounded by six thousand bottles. Even the finest genius may lie on the grass morning and evening, enjoying the breeze and gazing at the sky, without a breath of tender inspiration reaching them.
