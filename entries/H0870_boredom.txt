# 870 Boredom

G: Sinnliche Anregung
Gloss: Waiting for inspiration to arrive by itself, through wine or idleness; Hegel says it does not come so.
HGloss: The belief that Begeisterung comes from sensuous stimulation: but champagne yields no poetry, and the best genius may lie morning and evening in the green grass gazing at the sky and feel no breath of inspiration.
