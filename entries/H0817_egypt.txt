# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The land in which the opposed elements of the Persian world are united as a problem: spirit sunk in nature and the impulse to free it are held together in contradiction. Its symbol is the Sphinx, half animal and half human. Driven to comprehend itself, the Egyptian spirit works out its thought in stone and in enigmatic hieroglyphs without reaching a clear solution.

The force which urges it is the impulse of self-comprehension; and it has no other material or ground to work on, in order to teach itself what it is—to realize itself for itself
