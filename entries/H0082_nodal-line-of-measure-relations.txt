# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A nodal line of measures is the series formed when one and the same substrate, in the course of merely quantitative increase and decrease, reaches points at which the altered quantity suddenly becomes a new measure, a new quality, a new something. Up to such a point there is a margin within which the quality is indifferent to the change. The new quality does not grow gradually out of the old: gradualness concerns only the external side of the alteration, and on the qualitative side it is broken off by a leap. The progression goes on to infinity.
