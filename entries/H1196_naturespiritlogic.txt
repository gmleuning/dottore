# 1196 Nature–Spirit–Logic
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › α. The Concept of Philosophy (the Three Syllogisms)
G: Natur–Geist–Logik
Gloss: The order in which nature is linked to thought through mind: the mind comes to know nature and so grasps the thought within it.
HGloss: The second syllogism, nature–spirit–logic, which has spirit as the middle, the standpoint of spirit's reflection.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the second syllogism spirit is the mediator: it presupposes nature and joins it with the logical. This is the standpoint of spirit itself, the syllogism of spiritual reflection within the idea, in which science appears as a subjective cognition whose aim is freedom and which is itself the way of producing that freedom. The first appearance is thereby sublated.
