# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The deficiency of natural beauty explains why the ideal is necessary. The idea is not only substance but concept united with reality, and acquires actuality only through subjectivity that corresponds to the concept; only concrete individuality is true. Natural existence, even animal life, cannot show this inwardness as free, self-transparent individuality, so the imperfection of nature leads to the beauty of art as the one reality adequate to the idea.
