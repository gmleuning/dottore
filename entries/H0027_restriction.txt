# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Restriction is the limit of something posited as its own negative, a negative that is nonetheless essential to it. A bare limit lies quietly beside the existence of something; it becomes a restriction only when something transcends it in itself, referring to it from within as to something non-existent. Restriction is not anything alien or external to the finite: the finite's own determination is at the same time its restriction, and restriction is both itself and the ought, that in which the two are identical.
