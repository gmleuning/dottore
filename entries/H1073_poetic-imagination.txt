# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The poetic way of imagining shapes its subject inwardly, with the imaginative idea itself serving as what stone, colour or tone are to other arts. It holds together the extremes that ordinary consciousness separates, accidental particulars and abstract universals, and is figurative: it brings concrete reality before us so that essence is recognized in and inseparably from outer individuality.
