# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Abstract right is the first sphere of the free will, in which the will exists immediately as a person: the bare capacity for rights, abstracted from all particular aims, welfare and intention. Since it abstracts from the content of action, its commands are mostly prohibitions, and to have a right here is only a possibility or permission.
