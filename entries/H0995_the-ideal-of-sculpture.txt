# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ideal of sculpture presupposes, as all perfect art does, an imperfect stage, a sculpture still symbolic in character, such as the Egyptian. There art is hieroglyphic, a rough sketch that merely recalls its object, as a child's figure or a crude devotional image does, arousing a general idea. The ideal instead requires the living expression of the object's presence in a form fully shaped.
