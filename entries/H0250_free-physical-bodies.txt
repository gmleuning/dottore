# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The free physical bodies are the heavenly bodies taken as physical qualities. Matter as pure identity with itself exists as light, in the star and the sun; the opposition to light falls apart into a rigid body and a dissolved one, moon and comet; and this opposition, gone back into itself, is the planet, the body of the individual totality.
