# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Vanity and pride are what Hegel names as the chief cause of the soul's spinning itself into itself. The state arises mostly when someone shuts themself up in their subjectivity out of dissatisfaction with actuality. The mind thus settled in its inwardness easily loses its understanding of actuality and finds its way only among its subjective representations; and if any vitality remains, this solitary consciousness easily creates some content of its own, treats the purely subjective as objective and fixes it there.
