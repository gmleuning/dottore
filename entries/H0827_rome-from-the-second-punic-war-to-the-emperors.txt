# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The period opened by the second Punic war, which fixed the character of Roman dominion: Carthage is laid low and conquest extends to Macedonia, Syria and Greece. With no outward power left to confront Rome, patriotism gives way to corruption, wars of plunder are joined by civil strife, and at last the sovereignty of the world passes to a single possessor.
