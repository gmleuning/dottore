# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of the ideal sculptural form is the way the classical ideal is realized in the human figure. The difference between human expression of spirit and animal life gives only a vague criterion, since the human form is not ideal from the start; a closer one comes from Greek masterpieces, whose understanding Winckelmann above all advanced by precisely characterizing the forms.
