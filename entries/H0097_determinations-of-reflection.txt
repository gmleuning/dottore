# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The determinations of reflection, or essentialities, are essence as determined, the distinct moments across which it moves and in which it shines, each reflected into itself. First essence is simple self-reference, identity; second, difference, which is diversity and opposition; third, as contradiction, opposition reflected into itself, returning to its foundation. They have customarily been set up as universal laws of thought, such as that everything is equal to itself, but they are no qualitative determinacies that could simply stand side by side.
