# 368 Immediate Infection
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › β. The Animal's Process of Assimilation › b. Practical Process › iii. Digestive Process
G: Unmittelbare Infektion
Gloss: The immediate transformation of what is eaten by the animal's own living power, as though by contagion.
HGloss: The immediate infection of the food by the organism's own living power, which transforms it simply by taking it in.

N:
  immediate infection; immediate assimilation, direct transformation, contagion, infection of the food by life, conversion, transmutation into self; living power over the inorganic
  infection, the animal lymph, immediate conversion

Adv:
  immediately, directly, on contact

Phr:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Because the living being is the universal power over the outer nature opposed to it, assimilation is first immediate: the ingested material is at once fused with animality, infected with it and simply transformed. Only secondly is it mediated digestion, opposition of subject and outer world through the processes of animal water and animal fire.
