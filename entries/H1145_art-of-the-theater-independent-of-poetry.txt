# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The art of the theatre more independent of poetry arises when performance, freed from poetry's rule, makes what was means and accompaniment an end developed for itself, to the gain of music, dance and acting. In one system the actor is the poet's living organ; in the other, as in the Italian commedia dell'arte, the text is a mere frame for the actor's own art.
