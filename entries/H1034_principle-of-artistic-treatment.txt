# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The principle of artistic treatment in painting admits two extremes: depth of subject, religious and moral seriousness in ideal form, and, with insignificant subjects, their detail as they are and the painter's skill. Standing between the plastic ideal and immediate actual detail, painting has both an ideal kind, with the universal as its essence, and a kind presenting the individual closely.
