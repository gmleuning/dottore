# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The second period, a thousand years to the sixteenth century, has its locus in the Christian world, with Arabs and Jews referred to only as historical background. A new religion has arisen, whose essential principle, already known in idea through Neoplatonism, is that God, what has being in and for itself, is spirit.
