# 1125 Song
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper
G: Lied
Gloss: The song, the lyric that is sung.
HGloss: The song, in which the self expresses its mood most simply and is apt to be sung.

N:
  song; lied, lyric song
  song, song &c H1067, lay, ditty, canzone, chanson

V:
  sing

Adj:
  songlike

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Song spreads out the endless variety of lyric moods and reflections, where national differences and the traits of individual poets show most fully. Simple in material, movement, metre and language, it begins in the heart, holds tranquilly to one situation or feeling and rounds it into a whole, and every people is most at home in its songs.
