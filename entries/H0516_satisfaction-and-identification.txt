# 516 Satisfaction and Identification
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness › a. Immediate Self-Consciousness (Desire)
G: Befriedigung und Identifikation
Gloss: The satisfaction of desire, in which the self finds itself again.
HGloss: The self finding its own self-identity in the satisfaction of its desire, and with it the need of another self.

N:
  satisfaction and identification; satisfaction

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Satisfaction and identification complete the process of desire. Its product is that the I closes with itself and is satisfied for itself, though outwardly still single and prey to the endless alternation of desire and satisfaction. Inwardly, in negating immediacy, the result has the character of universality and of the identity of self-consciousness with its object: it posits otherness within itself and changes the selfless object into a free one, another I.
