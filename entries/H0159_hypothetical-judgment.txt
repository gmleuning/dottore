# 159 Hypothetical Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment [Tetrad] › c. Judgment of Necessity
G: Hypothetisches Urteil
Gloss: A judgment of conditional form: "if A, then B."
HGloss: The connection of two contents as necessary, though their being is not posited: if A is, then B is.
