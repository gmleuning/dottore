# 1004 Spirit–Logic–Nature
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › α. The Concept of Philosophy (the Three Syllogisms)
G: Geist–Logik–Natur
Gloss: The order in which mind and nature are linked through thought: the reason common to both.
HGloss: The third syllogism, spirit–logic–nature, which has the self-knowing reason, the logical idea, as the middle.

N:
  spirit–logic–nature; the third syllogism, logic as middle, the idea of philosophy, reason self-knowing, the eternal idea
  the logical idea as middle, the self-thinking idea, self-knowing reason, the absolute idea, the absolutely universal, the self-judging of the idea, the self-division of the idea, the two appearances, the common reason, logos, absolute spirit, the final syllogism, the consummation, the closing of the circle, the unity of nature and spirit, eternal self-enjoyment

V:
  know, unite, consummate
  think itself, divide itself, judge itself, link, join, mediate, bind together, manifest, engender eternally, enjoy itself, conclude, complete, close the circle

Adj:
  absolute, rational, consummate
  self-knowing, self-thinking, self-judging, self-dividing, universal, eternal, logical, final, concluding, all-mediating, speculative

Adv:
  eternally
  absolutely, rationally, logically, universally, speculatively, in and for itself, in the idea, in eternity

Phr:
  die ewige an und für sich seiende Idee sich ewig als absoluter Geist betätigt, erzeugt und genießt [Ger.] [Hegel, Encyclopaedia §577]
