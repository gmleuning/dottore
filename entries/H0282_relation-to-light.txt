# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The body's relation to light is the first determination of shaped corporeality. Shape's self-identical selfhood, its abstract manifestation of itself as simple individuality, is light; but shape is not itself luminous, so this property is a relation to light: transparency in the pure crystal, then refraction, then the darkening of the body from which colour arises.
