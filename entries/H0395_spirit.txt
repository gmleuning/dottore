# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit, or mind, is the idea that has reached its being-for-self, with nature as its presupposition: nature's truth and therefore absolutely first with respect to it, the concept returned out of its externality into identity with itself. Its essence is freedom, the ability to bear the negation of its immediacy and stay self-identical. Its knowledge is the most concrete and hence the highest and hardest, and it is the highest definition of the absolute.
Texts:
  The Philosophy of Mind
    §§377–577
Citations:

  For this reason formally the essence of mind is freedom, the concept's absolute negativity as identity with itself.
