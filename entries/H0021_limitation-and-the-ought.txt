# 21 Limitation and the Ought
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude › iii. Finitude as Such
G: Schranke
Gloss: The limit felt as a barrier to be overcome, and the demand to overcome it. What a thing ought to be stands against what it is.
HGloss: The limit as a restriction (Schranke) within something, and the ought (Sollen) that is to go beyond it; each implies the other.
