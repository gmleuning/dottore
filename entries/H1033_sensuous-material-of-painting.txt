# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sensuous material of painting is the surface, with light and colour. Painting contracts three dimensions to two, a reduction implicit in the principle of inwardness and no defect or caprice but the necessary advance beyond sculpture: the object does not remain a real spatial existent but becomes a reflection of spirit, a pure appearance in the domain of spirit presented to spirit.
