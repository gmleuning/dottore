# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Harmony stands higher than conformity to law: a relation of qualitative differences, a totality of them grounded in the nature of the thing, brought into a congruous unity in which their bare opposition is dissolved, as with the basic colours or the tonic, mediant and dominant. It is the highest stage of abstract form and approaches free subjectivity, yet is not yet soul.
