# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Destruction is what desire does to its object. Its relation to the selfless object is only negative, so that the object is simply consumed: it does not fashion the object, which would preserve its matter and give the subject's form a lasting hold, but annihilates its independence, since self-consciousness cannot yet endure an independent other. Desire is thus destructive in its satisfaction and self-centred in its content, and because satisfaction is single and passing, desire produces itself again.
