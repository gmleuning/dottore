# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The organic sphere begins when the idea enters into existence as life, immediate at first: body as an infinite process that particularizes itself and returns into itself, subjective in nature. It comprises the geological organism, the plant, still falling apart into members that are themselves individuals, and the animal, concrete subjectivity.
