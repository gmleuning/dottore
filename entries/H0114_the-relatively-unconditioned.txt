# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relatively unconditioned is each of the two sides, condition and ground, taken as standing on its own. Condition is for the ground the moment of unconditioned immediacy: an existence that is indifferent to being a condition, yet determined to become the moment of another. Ground, for its part, is the self-subsisting movement of reflection, which has condition outside it as its presupposition. Each is both unconditioned over against the other and conditioned by it.
