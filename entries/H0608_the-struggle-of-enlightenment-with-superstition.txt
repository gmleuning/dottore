# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The struggle of Enlightenment with superstition is the conflict of pure insight with faith, two forms of the same pure consciousness, in which each is the negative of the other. Insight takes every content from faith's calm thought and returns it to the earth. Faith, robbed of what filled it, sinks into longing for an empty beyond and becomes the same as Enlightenment, a relation of the finite to an unknown absolute, only dissatisfied where the other is satisfied.
