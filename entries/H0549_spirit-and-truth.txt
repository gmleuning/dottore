# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit and truth name reason in which truth has the pure concept existing for itself as its own immanent form: the I, certain of itself as infinite universality. Truth here is no longer only the agreement of a representation with its object but the concept that knows itself in objectivity. This truth that is aware of itself is mind, with which the Psychology begins.
