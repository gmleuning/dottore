# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Deportment is the mental expressing itself in the posture of the body. Its foundation is the upright stance, the absolute human gesture: human beings are not erect by nature but make themselves stand upright by the energy of their will, and although habit removes the need for intense effort, the stance must remain pervaded by will if one is not to collapse at once. Gait too is significant, and in it the soul must show its mastery over the body.
