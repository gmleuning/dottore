# 864 Genius
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration
G: Genie
Gloss: The artist's inborn gift for true artistic production, with the energy to carry it through.
HGloss: Genie, the general capacity for true production of art together with the energy to develop and work it out; it has, unlike thought, a side of natural gift.

N:
  genius; native genius, creative genius, the gift, the born artist
  genius, flair, brilliance, fire of genius, heaven-born genius, the divine spark

Adj:
  brilliant, gifted, ingenious

Adv:
  brilliantly, by genius

Phr:
  a stroke of genius
  poeta nascitur, non fit [Lat.] [the poet is born, not made]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Genius is the general ability for the true production of a work of art, with the energy to elaborate and complete it, existing only as subjective, since spiritual production is possible only to a self-conscious subject who makes such creation an aim. It requires a specific aptitude with an essential natural element, in which sense it is inborn, though it also demands long study and industry.
