# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Intensive quantum, or degree, is quantum whose limit is simple and identical with it. The self-external plurality of extensive quantum has gathered itself into a single determination: degree is a determinate magnitude but not an aggregate. The twentieth degree is not a sum of twenty degrees; it is that one degree, in which number is present as a sublated amount. Its determinateness lies outside it, in its connection with other degrees, so that ascent and descent along the scale form a continuous flow.
