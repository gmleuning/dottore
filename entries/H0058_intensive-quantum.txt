# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum › b. Extensive and Intensive Quantum (Degree)
G: Intensive Größe oder Grad
Gloss: A definite amount that is simple and undivided, a degree such as heat or brightness, which still points to a scale of others.
HGloss: Intensive Größe, Grad: the quantum whose determinateness is simple, a single degree, which has its many outside it in the scale of other degrees.
