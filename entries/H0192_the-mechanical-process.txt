# 192 The Mechanical Process
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › α. Mechanism
G: Der mechanische Prozess
Gloss: The pushing and pulling of mechanical things upon one another.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The mechanical process is the interaction of objects. Taken merely as self-enclosed totalities they could not act on one another, and would resemble monads, whose conception Hegel judges deficient: a self-reference that merely mirrors is passivity toward the other, and a pre-established harmony only presupposes the influence it should explain. The determinateness of each object thus has its ground in another, which sets the process going.
