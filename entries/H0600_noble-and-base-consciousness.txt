# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Noble and base consciousness are the two ways of relating to state power and wealth. The consciousness that finds itself equal to them, honouring authority and grateful for wealth, is noble; the base consciousness clings to the inequality: it sees the ruler's power as a shackle on its being-for-itself, obeys with concealed malice and stands ready to revolt, and loves wealth while despising it, since the enjoyment wealth gives is transitory.
