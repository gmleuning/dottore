# 8 Unity of Being and Nothing

G: Einheit von Sein und Nichts

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The unity of being and nothing says that pure being and pure nothing are the same. This sameness is not a rest in which the difference has disappeared. Being and nothing are absolutely distinct, yet equally unseparated and inseparable. What is true is neither of them taken alone, but that each has passed over into the other. The proposition "being and nothing are the same" is therefore one-sided if it leaves out their difference; what states the matter adequately is their movement into one another, which is becoming.
