# 8 Unity of Being and Nothing
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being › c. Becoming
G: Einheit von Sein und Nichts
Gloss: The fact that being and nothing, though opposites, cannot be held apart: each turns into the other as soon as it is thought.
HGloss: Being and nothing are the same, and yet absolutely distinct; their unity is not a resting identity but becoming.
