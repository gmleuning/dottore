# ---- Hegel ----
Def: With Thales the history of philosophy proper begins. His proposition that water is the absolute, the principle, is philosophical because it brings to consciousness that the One is the essence of everything, what alone has being in and for itself, and so steps back from immediate sense perception. Its defect is that the universal is expressed in a particular shape and lacks activity.
