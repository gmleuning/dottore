# 919 The Ideal of Classical Art
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › b. Classical Art

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ideal of classical art is attained where classical art actually achieves its innermost concept: it grasps the spiritual, drawing nature and its powers into its sphere, and gives it the human shape, deed and action, through which spirit shines clearly in complete freedom, the sensuous being no mere symbol but spirit's adequate existence. It is considered in its universality, its particular gods and their individuality.
