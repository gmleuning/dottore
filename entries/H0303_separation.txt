# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Separation is the dissociation of the neutral body, beginning its reversion to particular chemical bodies and their indifference through a series of processes, each dissociation being inseparably linked with a combination. Its forms include reduction, as when a metal combined with sulphur as acid is freed and assumes a pure form, completing the cycle of the chemical process.
