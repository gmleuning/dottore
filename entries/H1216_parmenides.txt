G: Parmenides
Gloss: The philosopher of Elea who taught that being is and non-being is not, so that change and plurality are illusion.
HGloss: Parmenides, with whom philosophy proper begins: pure being, the One, thought for which non-being simply is not.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: For Parmenides the One, grasped in its immediacy, is being. Only being is, and what is nothing is not at all; non-being cannot be known or expressed, and so change has no truth. With this, consciousness raised itself into the domain of free thought, and pure thought made itself its own object.
