# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Quantitative infinity is the sphere in which quantum, determined in itself and yet outside itself, shows that finitude and infinity each contain the moment of the other. The finitude of quantum is its being limited and its sending itself beyond itself; its infinity is its unlimitedness and its being turned back into itself. Unlike the qualitative infinite, which stands abstractly opposed to the finite, here the finite continues into its beyond and refers to itself in it, first in the infinite progress.
