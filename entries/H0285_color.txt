# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Colour is the manifestation in which the bright element and the dark element, each separately existent, are at once united through transparency in a concrete, individualized unity: brittleness begins darkening, which becomes compact metallic solidity. With Goethe, Hegel rejects Newton's theory that white light is composed of five or seven colours as a barbarism applying composition to light.
