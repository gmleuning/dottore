# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The transition of the finite into the infinite follows from the mutual connection of restriction and the ought, each of which contains the other. The finite is thus in itself the contradiction of itself: it sublates itself and ceases to be. At first it only becomes another finite, and so on to infinity. But in this ceasing to be, in the negation of itself, the finite has attained its being-in-itself: the ought, in going beyond restriction, and restriction, in pointing beyond itself, each rejoins itself. This identity with itself, negation of negation, is affirmative being, the other of the finite, which is the infinite.
