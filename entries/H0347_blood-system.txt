# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The blood system belongs to irritability as inward activity, self-contained living movement that can only be circulation. The blood, in its self-pursuing rotation, is the individual life of the whole, animal time; it is not moved by mechanical forces but is itself the principle of movement, universal substance from which everything draws nutriment.
