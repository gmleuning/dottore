# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The comet is the body of dissolution, opposite to the rigid moon: it behaves aberrantly, its eccentric path and physical existence showing contingency, a superficial concretion that may again reduce itself to dust. It is a formal process, an unstable translucent mass without a solid nucleus, yet Hegel insists it belongs to the solar system and is generated within it.
