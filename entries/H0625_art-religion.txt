# 625 Art-Religion

G: Kunst-Religion
Gloss: Religion in which the divine is expressed through works of art, as in ancient Greece.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Art-religion is the religion in which the artisan has become a spiritual worker and brings forth its shape in the form of self-consciousness. It is the religion of an ethical people who know their state as the work of their own self. It moves from the abstract work of art, statue, hymn and cult, through the living work, to the spiritual work in language, epic, tragedy and comedy, where the self becomes the absolute essence and art-religion is completed.
