Def: Drapery in sculpture is no mere drawback to the nude, since sensuous beauty is not sculpture's final aim; the Greeks showed most women clothed. Clothing arises from protection and shame, and ideally covers organs superfluous to spiritual expression. Greek dress is the ideal model, held by the body and shaped by its pose only as needed, otherwise falling freely, unlike stitched modern clothes.
