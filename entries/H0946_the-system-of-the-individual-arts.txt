# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The system of the individual arts is the third part of aesthetics, treating the work of art actualized in the sensuous element, since only as an object presented to sense does beauty explicitly become the ideal. The totality of artistic beauty here falls apart into independent members, each an individual art able to present all the art forms, developing as the forms do from symbolic to romantic and each flowering and decaying.
