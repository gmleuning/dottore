# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Independent or symbolic architecture builds works that express a universal idea for no other purpose. Art's primary need is to present a thought produced by spirit as a human work; unable at first to give abstract, indefinite national insights a spiritual shape, it seizes matter with mass and weight, so that meaning and sensuous reality relate only symbolically. The building is a wordless language present for its own sake, meant to stimulate thought.
