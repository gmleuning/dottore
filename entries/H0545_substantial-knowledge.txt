# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Substantial knowledge is reason as absolute substance that knows. Since reason is the identity of what self-consciousness thinks with the essence of things, it is not only absolute substance, the underlying unity of the subjective and the objective, but truth as awareness, a substance which is aware of itself as that unity and knows its own determinations to be those of what is.
