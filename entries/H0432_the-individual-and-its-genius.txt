# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The genius of an individual is its particularity, which in every situation and relationship decides its conduct and fate. A person is twofold: what they know themself to be in outward life and universal ideas, and what they are within, determined in a particular way. This inner particularity is their destiny, the oracle on which every resolution depends. Even waking, intellectual consciousness is ruled by it so powerfully that the individual stands in dependence on it, as on a selfish other that is nonetheless inseparably one with them.
