# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Direct ratio is the immediate ratio, in which the determinateness of each quantum lies in that of the other. Both have only one limit, itself a quantum, the exponent. One side counts as unit and the other as amount, and when one is altered the other is increased or decreased in proportion, so that the two really make up only one quantum. Because the exponent, as a mere quotient, does not settle which side is unit and which amount, the direct ratio passes into the inverse ratio.
