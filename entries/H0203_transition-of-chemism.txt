# 203 Transition of Chemism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › β. Chemism

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the transition of chemism it appears as the first negation of indifferent objectivity, still burdened with the immediate independence and externality of its object. It forms a totality of three syllogisms, running from neutrality through disruption to the concept that posits its own presupposition, but because of that immediacy they fall apart. As they sublate the externality, the concept is freed to exist for itself, and so becomes purpose.
