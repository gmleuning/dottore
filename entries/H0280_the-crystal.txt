# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The crystal is shape as realized totality, the activity of magnetism passed over into its product. The magnetic poles are neutralized, and one active form crystallizes the body outwardly by bounding the sphere and shapes its inner continuity through and through. Here magnetism and sphericity are united, restless activity comes to rest, and everything is set in its place.
