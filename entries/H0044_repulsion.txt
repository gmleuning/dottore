# 44 Repulsion
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self › c. Repulsion and Attraction
G: Ausschließen des Eins
Gloss: A unit's act of excluding others, which shows that its independence depends on the very others it excludes.
HGloss: Repulsion: the one's excluding of itself from itself into many ones, which as mutual exclusion proves their sameness (Ausschließen des Eins).
