# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The period in which Greece enters world history by its contact with the Persians and shows itself at its most glorious, free individuality in small states withstanding the united power of Oriental despotism. There follow the antagonism of Athens and Sparta, the Peloponnesian war, and the Macedonian empire, in which Alexander leads Greece into Asia and spreads its culture over the East.
