# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Shape is the individual body as a quiescent totality, a material mechanism whose external limits and inner connection are determined by its immanent, freely determining form. Form now manifests itself through its own activity rather than as resistance to outside force: without any stimulus the body contains a silent geometrician that organizes it outwardly and inwardly.
Texts:
  The Philosophy of Nature
    §§310–315
Citations:
  In this way, form manifests itself through its own activity, and does not show itself merely as a characteristic mode of resistance to outside force.

    — Encyclopedia §310, Addition (tr. Miller)
