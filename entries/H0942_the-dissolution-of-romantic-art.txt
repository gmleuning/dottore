# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dissolution of romantic art makes plain what romanticism already implied: the material art grasps becomes wholly contingent and external, since inwardness withdrawn into itself leaves the outer world free to go its own way. Any matter may then embody the heart, and everything, supreme or trivial, finds a place, until art splits into imitation of prosaic reality on one side and subjective humour on the other.
