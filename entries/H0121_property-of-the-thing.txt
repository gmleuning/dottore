# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A property is the thing's negativity of reflection, by which it relates to something else as to itself. Unlike quality, with which a something is immediately one, the property is a way of reciprocal relating that the thing maintains through alteration. A thing has the property of effecting this or that in another and of expressing itself in a characteristic way, under the condition of a suitable other, but the property is its own, its self-identical substrate. In its properties the thing in itself is present as ground.
