# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Natural disposition, or predisposition, is the natural aptitude of an individual as against what they have made of themself by their own activity. It includes talent and genius, a definite direction given to the individual mind by nature, talent producing novelty within a particular province and genius creating a new genre. As mere aptitudes they must be developed by universally valid procedures if they are not to waste away or degenerate; only by that development do they prove that they are there. There is no inborn genius for virtue, which is universal and has to be produced by one's own activity.
