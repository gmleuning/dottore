# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Greek orders are distinguished chiefly by the ratio of height to thickness of columns, the kinds of base and capital, and the spacing between columns, each choice altering the column's whole character. The shaft must stay plain, tapering with an almost imperceptible swelling, decorated only by fluting, since the column has nothing to do but support and rise firm, straight and independent.
