# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The soil is the resource of the substantial estate: land capable of exclusively private ownership, which demands to be formed in an objective way and not merely exploited. Its cultivation brings with it fixed property and settled life, so that agriculture, with marriage, is the foundation of states.
