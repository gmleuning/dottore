# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The real process of combination runs through stages in which differentiated bodies stand in sheer opposition, each essentially only in connection with its other, and by uniting with it produce a neutral body, salt. In the process in its totality these neutral bodies, returning into relation through water, form the chemical process in complete reality, elective affinity.
