# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The people properly so called in whom the empire reaches its culmination. Closely bound to the Medes, so that the two names melt into one and the passage of sovereignty between them makes no essential difference, they set out under Cyrus, extended their rule over all Anterior Asia and so came into contact with the Greeks. Cyrus died having united that region under one sovereignty.
