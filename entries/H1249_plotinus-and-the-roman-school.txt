# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ammonius Saccas founded the school; its most famous member is Plotinus, whose life Porphyry wrote. The whole tenor of Plotinus is guidance to virtue and to intellectual contemplation of the One as its source. The perfect overflows, what is brought forth turns back toward the One, and this return, in which the primary being beholds itself, is understanding, or thinking.
