# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sensations, being immediate and merely found, are single and passing determinations, alterations in the soul's substance posited in its being-for-self. That being-for-self is not just a formal moment: the soul is implicitly a reflected totality of sensation, sensing within itself the whole substantiality that it is in itself, and so it is the feeling soul. Sensation stresses the passive side, the finding of a determinacy, and has to do only with what is individual and contingent, which appears to the sentient soul as its own concrete actuality.
