# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the second division come the Sophists, Socrates, and the Socratics, with the principle of subjectivity. Beyond the object that thought has before it there is now the thinking subject that has these thoughts. This subjectivity of thinking is an essential feature, the self-relating, determining form, and the question becomes what content the universal receives through it.
