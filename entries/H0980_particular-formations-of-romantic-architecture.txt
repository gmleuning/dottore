# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular formations of romantic architecture, considered chiefly in Christian churches of the Gothic style, are the fully enclosed house as the basic form, the form of exterior and interior, and the manner of decoration. The Christian spirit's inward concentration yields a place shut in on every side whose impression of tranquil heart and majestic sublimity governs its shape.
