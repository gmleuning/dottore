# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Leibniz stands in glaring contradiction to Spinoza's substantial unity, in which everything determinate is transitory. Against absolute unity he makes the absolute multiplicity of individual substances his principle, though it is unified in God, the monad of monads. Monads, distinct from atoms, are utterly singular, indivisible, simply one.
