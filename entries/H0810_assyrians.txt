# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The people of Assyria proper, in northern Mesopotamia, whose empire consisted in effect of a single city such as Nineveh. Founded according to tradition by Ninus, who brought Babylonia, Media and Bactriana under his sway, it ended when the satraps rose against Sardanapalus, after which it broke up into Assyrian, Median and Babylonian realms.
