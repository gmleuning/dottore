# 762 Mechanization
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › a. The System of Needs › ii. Abstraction of Needs and Means
G: Das mechanische Arbeiten und die Maschine
Gloss: The replacement of human labour by machines, once work has been divided into simple mechanical steps.
HGloss: Divided labour grows ever more mechanical, until the human being can step away and let the machine take their place (§198).
