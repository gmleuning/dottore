# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Understanding, the formally identical intellect, is the first moment of thinking, working up recollected representations into genera, species, laws, forces and the categories generally, so that the material has the truth of its being only in these thought-forms. Its activity is abstraction, which separates the contingent from the essential; for it the object falls apart into form and content. It is a necessary moment of rational thinking, without which there is no firmness of character.
