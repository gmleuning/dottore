# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conformity to law stands above regularity and is the transition to the freedom of life, natural and spiritual, though it is not yet free subjective unity. It is a totality of essential differences that display unity and connection, admitting qualitative relations rather than mere calculable differences of size. The ellipse, and more freely the oval, show a law binding aspects that are essentially different, a secret bond the viewer only half grasps.
