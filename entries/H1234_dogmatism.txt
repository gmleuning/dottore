# 1234 Dogmatism
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism
G: Der Dogmatismus
Gloss: The schools that set up a criterion of truth and a positive doctrine to give the wise man his footing.
HGloss: Der Dogmatismus: the Stoics and the Epicureans, who each assert a principle and criterion, thought or sensation, as the ground of the individual's freedom.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Dogmatic philosophy sets up a single determinate principle or criterion. One school makes the principle of universality, thinking, the determinant: Stoicism. The other makes that of singularity, sensation: Epicureanism. Stoicism is Cynicism more clearly elaborated by thought, as Epicureanism shares the Cyrenaic principle of sensation. Skepticism, in contrast, negates every criterion.
