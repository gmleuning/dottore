# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ivory was used with gold by Phidias for the great statues at Olympia and on the Acropolis, the nude parts of laminated ivory, robes of removable gold plates. It is a clean, smooth and costly material, belonging to the time when statues were coloured; in the Middle Ages it served for small crucifixes, images and drinking vessels.
