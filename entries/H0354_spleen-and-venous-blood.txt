# 354 Spleen and Venous Blood
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › α. The Animal's Formation (Shape) › b. Systems of Shape › iii. Digestive System
G: Milz und Venenblut
Gloss: The spleen and the slow, dark venous blood that collects there.
HGloss: The first moment of the blood's digestive circuit: the spleen and venous blood, a state Hegel calls hypochondriacal and melancholic, the blood turned inward and sluggish.

N:
  spleen and venous blood; spleen, lien, splenic vein, venous blood, dark blood, black bile, atrabile, melancholy humour
  hypochondria, the hypochondriacal state, the melancholic state, splenetic humour

Adj:

F:
  Latin: lien, splen

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first moment of the blood's own digestive circuit is its dull, interior being-for-self, which Hegel calls becoming hypochondriacal and melancholic, its slumber: venous blood in general, which becomes this nocturnal force in the spleen. Here the blood is said to be carbonized, the process in which it becomes earth, that is, absolute subject.
