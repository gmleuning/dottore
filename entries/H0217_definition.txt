# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Definition reduces the subject matter to its concept, abstracting from the externalities of its concrete existence, and apprehends it as a determinate universal, simplifying the wealth that description collects for representation. Its content is given and contingent, and there is no principle for deciding which features belong to the concept and which to external reality, though for products of purposive activity the purpose readily supplies the definition.
