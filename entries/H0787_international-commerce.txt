# ---- Hegel ----
Def: International commerce is the movement by which civil society, driven by its inner dialectic, pushes beyond its limits to seek markets and means of subsistence in other lands. As the earth is the condition of family life, so the sea is the natural element of industry, the greatest means of communication, which links distant countries in trade.
