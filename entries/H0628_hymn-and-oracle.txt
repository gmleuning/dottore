# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Hymn and oracle belong to the higher element which the work of art demands beyond the statue: language, an existence that is immediately self-conscious. The god whose shape has language for its element is a work of art ensouled in itself. In the hymn self-consciousness stays with itself in its essence as devotion, which, kindled in each individual, is the spiritual stream conscious of itself as the same doing in all.
