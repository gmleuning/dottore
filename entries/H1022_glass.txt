# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Glass, in the form of pastes, is counted with gems and cameos among the materials in which Greek art repeated in miniature the whole range of sculpture, from the single god through every sort of grouping to cheerful and graceful conceits, in a perfection beyond price. Like the other small works it drew its subjects from myth and tradition.
