# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sign-making phantasy is intelligence using an intuition to represent something wholly different from what the intuition shows for itself: the sensory matter is given an alien meaning as its soul, as with a flag, a tombstone or the pyramid. Unlike a symbol, a sign need have nothing in common with what it signifies, so that intelligence displays in it a freer mastery, and its meaning must be learned.
