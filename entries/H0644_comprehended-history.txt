# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Comprehended history is the union of two preservations of spirit: history, spirit's existence in the form of contingency, and the science of appearing knowledge, its conceptual organization. History is spirit relinquished into time, a slow succession of spirits, a gallery of images, each taken inward in recollection and born again at a higher level. Together they form the recollection and the Golgotha of absolute spirit, the actuality and certainty of its throne, without which it would be lifeless and alone.
