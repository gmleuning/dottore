# 786 Public Poorhouses and Hospitals

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Public poorhouses and hospitals are the public institutions that supplement private charity and religious foundations in caring for the poor and the sick. Poverty and distress have a subjective side that calls for subjective aid from love and sympathy, but the public authority provides general arrangements, leaving charity enough still to do.
