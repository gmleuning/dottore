# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The judgement of the concept is that in which the predicate, such as good, bad, true or right, measures the fact against its concept as an ought it presupposes. It has been called the judgement of modality and taken to concern only the copula's value for thought, but it is objective and the truth of the earlier judgements, since it rests on the concept itself and not on external reflection. It is assertoric, then problematic, then apodictic.
