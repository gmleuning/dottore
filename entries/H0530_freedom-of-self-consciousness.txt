# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The freedom of self-consciousness is that of consciousness which thinks: an I that is an object to itself in concepts, so that in moving among them it moves within itself and is not in an other. It is first free only as the universal essence, withdrawn from the manifold of objective being. Its history is stoicism, which withdraws into the simple essentiality of thought; skepticism, which realizes that freedom by annihilating the determinate world; and the unhappy consciousness, which holds the resulting contradiction within one doubled consciousness.
