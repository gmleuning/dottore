Gloss: Clawed mammals: carnivores, rodents, bats, sloths.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Animals with claws form the second great class of mammals, taken together with rodents, bats and sloths: dogs and beasts of prey such as the lion. By opposing itself to its non-organic nature through its weapons, the animal shows that it is a subject for itself, and the classes are accurately distinguished on this basis.
