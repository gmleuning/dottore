# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The spiritual work of art is the work whose element and dwelling is language, where the particular spirits of peoples gather into one pantheon for a common undertaking. It unfolds as epic, in which the world is represented through the bard; tragedy, in which the hero speaks in person and the actor is essential to the mask; and comedy, in which the self lets the mask fall and shows itself as the fate of the gods.
