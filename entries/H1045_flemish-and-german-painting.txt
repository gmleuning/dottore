# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Flemish and German painting neither could nor wished to attain the free ideal forms of the Italians; it expressed depth of feeling and self-sufficient mind, joined to individual characters troubled by worldly cares who thereby won civil virtues. Its final achievement is utter absorption in the world and daily life, which divides painting into the most varied kinds.
