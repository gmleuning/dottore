# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Induction is the syllogism of reflection whose middle term is singularity completed, all the singulars, gathered into a genus and joined to a universal property on the ground that it is found in each of them. It is not the syllogism of mere perception but of experience. Yet it remains essentially subjective, since the collecting of singulars into the genus is an external reflection and the singulars persist in their immediacy.
