# 478 Mastery and Servitude
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness › b. Recognitive Self-Consciousness
G: Herrschaft und Knechtschaft
Gloss: The unequal relation that results from the struggle for recognition: one party rules, the other works.
HGloss: The unequal recognition of master and servant, in which the servant's fear and labour turn out to be the way to freedom.
