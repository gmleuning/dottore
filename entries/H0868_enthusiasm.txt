# 868 Enthusiasm
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration
G: Begeisterung
Gloss: The inspired state in which imagination and execution become one.
HGloss: Begeisterung, the activity of Phantasie and of technical execution considered as a state in the artist.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Enthusiasm, or inspiration, is the activity of imagination and technical execution taken in itself as the basic condition of the artist: the state of the artist actively forming both the inner conception and the outward execution of the work, a double activity for which it is necessary. It takes fire on a definite material that imagination seizes in order to express it artistically.
