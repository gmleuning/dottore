G: Die Wunder und Legenden
Gloss: The miracles and legends of the saints, where the divine breaks into the course of nature.
HGloss: Die Wunder und Legenden: the divine shown as breaking the natural connection of things, which for art easily becomes the merely fanciful and adventurous.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Miracles and legends represent the divine breaking into common contingent reality, inverting and interrupting the natural course of things so as to move hearts. Hegel objects that the divine rules nature only as reason, through the unchangeable laws it has implanted, so that legends easily pass into the abstruse, tasteless, senseless and laughable when faith is to be stirred by what is irrational.
