# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Nation and family are the two shapes of the ethical substance. As actual substance it is a people, and as actual consciousness the citizens, who have their certainty of themselves in a spirit that exists and is in force. The family is the same ethical life in the element of immediacy, a natural ethical community, the unaware concept of the people's conscious actuality, standing over against the universal spirit. Its relations are ethical not through natural tie, sentiment or love but only as spiritual.
