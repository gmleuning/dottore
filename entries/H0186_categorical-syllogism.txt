# 186 Categorical Syllogism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › c. Syllogism of Necessity
G: Kategorischer Schluss
Gloss: Inference whose connecting term is a kind or genus.
HGloss: The middle is the genus, the substantial nature of the extremes.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The categorical syllogism has one or both premises categorical and takes objective universality as its middle term. In its full import it is the first syllogism of necessity, in which a subject is joined with a predicate through its substance, the essential nature of the singular and not just any property. The contingency of connecting a subject with just any quality through just any middle falls away, and with it the endless demand for proof.
