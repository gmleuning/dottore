# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Moral duty is the good as the universal, abstract essentiality of the will, which the particular subject is obliged to do for duty's sake. Hegel credits Kant with this insight, but held alone, without passing over to ethical life, the principle is an empty formalism, from which no particular duties can be derived.
