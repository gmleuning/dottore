# ---- Hegel ----
Def: With growth is bound up the individuality's return into itself, the production of the bud: a fresh plant upon the old one, or the old plant's simple return into the tendency to become a new one. Here the process closes, the plant preserving itself by reproducing itself, which is at once the production of another plant.
