# 1109 The Novel

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The novel, which Knox calls romance, is the modern popular epic: it has again the wealth of interests, characters and events and the broad background of a world, but lacks the primitive poetic situation of epic proper, presupposing a prosaically ordered world in which it wins back poetry's right. Its typical collision is between the poetry of the heart and the opposing prose of circumstances.
