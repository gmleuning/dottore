# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Associative imagination relates images to one another, subsuming individual representations under a universal that forms their connection and giving them a subjective bond in place of an objective one. What enables images to relate is what they have in common, so that universal representations arise by the spontaneous activity of intelligence, not by many similar images piling up on one another while a mere spectator looks on.
