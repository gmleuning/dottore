# ---- Hegel ----
Def: With the modern era thinking emerges on its own account, separating itself from theology and arising essentially as something subjective, in antithesis to a being that subsists outside it. Its exclusive concern is to reconcile this opposition and conceive the cleavage of being and thinking at its most extreme; from this point onward all philosophy concerns itself with this unity.
