# 729 The Right of Insight into the Good
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality › γ. The Good and Conscience
G: Das Recht der Einsicht in das Gute
Gloss: The right of the will to accept as good only what it knows to be good.
HGloss: The subjective will's right that what it recognizes as valid be seen by it as good.

N:
  the right of insight into the good; the subject's right to recognize only what it knows as good, moral autonomy, rational insight, informed conscience
  the right of the subjective will, the right of insight, moral insight, conviction, recognition of the good, the right to know the good

V:
  recognize, judge, assent, insist on understanding
  acquiesce, accept, consent
  know, approve, acknowledge

Adj:
  autonomous, rational, insightful, reflective
  knowing, convinced, subjective

Adv:
  on one's own insight
  with insight, knowingly, on one's own conviction

Phr:
  I will accept nothing I do not see to be good
  see for oneself
  to be convinced

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The right of insight into the good is the subjective will's highest right: that what it is to recognize as valid be seen by it as good, and that its action be imputed to it as right or wrong, good or evil, according to its knowledge of the value the action has.
