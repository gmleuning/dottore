# 1077 Tempo
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse
G: Das Zeitmaß
Gloss: The time-measure of verse: the length of syllables, their grouping into feet, and the beat that orders them.
HGloss: Das Zeitmaß, the first element of rhythmic verse: the fixed measure of long and short syllables in time.

N:
  tempo; time-measure, quantity, duration, length, pace, movement
  quantity

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Tempo is the fixed time of syllables in their difference of long and short. Since words of many syllables and monosyllables yield an accidental mixture without measure, poetry, as music fixes the duration of notes by tempo, lays down particular combinations of longs and shorts as the law governing the temporal duration of the succession of syllables.
