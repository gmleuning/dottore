# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Bronze was antiquity's most widespread and favoured material, cast by the Greeks with supreme mastery for gods and other works. Its darker tone and sheen lack marble's abstractness but are warmer; skilful casting made works cheaper and quicker, so pure as to need no chiselling, and its malleability allows every variety of subject and size.
