# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third element is that of the community, in which spirit comprehends the history that was enacted in appearance and recognizes in it the idea of God, his life and movement. The community is made up of single subjects who are in the Spirit of God. Each must traverse in itself the process of reconciliation, and so becomes spirit and a citizen of the kingdom of God, on the presupposition that reconciliation has already been accomplished.
