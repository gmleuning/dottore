# 862 Circumspection
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › a. Fancy
G: Besonnenheit
Gloss: The clear-headed awareness with which the artist sorts, orders and masters the material.
HGloss: The waking Besonnenheit of the understanding: without it, without sorting and distinguishing, the artist cannot master the content, and it is foolish to think the true artist does not know what he does.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Circumspection is the watchful discrimination of the understanding which, with depth of heart, the artist needs in order to fuse rational content with outward shape. It is absurd to suppose that poems like Homer's came to the poet in sleep: without circumspection, sorting and criticism no artist can master the material, and it is silly to believe that the genuine artist does not know what is being done.
