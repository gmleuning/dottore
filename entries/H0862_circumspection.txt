# 862 Circumspection
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › a. Fancy
G: Besonnenheit
Gloss: The clear-headed awareness with which the artist sorts, orders and masters the material.
HGloss: The waking Besonnenheit of the understanding: without it, without sorting and distinguishing, the artist cannot master the content, and it is foolish to think the true artist does not know what he does.

N:
  circumspection; Besonnenheit, deliberation, presence of mind, clear-headedness, sobriety, judgment, discernment, discrimination, self-possession, control, artistic intelligence

V:
  deliberate, weigh, sort, discriminate, know what one is doing

Adj:
  circumspect, deliberate, clear-headed, sober, self-possessed, considered

Adv:
  deliberately, advisedly

Phr:
  es ist thöricht zu glauben, der ächte Künstler wisse nicht was er thut [Ger.] [Hegel, Aesthetics]
  Gedichte wie die homerischen seyen dem Dichter im Schlafe gekommen [Ger.] [Hegel, Aesthetics, as an absurd belief]
