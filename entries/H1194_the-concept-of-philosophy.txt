# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The concept of philosophy is the self-thinking idea, truth that knows, the logical as universality verified in concrete content as its actuality. With it science returns to its beginning, the logical now its result as the spiritual. Three syllogisms develop this appearance, each of logic, nature and spirit in turn serving as middle, until the eternal idea eternally sets itself to work, engenders and enjoys itself as absolute spirit.
