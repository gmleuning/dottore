# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Adornment is among the external accessories that great sculpture keeps simple and sparing, since the figure itself and its expression, not outward ornament, must give the spiritual meaning. Ornaments and hairdress still help distinguish gods, as Winckelmann observed of the rising locks of Zeus or Ceres' diadem; a viper bracelet misled scholars into seeing Cleopatra.
