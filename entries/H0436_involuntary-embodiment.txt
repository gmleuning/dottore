# 436 Involuntary Embodiment
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › γ. Actual Soul
G: Unwillkürliche Verleiblichung
Gloss: The body's expression of the soul without the soul's willing it.
HGloss: The soul's expression in the body that occurs without its will: laughing, weeping, the voice.

N:

  suffusion, flush, quiver, flutter, tingling, throb, palpitation, trepidation
  unwilled expression, pallor, trembling, laughter, weeping, sighing

V:
  betray, give away, show, reveal unwittingly
  let slip
  pale, tremble, laugh, weep

Adj:
  involuntary, unconscious, reflexive, unwitting, spontaneous, telltale
  instinctive, automatic, unthinking, unintentional
  unwilled

Adv:
  involuntarily, despite oneself, unwittingly
  in spite of oneself

Phr:
  the face betrays the heart
  laughing and crying
  to give oneself away
