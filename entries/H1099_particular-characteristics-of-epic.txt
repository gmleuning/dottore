# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular characteristics of epic proper are drawn above all from the Homeric poems, since little can be said generally apart from history. They concern the general state of the world needed as ground for an epic event, the nature of that individual event, and the form in which the two are woven into a work of art, an action ramified into its whole age and nation.
