# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Quality is determinateness taken by itself as existing immediately: totally simple, not yet detached from being, so that nothing further can be said of it as such. Since existence contains nothing as well as being, quality is posited in both determinations. As existent it is reality; affected by a negating it is negation, still a quality but one that counts as a lack and is further determined as limit or restriction. Both are existence, so reality conceals its negation, and negation is not mere nothing but a quality determined with non-being.
