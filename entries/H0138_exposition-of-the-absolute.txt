# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The exposition of the absolute is the display by which the absolute, the unity of inner and outer, shows itself. It is absolute form and absolute content together, an identity each of whose moments is itself the whole, so that determinateness has become a wholly transparent shine. Essence, existence, whole, parts and force have gone to ground in it. Since its form is simple self-identity the absolute does not determine itself, and the exposition appears as a negating of what reflection brings to it.
