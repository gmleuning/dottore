# 784 Provision of Education

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The provision of education is civil society's right and duty, as a universal family, to superintend and influence education insofar as it bears on the child's capacity to become a member of society. Society's right here is paramount over the preferences of parents: it may compel them to send their children to school and to have them vaccinated.
