# 784 Provision of Education
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › c. Public Authority and Corporation › i. Public Authority (Police) › 2. Common Utility › c. Instantiation of Citizenship
G: Erziehung
Gloss: Society's right to oversee the education of children, through schools, school attendance and vaccination.
HGloss: As universal family, civil society has the duty and right to oversee education, even against the parents' will: to make them send their children to school and have them vaccinated (§239).
