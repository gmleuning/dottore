# 993 Substantive Individuality
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › i. Principle of Sculpture › 2. Beautiful Sculptural Form
G: Substantielle Individualität
Gloss: Individual character as the embodiment of what is enduring.
HGloss: Individuality embodying the substantial, the god or hero as the universal in an individual shape.

N:
  substantive individuality; self-contained individuality, essential character, enduring type
  substantial individuality, the substantial character, the enduring, the abiding, the permanent, the universal in the individual, ethos, the god as substantial individual, fixity, constancy
  haecceity, individuation

V:
  individuate, persist
  endure, abide, embody, stand fast

Adj:
  substantive, essential, self-contained
  substantial, enduring, abiding, permanent, lasting, constant, universal, objective

Adv:
  substantively
  substantially, enduringly, abidingly, permanently

Phr:
  individual yet universal
  the universal in one figure
  a god among gods

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Substantive individuality is sculpture's essential task: to implant in the human figure spiritual substance in an individuality not yet subjectively particularized, bringing figure and substance into harmony so that only the universal and permanent in the bodily forms is emphasized and the fortuitous and transient stripped away, while the figure still does not lack individuality.
