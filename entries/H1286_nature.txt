# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The philosophy of nature is a thinking consideration of nature, the idea in the form of otherness, which seeks the universal in forces, laws and genera and grasps it in its own immanent necessity, presupposing empirical physics. Since externality is nature's specific character, nature shows necessity and contingency rather than freedom. It proceeds through mechanics, physics and organics.
