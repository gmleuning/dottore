# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Epic is the first language of the spiritual work of art, with a universal content in the sense of the completeness of a world, though not yet that of thought. Its element is representation. The bard is the single actual self from whom the world is brought forth and who vanishes into the content, moved not by nature but by memory. It forms a syllogism joining the universal world of the gods with the singular bard through a people in its heroes.
