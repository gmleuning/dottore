# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Life and death are the terms in which recognition is first tested. Freedom is proven only by putting life at stake, but this trial by death does away with the very truth that was to come of it. Death is the natural negation of consciousness, a negation without self-sufficiency, which leaves no one to give or receive recognition: its deed is abstract negation, not the negation of consciousness, which preserves what it sublates. Self-consciousness learns that life is as essential to it as pure self-consciousness.
