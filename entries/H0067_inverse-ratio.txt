# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inverse ratio is the direct ratio sublated: the exponent now has the value of a product, the unity of unit and amount, and the ratio itself has become alterable. As one side increases the other decreases, so that each is what it is by relating negatively to the other, while the exponent, a fixed quantum, is the limit of their reciprocal limiting, a beyond each approaches endlessly. Since the exponent preserves itself in the negation of every particular value of its moments, the ratio becomes the ratio of powers.
