# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The house is the fundamental type that supplies architecture's chief proportions. Classical building devises its plan from spiritual purposes by the intellect, with no direct natural model, so that it is freer than sculpture, yet abstract and dry in its mathematical forms. The relation of load and support must be kept in due proportion, governed among the Greeks by a secret eurhythmy.
