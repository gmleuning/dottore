# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The religions of transition belong still to nature religion but move toward spirit by taking the manifold of nature back into a concrete unity. In the religion of light God is the good, still in a natural mode. In the second form subjectivity passes through negation, so that death and rising again belong to God himself. In the Egyptian religion the meaning struggles to make itself visible, as an enigma.
