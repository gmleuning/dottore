# 589 Ethical Action

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ethical action is the deed that sets the unmoved into motion. Ethical consciousness knows what it has to do and has decided for one law, divine or human, and is therefore character. Acting links the unconscious to the conscious and turns the harmony of the two laws into their opposition, bringing a violated power out against the agent. In the deed the agent meets guilt, and at last destiny, in which the shape of ethical substance perishes.
