# 795 The Sovereign as Natural Individual

G: Der Souverän als natürliches Individuum

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sovereign as natural individual is the monarch determined by birth. Since the ultimate self of the state's will, taken abstractly, is immediate individuality, the determination of naturalness lies in its concept: this individual is raised to the dignity of monarchy in an immediate, natural fashion, through birth.
