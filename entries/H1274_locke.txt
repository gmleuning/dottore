# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Locke's thought is that our general ideas rest upon experience. Against Spinoza, who begins from definitions, he shows how ideas such as substance, cause, and effect stem from experience; this concerns their empirical origin in consciousness. General ideas are creations of the understanding, which makes them from representations obtained from sensation and reflection by a combining activity.
