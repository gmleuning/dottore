# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular aspects of the ideal form, treated chiefly after Winckelmann, concern how Greek artists made the human form express spirit. That expression must spread over the whole body but is concentrated in the face, the other members reflecting spirit only in their position. So Hegel considers the head, then the position and movement of the body, and then the principle of drapery.
