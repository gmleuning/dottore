G: Die oberdeutsche Malerei
Gloss: The painting of the old German masters, Dürer, Holbein and Cranach.
HGloss: Die oberdeutsche Malerei: the old painting of upper Germany, close to the Netherlandish school, intense in piety and expression.

Adj:
  German, Düreresque

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: German painting, which Hegel joins to that of the Low Countries, expresses depth of feeling in faith together with the stubborn individuality of characters. North German masters excelled in torments and ugliness, showing in the Passion the crudity and hatred of soldiers, with confused crowds and little harmony, though painters such as Dürer kept inner nobility and rose above mere barbarity.
