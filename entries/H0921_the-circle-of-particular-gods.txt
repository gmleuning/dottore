# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The circle of particular gods follows because divinity made visible in immediate existence necessarily becomes many shapes: polytheism is essential to classical art. Each god is an independent individual, no mere allegory of a universal quality but a comprehensive totality bearing attributes of others, so that their blessedness is universal self-repose with power to act in many ways. They form no systematic whole.
