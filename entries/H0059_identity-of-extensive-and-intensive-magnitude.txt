# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The identity of extensive and intensive magnitude is that they are one determinateness of quantum, distinguished only in that one has its amount within and the other without. Degree has its determinateness through its connection with an amount that is its own, and so is extensive; extensive magnitude collapses into the oneness of degree and steps outside it. With this identity there comes forth a something, mediated with itself through the negation of these distinct terms, which has a quantum while its qualitative existence is indifferent to it.
