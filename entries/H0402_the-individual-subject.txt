# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The individual subject is the soul differentiated into singularity, still only as an individualization of natural determinacy and not yet the opposition proper to consciousness. This natural determinacy takes the form of predisposition, temperament, character, physiognomy and the other dispositions and idiosyncrasies found in families and single persons. Here the sphere of contingency begins, and the peculiarities of individuals should not be rated highly: the more cultivated someone is, the less their behaviour shows anything peculiar to themself alone.
