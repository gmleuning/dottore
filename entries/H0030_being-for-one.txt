# 30 Being-for-One
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self › a. Being-for-Self as Such
G: Sein-für-Eines
Gloss: The standing of whatever falls within a self-contained thing: it is there only as an aspect for that thing, not as an independent other; ideality.

N:

  phase, light
  the ideal moment, the moment, what is only for another

V:
  be for one, be for someone, appear to, present itself to, count for, matter to; appropriate, assimilate, internalize, take in, make one's own
  be ideal, be held as a moment

Adj:
  ideal, subjective, perspectival, apparent, phenomenal, internal, internalized, appropriated, assimilated, owned; for-me, mine
  for-one, sublated

Adv:
  for me, to me, in my view, in my eyes, from my standpoint; ideally, in idea
  for one

F:
  German: Idealität, Jemeinigkeit, Sein-für-Eines
