# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Geognosy considers the structure of the earth's formations as the record of a past process: from primitive rocks through the stratified formations upon them to alluvial terrain. Following Ebel, Hegel notes that fossils lie only in particular strata, few in the oldest and more various in the later, and buried forests in alluvial land.
