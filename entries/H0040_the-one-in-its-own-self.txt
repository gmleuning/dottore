# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The one in its own self simply is. Its being is not an existence or a determination through reference to an other, for it has negated that whole circle of categories, and so it cannot become any other: it is unalterable. Its indeterminateness is that of self-reference, absolutely determined being. Any direction from itself towards another is at once reversed. In it there is nothing, and this nothing, distinguished from its being, is posited as the void, the quality of the one in its immediacy.
