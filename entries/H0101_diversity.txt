# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Diversity is identity broken apart: its moments, identity and difference, are each reflected into itself and so subsist indifferent to one another, the diverse remaining what it is. Since neither is referred to the other, the difference is external to them, and they are merely different in general. Reflection here becomes external, comparing them for likeness and unlikeness from a third standpoint, until these indifferent determinations collapse into opposition.
