# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › iii. Different Kinds of Sculpture › 2. Materials for Sculpture › b. Ivory, Gold, Bronze, Marble
G: Gold
Gloss: Gold used for the drapery and ornament of cult statues.
HGloss: Gold, laid over the ivory figure as robe and ornament, its splendour serving the god's majesty.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Gold, joined with ivory, clothed the colossal statues of Phidias, whose robes and mantle were gold plates that could be removed. The costliness of their divine images mattered greatly to the Athenians, and the statue of Athene at Plataea was only superficially gilded while that at Athens bore solid plates of gold.
