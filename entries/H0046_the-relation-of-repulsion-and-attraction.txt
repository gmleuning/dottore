# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of repulsion and attraction is that of two determinations which break out of the difference of the one and the many, each first appearing on its own and yet essentially joined with the other. Taken abstractly, repulsion would be the mere dispersion of the many; but even as negative it is connection, so attraction lies within it, and attraction in turn is only through repulsion. Each presupposes the other, and in the other relates to itself. Their unity is a becoming that collapses into simple immediacy, which is quantity.
