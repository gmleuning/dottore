# 420 Hearing
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › α. Natural Soul › c. Sensation › i. The Senses › 1. Sight and Hearing
G: Gehör
Gloss: The sense of sound, which perceives the inner trembling of bodies.
HGloss: Gehör, the sense of the body's inward ideality: it perceives the tremor in which the body gives up its material subsistence as sound.
