# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The assertoric judgement is the judgement of the concept in its immediacy: its subject is a concrete singular and its predicate expresses the relation of that singular's actual constitution to its concept, as in "this house is bad" or "this action is good". The singular ought to be something and may or may not conform. Since the connection is only asserted, its warrant is a subjective assurance, and its being good or bad hangs on an external third.
