# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Time, bar and rhythm treat the purely temporal side of musical sound. Music sets bodies vibrating and takes up only the duration of that movement, so that time dominates it; the bar is the mathematically regulated measure of time; and rhythm animates this abstract rule by stressing some beats and subordinating others. The self finds itself again in the ordered flow.
