# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Chemical colour belongs to the body's own specified matter: the metallic principle is the material principle of all colouring, a universal pigment, and every pure metal has its particular colour. The chemical determination of colour, as by acid, involves a different principle of lightening darkness, a more immanent self-manifestation of a fiery activity.
