# 470 Fury
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › ii. Derangement › 3. Mania or Insanity
G: Raserei
Gloss: A mania to injure others, even a sudden urge to kill.
HGloss: A positive mania for injuring others, even a suddenly awakening desire to kill, which drives its victims against their own horror of murder to kill even those they love.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fury is the form in which the malice of the insane becomes a positive mania for injuring others, even a suddenly awakened desire to kill, which with irresistible force drives those in its grip, despite an abhorrence of murder that may be present in them, to kill even people they otherwise love tenderly. This malice does not exclude moral and ethical feelings, which, because of the distress and unmediated opposition within the sufferer, can even be intensified.
