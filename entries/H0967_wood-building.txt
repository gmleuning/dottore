# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Building in wood is the first and most natural way to meet the temple's mechanical problem, since its basic determinants are the post that bears and the cross-beams that bind posts together. The tree affords both without extensive workmanship, wood already having a definite linear form whose pieces can be joined at right or other angles into corner posts, supports, beams and roof.
