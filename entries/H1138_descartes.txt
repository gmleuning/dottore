# 1138 Descartes
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy [Tetrad] › d. Modern Philosophy › i. Rationalism
G: Descartes
Gloss: The thinker who began philosophy anew from the doubting, thinking self, making the certainty of one's own thought the foundation of all knowledge and dividing reality into mind and extended matter.
HGloss: Descartes, who began from thought: I think, therefore I am, the beginning of modern philosophy.

N:
  Descartes; mechanism, interactionism, Cartesianism, methodic doubt, Cartesian doubt, Cartesian theatre, Cartesian Circle, the cogito, mind-body dualism, clear and distinct ideas, the evil demon, the pineal gland, vortices, subtle matter, provisional morality, generosity, intuition, deduction, simple natures, wonder, desire, love, hatred, joy, sadness, animal spirits, beast-machine, laws of nature, metaphysics of subjectivity [retrospective, Heidegger]

V:
  doubt, found, divide mind and body, intuit, deduce

Adj:
  Cartesian, dualist, foundationalist, mechanistic, interactionist

Adv:
