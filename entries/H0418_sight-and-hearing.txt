# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sight and hearing are the senses of physical ideality, the first class of senses. Because they sense the abstractly universal and ideal, their totality cannot be concrete but is divided between two moments: for sight the ideal takes the form of simply relating to itself, for hearing the form of producing itself through the negation of the material. Sight is related to space become physical, which is light, and hearing to time become physical, which is sound. Neither requires the direct contact with the object that taste does.
