# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of epic shows first in its simplest, imperfect forms, epigraph, maxim and gnomic collections, where the necessary and self-grounded is expressed without personal feeling, then is rounded into a totality whose centre is a specific sphere of life. Perfect epic presents a whole world of a people through a concrete event, so that epic works become a nation's bibles.
