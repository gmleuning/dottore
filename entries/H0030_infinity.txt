# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The infinite is the negation of negation, being that has reinstated itself out of restrictedness, and so may be taken as a new definition of the absolute. But by merely negating the finite it is not yet free of finitude. The true concept of infinity, the infinite of reason, must be distinguished from the bad infinity of the understanding, which in wanting to keep the infinite pure of the finite only makes it finite. The infinite is first simply the affirmative, then alternates with the finite, and is finally the self-sublation of both in one process.
