# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Constitutional law is the state's relation to itself, its internal constitution and its sovereignty against other states. The state is the end immanent in family and civil society, and its strength lies in the unity of its universal end with the particular interest of individuals, who have duties to it insofar as they also have rights.
