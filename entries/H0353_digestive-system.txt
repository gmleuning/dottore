# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The digestive system is the system of reproduction, which creates no structure of its own as the other systems do, its function being assimilation. In crass form it is cellular tissue, glands and skin; turned outward it takes in food by the mouth and works on it with saliva, juices and bile; turned inward it is stomach and intestine.
