# 1094 Classical Metres in Modern Languages
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › iii. Unification of Verse and Rhyme
G: Antike Versmaße in neueren Sprachen
Gloss: The imitation of ancient metres in modern languages, such as the German hexameter.
HGloss: The attempt to carry the classical metres into the modern languages, as in Klopstock's odes and Voss's Homer, where the accent of meaning resists quantity.

N:
  classical metres in modern languages; accentual hexameter, German hexameter, English hexameter, English Sapphics, imitation of classical metres, Klopstock's odes, Voss's Homer

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Classical metres in modern languages were attempted by Klopstock, who largely forsook rhyme to imitate the ancients, and by Voss, while Goethe doubted whether their wide folds suit us. Modern languages lack natural quantity, since verbal accent given by meaning makes lengths uncertain, so that ancient plasticity cannot be achieved and imitation succeeds only in the more strongly marked rhythms.
