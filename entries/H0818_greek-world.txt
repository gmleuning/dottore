# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The second stage of world history, comparable to youth in its concrete freshness of soul. Its centre is individuality conditioned by beauty: spirit transforms what is natural into an expression of itself, in the culture of the body, the shaping of the gods and the form of the state. The universal is willed as custom, without reflective subjectivity, and slavery is a condition of its democracy.
