# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Music's treatment of its subject matter can free itself from any text, but then remains empty and is not yet art; only as the adequate expression of a spiritual content is it genuine art. Its task is to make the veiled inner life resound, so that inwardness is the form in which it grasps everything, above all as feeling, from cheerfulness to grief, awe and love.
