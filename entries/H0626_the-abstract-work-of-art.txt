# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The abstract work of art is the first work of art, immediate, abstract and singular. It must move from this objective mode toward self-consciousness, while self-consciousness in the cult seeks to overcome its difference from its own spirit. It appears as the statue of the god, which is a thing without self-consciousness; as the hymn, in which the god exists in language; and as the cult, in which the two sides give up their one-sidedness and become one.
