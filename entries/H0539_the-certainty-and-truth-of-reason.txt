# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The certainty and truth of reason is reason as it first comes on the scene, asserting that I am I in the sense that the I is all reality and presence and nothing else counts as other. It has behind it, and forgotten, the path on which otherness vanished: that what is in itself is only in so far as it is for consciousness. As yet the reality is only a pure universal, the simple category, the unity of self-consciousness and being. An idealism that lets this unity fall apart again into consciousness and an in-itself facing it is one-sided and bad.
