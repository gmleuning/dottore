# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Drama proper, the third genre between tragedy and comedy, reconciles their difference so that the two sides meet in a concrete whole, though it is of less striking importance. The Greek satyr plays and Plautus' tragicomedy belong here, and modern plays rest on the view that despite conflicts of character and passion human action can produce a harmonious situation.
