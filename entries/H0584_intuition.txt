# 584 Intuition
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › III. Mind (Psychology) › α. Theoretical Mind
G: Anschauung
Gloss: The mind's direct, unreflective grasp of an object.
HGloss: The mind's immediate knowing of an object it finds: feeling, attention, intuition.

N:
  intuition; immediate cognition, direct apprehension, beholding, viewing, sensuous intuition, perception of the given, insight
  cognizance, acquaintance, apprehension
  immediate apprehension, direct awareness, sensuous knowing

V:
  intuit, behold, view, apprehend directly, see at a glance
  see, discern, perceive, descry
  apprehend, grasp directly

Adj:
  intuitive, immediate, direct, perceptual, unmediated
  unreflective, non-discursive

Adv:
  intuitively, at a glance, at first sight
  immediately, directly
