# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Hardening is the first form of habit, in which immediate sensation is posited as negated, as something indifferent. Against frost, heat and weariness, against pleasant tastes, and in the hardening of the heart against distress, habit is a strength by which what affects us, though still sensed, is reduced to something external and immediate. The soul's universal being maintains its abstract being-for-itself in it, and self-feeling, consciousness and other purposes are no longer taken up with it.
