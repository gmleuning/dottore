# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The living work of art arises in the cult of an ethical people whose god is friendly to consciousness, since the essence has the self within it. The frenzy of the mysteries and the beautiful body of the festival are its two sides: in the one the self is outside itself, in the other the spiritual essence is embodied, but each lacks balance with the other. Their completion, in which inwardness is as outward as outwardness is inward, is language.
