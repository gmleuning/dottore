G: Natürliche Länge und Kürze der Silben
Gloss: The natural length and shortness of syllables, by vowel or by position.
HGloss: The natural length and brevity of syllables, given by the sounds themselves or by position, on which ancient rhythm rests.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Natural syllables have their length and shortness grounded in the speech sounds themselves, vowels and consonants. Diphthongs and long vowels are long by nature, and in ancient languages position, two or more consonants between vowels, lengthens a short syllable through the longer time needed to articulate it; modern languages use other criteria.
