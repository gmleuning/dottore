# 392 Disease from the Universal Subject
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › γ. The Animal's Genus Process › c. Genus and Individual (Disease and Death) › i. Nosology
G: Krankheit, die vom allgemeinen Subjekte ausgeht
Gloss: Illness that begins in the mind and passes into the body.
HGloss: The third kind of disease, which starts in the universal subject, the mind, as when terror or grief strikes the body or a passion wears it down; here disease reaches the soul, and leads over into derangement (&c H0455).

N:
  disease from the universal subject; mental illness, psychosomatic illness, nervous disease, illness of the spirit, sickness of heart, the mind's disease passing into the body, hypochondria, nerves, nervous exhaustion, neurasthenia
  depression, anxiety, eating disorder, addiction, burnout, trauma, PTSD, fatigue, withdrawal symptom, overdose, hangover, wasting away

Adj:
  psychosomatic, nervous, hypochondriac, neurasthenic, heartsick, careworn

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A third form of disease originates in the universal subject, especially in the human being: diseases of the soul, caused by terror, grief and the like, which can even end in death. They stand beside diseases of elemental origin and those of a particular system as the ways in which subjectivity can be deranged.
