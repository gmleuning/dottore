# 392 Disease from the Universal Subject
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › γ. The Animal's Genus Process › c. Genus and Individual (Disease and Death) › i. Nosology
G: Krankheit, die vom allgemeinen Subjekte ausgeht
Gloss: Illness that begins in the mind and passes into the body.
HGloss: The third kind of disease, which starts in the universal subject, the mind, as when terror or grief strikes the body or a passion wears it down; here disease reaches the soul, and leads over into derangement (&c H0455).
