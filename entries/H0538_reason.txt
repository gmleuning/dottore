# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reason is the certainty of consciousness that it is all reality. Self-consciousness, which had saved its freedom at the cost of the world, now turns its negative relation to otherness into a positive one: certain that all actuality is nothing but itself, it behaves toward the world as idealism and for the first time takes an interest in its continuing existence. This certainty is at first only an assertion that has forgotten the path leading to it, and reason must make it true by finding itself in what is.
