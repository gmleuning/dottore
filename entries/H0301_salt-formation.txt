# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Neutralization is the process in which a differentiated body, in sheer opposition to its other and essentially itself only in connection with it, posits itself as identical with its negative: acid and base have a restless urge to sublate themselves by uniting with their opposite. The product is the concrete neutral body, a salt, the fourth kind of body.
