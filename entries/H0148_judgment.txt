# 148 Judgment [Tetrad]
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity
G: Urteil
Gloss: A statement joining a subject to a predicate, saying that an individual is of a certain kind; the dividing of a concept into its parts.
HGloss: The concept's own self-division into its moments, an original division (Ur-teil): the singular is the universal.
