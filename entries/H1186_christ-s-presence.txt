# 1186 Christ's Presence

G: Die Gegenwart Christi
Gloss: God present in the world as an individual human being, in Christ's life and teaching.
HGloss: Die Gegenwart Christi: the unity of divine and human nature appearing for immediate certainty as one individual man, whose life and teaching proclaim the kingdom of God.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The appearance of the God-man can be regarded in two ways. Seen without religion, Christ is an ordinary human being, a teacher and martyr like Socrates; the religious view comes only with the Spirit. As a human being he lives only for the truth and its proclamation. His teaching announces a new consciousness, the reconciliation of humanity with God, which is the kingdom of God, with a revolutionary attitude toward the existing world.
