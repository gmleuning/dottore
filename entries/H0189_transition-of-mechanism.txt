# 189 Transition of Mechanism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › α. Mechanism › c. Absolute Mechanism
G: Übergang des Mechanismus
Gloss: The passage of mechanism beyond purely external relations.
HGloss: Mechanism whose self-determination is law, and which passes beyond indifferent externality into chemism.

N:
  transition of mechanism; passage beyond externality, inner tendency, orientation, self-relation of the object; breakdown of indifference
  the passage out of mechanism, the sublation of externality, the transition to chemism, the law become immanent

V:
  pass beyond, outgrow, transcend, become inward
  pass over, go beyond, sublate

Adj:
  transitional, tending, oriented
  passing, self-sublating

Adv:
  beyond the mechanical, toward affinity
  in transition, beyond externality

Phr:
  more than a machine
  beyond mere mechanism
  to break the mould
