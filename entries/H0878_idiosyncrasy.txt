# 878 Idiosyncrasy
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 3. Authorship › a. Mere Manner
G: Zufällige Eigentümlichkeit
Gloss: A merely accidental peculiarity of the artist, which belongs to them and not to the matter.
HGloss: Manier as the artist's contingent peculiarity (zufällige Eigentümlichkeit), concerning not the general nature of the subject but only the particular artist.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Idiosyncrasy is the accidental peculiarity of the individual artist that constitutes manner. It does not concern the general kinds of art, which rightly require different modes of representation, as the epic poet differs from the lyric, but only this personality and its accomplishment, and may go so far as to contradict the ideal. Art demands that the artist extinguish it.

  But art as such cancels the mere accidentality of the topic as well as of its external appearance and therefore demands of the artist that he shall extinguish in himself the accidental particular characteristics of his own subjective idiosyncrasy.
