# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The historical development of lyric must be treated concretely, since in scarcely any art do period, nationality and the poet's genius so determine form and content. It follows the principal forms of art: oriental lyric, absorbed in outer things without individual independence or romantic depth; Greek and Roman, with Pindar at its summit; and the romantic lyric of Germanic, Latin and Slavonic peoples.
