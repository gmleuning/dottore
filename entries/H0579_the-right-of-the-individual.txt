# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The right of the individual is realized in burial, the duty of the family toward its dead member. Death is a natural event, not something a consciousness does, and the dead are left passive to unreasoning life and the forces of matter. The family adds to nature's movement the movement of consciousness: it takes the deed of destruction upon itself, keeps dishonouring acts away, weds its kin to the womb of the earth, and so makes the dead a member of a community again.
