# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The different styles of romantic building are the Romanesque, developed from Roman architecture, the Gothic proper that arose in the thirteenth century, and the secular building of the Middle Ages. Hegel offers remarks on their chief forms, not a history, tracing the Christian basilica to public imperial halls and keeping the older name Gothic.
