# 722 Purpose
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality › α. Purpose and Responsibility
G: Vorsatz
Gloss: The aim the will set itself in acting, for which alone it is answerable.
HGloss: Vorsatz: the deed as the will purposed it, what lay in its knowledge and aim, as distinct from consequences that fall outside it (§118).
