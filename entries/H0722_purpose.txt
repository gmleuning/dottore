# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Purpose (Vorsatz) is the deed as the will intended it. Action passes into external existence and has a multitude of consequences; those that are the outward form of the action's aim belong to it, while others are external and contingent. The will has the right to own only those consequences that lay in its purpose.
