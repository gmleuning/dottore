# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External execution is required because drama portrays a present action that moves outward into reality and cannot be confined to words. It needs the whole person in speech, gesture and expression, and a definite locality, so that poetry calls on nearly all the other arts, using them as perceptible foundation while some, as music and dance, develop their own independent beauty.
