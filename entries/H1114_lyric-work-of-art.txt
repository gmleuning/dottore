# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The lyric work of art allows boundless variety of turns and tones, so that only its difference from epic can be considered, in unity, development and outer metre and delivery. Its centre of unity is the poet's inner life, which, to hold the poem together, must enter a definite mood or situation and identify with it; most lyrical is a mood of heart gathered on a concrete situation.
