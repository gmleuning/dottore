# 879 Idiom
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 3. Authorship › a. Mere Manner
G: Die Darstellungsweise der Schule
Gloss: A way of working that belongs to an artist and their followers and pupils, become habit through repetition; a school's manner.
HGloss: Manier as a mode of presentation (Darstellungsweise) proper to the artist, his followers and pupils, formed into habit by frequent repetition; it finds most room in painting and music.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Idiom is the special mode of representation belonging to one artist, with followers and school, which repetition turns into habit, in painting and music especially, showing itself in treatment of tone, light and colour and in handling the brush. The more specialized it is, the greater the risk that it degenerates into a cold, soulless repetition in which art sinks to mere manual skill.
