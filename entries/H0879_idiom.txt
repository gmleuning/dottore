G: Die Darstellungsweise der Schule
Gloss: A way of working that belongs to an artist and their followers and pupils, become habit through repetition; a school's manner.
HGloss: Manier as a mode of presentation (Darstellungsweise) proper to the artist, his followers and pupils, formed into habit by frequent repetition; it finds most room in painting and music.
