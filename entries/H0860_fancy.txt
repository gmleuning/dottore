# 860 Fancy
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration
G: Phantasie
Gloss: The artist's power to grasp the actual world and give an inner content an outward, concrete shape.
HGloss: Phantasie, productive imagination as opposed to passive fancy: it grasps reality and gives its rational content a consciousness of itself not in general thoughts but in concrete, individual shape.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fancy, in Knox's rendering of Phantasie, is the most prominent artistic ability and must not be confused with the merely passive imagination, for it is creative. It does not stop at assimilating outer and inner reality but brings to outward appearance the truth and rationality of the actual world, not in general propositions and ideas but in concrete configuration and individual reality.
