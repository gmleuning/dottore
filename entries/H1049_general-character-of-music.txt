# 1049 General Character of Music
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › ii. Music
G: Allgemeiner Charakter der Musik
Gloss: The general nature of music.
HGloss: Music as the art of the inward, which cancels spatial objectivity and has its element in the vibrating, vanishing tone.

N:
  general character of music; inwardness, temporality, sound, the abolition of space, pure feeling
  the nature of music, subjective inwardness, interiority, the inner life, feeling, tone, the note, vibration, the vanishing of the tone, transience, evanescence, objectless inwardness

V:
  sound, resonate
  vibrate, ring, die away, fade, vanish, express, echo, move

Adj:
  temporal, inward, sonorous
  inner, subjective, tonal, evanescent, transient, fleeting, objectless, non-spatial, audible
  ineffable

Adv:
  in time
  inwardly, in sound, in tones, fleetingly, successively

Phr:
  sound vanishes as it arises
  sound that vanishes as it sounds

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of music is set out by comparing it with the visual arts and poetry, then showing how it grasps and portrays content, and finally explaining its special effect on the mind. As a romantic art it dissolves the classical identity of inner life and external existence, being able to express only feeling and accompanying articulated ideas with its sounds.
