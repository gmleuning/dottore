# 7 Becoming
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being
G: Werden
Gloss: Change at its most basic: the passing of being into nothing and of nothing into being. The first concept with real content, since it holds both together.
HGloss: The truth of being and nothing: the movement in which each has already passed over into the other, their immediate vanishing in one another.
