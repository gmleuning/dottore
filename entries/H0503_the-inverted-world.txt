# 503 The Inverted World

G: Die verkehrte Welt

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The inverted world is the second supersensible world, in which the calm realm of laws, the immediate likeness of the perceived world, turns into its opposite. What is like itself repels itself and what is unlike posits itself as like, so that what is sweet in the first law is sour here, and the north pole is the south. This is not a second place set beside the first. It must be thought as inner difference: the supersensible world contains the other within itself and is the inversion of itself, its own opposite in one unity, which is infinity.
