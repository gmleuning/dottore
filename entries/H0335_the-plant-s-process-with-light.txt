# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In its process with light the plant is drawn out of itself by its own self, which lies outside it, and climbs toward it, branching into many individuals. All its energies develop in light, which gives it scent, flavour, colour and firmness of shape and holds it upright; here the plant appropriates light and constitutes itself independently before it.
