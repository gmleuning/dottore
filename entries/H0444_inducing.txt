# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inducing is the intentional evoking of the magnetic state, animal magnetism proper. Just as the spontaneous state ends in dependence on an alien power, so an external power can seize a person at the rupture, implicit in them, between feeling life and thinking consciousness, and bring that rupture into existence. The power is chiefly another subject, though certain substances can exercise it, and only those already disposed to the state enter it easily. Its proximate effect is sleep, the separation of the sentient soul from intellectual consciousness.
