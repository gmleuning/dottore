# 1123 Odes of Mastery
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › b. Ode
G: Die pindarische Ode
Gloss: The Pindaric ode, in which the poet masters his great subject.
HGloss: The Pindaric ode, where the poet's inspiration masters the subject, celebrating victors while ranging freely through myth.

N:
  odes of mastery; Pindaric ode, Pindar, victory ode, epinician ode, triad, strophe and antistrophe
  epinicion

Adj:
  Pindaric

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Odes of mastery, such as many of Pindar's, arise from the opposition between the topic's captivating might and the poet's freedom flashing out against it. This drives the swing and boldness of language and images, apparent lawlessness, digressions and sudden transitions, while the poet's genius resolves the discord into a unified whole that raises the poet above the subject.
