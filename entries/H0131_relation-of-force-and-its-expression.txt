# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of force and its expression is the higher turning back into itself of the whole, in which the unity ceases to be external to the manifold. Force is the negative unity into which the contradiction of whole and parts has resolved: it passes into its expression, which vanishes back into force as its ground, and in this alteration, posited through itself, it remains what it is. Its movement is less a transition than a translation of itself.
