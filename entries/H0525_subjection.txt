# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subjection is the surrender in which the one who prefers life gives up recognition and becomes the bondsman. The bondsman's self-will yields to the master's will and receives the master's purpose for its content, while the master takes up only care for the bondsman's natural life. Immediacy is thus sublated at first on one side only, so that the identity of the two self-consciousnesses comes about in a one-sided way and their contradiction is only relatively resolved.
