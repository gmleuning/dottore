# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Truth is what spirit seeks and enters into. Freedom began in self-consciousness, but spirit is concretely free, not merely formally as self-consciousness is: it enters into all truth. Since it is implicitly reason, and the certainty that there is nothing but reason, it seeks nothing but truth. Its standpoint is that no abyss lies between the object and the knowing subject, and that there is no impenetrable barrier to knowledge.
