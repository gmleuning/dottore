# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feeling, also called touch where it resides chiefly in the fingers, is the sole sense of the third class, that of earthly totality, and the most concrete of all the senses. It relates not to the abstractly ideal or to separated qualities of bodies but to their solid reality. Only for it is there strictly another that subsists for itself, an individual standing against an individual senser. So weight, cohesion, hardness and softness, roughness and smoothness, heat and shape in three dimensions all fall to it.
