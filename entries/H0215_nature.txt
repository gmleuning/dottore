# 215 Nature
# (top of the system)
G: Natur
Gloss: The world of space, time and matter, understood as thought in the form of outwardness, where everything lies outside everything else.

N:
  nature; the natural world, the physical world, the material world, creation, the cosmos, the universe, the world, the earth, Mother Nature, Dame Nature; externality, self-externality, otherness of the idea; the order of nature, course of nature, natural order; philosophy of nature, physiology [archaic sense], natural philosophy
  world, universe, cosmos, kosmos†, macrocosm
  the idea in its otherness

V:
  be natural, grow, come naturally; naturalize; externalize; go forth, alienate itself
  lie outside, exist naturally

Adj:
  natural, physical, material, cosmic, mundane, earthly, worldly, elemental, organic; external, outward, sensuous; native, inborn, innate
  terrestrial, sublunary†
  bodily, corporeal
  exterior

Adv:
  naturally, by nature, according to nature, in the course of nature, physically
  in nature, outwardly
