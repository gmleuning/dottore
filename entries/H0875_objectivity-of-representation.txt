# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Objectivity of representation is not the reproduction of familiar external reality, nor an inwardness too reserved to come to clear expression. True objectivity requires that nothing of the genuine matter that inspires the artist be held back: everything must be wholly unfolded so that the universal substance of the subject appears as clearly as its individual shape. The works are the artist's best part and truth.
