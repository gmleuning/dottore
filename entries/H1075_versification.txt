# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Versification arises because a poetic idea is not only clothed in words but uttered, passing into the sound of words and syllables. Versified prose makes no poetry, yet metre or rhyme is absolutely necessary to it, as its one sensuous fragrance. It opens a new ground beyond ordinary speech, so that to ban verse as unnatural is superficial.
