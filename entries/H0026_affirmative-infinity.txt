# 26 Affirmative Infinity
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › c. Infinity
G: Affirmative Unendlichkeit
Gloss: The unlimited understood as including the limited rather than lying beyond it; completeness rather than endlessness. Pictured as a circle, as against an endless line.
HGloss: True infinity, which contains the finite as a moment within itself: being returned to itself, imaged by the circle rather than the endless line.
