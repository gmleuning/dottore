# 1220 Leucippus
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Leukipp
Gloss: The founder of atomism, who taught that the world is made of indivisible atoms moving in the void.
HGloss: Leucippus, with whom the One becomes principle as the atom, being-for-self, and the void as the negative.

N:
  Leucippus
  the Atomists, atoms and void
  the founder of atomism, the full and the empty, the indivisibles

Adj:
  atomist

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: With Leucippus and Democritus the principles become more ideal: the atom and the nothing, or void. The one is what has being for itself, simple relation to self through negation of other-being, and so an essential thought-determination that must play its part in every logical philosophy, though not as what is ultimate. The void is the negative of the one.
