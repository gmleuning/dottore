# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the Persian religion of light God is known as what has being in and for itself, the good, a unity that determines itself. The good still exists in a natural way, as light. Because this idea of God is abstract, it cannot take the antithesis into itself and overcome it, and so light has darkness over against it, personified superficially as Ormazd and Ahriman.
