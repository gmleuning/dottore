G: Das Gleichnis
Gloss: A comparison stated openly with "like" or "as", setting the image beside the meaning.
HGloss: Das Gleichnis: image and meaning both expressly set out side by side, the poet lingering on the likeness, as in Homer's similes.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Simile sets image and meaning entirely apart, each presented by itself and then related through the likeness of their content. It may seem idle repetition and wearisome superfluity, since the meaning is already explicit. Its proper purpose lies in the poet's imagination, which, however clear about the subject, is driven to give it a concrete shape and so displays its own boldness.
