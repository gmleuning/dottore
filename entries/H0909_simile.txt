G: Das Gleichnis
Gloss: A comparison stated openly with "like" or "as", setting the image beside the meaning.
HGloss: Das Gleichnis: image and meaning both expressly set out side by side, the poet lingering on the likeness, as in Homer's similes.

N:
  simile
  comparison, likeness, similitude, analogy
  the epic simile, Homeric simile

V:
  liken, compare

Adj:
  analogical, similitive

Adv:
  by way of likeness

Phr:
  my love is like a red, red rose [Burns]
