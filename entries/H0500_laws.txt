# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Law is the simple difference which appearance comes to be when related to the inner: the stable picture of unstable appearance. The supersensible world is thus a motionless realm of laws, beyond the perceived world and yet present in it. It is the understanding's first truth but not appearance brought to fulfilment, for each law has its determinateness, so that laws are indefinitely many. Gathered into one, as universal attraction, they say no more than the mere concept of law, namely that all actuality is in itself lawful.
