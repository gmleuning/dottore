# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Cult is the movement in which the god who stirred in the element of self-consciousness and the god motionless as a thing give up their separate determinations and become one, so that the self becomes conscious of the divine essence coming down to it. It begins with the pure sacrifice of a possession, in which the owner renounces property and personality; the essence has already sacrificed itself in becoming animal and fruit, and in the shared meal the self has consciousness of its unity with it.
