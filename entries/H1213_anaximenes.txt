# 1213 Anaximenes
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Anaximenes
Gloss: The Milesian who held that all things come from air, thinned into fire or thickened into water and earth.
HGloss: Anaximenes, for whom the principle is air, the infinite in sensuous form and soul-like, from which things arise by rarefaction and condensation.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Anaximenes, a pupil of Anaximander, makes air the principle. With the early Ionians the principle always takes a natural form, water, air, and the like, so that the universal has a physical shape. Hegel holds the material element nonetheless to have philosophical significance, though each such principle is something particular and therefore defective.
