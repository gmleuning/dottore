# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Cognition is spirit's getting to know things, in the certainty that in concerning itself with them it will know them. It does this through the stages of intuition, representation and thought. Spirit is not afraid of things or diffident about them: it proceeds from the certainty of reason that things cannot resist it, that they are in themselves what it knows them to be, and in this way it reaches truth. This is the standpoint of spirit.
