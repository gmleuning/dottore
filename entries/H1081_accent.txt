# 1081 Accent
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse
G: Der Rhythmus (Akzent und Zäsur)
Gloss: The life that stress and pause give to the bare measure.
HGloss: Der Rhythmus proper, the animation of the time-measure by accent and caesura.

N:
  accent; stress, emphasis, rhythmic animation
  rhythm, accentuation &c H0662

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Accent, with caesura, is what really animates rhythmic time measure, corresponding to the rhythmical beat in music. Each time relation has its own accent, certain positions emphasized by law and attracting the rest into a rounded whole, and this opens great variety in the value of syllables as accent falls on long or short ones.
