# Nature › 3. Organics
G: Vegetabilische Natur
Gloss: Life in the form of a plant: an organism that grows outward into parts, each of which could become a whole plant.
HGloss: The first subjective life: the plant, which is not yet a self but is outside itself in its members, each an individual.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The plant is the first, immediate stage of subjective vitality, in which the objective organism and its subjectivity are still identical. It differentiates and sustains itself by coming out of itself and falling apart into many individuals, each part, bud or branch, being also the whole plant, so that its parts differ only superficially and take over one another's functions.
