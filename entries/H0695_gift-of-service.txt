# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The gift of service is the third species of the contract of gift: a service given without payment, such as the mere safe-keeping of another's property (deposit). Hegel adds that a gift conditional on the donor's death is a testamentary disposition, which lies outside the concept of contract and presupposes civil society.
