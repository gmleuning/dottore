# 326 Parenchyma
# Nature › 3. Organics › II. Plant Nature › α. The Plant's Process of Formation › a. Formation as Such › ii. Cellular Tissue and Vessels
G: Parenchym
Gloss: The plant's soft ground tissue of thin-walled cells.
HGloss: The regular cellular tissue, the plant's undifferentiated substance from which its other formations arise.

N:
  parenchyma; cellular tissue, the regular cellular tissue, ground tissue, cell walls, cellulose, utricles, vesicles
  cells, pith, medulla

Adj:
  cellular, vesicular, vasicular†, medullary

Phr:
  omnis cellula e cellula [Lat.] [Virchow]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general texture of the plant is cellular tissue, made up, as in the animal organism, of small cells, a universal product of animal and vegetable life. In the botanical account Hegel quotes, its regular, loose, spongy form with broad cells is the parenchyma, found especially in bark and pith, unlike the tight, fibrous bast with its narrow cells.
