# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Being-for-itself in measure arises where the qualitative moment, still immediate and with a magnitude of its own, is bound to its specific relation of measure. In any such relation, as in the law of falling bodies, there is an empirical coefficient, an immediate quantum not derived from the law, which is the being-for-itself of the measure, though only as immediate. As the unity of its two sides, one immediate and external and the other specified within, measure becomes complete self-subsistence: a something which is the unity of qualities in a relation of measure.
