# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit
G: Geist
Gloss: The mind as an active power of knowing and willing; psychology, in which intuition, representation, thinking and will are stages of one activity rather than separate faculties.
HGloss: Spirit determined in and for itself, the subject which is its own object: theoretical, practical and free mind.
