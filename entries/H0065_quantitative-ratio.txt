# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Quantitative ratio is the infinite quantum as the unity of quantitative and qualitative determinateness. In ratio a quantum is no longer indifferently determined: it refers to its beyond, which is another quantum, and each has its determinateness in this reference, so that in the other it has returned into itself. The relation is itself a magnitude, a self-enclosed whole. It runs from direct ratio through inverse ratio to the ratio of powers, where the qualitative moment, fully posited, becomes measure.
