# 266 Physics of Particular Individuality
# Nature › 2. Physics
G: Physik der besonderen Individualität
Gloss: The physics of bodies in their particular, measurable properties.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The physics of particular individuality treats bodies in which the former elemental determinatenesses are subject to individual unity. This unity is now an immanent form acting independently, which determines matter in opposition to its gravity, so that matter, spatially determined by its own form, individualizes mechanics: in specific gravity, cohesion, sound and heat.
