# 798 External Sovereignty
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › γ. The State › a. Constitutional Law
G: Äußere Souveränität
Gloss: The state as independent in relation to other states.
HGloss: The state's individuality in relation to other states, its independence.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External sovereignty is the state's individuality in relation to other states. Spirit in its freedom is an infinitely negative relation to itself, a being-for-self that excludes others. Its independence is the primary freedom and highest dignity of a people, and is shown in war and in the estate of courage.
