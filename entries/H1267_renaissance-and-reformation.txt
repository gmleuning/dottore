# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the Renaissance humanity came to know itself justified and free, with the strength to act in its own interests; the arts and sciences flourished, and people turned to the works of the ancients, in which human beings count for something. In the Reformation the principle of subjectivity became a moment of religion itself, with Luther's German Bible essential to subjective freedom.
