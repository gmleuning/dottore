# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The terrestrial organism is the first, merely implicit organism, which does not exist as a living creature because life is essentially a self-mediating activity. Subjective life makes this organism its own presupposition, an immediacy set over against it as condition and outer subsistence: only the shape of an organism, the earth-body as a universal system of individual bodies.
