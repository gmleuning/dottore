# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › a. Syllogism of Existence (Qualitative Syllogism) [Tetrad]
G: Zweite Figur
Gloss: The form of inference whose connecting term is an individual instance.

N:
  second figure; P–S–U, particular–singular–universal; individual as middle term
  U-S-P, the singular as middle, the mean as individual, the particular conclusion

V:
  infer through the individual
  mediate, conclude, infer through the singular

Adj:
  secondary, negative-concluding
  second-figure, singular, mediating

Adv:
  in the second figure
  through the individual

F:
  Latin: Cesare, Camestres, Festino, Baroco, figura secunda
  Arabic: al-shakl al-thānī

Phr:
  no fish is a mammal
  every whale is a mammal
  therefore no whale is a fish
