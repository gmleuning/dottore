# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Italian painting brings to religious subjects a living actuality of spiritual and bodily existence, natural serenity of soul matched by beautiful form. When deep religious feeling animates it, figure and expression reach an original harmony that vividly recalls the classical ideal within Christian art: even grief remains a transfigured suffering of the soul, and a pure song sings through every form.
