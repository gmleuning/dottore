# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Indian philosophy reaches an intellectual substantiality: spirit raises itself to universality and is for itself, wholly abstractly, with nature only the means of its liberation. This is a great discovery of the Orient and the essential foundation of philosophy. European thought stands at the opposite pole, reflective subjectivity, in which the I with its beliefs, will, and intentions counts for so much.
