# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sister has the highest intimation of ethical essence, though it does not rise to consciousness, since the law of the family remains inner feeling. In her brother she meets a being of the same blood, equal to herself, toward whom there is no desire, so that her recognition in him is pure and unmixed with any natural relation. The loss of a brother is therefore irreplaceable to her, and her duty to him is the highest.
