# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Hoofed animals, such as swine, elephants, horned cattle and horses, are a class of mammals whose strength lies in the upper body. Most can be tamed to work, and the formation of their extremities shows a special relation to their non-organic nature; following Lamarck, their feet serve only for walking or running.
