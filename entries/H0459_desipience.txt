# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rambling, or desipience, is the counterpart of absent-mindedness: an interest taken in everything that springs from the inability to fix attention on anything determinate, so that the sufferer stumbles from one object to another. It is mostly incurable. It always comes from a weakness in the power of intellectual consciousness to hold together the whole of its representations, and it often passes into delirium, an unconscious reversal of what is immediately present.
