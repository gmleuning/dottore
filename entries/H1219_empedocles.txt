# 1219 Empedocles
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Empedokles
Gloss: The Sicilian who explained the world by four roots, earth, water, air and fire, joined by love and parted by strife.
HGloss: Empedocles, who joins the elements by love and parts them by strife, a synthesis still without the concept.

N:
  Empedocles
  the four elements, love and strife, the roots
  Empedocles of Acragas, the four roots, Etna, mixture and separation

Adj:
  Empedoclean

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Empedocles holds to four elements that neither come to be nor pass away, everything arising through their union and separation, and adds friendship and enmity as further principles. In these, Aristotle reports, good and evil emerge for the first time as principles. Purpose as the self-determining concept is what Aristotle misses in earlier philosophy and believes he can find in friendship.
