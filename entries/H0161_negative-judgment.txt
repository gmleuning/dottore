# 161 Negative Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment › a. Judgment of Existence (Qualitative Judgment)
G: Negatives Urteil
Gloss: A judgment that denies a quality of a thing, as "the rose is not red."
HGloss: The singular is not this abstract universal but a particular: the rose is not blue, but of some other colour.
