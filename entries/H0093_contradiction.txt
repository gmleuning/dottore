# 93 Contradiction
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › I. Essence as Reflection Within Itself › β. Determinations of Reflection
G: Widerspruch
Gloss: The condition in which a thing both includes and excludes its opposite; a self-opposition that drives change rather than a mere error in reasoning.
HGloss: The positive and negative each containing and excluding the other; the root of all movement and life, not an error to be avoided.
