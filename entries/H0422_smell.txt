# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Smell is the first of the senses of real difference, the sense of the abstract process. It receives a body as it undergoes the simple, indeterminate process of dissipation or evaporation, the silent and imperceptible wasting of all bodies under the action of air. Unlike taste, it does not yet need direct contact with its object.
