# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Phenomenology of Spirit of 1807, announced as the first part of the system of science, presents the coming-to-be of science, or of knowing. It leads consciousness from immediate, sensuous certainty along a long path to the element of science, supplying the individual with the ladder to that standpoint. The dialectical movement consciousness practises on itself, out of which each new object arises, is experience, so that the work is a science of the experience of consciousness.
