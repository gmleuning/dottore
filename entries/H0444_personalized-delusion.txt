# 444 Personalized Delusion
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › ii. Derangement › 2. Madness Proper › c. Fixations
G: Wahn, Gott, Christus oder König zu sein
Gloss: The delusion that one is a great person: God, Christ or a king.
