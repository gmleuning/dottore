# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute difference is difference in and for itself, the difference of essence. It is not made by something external but refers to itself and is therefore simple: in the difference of A and not-A it is the simple "not" that constitutes it. It is not the otherness of existence, where two things lie outside each other and each is something immediate for itself; the other of essence is the other in and for itself, simple determinateness in itself. As difference from itself, it contains identity.
