# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Voluntary embodiment is the bodily expression the soul produces with freedom, when it is no longer confined to the involuntary expression of its sensations but appears in the body with all the freedom it has won. It gives the human body a mental stamp that sets human beings apart from animals far more than any natural trait. It is concentrated chiefly in the face, and shows itself in posture, in the play of features and in gesture. Through habit such expressions become mechanical, needing no special effort of will.
