# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The question whether wooden or stone building was original turns on purpose: the tree supplies posts and beams ready formed, rectilinear pieces suited to the severe purposiveness of classical architecture, while stone is a shapeless mass that must be split and worked and invites excavation. Yet classical architecture, developing into beauty, builds in stone while keeping recognizable the principle of timber.
