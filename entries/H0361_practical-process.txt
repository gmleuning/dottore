# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The practical process begins with a division within the organism, the feeling of externality as negation of the subject, which is also its self-certainty against that negation: a feeling of lack and the urge to remove it. Only the living feels lack, since only a subject can contain and endure its own contradiction.
