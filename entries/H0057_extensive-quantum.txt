# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Extensive quantum is quantum whose limit, as limit, is a plurality: it is discrete in itself and has no being different from its limit and no limit outside it. Its direct opposite is not discrete magnitude but intensive magnitude. It has continuity in it, since its many is everywhere of one kind, and the limit appears as the limiting of a unity. Taken immediately it is number, the amount of one and the same unit, and it differs from number only in that in number the determinateness is expressly posited as plurality.
