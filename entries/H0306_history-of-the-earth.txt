# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The history of the earth lies in the past: the members of this implicit organism do not contain the life-process within themselves but form an external system whose shapes display an underlying idea. Hegel holds that creation is eternal rather than a single event and that nature as universal has no history, though the earth must be considered as having come to be.
