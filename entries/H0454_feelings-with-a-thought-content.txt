# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feelings of the third kind arise when the substantial content of right, morality, ethical life and religion, which derives from thinking, is taken up into the feeling will. They are distinguished by their own content and justified by it. Shame and remorse belong here, remorse being the felt discord between what one has done and one's duty or advantage. The content does not need the form of feeling, which is only that of immediate, particular subjectivity: what is rational in good feeling is the same content that thought has in its universality and truth.
