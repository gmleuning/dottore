# 1041 Leibniz
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy [Tetrad] › d. Modern Philosophy › i. Rationalism
G: Leibniz
Gloss: The thinker who answered the single substance with infinitely many self-contained centres of activity, each mirroring the whole universe and kept in step by a harmony set in advance.

N:
  Leibniz; monads, pre-established harmony, the best of all possible worlds, principle of sufficient reason, principle of contradiction, identity of indiscernibles, theodicy, calculus, apperception, unconscious perception

V:
  harmonize, justify, calculate

Adj:
  Leibnizian, monadic, optimistic

Adv:
  harmoniously

F:
  German: Monadologie
  French: petites perceptions [unconscious perception]

Phr:
  the best of all possible worlds [Leibniz, mocked by Voltaire]
  monads have no windows [Leibniz]
  two great principles [Leibniz]
