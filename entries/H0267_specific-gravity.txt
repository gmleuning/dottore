# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Specific gravity or density is the first, simple specification of matter, the relation of the weight of a mass to its volume. In it matter, in its selfhood, tears itself free from its abstract relation to the central body and universal gravity, ceases to fill space uniformly and opposes a specific inwardness to abstract externality; pores explain nothing.
