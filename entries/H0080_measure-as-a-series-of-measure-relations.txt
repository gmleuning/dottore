# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A series of measure relations arises because something that is in itself a relation of measure is independent and yet able to unite with others of the same kind, preserving itself while specifying a new relation with each. Its specific character is not expressed in one combination alone. Compared with many others it yields different ratios and exponents, so that the independent measure displays its qualitative exponent as the series of these amounts, of which it is itself the unit: a series of specific ways of relating to others.
