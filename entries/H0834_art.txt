# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Art is one way of bringing to mind and expressing the divine, the deepest interests of humanity and the most comprehensive truths of spirit, a task it fulfils only when free in its end and means and placed in the same sphere as religion and philosophy. It presents the absolute in sensuous form. Its beauty, born of the spirit, stands higher than that of nature, though in its highest vocation art is for us a thing of the past.

    — Aesthetics, Introduction (tr. Knox)
