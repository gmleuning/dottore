# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sea is the neutral earth, whose composite movement of ebb and flow follows the positions of sun and moon and the earth's shape. It holds the principle of life more immanently than the land and brings forth mainly animal life, luminous and slight, but its fluidity draws incipient subjectivity back and remains at elemental life.
