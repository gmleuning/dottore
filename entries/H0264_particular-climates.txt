# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular forms of the earth's process depend on its position in relation to the sun, in climates and seasons. There is first the difference of heat and cold, of winter and summer, which is not merely quantitative but shows itself at specific nodal points, such as equinoxes and solstices; then the essential, qualitative alteration, the tension within the earth and between earth and atmosphere.
