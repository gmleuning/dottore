# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The image is the content of intuition placed in the inwardness of intelligence, in its own space and time, freed from its immediacy and abstract individuality and taken into the universality of the I. It lacks the full determinacy of the intuition, is wilful or contingent, and cut off from its original place and time. What is represented gains lasting existence only at the cost of the clarity and freshness of what is intuited.

  However, what is represented gains this immortality only at the cost of the clarity and freshness of the immediate individuality, the all round determinacy, of what is intuited; the intuition is obscured and blurred, when it becomes an image.
    — Encyclopedia §452, Addition (tr. Wallace and Miller, rev. Inwood)
