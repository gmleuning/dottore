# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The doctrine of being is the first book of the Logic, in which being is considered as the indeterminate immediate and as the sphere within which its own determinations and the movement of its reflection fall. It is one sphere of the concept, set off against essence. In it being posits itself in three determinations: as determinateness, that is quality; as sublated determinateness, magnitude or quantity; and as qualitatively determined quantity, measure.
