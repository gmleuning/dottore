# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Embodiment is the way the determinations that originate within the soul and mind become sensed: to be as if found, they must be posited as distinct from the subject and as identical with it, and this happens only by their externalizing themselves in the body. Just as outer sensations make themselves into symbols for the inner, inner sensations necessarily embody themselves, each in a particular organ or system, as anger and courage are felt in the breast and the blood. The body can serve for this because it offers the soul no resistance.
