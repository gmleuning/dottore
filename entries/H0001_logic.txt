# 1 Logic
# (top of the system)
G: Logik
Gloss: The structure of reality and, in the same concepts, of thought: the basic determinations all thinking relies on, each leading to the next. Then the study of these concepts in their own right and in that order.
HGloss: The science of the pure idea, of thought as the self-movement of the concept, in which the determinations of thought are at once the determinations of things.
