# 1 Logic
# (top of the system)
G: Logik
Gloss: The structure of reality and, in the same concepts, of thought: the basic determinations all thinking relies on, each leading to the next. Then the study of these concepts in their own right and in that order.

N:
  logic, science of logic; speculative logic, dialectical logic, objective logic, subjective logic; dialectic, dialectics; science of thought, science of pure reason; pure thought; thinking, ratiocination &c (inference) H0165
  metaphysics, first philosophy, ontology, theory of categories; table of categories
  thought thinking itself; reason; realm of shadows; the system of pure reason; categories, predicaments†, forms of thought
  the system of categories

V:
  think, reason, ratiocinate, cogitate, excogitate†; think through, think out, follow out, carry through; reason a priori; categorize; deduce, derive, develop, unfold; logicize, syllogize &c H0165
  reflect
  draw an inference

Adj:
  logical, dialectical, speculative, categorial, ontological, metaphysical; pure, formal, abstract; presuppositionless; rational, noetic, intelligible; systematic, immanent, self-developing
  reflective
  intellectual, mental

Adv:
  logically, dialectically &c adj.; in thought, in pure thought, in the element of thought
