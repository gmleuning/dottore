# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Appearance is the sphere in which essence, which must appear, comes forth. Being is essence, and essence as simple self-equality is likewise being, a being that has come forth out of negativity and inwardness. Its determinations gain an independent subsistence whose reflective shine is completed in appearance. It is first concrete existence and the thing; then appearance as the truth of the thing, with a world in itself opposed to it; and finally essential relation, in which what appears shows the essential.
