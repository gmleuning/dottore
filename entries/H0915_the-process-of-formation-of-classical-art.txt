# 915 The Process of Formation of Classical Art
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › b. Classical Art
G: Der Gestaltungsprozess der klassischen Kunstform
Gloss: The development by which classical art arises.
HGloss: The process by which classical art arises, from the degradation of animal forms to the victory of the new gods.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The shaping of classical art is the process by which spirit withdraws into itself from nature, lifts itself above it and overcomes it, so that it can make nature a positive existence of its freedom. What it transcends is not bare nature but the symbolic form, with its gods seen in animals; invalidating and transforming this false link produces the ideal. Greek formation of foreign material is essentially negative transformation.
