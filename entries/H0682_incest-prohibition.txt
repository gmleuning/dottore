# 682 Incest Prohibition
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › α. Family › a. Marriage
G: Inzestverbot
Gloss: The prohibition of marriage within the family.
HGloss: The prohibition of marriage between blood relatives, since marriage is a free act of independent persons.
