# 1188 Passage into Spirit
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › II. Religion › γ. The Consummate Religion › b. Representation and Appearance (the Kingdom of the Son) › iii. Reconciliation
G: Der Übergang in den Geist
Gloss: Christ's resurrection and ascension, by which his presence passes into the Spirit living in the community.
HGloss: Der Übergang in den Geist: the death of death, the resurrection and ascension, through which the sensuous presence becomes the Spirit of the community.

N:
  passage into spirit
  the Resurrection
  the Ascension, the outpouring of the Spirit, Pentecost, the community of the Spirit

V:
  rise, ascend, overcome death

Adj:
  risen

Phr:
  the death of death

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Christ's death is the transition to a glorification that restores the original glory, and the story of the resurrection and ascension begins where this history is interpreted spiritually. The Spirit discloses its meaning to Christ's friends, and the relationship to a human being is transfigured by the Spirit. With the certainty that divine and human nature are united, the community begins.
