# 25 Alternating Determination of the Finite and the Infinite
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › c. Infinity
G: Wechselbestimmung des Endlichen und Unendlichen
Gloss: The unlimited held apart from the limited, which makes it limited in turn; an endless alternation between the two, like a series that never arrives. Also called the bad or spurious infinite.
HGloss: The spurious (bad) infinite, fixed beyond the finite and so itself finite: the endless alternation of finite and infinite, the infinite progress.
