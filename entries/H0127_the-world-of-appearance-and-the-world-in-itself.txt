# 127 The World of Appearance and the World-in-Itself
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › β. Appearance Proper
G: Die erscheinende und die an sich seiende Welt
Gloss: The world as it appears, contrasted with the world as it is in itself, each the inverse of the other.
HGloss: The supersensible world, which is the inverted world of appearance: what is positive in one is negative in the other.
