# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The chemical process starts from objects in tension with one another, their affinity, each striving to sublate its own one-sidedness and so the other's, to produce a reality adequate to the concept. They meet in a medium of communication, water among bodies, and in spirit the sign and language. Their opposed independence is sublated and tension blunted in a tranquil neutrality, where the process dies out until something external rekindles it.
