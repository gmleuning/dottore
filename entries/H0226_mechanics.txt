# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mechanics, the first sphere of the philosophy of nature, treats self-externality: first in complete abstraction as space and time, then as individualized and related, as matter and motion in finite mechanics, and finally matter in free motion in absolute mechanics. When the relation of matter and motion ceases to be external, there is their absolute unity, matter that moves itself.
