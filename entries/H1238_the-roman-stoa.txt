# 1238 The Roman Stoa
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 1. Dogmatism › a. Stoic Philosophy
G: Die römische Stoa
Gloss: The Stoicism of imperial Rome, Seneca, Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius, a philosophy of inner freedom and endurance.
HGloss: The Roman Stoics, Seneca, Epictetus and Marcus Aurelius, in whom the abstract freedom of self-consciousness becomes moral exhortation in a world of despotism.

N:
  the Roman Stoa

  the Moral Letters, imperial Stoicism, the emperor-philosopher

V:
  endure, accept, bear and forbear

Phr:
  the impediment to action advances action [Marcus Aurelius]
  some things are up to us

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The later Stoics of the Roman age are Seneca; Epictetus, a Phrygian who began as a slave, fled to Nicopolis when Domitian banished the philosophers from Rome, and whose teaching his pupil Arrian compiled in the Enchiridion; and last of all the emperor Marcus Aurelius Antoninus.
