# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Childhood is the time of natural harmony, of the individual's peace with itself and with the world: the beginning without opposition, as old age is the end without it. Whatever oppositions arise remain without deeper interest. The child lives in innocence, without lasting pain, in love for its parents and in the feeling of being loved by them. This unity with its genus and with the world is immediate and therefore merely natural, and so it must be sublated: the individual must advance to oppose itself to the universal and grasp its own independence.
