# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Service and counsel are the noble consciousness's relation to state power before that power has a will of its own. The proud vassal sacrifices existence in service, enjoys honour, and speaks of the common good in the form of counsel. Since surviving the danger of death leaves a particular self-interest behind, the counsellor reserves a private opinion and will against state authority, so that the counsel is ambiguous and suspect and the one giving it is always close to rebellion.
