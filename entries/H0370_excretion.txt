# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Excretion is the animal's abstract repulsion of itself, by which it makes itself external, the conclusion of assimilation. What it thus puts out is something non-organic in which it is not identical with itself; excrement means only that the organism, recognizing its error, rids itself of its entanglement with outer things.
