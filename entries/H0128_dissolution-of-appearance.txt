# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dissolution of appearance is the collapse of the difference between the two worlds. Each is the inversion of the other, the north pole of one being the south pole of the other, and what is positive here negative there. Precisely in this opposition their difference disappears: the world that was to be in itself is the world of appearance, and the latter is essential within. Each is in itself the totality of both, and this unity is essential relation.
