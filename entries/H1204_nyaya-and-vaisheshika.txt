# 1204 Nyaya and Vaisheshika

G: Nyāya und Vaiśeṣika (Gotama und Kaṇāda)
Gloss: The Indian schools of logic and atomism, which analyse knowledge into its sources and the world into its categories and indivisible parts.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Nyaya of Gotama and the Vaisheshika of Kanada belong together. The first is a philosophy of reasoning, an especially elaborate dialectic concerned with proof and with what is of interest to know; the second is a philosophy of the particular, which occupies itself with particular objects and principles, enumerating substance, quality, and the other categories.
