# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sensuousness is the object as it first offers itself to immediate certainty, in its seemingly infinite wealth in space and time, taken just as it is and with nothing left out. For this certainty the thing is, and only because it is; its simple immediacy constitutes its truth, and consciousness is for its part only a pure this that knows the singular. On a closer look much more is in play: what was taken to be immediate is only an example of pure being, split into a this as I and a this as object, each mediated through the other.
