# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The concept of quantitative infinity is that quantum's alteration into another quantum goes on without end because quantum is inherently self-contradictory. It becomes another yet continues in its otherness, so that the other is again a quantum, but the other of quantum as such, its own unlimitedness. Quantum is an ought: it implies being determined for itself, and this is rather being determined in an other. The sending beyond itself that makes it finite is equally the determination of the infinite.
