# 62 Concept of Quantitative Infinity
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum › c. Quantitative Infinity
G: Begriff der quantitativen Unendlichkeit
Gloss: The idea that a quantity always points beyond itself to a larger or smaller one.
HGloss: The quantum's determination to be beyond itself, the infinite as its own other.

N:
  concept of quantitative infinity; self-surpassing of quantum, self-transcendence of quantity, inherent beyond, built-in incompleteness; open-endedness, indefinite extensibility, potential infinite; the next number, successor, the always-more; insatiability, unquenchability
  the beyond of quantum, the ought of quantity
  infinitesimal, the transfinite

V:
  point beyond, outrun itself, exceed itself, go beyond itself, overreach; extend indefinitely
  surpass itself, exceed

Adj:
  open-ended, indefinitely extensible, unfinished, incomplete, insatiable, unquenchable, self-surpassing, potential
  exceeding, unbounded

Adv:
  always more, ever more, beyond any given amount
  beyond itself, further

F:
  Latin: infinitum potentia
  Greek: apeiron

Phr:
  there is always a bigger fish
  the sky's the limit
  beyond measure
  out of all proportion
