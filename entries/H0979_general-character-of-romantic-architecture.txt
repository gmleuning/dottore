# 979 General Character of Romantic Architecture

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of romantic building is that enclosure is fundamental while mere utility is transcended: houses of God, entirely suited to worship, rise freely to a pinnacle so that purpose vanishes in the look of independent existence. In grandeur and sublime peace they are lifted into an infinity of their own, while the fullest variety of detail is led back into simple unity.
