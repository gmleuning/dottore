# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In cohesion the immanent form posits a mode of spatial juxtaposition of material parts distinct from that determined by gravity. Bodies now react to pressure and thrust not merely as masses but in their own way of maintaining themselves in unity. Cohesion still conflicts with gravity and belongs to the becoming of individuality as totality, not yet to completed shape.
