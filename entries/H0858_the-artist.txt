# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The artist is the subjective productive activity from which the work of art springs, the work being there for the contemplation and feeling of others. This activity, the artist's imagination, permits only a few generalities, since there is no recipe for creation. It is treated as genius and inspiration, as the objectivity of creative activity, and as true originality.
