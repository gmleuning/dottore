# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Satire is the form in the transition out of classical art, where the opposition between spirit and the corrupt world persists, giving a prosaic relation in place of poetic reconciliation. Unlike symbolic forms, whose shape and meaning were strangers yet friendly, here sides once perfectly interfused separate because they can no longer agree, turning from peaceful reconciliation to enmity. It flourished on Roman soil.
