# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Maturity is the recognition of the objective necessity and rationality of the world as one finds it. The adult views the ethical order as essentially complete, not as something that first needs to be produced, and works for it rather than against it. Giving up the plan of transforming everything is no mere resignation: the world is the actualization of divine reason, yet it is not dead but perpetually produces itself anew. The adult's work lies in this preserving production, which both brings forth what is already there and advances it, and what was true in the ideals survives in practical activity.
