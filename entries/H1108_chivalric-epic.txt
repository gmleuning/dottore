G: Das ritterliche Epos
Gloss: The epic and romance of knights, their honour, love and adventure.
HGloss: The chivalric epic, whose matter is the knight's honour, love and loyalty, and its adventures, down to Ariosto's comic play with them.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Chivalric epic concerns love, honour and, with religious aims, mystic knighthood: individual actions touching no national interest, by heroes free yet lacking the realism of the Greek ones, tending to ballad or romance-like adventure around great sagas, of Charlemagne, Arthur and Amadis. Later Ariosto brought chivalry into comic light, letting its fantasy merrily destroy itself.
