# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The disjunctive judgement says that A is either B or C. Its subject is the genus in simple form and its predicate the same universality as the totality of its differentiated species, so that both sides have one content and extent. The species together constitute the genus, "as well as" expressing the identity of the particular with the universal, and they exclude one another by "either-or". In it the necessity of the concept is posited.
