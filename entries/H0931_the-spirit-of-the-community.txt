# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The spirit of the community broadens the reconciliation made visible in Christ to the plurality of human beings, since God's true reality is not immediate existence but spirit, existing in knowing and inner life. The individual rises to freedom only by converting natural, finite personality, in three ways: martyrdom repeating the passion bodily, repentance and conversion inwardly, and miracles superseding the course of nature.
