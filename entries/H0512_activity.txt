# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Activity is the process in which self-consciousness negates the two one-sided moments, its own subjectivity and the independence of the object, and thereby brings their identity to be for it. The object, selfless in itself, can offer no resistance: the dialectic of self-sublation which is its nature exists here as this activity of the I. Self-consciousness is not just being but absolute activity, and takes possession of an object whose independence is only pretence.
