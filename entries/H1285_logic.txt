# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Logic, the first part of the Encyclopaedia, is the science of the pure Idea, the Idea in the abstract element of thinking. Its thinking is not merely formal: it is the self-developing totality of determinations and laws that thought gives to itself. Logic is the hardest science, since it deals with pure abstractions and demands practice in moving within pure thought, and could also be called the easiest, since its content is our own thinking and its most familiar determinations.
