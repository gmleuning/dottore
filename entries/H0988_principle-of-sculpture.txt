# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The principle of sculpture proper is the miracle of spirit giving itself an image of itself in something purely material, so forming the external thing that it is present in it and recognizes there the fit shape of its inner life. It asks what kind of spiritual life can appear in visible spatial form, how forms must be shaped to show it beautifully, and why this unity is the classical ideal.
