# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ionic architecture, still simple, rises from Doric's solidity to the slender, graceful and elegant: columns seven to ten diameters high, set on moulded bases, tapering to a slender top with deep flutes, and capitals enriched with shell-like curls. It requires a lighter architrave and no longer points to an origin in building with wood.
