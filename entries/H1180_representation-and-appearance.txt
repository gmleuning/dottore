# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The second element considers the eternal idea in the form of consciousness or representation, as it passes out of universality and infinitude into the determinacy of finitude. Here the other, called the Son, receives the determination of otherness, existing as free and actual, outside and apart from God. The idea has to be present to the subject, who is not only a thinker but a concrete, actual self-consciousness.
