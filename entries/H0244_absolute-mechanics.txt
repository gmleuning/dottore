# 244 Absolute Mechanics (Astronomy)
# Nature › 1. Mechanics
G: Absolute Mechanik
Gloss: The mechanics of heavenly bodies, which move freely by their own law.
HGloss: Matter that has its centre and motion within itself: the solar system, freely moving masses whose laws are the concept of matter itself.
