# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The reciprocal action of things follows because things that have properties are many, distinct through themselves, and stand in essential relation by means of those properties. The property is the very relation between them, apart from which the thing is nothing. A thing differs from others only through its property; without it nothing remains but an external gathering of abstract being. So the things vanish as self-subsisting extremes into the continuity that is the property, and thinghood passes over into property.
