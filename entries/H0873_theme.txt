# 873 Theme
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › c. Enthusiasm
G: Der bestimmte Inhalt
Gloss: The definite content that seizes the artist and asks to be given shape.
HGloss: The determinate content, taken up by Phantasie, which is the true source of Begeisterung.

N:
  theme; subject, subject matter, content, matter, topic, motif, argument, burden, material

Adj:
  thematic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The theme is the specific object and subject matter on which artistic interest has already concentrated before inspiration arises, and to which an artistic urge of the right kind holds firm. Where such interest is lacking, resorting to sensuous stimulus or mere will only proves that heart and imagination have fastened on nothing true. True inspiration kindles on such material and is filled by it.
