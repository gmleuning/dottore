# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The artificer is spirit appearing as an artisan who brings itself forth as an object by instinctive work, as bees build cells, without yet grasping the thought of itself. The first works are of the abstract understanding, the crystals of pyramids and obelisks, straight lines and plane surfaces that do not have their meaning in themselves and receive spirit only from outside. The work advances by mingling natural and thinking forms until the artisan becomes a spiritual worker.
