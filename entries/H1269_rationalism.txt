# 1269 Rationalism
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › d. Modern Philosophy
G: Rationalismus
Gloss: Philosophy that takes reason, rather than the senses, as the source of certain knowledge.
HGloss: The philosophies that take reason as the source of knowledge.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the first period of metaphysics free thought proceeding from itself is the principle, and what is deemed valid is valid only through thinking: in order to know truth we must attain it by meditative thinking alone. But the concrete is not yet developed necessarily from thought; thought works on a content given by representation, observation, and experience.
