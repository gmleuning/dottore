# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Symbolizing phantasy is the activity of intelligence that, still guided by the given content of images, chooses to express its universals only through sensory material whose own meaning corresponds to them, as the eagle displays the strength of Jupiter. Allegory expresses the subjective element through an ensemble of details; poetic phantasy uses its material more freely than the plastic arts, yet must select what is adequate to the idea.
