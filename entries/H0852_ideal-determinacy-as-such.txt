# 852 Ideal Determinacy as Such
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › ii. The Determinacy of the Ideal
G: Die ideale Bestimmtheit als solche
Gloss: The ideal made definite without losing its calm, as in a serene divine figure at rest.
HGloss: The ideal in its determinacy, as the divine shown at rest, eternal power in individual shape.

N:
  ideal determinacy as such; the divine as unity, the gods made definite, individualized divinity
  ideal determinacy, the determinate ideal at rest, divine repose, the blessed calm, serenity, self-repose, the god at rest, untroubled unity

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Ideal determinacy as such is the divine, which art above all must make the centre of its representations, made determinate. Pure unity can only be thought, but as determinate the divine splits into many self-reposing gods, appears as an actual human being, and lives in the senses and hearts of saints and martyrs. Thereby the whole human heart, every deep interest of the soul, becomes the living stuff of art.
