# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Limit is the non-being of the other, through which something is what it is. It is not only the non-being of the other but equally that of the something, so that something through its limit both is and is not. Something has its existence outside its limit, yet only in its limit does it have existence; the limit separates the two and is their common distinguishedness. In the limit something is therefore restless, a contradiction that drives it beyond itself, as the point passes into the line.
