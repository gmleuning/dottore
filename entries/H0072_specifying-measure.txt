# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Specifying measure is the measure of one thing acting on the indifferent, external quantum of another. It is first a rule, an arbitrary unit applied from outside, but as specific measure its nature is to relate to an other. A something with an inner measure does not simply take on an alteration of magnitude that comes from outside: it reacts against it, assimilates it in its own way and makes something else of it, showing itself to be for itself in this externality. Measure then exists as a ratio whose exponent is its specific character.
