# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The different kinds of sculpture arise because the ideal, being individuality, broadens into a group of gods and its outward execution divides into sorts. These are considered by mode of portrayal, as single statues, groups and reliefs, the last passing toward painting; by the external material in which they are realized; and by the stages of sculpture's historical development.
