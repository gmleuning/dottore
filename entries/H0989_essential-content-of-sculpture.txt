# 989 Essential Content of Sculpture

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The essential content of sculpture is the objectivity of spirit in self-repose, the stage at which spirit has not distinguished itself from its universal substance or from its bodily existence and so has not withdrawn into subjective self-awareness. It excludes the self that holds to its own particular, accidental character, as in self-satisfaction, in favour of the universal and eternal content of spirit.
