# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The materials of sculpture must be examined because individuality particularizes not only subjects and modes but matter, different kinds of subject and treatment having a secret sympathy with kinds of material. The Greeks amaze by technical skill as much as invention, since complete mastery of the material belongs to the ideal, which wholly enters the sensuous and fuses inner with outer.
