# 1129 Church Song
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › c. Song › i. Song Proper
G: Kirchenlied
Gloss: The hymns of the congregation.
HGloss: The church song, the congregation's common voice before God.

N:
  church song; chorale, church hymn, Lutheran chorale, hymn tune, Kirchenlied
  carol, motet

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Church song shows that a higher, universal content is not excluded from song: most Protestant hymns for the edification of the faithful express longing for God, prayer for mercy, repentance, hope, confidence and doubt. They arise as the concern of an individual heart but are expressed universally, so that everyone may or should share them.
