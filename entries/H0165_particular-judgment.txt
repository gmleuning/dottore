# 165 Particular Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment › b. Judgment of Reflection

Gloss: A judgment of this kind about some things: "some plants are healing."
HGloss: Some singulars are universal; positive and negative at once, pointing toward totality.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular judgement takes as subject not this one but some: the singular extended by external reflection. Its form is that some singulars are a universal of reflection. It looks positive but is just as much negative, since some is both comprehensive and short of universality, so that "some humans are happy" at once implies that some are not. Positive and negative here no longer fall apart, but the judgement remains indeterminate.
