# 1102 Epic as a Fully Unified Whole
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › a. Epic Poetry › ii. Particular Characteristics of Epic
G: Das Epos als einheitsvolle Totalität
Gloss: The epic as a complete whole.
HGloss: The epic as a whole, unified yet broad, episodic, governed by circumstance.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The epic as a fully unified whole must gather everything connected with the action and its ground, differ from lyric and drama in development, and round a widely dispersed content into concrete unity. Its content is the entirety of a world in which an individual action happens, with both inner life and outer reality, the whole poetry of human existence.
