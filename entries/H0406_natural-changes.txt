# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The natural alterations are the differences that, in the soul determined as an individual, take the form of changes in one subject persisting through them, moments of its development. They are at once physical and mental: the course of the ages of life, the sex relation in which the individual stands in real opposition to itself, and the alternation of waking and sleep. Through them the immediate individuality of the soul is brought into conformity with its universality, a process of development which is education.
