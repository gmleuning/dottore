# 71 The Specific Quantum
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › α. Specific Quantity
G: Das spezifische Quantum
Gloss: A definite amount bound up with a particular quality; the size at which a thing is what it is.
HGloss: A quantum that is the existence of a quality, so that the thing is what it is only within this amount.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The specific quantum is measure in its immediacy, quantum which as self-referring externality is itself quality: an immediate quantum with an immediate quality belonging to it. Whatever is has a measure, a magnitude that belongs to its nature, so that if it exceeds or falls short of it the thing perishes. The quantum can yet vary within limits without the quality changing, and so a thing can be taken unawares: a change that seems merely quantitative suddenly turns qualitative, as with the bald head and the heap.
