# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Religion is the consciousness of absolute truth in the way that it occurs for all human beings, and therefore primarily in the form of representation. Philosophy has the same content and only transforms representation into concepts. Within religion God is felt, pictured and thought, and in worship the subject joins itself with God. God can be known, for it is God's nature to be revealed.
