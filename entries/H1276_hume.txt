G: Hume
Gloss: The thinker who showed that experience cannot justify necessary connection, reducing cause and effect to habit and so undermining the claims of both reason and experience to certainty.
HGloss: Hume, whose skepticism showed that experience cannot ground universality and necessity.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Examining what is subsumed under experience, Hume found there the determination of the universal and of necessity, and drew attention consistently to the fact that if we keep to the empirical standpoint, experience is indeed the foundation of what we know but contains neither universality nor necessity. Perception gives one thing following another, not the connection of cause and effect.
