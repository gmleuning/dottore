# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Characterization distinguishes painting from sculpture's ideal plasticity: the mental and physical particularity of personality has free scope and need not be raised to ideal beauty. Painting develops the whole variety of accidental particulars, so that we see not plastic ideals of gods and men but particular persons, since what is central is the soul's deep feeling and living subjective character.
