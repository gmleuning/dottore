# 426 Folly (Fixation of the Subjective as Objective)
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › ii. Derangement

Gloss: The taking of what is merely subjective for what is objectively real; delusion.
