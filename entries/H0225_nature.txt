# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Nature is the idea in the form of otherness. Since the idea is thus the negative of itself, externality is not merely nature's relation to the idea but the determination in which nature exists as nature, showing necessity and contingency rather than freedom. It is to be regarded as a system of stages, one arising necessarily from another, generated not naturally but in the inner idea that grounds it.
