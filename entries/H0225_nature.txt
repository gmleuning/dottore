# 225 Nature
# (top of the system)
G: Natur
Gloss: The world of space, time and matter, understood as thought in the form of outwardness, where everything lies outside everything else.
HGloss: The idea in the form of otherness: externality is the determination in which nature is nature, a system of stages in which the concept is hidden and seeks itself.
