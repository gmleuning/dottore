# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conscious symbolism, the comparative form of art, is that in which the meaning is not only explicitly known but expressly posited as different from the external way of representing it. Their relation no longer rests in the meaning itself but is a more or less accidental connection produced by the poet's subjective activity, wit and invention, which may start either from something perceived or from an inner idea.
