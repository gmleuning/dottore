# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The protection of the market is the public authority's right to control the exchange of goods of everyday use. Because goods in universal daily demand are offered for sale not to an individual but to the public as universal purchaser, the authority may inspect them and fix the prices of the commonest necessaries, defending the public against fraud.
