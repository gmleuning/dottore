# 1121 Prophetic Lyric
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › a. Religious Lyric
G: Prophetische Lyrik
Gloss: The lyric of the prophets, which speaks to the people in God's name.
HGloss: The prophetic lyric of the Hebrew prophets, which laments and warns the people from its relation to God.

N:
  prophetic lyric; the prophets, prophecy, oracle, the Hebrew prophets, vatic poetry, vates, jeremiad

Adj:
  prophetic, vatic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Prophetic lyric belongs to the Hebrew prophets, who, opposed to the multitude, usually strike a fundamental tone of grief and lament over their people's condition, sensing its estrangement and falling away from God, and are fired by sublime zeal and political wrath, which carries them toward a lyric of exhortation.
