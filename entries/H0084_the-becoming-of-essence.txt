# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The becoming of essence is the movement in which being, through measure, passes into essence. Being first becomes absolute indifference, mediating itself into a simple unity through the negation of every determinateness of quality, quantity and measure, with these as mere states of a substrate. This indifference proves a contradiction: it repels itself from itself in an infinite negative self-reference, so that its determinations are no longer self-subsisting but only posited, referred to their unity. Being which, through the sublation of being, is simple being with itself is essence.
