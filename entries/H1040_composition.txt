# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Composition is the arrangement of figures and natural objects, by juxtaposition and grouping, into a whole complete in itself that presents a definite situation. Its chief requirement is the happy choice of a situation suited to painting, from a bunch of flowers to great public events, and the painter must use background, light and incidental figures to bring out the motives within it.
