# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Classical art is the centre of art: a self-enclosed, free totality uniting content with its wholly adequate shape, the reality coinciding with the concept of beauty that symbolic art strove for in vain. Its inner being is free, independent meaning that signifies itself, that is, spirit, which makes itself its own object and takes an external form identical with its inwardness, in the human shape.
