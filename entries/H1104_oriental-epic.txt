# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Oriental epic stays close to the substantial whole in which individual consciousness is absorbed, so that characters never reach the independence drama demands. Epics proper occur only in India and Persia, in colossal proportions; China, prosaic in outlook, has none; and the Indian Ramayana and Mahabharata show splendour and fantastic confusion, loosely unified and measurelessly enlarged.
