# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feelings of the second kind are those whose content comes from intuition or representation, so that they are more determinate than the bare pleasant and unpleasant: delight, joy, hope, fear, anguish, pain. Joy is the feeling that my inner determinacy accords with a single event, thing or person, contentment a lasting and peaceful harmony, fear the feeling of my self together with an evil that threatens it. None has a content belonging to its own nature; it enters from outside. Nor need the content become feeling at all.
