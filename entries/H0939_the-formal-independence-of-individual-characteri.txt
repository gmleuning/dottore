# 939 The Formal Independence of Individual Characteristics
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › c. Romantic Art
G: Die formelle Selbständigkeit der individuellen Besonderheiten
Gloss: The freedom of individual character in romantic art.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The formal independence of individual character is the third sphere of romantic art, in which religious material and chivalry with its lofty aims have vanished, and the particular, existent world stands on its own feet. What gives satisfaction is thirst for present reality, delight in the finite, so that people wish to see the present as it is, recreated by art, even at the cost of beauty and ideality.
