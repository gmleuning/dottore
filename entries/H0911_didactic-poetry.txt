# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Didactic poetry arises when a meaning is grasped on its own account, already fully formed in prose, and merely embellished externally with artistic ornament for instruction. It is not among the proper forms of art, since art can concern only externals such as metre, diction and episodes that stand beside the content and do not penetrate it. Early Greek philosophy, Hesiod, Lucretius and Virgil offer examples.
