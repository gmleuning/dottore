# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feeling is the subjective form of the certainty of God: in it a content is my own, as this particular individual, and I am aware of myself along with it. What is in the heart belongs to one's inmost being. But feeling as such is neither good nor bad, true nor false; any content can be in it, so that it cannot by itself justify what it contains.

    — Lectures on the Philosophy of Religion, The Knowledge of God › Feeling (tr. Brown, Hodgson and Stewart)
