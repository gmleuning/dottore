# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Self-certainty is the side of substantial knowledge that belongs to the one who knows. The form of reason distinguishes the self-knowing subject from its object, but because this takes place within the one substance, the knower is certain that the object is not truly alien and that its determination is what spirit is in itself, namely rational. Spirit, being implicitly reason, is reason relating itself to reason, and the certainty of reason consists in this relating to what is rational.
