# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The region of phantasy and sensibility, which advances beyond Chinese uniformity by letting differences appear, only to fix them as natural castes. Its idealism is one of imagination without distinct conception, as of Spirit in a dream. Because the distinctions are attributed to Nature, their religious unity yields a theocratic aristocracy, in which the Brahmins count as gods bodily present.
