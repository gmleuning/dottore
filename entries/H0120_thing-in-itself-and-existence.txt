# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The thing in itself is the concrete existent as the essential immediate that results from sublated mediation, the unmoved, indeterminate unity that is the substrate of external existence. It is supposed to have no determinate manifold in it, and gets one only from an external reflection, as colour from the eye and smell from the nose. But that external existence is the other side of the same concrete existence, and the thing in itself, abstracted from all relation, shows itself to be empty.
