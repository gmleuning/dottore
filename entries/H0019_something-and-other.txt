# 19 Something and Other
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude
G: Etwas und Anderes
Gloss: The relation of a thing to what it is not. Each side is "something" and each is the "other" of the other, so which is which depends on point of view.
HGloss: Something and its other, each equally a something and an other, so that otherness falls within something itself.
