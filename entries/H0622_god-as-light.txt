# ---- Hegel ----
Def: God as light, the luminous essence, is spirit intuiting itself in the form of being filled with spirit, the shape of shapelessness corresponding to sense certainty. It is the all-containing light of sunrise, which suffuses everything and preserves itself in a formless substance, with darkness as its simple other. Its creations are effusions of light and streams of consuming fire, and its sublime essence wanders without constancy, dissolving its own beauty.
