# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The state as a living universal spirit that is also the self-consciousness of its individual citizens, for which democracy is the fitting constitution. Justice and the common good are willed as custom, before subjective conviction and intention have appeared, so that the affairs of the community can be entrusted to the will and resolve of the citizens.
