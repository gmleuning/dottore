# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The estrangement is a cleavage that runs to the innermost being, in which human beings know themselves to be a contradiction and suffer infinite anguish. Toward God it appears as humiliation and remorse at not being what one should be; toward the world as unhappiness, which drives people back into themselves, as with the Stoic and the Skeptic. Both forms are one-sided and call for a transition to reconciliation.
