# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The main thesis of the Pythagorean philosophy is that number is the being of things, the organization of the universe being a system of numbers and numerical relationships. It is a bold step beyond sensible principles. The mathematical domain serves Pythagoras in education because it stands midway between the sensible and supersensible worlds, a preliminary exercise leading to what is in and for itself.
