# 369 Mediated Digestion
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › β. The Animal's Process of Assimilation › b. Practical Process › iii. Digestive Process

Gloss: Digestion carried out through the organs of the digestive system.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In mediated digestion the organism, angrily opposing the outer world, is divided within itself, working on food with animal juices and bile; the end product, chyle, is the same as the lymph produced by immediate transformation. Hegel therefore counts this mediated process in higher animals a superfluity, like seed production in plants.
