# 231 Inert Matter
# Nature › 1. Mechanics › II. Finite Mechanics (Matter and Motion)
G: Träge Materie
Gloss: Matter considered as indifferent to rest or motion; mass, inertia.
HGloss: Matter as indifferent to rest and motion, self-subsistent only by its mass and moved only by another.
