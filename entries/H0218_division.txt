# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Division is the step after definition, moving toward the particular. It would need an immanent principle beginning from the universal, which synthetic cognition lacks, so that it takes its determinations from what is given, ordering empirical particulars and finding grounds of division by comparison. Its laws are therefore empty formal rules: it is indifferent to the exhaustion of a concept whether a dozen further species of parrot are discovered.
