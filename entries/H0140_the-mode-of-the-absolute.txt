# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The mode of the absolute is the externality of the absolute, its going out of itself into the changeability and contingency of being. Yet it is not merely this: it is externality posited as externality, a mere way and manner, shine as shine, and so the reflection of form into itself. Only in the mode, therefore, is the absolute posited as absolute identity, as self-referring negativity, and the exposition, which passed from identity to attribute and mode, proves to be the absolute's own movement.
