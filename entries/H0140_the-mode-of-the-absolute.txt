# 140 The Mode of the Absolute
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › α. The Absolute
G: Der Modus des Absoluten
Gloss: A particular, outward manner in which the all-inclusive whole shows itself; a passing form or mode.
