# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Elasticity is cohesion displaying itself in motion, the totality of cohesion. A body gives way to another's violence while keeping its own character: its parts, mutually external, do violence and give way to one another, and being no less self-subsistent they sublate the negation suffered and reinstate themselves. Yielding and self-preservation outwardly are bound up with this inner activity.
