# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Drive and activity make up the process of desire. Self-consciousness, immediate and single, is the contradiction between its certainty of itself and an external object, and a being that bears a contradiction and feels it necessarily has the urge to sublate it. It is aware of itself implicitly in the object, which answers to the urge, and by its activity negates both one-sided moments, so that the subject is made objective and the object subjective.
