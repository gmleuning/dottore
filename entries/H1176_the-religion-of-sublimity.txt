# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the Jewish religion, the religion of sublimity, spirit is withdrawn from natural and sensible shapes and known in pure thought as one subjective unity, which for us first merits the name of God. This One is absolute power, and the natural is merely posited by it, without independence. The power is also wisdom, whose moments are goodness and justice. The world is God's creation, and God is its initiator, not its result.
