# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Tragedy is the higher language that draws together the scattered moments of the divine world and the world of action. Its speech is no longer narrative: the hero speaks in person, showing the audience self-conscious people who know their rights and purposes and can state them. They are artists who express the inner essence and the pathos to which they belong, and are actual people who put on the masks of the heroes, so that the actor is essential to the mask.
