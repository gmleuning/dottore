# 598 Sign-Making Phantasy
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › III. Mind (Psychology) › α. Theoretical Mind › b. Representation › ii. Imagination › 3. Phantasy
G: Zeichenmachende Phantasie
Gloss: Imagination that makes signs, in which a sensory thing stands for a meaning that has nothing to do with it.
HGloss: Phantasy that makes signs, in which the intuition stands for something other than itself, as the pyramid holds a soul.
