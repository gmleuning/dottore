# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Repentance and conversion transfer the negation of the sinful self into the inner life, expressed only in spiritual grief and change of heart, and so give ground more fit for ideal art than bodily torture. Faith can make a crime alien to its doer and wash it away. Painting does best to concentrate the conversion in a single picture, as with Mary Magdalene.
