# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fichte's philosophy is a more consistent development of Kant's. It begins with the I, the transcendental unity of self-consciousness, which is what is certain. Where Kant took the categories empirically from logic, Fichte's great merit is to have called for and attempted their derivation from the I. The I is active and produces its determinations, yet remains burdened with the antithesis of the not-I.
