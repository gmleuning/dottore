# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The absolute is the simple, solid identity into which every determinateness of being, essence and reflection has dissolved, so that determining it seems to be pure negation and the absolute a void of all predicates, yet equally their position. The task of saying what it is cannot be met by external reflection that picks up determinations here and there; it can only be the absolute's own exposition, a displaying of what it is, through its attribute and its mode.
