G: Der Bruder
Gloss: The brother, who leaves the family for the life of the community and becomes its citizen.
HGloss: Der Bruder: the member of the family in whom its spirit becomes individuality turned outward, passing from the divine law to the human law of the community.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The brother is the side by which the spirit of the family becomes an individuality that turns toward an other and passes into the consciousness of universality. He leaves the immediate, elemental and therefore properly negative ethical life of the family to win and bring forth the actual ethical life that is conscious of itself. He passes from the divine law, in whose sphere he had lived, into the human law, finding his self-conscious essence in the polity.
