G: Judäa
Gloss: The land of the Jews, whose religion of the one God set spirit above nature.
HGloss: Judäa, where nature is reduced to the created and the One is grasped as pure spirit, though bound to one people.

N:

Phr:
  Hear, O Israel: the Lord our God is one Lord, Shema Yisrael [Heb.] [Bible]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The people among whom the spiritual is purified of nature and becomes the pure product of thought: God is grasped as the one creator, and nature is reduced to a creature, external and undivine. This marks the separation of East and West. Yet the unity remains exclusive, since only the one people recognizes this God and is acknowledged by him.
