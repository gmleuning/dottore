# 1248 Forerunners: Philo, the Kabbalah, the Gnostics

G: Philo, die Kabbala und die Gnostiker
Gloss: The Alexandrian thinkers who joined Jewish and Christian faith to Greek philosophy before Plotinus: Philo, the Kabbalah and the Gnostics.
HGloss: Philo, the Kabbalah and the Gnostics, in whom the oriental and the Greek meet: God as the abyss and the Logos as his first-born, the beginning of the Alexandrian concrete idea.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Philo of Alexandria confines the idea of God to pure being, of which one can know only that it is. But God is concrete and living, and differentiates and determines himself in the Logos, the first-born Son, who rules the world and contains all the ideas. The Cabalists and the Gnostics are treated after Philo.
