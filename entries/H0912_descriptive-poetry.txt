# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Descriptive poetry is the opposite of didactic: it starts from external things, natural surroundings, buildings, seasons and times of day, and sketches them in their ordinary appearance without penetrating them with spiritual meaning. Such sensuous content belongs only to the external side of art, which has a right to appear only as the reality of spirit, individuality and action, not as externality cut off from spirit.
