# 193 The Formal Mechanical Process
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › α. Mechanism › b. The Mechanical Process
G: Der formale mechanische Prozess
Gloss: Mechanical interaction as the mere passing on of motion or condition.
HGloss: The communication of determinations between objects, as motion passed on, a mere sharing.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The formal mechanical process is the positing of the objects' identical connection by giving the determinateness it generates the form of universality: this is communication, which passes on without turning into the opposite. In spirit a determinateness spreads unaltered from one person to another, like a scent in unresisting air; laws, morals and rational conceptions pervade individuals unawares, as motion or heat spreads among bodies.
