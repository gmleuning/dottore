# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The concept as such contains the three moments of universality, particularity and singularity, each of which is as much the whole concept as it is a determinate moment. It is first pure universality, which, being a totality, determines and distinguishes itself; second, this particular or determinate concept; third, singularity, the concept reflecting itself out of difference into absolute negativity. The understanding is here not the faculty of fixed concepts but of the concept as such.
