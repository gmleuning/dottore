# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The hypothetical judgement says that if A is, then B is, so that the being of A is just as much the being of another. It posits the necessary connection of immediate things, which the categorical judgement had not yet posited. It asserts neither that A is nor that B is: only their connectedness is posited as existing. In it the being of finite things is expressed as it truly is, as being their own and also not their own.
