# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Otherness is the one determination under which sensory consciousness has its object besides bare being: that of an independent other confronting me, something reflected into itself, an individual facing me as an individual. Whether the object is inner or outer makes no difference. It is not yet determined as external within itself, spread out in space and time, which belongs to intuition, but only according to its relation to consciousness, as something external to it.
