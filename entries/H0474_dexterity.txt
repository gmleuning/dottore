# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Dexterity is the third form of habit, in which the soul's abstract being is not only held fast for itself but imposed as a subjective purpose upon the body, which is to be subdued and made wholly pervious to it. A particular organ is trained for a determinate purpose until the body becomes an instrument, so that as soon as a representation, such as a sequence of notes, is in me, the body expresses it correctly, unresisting and fluent. Upright posture, seeing and even thinking require such habit.
