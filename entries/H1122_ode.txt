G: Ode
Gloss: The ode, in which the poet's self rises to a great subject.
HGloss: The ode, in which the poet's inwardness takes up a lofty object and both struggle for mastery.

N:
  ode; the lofty ode
  ode, epode†

Adj:
  odic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Odes are lyric in which the poet's own subjective personality is most important. The poet may choose a weighty topic, gods, heroes, love or friendship, and seem wholly mastered by it, yet masters it in turn, interrupting its course with personal reflections, or may confer dignity on trifles; the captivating topic and free subject stand in tension.
