# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Unity is what spirit is absolutely: the certainty of the unity of what is its own with itself. Consciousness already lies within this unity, but spirit rises above its standpoint, holding it as an ideal moment. In consciousness there is a unity of the nature and life of things with the essence of consciousness; this is reason, and spirit is the certainty of this rationality. What holds fast to the barrier of the sensory and the understanding lacks spirit.
