# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of drama to its public is unlike that of epic or lyric, whose readers may simply lay a work aside: a drama faces an assembled audience for which it is written and to which the author is beholden, and which may praise or blame. The audience is mixed in taste, but contempt for it, fashionable since Tieck, misses drama's proper effect.
