# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The form of exterior and interior in romantic building gives the inside new importance, since the whole is meant as enclosure, and the interior shows through the outer shape and determines it. Within, load and support lose their strict difference: pillars branch like trees into pointed arches, slender beyond the eye's measure, drawing it upward until it finds rest as the heart finds rest in God.
