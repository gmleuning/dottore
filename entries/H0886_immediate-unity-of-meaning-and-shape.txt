# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The immediate unity of meaning and shape is the intuition in which what appears is itself taken to be the immediate presence of the absolute, with no distinction of inner and outer, soul and body, meaning and form. Its most thoroughgoing instance is the religion of Zoroaster, preserved in the Zend-Avesta, which takes the light of nature, sun, stars and fire, to be the absolute itself, without separating the divinity from the light.
