# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Metaphor, image and simile make up the figurative sphere, uniting the clarity of allegory with the pleasure of the riddle. A meaning clearly before the mind is illustrated through a cognate external expression, without any riddle to be solved first, so that it shines through perfectly. In these forms the poet joins meaning and image, with the two sides separated to different degrees.
