# 34 Being-for-Self
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality)
G: Fürsichsein
Gloss: Self-containedness; the condition of a thing that relates only to itself, holding whatever is other within itself as an aspect of its own; independence.
HGloss: Being that has sublated all relation to other into relation to itself; infinite being turned back into itself; the truth of quality.
