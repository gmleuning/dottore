# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Being-for-itself is qualitative being brought to completion, infinite being. In it the difference between being and determinateness, or negation, is posited and equalized, so that negation is simple self-reference. Something is for itself in so far as it sublates otherness and its connection with an other, having them in it only as sublated moments; it is the infinite turned back into itself. It is first immediately an existent for itself, the one; then the one passes into many ones through repulsion and attraction, and quality passes over into quantity.
