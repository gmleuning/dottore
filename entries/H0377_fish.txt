# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fish are the least perfect of the true classes of vertebrates. Water is their natural element, as their whole structure shows, restricting their articulation and forcing it inward; their blood is hardly warmer than their element. Breathing through gills, without trachea or larynx, they abandon their young and attain no feeling of unity with them.
