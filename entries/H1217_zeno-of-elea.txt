# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Zeno says the same as Parmenides but proceeds more dialectically: he confronts the many and shows that the manifold is not, that it annihilates itself, since whatever involves change is self-contradictory. It was primarily he who elaborated the dialectic, as in the arguments against motion, among them the Achilles, in which the faster body cannot overtake the slower.
