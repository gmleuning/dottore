# 1118 Dithyramb and Psalm
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › a. Religious Lyric
G: Dithyrambe und Psalm
Gloss: The ecstatic song of Dionysus, and the Hebrew psalm.
HGloss: The dithyramb and the psalm, in which the soul is carried away beyond itself toward the god in ecstasy or longing.

N:
  dithyramb and psalm

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Dithyramb and psalm are the more lyrical form of religious elevation, a soaring of the soul in worship in which the poet, shaken by the might of the object, cannot shape any single objective picture and gets no further than exultation. The Old Testament Psalms are the impressive instance of this flight of praise toward the one God.
