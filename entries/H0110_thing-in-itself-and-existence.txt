# 110 Thing-in-Itself and Existence
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › α. Existence › a. The Thing and Its Properties
G: Ding an sich und Existenz
Gloss: The thing as it is in itself, apart from its relations, and the thing as it exists among other things.
HGloss: The thing-in-itself as the empty abstraction of the thing without its relations, set over against its existence.

N:
  thing-in-itself and existence; thing-in-itself, noumenon, noumena, the intelligible, the unknowable, the inscrutable, x, unknown substrate; existence, outward existence, relational being; the noumenal and the phenomenal
  the thing in itself, the unknowable thing, the abstract thing, external existence

V:
  be in itself, withhold itself, elude knowledge, lie behind appearance; exist, appear, relate
  stand apart

Adj:
  noumenal, intelligible, unknowable, inscrutable, inaccessible, transcendent, in itself; phenomenal, existent, relational
  unfathomable, incognizable†, inexplicable, inapprehensible†
  in-itself, abstract

Adv:
  in itself, intrinsically; for us
  apart from relations

F:
  German: Ding an sich, an sich, für uns
  Latin: mundus intelligibilis, esse in se and esse ad aliud, in se
  Arabic: ʿayn [essence, entity]
  Sanskrit: svalakṣaṇa [the particular in itself]
  Chinese: wu zishen [thing in itself]

Phr:
  we can have no cognition of an object, as a thing in itself, but only as an object of sensible intuition, that is, as phenomenon [Kant, Critique of Pure Reason Bxxvi, tr. Meiklejohn]
  the thing-in-itself is a caput mortuum [Hegel]
