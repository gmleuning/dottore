# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Weapons, with other accessories such as vessels and horses, belong more in works that leave the gods' simple repose to represent action, in groups or reliefs. Some external marks are integral to the individual figure, among them clothing, weapons, hair and ornament, so that Pallas is known by her helmet, and by the form of her dress.
