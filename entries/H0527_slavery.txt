# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Slavery is the historical shape of the relation of master and bondsman among peoples who had not risen to the concept of absolute freedom. The Greeks and Romans did not know that the human being as such, as this universal I and rational self-consciousness, is entitled to freedom; they held a person free only if born free. Freedom still had the character of something natural, and so there was slavery in their free states and war by slaves seeking recognition of their human rights.
