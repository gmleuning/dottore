# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Physiognomy and phrenology are observation's attempts to find the law relating self-consciousness to its immediate actuality, how the inner is expressed in the outer. The whole individuality is now the object: the original, inborn body and the traits that arise through acting. Physiognomy takes the outer as a sign and expression of the inner; phrenology takes it as a motionless thing, the skull. Observation thereby makes the actuality of spirit into a thing, ending in the judgement that the being of spirit is a bone.
