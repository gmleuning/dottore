# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Magnitude is determinateness that has become indifferent to being, a limit which is just as much no limit, as against quality, the first and immediate determinateness. It is being-for-itself identical with being-for-another, the repulsion of the many ones turned into their continuity. The sphere runs from pure quantity through quantum to quantitative ratio, in which quantum returns to itself in its otherness and passes into measure. In ordinary use magnitude means a quantum, a determinate quantity, which Hegel distinguishes from quantity as such.
