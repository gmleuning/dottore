# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Externality is the character of the sensory object in so far as it is something other than consciousness: a unit outside me. For sensory consciousness as such this is not yet the object's being external to itself, dispersed in space and time, which is the form in which intuition grasps it. The object is simply given, so that I know neither why it is, nor why it is constituted as it is, nor whether it is anything true.
