G: Kant
Gloss: The thinker who turned philosophy into a critique of reason, showing that the mind supplies the forms and categories of experience, so that we know things only as they appear, never as they are in themselves.
HGloss: Kant, who found the categories in the self and declared the thing in itself unknowable.
