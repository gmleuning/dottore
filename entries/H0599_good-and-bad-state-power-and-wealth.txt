# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Good and bad, state power and wealth, are the two spiritual powers of the actual world. The self-equal, unchanging essence is the good, the foundation and result of individuals' doing; the essence that gives itself up and lets individuals become aware of their singleness in it is the bad. Self-consciousness judges them, and at first takes state power as good and wealth as bad; but since each is the permeation of both moments, this first judgement is not yet spiritual, and each can be judged either way.
