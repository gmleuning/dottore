# 181 Syllogism of Reflection
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism
G: Schluss der Reflexion
Gloss: Inference whose connecting term is a collection of cases.
HGloss: Inference whose middle is the subject's reflected totality: all, then many, then one like another.

N:
  syllogism of reflection; empirical inference, inference from collections, reasoning from cases; middle term as totality of instances; aggregation of cases

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The syllogism of reflection arises when the abstractness of the terms is sublated, each determinateness being posited as concrete, with the others shining in it. Its middle is no isolated particularity but the totality of determinations, the posited unity of the extremes, though only as a reflection that embraces them, not yet the identity of the concept. It runs as the syllogism of allness, of induction and of analogy.
