# 276 Physics of Total Individuality
# Nature › 2. Physics
G: Physik der totalen Individualität
Gloss: The physics of bodies that give themselves a definite shape and set of properties.
HGloss: Bodies as total individuals, matter that forms itself from within: shape, specification and the chemical process.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The physics of total individuality treats bodies as individuals whose centre is no longer a point matter merely seeks but a subjectivity immanent in it, with form-determinations developed from within outwards. Such individuality is for itself yet conditioned, involving relation to another, and as it develops these are sublated until it becomes in principle life.
