# ---- Hegel ----
Def: At this stage of transition the dualism of light and darkness begins to be unified: the negative now falls within the divine subject itself and no longer in a separate evil power. God is an eternal history, affirmative being that dies, becomes estranged from and loses itself, and through that loss finds and returns to itself. Thus the dying of God is internal to God, and it is followed by rising again.
