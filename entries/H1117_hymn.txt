# 1117 Hymn

G: Hymnus
Gloss: The song of praise to God or the gods.
HGloss: The hymn, in which the self, raised to the divine, sings its praise with self-forgetting objectivity.

N:
  hymn; hymnody, song of praise, the Homeric hymns
  hymn, paean, anthem, canticle

V:
  hymn, praise

Adj:
  hymnic, hymnal, paeanic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The hymn is the religious lyric in which the poet concentrates the sphere of the divine into one objective picture and, having completed it in inner contemplation, sets it before others to praise the power and glory of the god celebrated. The Homeric Hymns give situations and stories of the god, mostly mythological and illustrated solidly as in epic.
