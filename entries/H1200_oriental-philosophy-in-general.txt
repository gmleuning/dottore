# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of the Oriental standpoint is that the substantial element by itself is the main thing. The individual is without right, will, or consciousness of its own and is submerged in substance, which religion posits directly as what is essential. The Greek freedom of the individual, which sets thought to work in validating its universality, is the higher standpoint.
