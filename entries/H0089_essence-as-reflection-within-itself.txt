# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Essence as reflection within itself is essence in its first sphere. Issuing from being, it is not immediately in and for itself but a result, and at first it is taken as something that stands over against another, as essential against unessential. Yet essence is being sublated in and for itself, so that what stands against it is only shine, and this shine is essence's own positing. First essence is reflection; then its determinations, the essentialities, are considered; and last, as the reflection of its own determining, it turns into foundation.
