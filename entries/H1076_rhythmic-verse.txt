# 1076 Rhythmic Verse

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rhythmic versification is the system without rhyme, whose chief thing is not isolated sound but temporal duration and movement. It rests on the fixed measure of long and short syllables fitted into relations and metres, the enlivening of rhythm by accent and caesura against word accent, and the euphony of sounds, which classical languages possess in full.
