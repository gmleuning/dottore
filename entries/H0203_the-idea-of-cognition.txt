# 203 The Idea of Cognition
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › III. Idea
G: Die Idee des Erkennens
Gloss: Mind seeking to bring the world and its own thinking into agreement, by knowing and by acting.
HGloss: The idea that has itself as its object, divided into the theoretical idea of truth and the practical idea of the good.
