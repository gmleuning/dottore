# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The syllogism of analogy has as its middle a singular taken in its universal nature: another singular sharing that nature is concluded to share the predicate, as when the earth has inhabitants, the moon is an earth, and so the moon has inhabitants. It is superficial in proportion as the shared universal is a mere quality or mark and identity is taken for similarity. Its form, not any empirical content put into the premises, is what counts.
