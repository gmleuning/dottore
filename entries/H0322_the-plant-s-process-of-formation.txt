# 322 The Plant's Process of Formation
# Nature › 3. Organics › II. Plant Nature
G: Gestaltungsprozess der Pflanze

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The plant's process of formation is its inner relation to itself, which in so simple an organism is immediately a relation outward. It transforms absorbed nutriment into its own specific nature and organs, divides outwardly into root and leaf and inwardly into fibre and vessels, grows by fresh formations, and finally produces a new individual, the bud.
