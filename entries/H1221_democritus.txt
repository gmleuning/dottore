# 1221 Democritus
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Demokrit
Gloss: The philosopher of Abdera who carried atomism into an account of the senses and of the cheerful life.
HGloss: Democritus, who carries on the atomism of Leucippus, telling what is by convention, sweet and bitter, from what truly is, atoms and void.

N:
  Democritus
  the atom
  Democritus of Abdera, the laughing philosopher, cheerfulness, convention and reality

Adj:
  Democritean

Phr:
  by convention sweet, by convention bitter, in reality atoms and void [Democritus]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Democritus, the friend and pupil of Leucippus, is said to have elaborated his teacher's doctrine, though what belongs historically to him cannot be decided. Atomism takes the full and the empty, atom and void, as the principles of all things, what is true and has being in and for itself. As a whole the doctrine remains quite undeveloped.
