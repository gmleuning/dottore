# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Kant holds that cause and effect and the other categories are intrinsically modes of conjunction, belonging to the subject, and asks how synthetic judgments a priori are possible. We do not know the thing in itself: we have determinations of feeling and categories of thought, modes of the activity of the I, so that what we know are only phenomena.
