# 14 Quality
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › a. Determinate Being as Such
G: Qualität
Gloss: The definite character of an existing thing. It marks out what the thing is, and by the same token what it is not.
HGloss: Determinateness as immediately existing, one with being; as reality and as negation, since every determination is a negation.
