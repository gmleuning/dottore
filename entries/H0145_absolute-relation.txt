# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The absolute relation is necessity as being that is simply reflection: a distinguishing whose moments are each the whole of necessity, so that their subsisting is one and their difference only the shine of the movement in which the absolute expounds itself. It is manifestation, self-identical absolute actuality. In its immediate concept it is the relation of substance and accidents; as real it is causality; and returning upon itself it is reciprocity, whose truth is the concept.
