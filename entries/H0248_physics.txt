G: Physik
Gloss: The study of matter in its qualities, as particular kinds of body with their own properties.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Physics treats matter that has individuality, being-for-self developed within itself, and so is determined from within. Matter wrests itself from gravity, manifests itself, and develops its spatial determinations out of its own immanent form instead of receiving them from a centre it merely sought. The sphere runs from universal individuality, through particular individuality, to the total individual body.
