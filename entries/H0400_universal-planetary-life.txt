# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Universal planetary life is the share that mind, in its substance as natural soul, takes in the life of the planet: it feels differences of climate, the changes of the seasons and the times of day. In mind this natural life surfaces only in occasional dark moods. Animals live wholly in such sympathy with nature, but the more cultivated a human being is and the freer the spiritual foundation of its life, the less these connections matter. To raise itself above entanglement in merely natural life is the very activity of mind.
