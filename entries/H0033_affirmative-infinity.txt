G: Affirmative Unendlichkeit
Gloss: The unlimited understood as including the limited rather than lying beyond it; completeness rather than endlessness. Pictured as a circle, as against an endless line.
HGloss: True infinity, which contains the finite as a moment within itself: being returned to itself, imaged by the circle rather than the endless line.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Affirmative or true infinity is the process in which the finite and the infinite are each only moments, each returning to itself through its negation. The finite sublates itself, and its infinity consists in this; the infinite is the transcending of the finite and contains it essentially. Neither is taken as a fixed unit, and their unity is not a motionless sameness but a becoming. It is not a beyond but is there, present, with determinateness in it. Its image is not the straight line that runs on but the circle, closed and wholly present, without beginning or end.
