# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The night-like pit is intelligence as the unconscious store in which a world of infinitely many images and representations is preserved without being in consciousness. To conceive it is to grasp the concept as concrete, as a seed holds in virtual possibility the whole development of the tree, so that no special fibres or places are needed to keep them. The images are ours only formally, not yet at our command.
