# 513 Desire and Destruction
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness › a. Immediate Self-Consciousness (Desire)
G: Begierde und Vernichtung
Gloss: Desire that destroys its object.
HGloss: Desire that destroys its object, and so must always reproduce it as other.

N:
  desire and destruction; destruction

F:
  German: Begierde

Phr:
  to eat one's fill

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Desire destroys the object it meets. Certain of the nullity of the other, self-consciousness sets down that nullity as its truth, annihilates the self-sufficient object and thereby gives itself a certainty of itself that has become objective. Yet the destroying cannot finish its work: for there to be a sublating there must be something other, so that through its negative relation to the object self-consciousness does not get rid of it but engenders it again, and the desire along with it.
