# 1263 Jewish Philosophers
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › c. Medieval Philosophy › i. The Idea of Christianity › 2. Arabic Philosophy
G: Jüdische Philosophen
Gloss: The Jewish philosophers of the Islamic world, chief among them Maimonides.
HGloss: Jewish philosophy within Arabic culture, Moses Maimonides above all, whose Guide reconciles the Law with Aristotle and reports the doctrines of the Kalam.

N:
  Jewish philosophers
  Maimonides [Jewish], the Guide for the Perplexed
  Moses Maimonides, Rambam, Saadia Gaon, Judah Halevi, Ibn Gabirol, Avicebron

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Among the Jews in the Arabic world Moses Maimonides deserves mention, born in Egypt and living in twelfth-century Cordoba. His Guide of the Perplexed, like the work of the church fathers and Philo, takes the historical configuration as fundamental and treats it metaphysically. He is also a source for the Arabs' use of Greek philosophy.
