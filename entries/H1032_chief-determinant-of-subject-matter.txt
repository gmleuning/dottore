# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The chief determinant of painting's subject matter is subjectivity aware of itself. The individual shows an inner life of its own rather than passing wholly into the universal, and lets the external go its own way; painting can therefore take in nature, religion and human life down to fleeting moments, and makes the heart its content, bringing before us the individual's feeling.
