# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The general character of painting rests on its essential principle, the subjectivity of mind that embraces heaven and earth in feeling, idea and action, which places its centre in romantic and Christian art. The question is which content by its nature matches painting's means. Antiquity painted well but could not bring painting to the development it reached in the Christian Middle Ages and after.
