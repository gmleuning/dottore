G: Die Vorsokratiker
Gloss: The first Greek thinkers, who moved explanation from myth to principle: from a single stuff such as water, to number, to unchanging being, to ceaseless becoming, to atoms and to mind.
HGloss: The Presocratics, who found the principle in water, the infinite, number, being, becoming, atoms and nous.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first division runs from Thales to Anaxagoras and takes in the Ionians, the Pythagoreans, the Eleatics, Heraclitus, Empedocles and the atomists, and Anaxagoras. The progression runs from wholly abstract characterizations in natural shape, such as water, air, and the infinite, to number, and then with the Eleatics to pure being, where thought is torn free from sensuous shape.
