# 1210 Presocratics
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle
G: Die Vorsokratiker
Gloss: The first Greek thinkers, who moved explanation from myth to principle: from a single stuff such as water, to number, to unchanging being, to ceaseless becoming, to atoms and to mind.
HGloss: The Presocratics, who found the principle in water, the infinite, number, being, becoming, atoms and nous.
