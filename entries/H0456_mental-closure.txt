# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mental closure is the first main form of derangement, a quite indeterminate submergence within oneself. It appears as imbecility, whether natural and incurable or brought on by misfortune or one's own fault; as absent-mindedness, an unawareness of the immediate present and a submergence in abstract self-feeling that suspends sober consciousness; and, in contrast, as rambling, the inability to fix attention on anything determinate. In all of these the soul lacks the power to hold fast to a definite content.
