# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The siliceous moment belongs to the antithesis, running through all formations, of siliceous, argillaceous and calcareous. In granite it is silica or quartz, absolute earth, the rigid, punctiform principle. In siliceous formations, where granite forms the basis, a more definite difference is present than on the neutral, calcareous side, and the rocks around granite modify it.
