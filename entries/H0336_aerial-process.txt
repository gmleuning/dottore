# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the process with air the plant takes in elemental matter, differentiates it and gives it out again as a specific gas, converting air into water and water into air in a breathing in and out. Hegel rejects the view that it merely picks out ready-made parts: organic life wholly transforms the inorganic.
