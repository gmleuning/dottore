# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The different genres of poetry arise within an art that takes spiritual life as its matter and keeps the middle way between the perceptible and pure inwardness, in imagination, giving a complete world through language. Its works exist only as spoken by living persons, and it divides into epic, which sets out the matter objectively, lyric, which expresses the subject, and drama, which unites them.
