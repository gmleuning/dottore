# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Architecture wavering between building and sculpture adopts more concrete meanings and for their symbolic expression uses more concrete forms, alone or assembled into great buildings, yet handles them architecturally rather than sculpturally. As art enters actual historical views and religious ideas, it loses itself in the contingent, so that architecture and sculpture are confused, though architecture remains decisive.
