# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the life-process the living individual, a subject that is its own purpose, sets itself against the presupposed objective world, which counts for it only as negative and dependent, and is certain of the nullity of that otherness. The process begins with need: the living being posits itself as negated, refers to what is other, and strives to make the world its own. Feeling its own rupture and contradiction as pain is the prerogative of living natures.
