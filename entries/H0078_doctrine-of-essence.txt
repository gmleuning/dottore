# 78 Doctrine of Essence
# Logic
G: Lehre vom Wesen
Gloss: The study of concepts by which things are understood through what lies beneath their surface. These concepts come in pairs, each defined only by its relation to the other.
HGloss: The truth of being: being gone into itself, a reflection in which each determination is only in relation to its other.
