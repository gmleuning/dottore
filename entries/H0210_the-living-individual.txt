# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The living individual is the idea of life in its immediacy, a creative universal soul that determines itself, particularizes itself and splits into the extremes of a judgement that at once becomes a syllogism. It is a concept with an objectivity that corresponds to it only because the concept, as negative unity of that externality, posits it as corresponding. Objectivity is here no longer what the concept is lost in but is posited by it.
