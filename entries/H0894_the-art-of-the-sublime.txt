# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The art of the sublime in the strict sense, Hebrew poetry, grasps the one substance as pure inwardness and might, independent of finitude: God is imageless, and the world, however splendid, is posited as negative, created, subject to his power and serving him. External existence is degraded before the substance, and this is the only way the shapeless God can be illustrated. In the ideal, by contrast, inner and outer pervade each other.
