# 817 Origins of Enthusiasm
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › c. Enthusiasm
G: Entstehung der Begeisterung
Gloss: Where inspiration comes from: not from stimulants or mere resolve, but from an occasion that brings a content.
HGloss: Hegel asks how Begeisterung arises, rejects sensuous stimulus and the bare will to produce, and finds it in an occasion, inner or outer, that hands the artist a content.

N:
  origins of enthusiasm; the sources of inspiration, the springs of creation, the creative impulse, stimulus, spur, prompting, the spark
