# 866 Culturation
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › b. Genius
G: Naturgabe
Gloss: A gift that is inborn yet belongs to a people rather than to one person, like the Italian improvisers' readiness in verse.
HGloss: Genius as Naturgabe: a natural gift which Hegel ties to the particular nationality of peoples, so that a people's art and its manner of production go together, as with the improvisers native to Italy.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Culturation names the tie between artistic gift and a people's nature: because artistic creation includes immediacy and naturalness, the arts are more or less national. Italians have song and melody almost by nature, the Greeks perfected epic and sculpture, the Romans transplanted art from Greece. Poetry spreads most widely, yet folk song is deeply national, and Italian improvisers show marvellous talent.
