# 1260 Kalam
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › c. Medieval Philosophy › i. The Idea of Christianity › 2. Arabic Philosophy
G: Die Philosophie der Medabberim
Gloss: Islamic dialectical theology, whose speakers defended the faith by reason and held that God creates the world anew at each instant.
HGloss: Die Philosophie der Medabberim (the Mutakallimūn), reported by Maimonides: atoms and accidents created anew each moment, all necessary connection denied.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Maimonides names among the Arabs the school of the medabberim, or speakers. They accepted atoms and the void, and held that no accident endures for two moments, God creating another in its place at every instant. Hegel finds here the complete dissolution of all interdependence and rationality, the Oriental standpoint of the one substance brought to determinate consciousness.
