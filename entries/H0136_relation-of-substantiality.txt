# 136 Relation of Substantiality
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › III. Actuality › γ. Absolute Relation
G: Substantialitätsverhältnis
Gloss: The relation of a substance to its passing states, or accidents, which arise and vanish within it.
HGloss: Substance as the power over its accidents, which are only its shining; the totality that posits and negates them.
