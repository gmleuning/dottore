# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The three functions have reality in three systems of shape, nervous, circulatory and digestive, each a totality differentiated within itself. These systems are not separate: every point contains all three, blood, nerves and glandular lymphatic substance being everywhere present, although in each system one function predominates, and animal lymph is their unity.
