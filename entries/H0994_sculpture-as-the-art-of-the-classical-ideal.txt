# 994 Sculpture as the Art of the Classical Ideal

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sculpture is more than any other art the art of the ideal, forming the centre of classical art: beyond the symbolic in the clarity of its spiritual content and its adequacy to it, yet still ignoring the inner subjectivity indifferent to outward shape. Its figures come from an imagination that sets aside all accidents, creating as the gods do by eternal ideas, a sense preeminently at home in Greece.
