# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The advance is the universal determining itself and becoming for itself, a singular and a subject, since only in its consummation is it the absolute. The concrete totality that begins already holds the beginning of development in it: as concrete it differentiates itself, and the method finds the determination of the universal within it. Through the negative of the negative it returns to immediacy, so that the form of the method may be counted as threefold or fourfold.
