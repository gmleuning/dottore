# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Concrete existence is essence that has come forth into immediacy. The truth of being is not to be an immediate something but this: whatever is, exists concretely. It proceeds from ground and condition by sublating the mediation between them, so that it may be said both to have a ground and to have none. It is the second kind of being, after immediate being, and it is not the product of a proof or a derivation from outside but the movement of the fact itself. As negative unity it is a thing.
