# 1002 Logic–Nature–Spirit
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › α. The Concept of Philosophy (the Three Syllogisms)
G: Logik–Natur–Geist
Gloss: The order of the system that runs from pure thought through nature to mind: thought expresses itself in the world and returns to itself in mind.
HGloss: The first syllogism, logic–nature–spirit, which has nature as the middle.
