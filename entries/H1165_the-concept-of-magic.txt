# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Magic rests on a condition in which human beings still live in immediate desire, force and action. They ask no theoretical questions about causes, and their will is not yet restrained by anything universal such as right or duty; desire governs. There is fear in this condition, but it is fear of the contingent forces of nature and not yet the fear of the Lord, which is the beginning of wisdom.
