# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Right and welfare are related in that welfare, the particular will's content, cannot justify a wrong action, and an intention to promote one's own or others' welfare does not make a wrong right. But in extreme danger to life the right of necessity arises: only the necessity of the immediate present can justify a wrong.
