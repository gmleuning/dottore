# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feelings whose content derives from thinking are those in which the substance of right, morality, ethical life and religion is taken up into the feeling will, such as shame and remorse. They are distinguished and justified by that content. But the content is not inseparably bound to feeling: remorse may be felt even over a good deed, and one can weigh an action against duty calmly in representation.
