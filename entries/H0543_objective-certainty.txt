# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Objective certainty is the other side of reason's assurance: that the determinations of self-consciousness are objective, determinations of the essence of things. From here reason knows, positing its difference within itself, as rational self-consciousness. Being spiritual, an activity of spirit, it has the determination that what spirit is, things are also. It presupposes that objects, things in themselves, are determinations of reason, which is what spirit is itself.
