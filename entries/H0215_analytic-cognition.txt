# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Analytic cognition is the first premise of the syllogism of cognition, the immediate communication of the concept, in which mediation does not yet belong to it. Its principle is abstract identity, excluding transition into an other. Starting from a presupposed concrete subject matter, it brings out conceptual determinations immediately contained there. To say that analysis only explicates what is already in the object is one-sided.
