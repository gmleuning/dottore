# 890 The Pantheism of Art
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › a. Symbolic Art › ii. The Symbolism of the Sublime
G: Der Pantheismus der Kunst
Gloss: Art that sees the one divine in all things.
HGloss: The affirmative sublime, the pantheism of Indian and Persian poetry and of Christian mysticism.

N:
  the pantheism of art; the One in all things
  artistic pantheism, pantheistic poetry, the all-one, the divine in all, the immanence of the One

V:
  merge, dissolve, unite, love mystically
  pervade, immerse, lose oneself, see God in all

Adj:
  pantheistic, mystical, devotional
  immanent, all-pervading, God-intoxicated

Adv:
  mystically, in all things
  pantheistically

F:
  Arabic: waḥdat al-wujūd [unity of being], fanāʾ [annihilation in God], ʿishq [divine love]
  Persian: Ḥāfiẓ
  Sanskrit: the Bhagavad Gītā, tat tvam asi
  German: Entbildung [the stripping away of images] [Eckhart], Gelassenheit [releasement] [Eckhart, Heidegger]

Phr:
  God in all things
  a world in a grain of sand
