# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The pantheism of art is not the crude belief that every single thing is God, which no religion or philosophy holds, but the intuition of the all as one substance immanent in individuals, which vanish before it. Belonging primarily to the East, it sees the divine in the most excellent of things while passing beyond each, so that the substance rises sublime above them. Only poetry can express it, not visual art.
