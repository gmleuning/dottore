# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Melody is the poetic element of music, the soul's language, pouring inner joy and sorrow into notes while raising itself above the force of feeling into a free abiding with itself. Time, rhythm and harmony are only its lawful ground. It floats independently above them yet exists only through them, finding true freedom not against necessity but in freeing itself from arbitrary caprice.
