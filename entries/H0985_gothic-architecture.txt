# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Gothic architecture proper developed its special form in the thirteenth century, with the distinctive character already described for the Christian church. Though its origin with the Goths is disputed and some call it German, Hegel keeps the older name, and he distinguishes it essentially from Arabic building, whose mark is the horseshoe arch and not the pointed one.
