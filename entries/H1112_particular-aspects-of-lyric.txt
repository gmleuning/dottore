# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular aspects of lyric are the personality of the poet and the ramification of kinds, which matter here as they did not in the study of primitive epic, because lyric's principle is the individualizing and particularizing of subject matter and forms. They are treated as the lyric poet, the lyric work as product of subjective imagination, and the kinds.
