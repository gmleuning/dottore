# 1215 Xenophanes
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › i. From Thales to Aristotle › 1. Presocratics
G: Xenophanes
Gloss: The poet-philosopher of Colophon who mocked the gods of Homer and taught one unmoved god.
HGloss: Xenophanes, the beginner of the Eleatic school, who denies coming-to-be and makes the One the principle, against the gods made in man's image.

N:
  Xenophanes
  Xenophanes of Colophon, the critique of anthropomorphism, the one god, the rhapsode

Phr:
  if oxen and horses had hands, they would draw the gods like oxen and horses [Xenophanes]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Xenophanes belongs with the Eleatics, who find no satisfaction in the sensible and mythical world and so arrive at the pure, abstract thought that being belongs only to the One. To him is ascribed the argument that nothing can come to be. In his fragments he speaks strongly against the Greek representation of the gods in human shape and with human vices.
