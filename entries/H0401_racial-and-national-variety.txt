# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Racial and national variety is the particularizing of the universal planetary life into the concrete differences of the earth, so that it breaks up into natural minds that express the nature of the geographical regions and, descending further, into the local minds of peoples. Hegel treats these as natural differences of the soul, and holds that since human beings are implicitly rational, descent gives no ground for some to be free and others dominated. His detailed account in the Additions nonetheless ranks races and peoples in terms that are openly racist.
