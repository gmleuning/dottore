# 785 Care for the Poor

G: Die allgemeine Macht an der Stelle der Familie bei den Armen
Gloss: Poverty, the poor and the rabble, and society's relief of them.
HGloss: Wealth piles up at one pole while a mass sinks into poverty and loses its sense of right, honour and self-respect: the rabble, which Hegel names a problem that torments modern society (§241-245).
