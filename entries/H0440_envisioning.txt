# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Envisioning is the clairvoyant soul's unmediated awareness of events still external to the feeling subject. Ordinary consciousness knows what is distant in space only by removing the distance through mediation, but space belongs to external nature and not to the soul, which transforms the external by its ideality; so the subject sunk to merely feeling soul is no longer bound to space. It likewise rises above time past, though a fully determinate and intelligent knowledge of the future is impossible.
