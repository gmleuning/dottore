# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Gesture, in the narrower sense, is the mental expression that proceeds from the rest of the body, as the play of features proceeds from the face. The arm and especially the hand, the tool of tools, are suited to endlessly many expressions of the will. The ground of the symbolic meaning of particular gestures, as of nodding or shaking the head, is not always easy to discover. Cultivated people need fewer gestures, since in speech they have the worthiest and most fitting means of expressing themselves.
