# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The conditions from which the Greek spirit forms itself: a land broken into peninsulas, islands and small districts linked by the sea, matching the versatility of independent peoples; and a mixture of native and foreign stocks, from which a unity is won through law and custom in place of natural bonds. The result is a spirit that shapes the natural, as the sculptor shapes stone, into its own expression.
