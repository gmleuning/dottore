# 486 Universal Self-Consciousness
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness
G: Allgemeines Selbstbewusstsein
Gloss: Self-awareness that knows itself in and through other free selves.
HGloss: The affirmative knowledge of self in the other self, in which each is free and knows the other as free.

N:
  universal self-consciousness; the we, the I that is we, intersubjectivity, mutual recognition, collective consciousness, shared self-consciousness, community of minds, spirit
  universal self-awareness, the We that is I, affirmative knowing of oneself in another, the free among the free, shared selfhood

V:
  recognize one another, share, belong, commune
  know oneself in others, recognize, universalize

Adj:
  universal, collective, communal, intersubjective, shared, mutual
  free

Adv:
  mutually, together, collectively
  universally, in one another, among equals

F:
  Arabic: ʿaql jamʿī [collective mind]
  Chinese: jiti yishi [collective consciousness]
  Nguni: ubuntu [I am because we are]

Phr:
  I that is We and We that is I [Hegel]
  umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu [Zulu] [a person is a person through other persons]
