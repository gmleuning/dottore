# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Practical mind is mind as will, which is aware of itself as concluding within itself and fulfilling itself out of itself, and so steps into actuality. Its finitude lies in its formalism, and its vocation is to bring freedom into existence by making freedom its content, rising to the thinking will that can become objective mind. It develops from practical feeling through urge and wilfulness to happiness and the free will.
