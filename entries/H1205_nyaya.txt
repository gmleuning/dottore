# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Nyaya of Gotama is a philosophy of reasoning, an elaborate dialectic that proceeds by naming, definition, and investigation. It treats the kinds of proof, perception, inference, comparison, and affirmation, and the objects to be proven, and it sets out a formal syllogism in five propositions.
