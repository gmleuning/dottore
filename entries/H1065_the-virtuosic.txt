# 1065 The Virtuosic
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › ii. Music › 3. Relation Between Expression and Content › c. The Execution of Musical Works of Art
G: Virtuosität
Gloss: Performance in which the player's own brilliance becomes the matter: virtuosity, improvisation, display.
HGloss: The second kind of execution, in which the performer is no longer a mere reproducer but co-creator, and virtuosity shows the mastery of the living artist.

N:
  the virtuosic; showpiece, tour de force, display, pyrotechnics, flourish, brilliance
  virtuosity, bravura, virtuoso, cadenza, improvisation

V:
  improvise, extemporize

Adj:
  virtuosic, improvisatory, masterly

Adv:
  with virtuosity, masterfully

Phr:
  the virtuoso
  the performer as co-creator
