# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The language of disruption is the speech of the spirit for which every fixed moment inverts into its opposite: power into powerlessness, good into bad, noble into base. It is witty, destructive judgement, which dissolves all that should count as essence, like the speech of the musician who jumbles every kind of air. Deceiving itself and others, it is for that reason the greatest truth in the shameless statement of the deceit, as against the honest consciousness that holds each moment fixed.
