# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subservience to a specific end is the basic character of architecture proper: the spiritual meaning does not reside exclusively in the building, which would then be an independent symbol, but already exists in freedom outside it, either shaped by another art, sculpture above all, or practically proven by human beings in their actual lives; the two may also meet.
