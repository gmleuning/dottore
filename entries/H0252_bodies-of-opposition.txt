# 252 Bodies of Opposition
# Nature › 2. Physics › I. Physics of Universal Individuality › α. Free Physical Bodies

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The bodies of opposition are the two forms into which darkness, the opposition to light's abstract identity, falls apart: bodily difference or rigid being-for-self, the moon, and opposition existing for itself, sunk in itself as dissolution and neutrality, the comet. Neither rotates on its axis; both are dependent bodies, moments of the dissolving earth that stand free.
