# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Refraction concerns the first determinateness of the transparent medium, specific gravity, which shows itself only in comparison with a medium of different density. An image seen through water into air is displaced, as if the volume of air containing the water were contracted by the water's density. The stick that appears broken is a merely sensuous description; the physical meaning is far harder.
