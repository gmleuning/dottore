# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The relation of substantiality is absolute necessity as being that is because it is, being absolutely mediated with itself. This being is substance, the being in all being: neither an unreflected immediate nor something abstract behind appearance, but immediate actuality itself. Its shining is accidentality, the coming-to-be and passing away in which possibility and actuality turn into one another; this movement is the quiet coming forth of substance itself, which is power.
