# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The transition from independent to classical architecture appears first in the subterranean building of Indians and Egyptians, more prodigious than their surface works. Excavating removes rather than builds and may precede construction above ground; though symbolic, such caves are no longer independently symbolic, since their purpose is to enclose, with walls and roofs, the more symbolic works set up within.
