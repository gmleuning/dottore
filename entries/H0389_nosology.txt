G: Nosologie
Gloss: The study and classification of diseases.
HGloss: Disease as the disproportion of the organism's being and its self, when one system sets itself up apart from the whole. Hegel sorts diseases by where they start: in the elements, in a particular system, or in the mind; fever is the stage where the whole organism takes up the struggle.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Disease is a disproportion between the organism's being and its self, when a system or organ, drawn into conflict with an inorganic power, establishes itself in isolation against the whole. Its kinds arise from the elements, a particular system or the soul; in its course the affection spreads to the whole organism, and fever is the beginning of cure.
