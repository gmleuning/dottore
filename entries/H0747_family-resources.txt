# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Family resources are the family's external existence as a universal and enduring person: possessions determined as permanent and secure, not mere property. In them the arbitrariness of an individual's needs and the selfishness of desire are transformed into care and gain for a common purpose, something ethical.
