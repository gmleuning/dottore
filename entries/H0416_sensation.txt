# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sensation is the form in which mind stirs dully in its unconscious and unintellectual individuality. The waking soul finds within itself, as its own, the determinacies of the content of its sleeping nature; each is simply found, not produced, and yet is taken up into the soul's universality and made ideal. Everything in sensation is immediate, undeveloped in content and not yet set over against a subject as an object, and so it is limited and transient. Everything that emerges in consciousness and reason first appears in it.
