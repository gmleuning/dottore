# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fidelity, in romantic subjectivity's worldly sphere, is not constancy in love or the friendship of equals, such as youth forms, but loyalty toward a superior, someone of higher rank or a master. The Greeks knew it in servants such as the swineherd of Odysseus, and Shakespeare shows it as a matter wholly of the mind in Kent's wish to serve Lear.
