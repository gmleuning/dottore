# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Opposition is the determinate reflection, difference, brought to completion: the unity of identity and diversity, whose moments are diverse within one identity and so opposed. Each side contains its relation to its non-being and is the whole only through it. Likeness reflected into itself and containing the reference to unlikeness is the positive; unlikeness containing the reference to likeness is the negative. Each has the other in it, and each becomes a self-subsisting side of the opposition.
