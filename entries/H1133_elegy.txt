G: Die Elegie
Gloss: The poem of lament and reflection, mourning a loss or a vanished time.
HGloss: Die Elegie: the reflective lyric of mourning and longing, in which feeling is mixed with general reflection.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Elegy is grouped with sonnet, sestina and epistle as a form in which reflection enters lyric and rises beyond song. Unlike sonnet and sestina, whose singability gives way to verbal music, it may keep a more epic character in its measure of syllables, in reflections and apophthegms, and in a descriptive portrayal of feelings.
