# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Historical stages of sculpture arise because the ideal has behind it the symbolic mode it must surmount and ahead the romantic that supersedes it, and both use the human form. So the history includes Egyptian sculpture as preamble, Greek sculpture with the Roman as its proper development, and Christian sculpture with its own principle.
