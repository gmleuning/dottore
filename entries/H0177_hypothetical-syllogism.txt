# 177 Hypothetical Syllogism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › c. Syllogism of Necessity
G: Hypothetischer Schluss
Gloss: Inference whose connecting term is a condition: if A then B; A; therefore B.

N:
  hypothetical syllogism; conditional inference, affirming the antecedent, denying the consequent, chain argument
  the hypothetical inference, the if-then syllogism, the antecedent, the consequent

V:
  infer conditionally, affirm the antecedent, deny the consequent
  condition

Adj:
  hypothetical, conditional, inferential
  consequential

Adv:
  if so, then; given that, it follows
  hypothetically, conditionally

F:
  Latin: modus ponens, modus tollens
  Arabic: istithnāʾī [exceptive syllogism]

Phr:
  if it rains, the ground is wet
  it rains
  the ground is wet
