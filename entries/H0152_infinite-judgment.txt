# 152 Infinite Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment [Tetrad] › a. Judgment of Existence (Qualitative Judgment)
G: Unendliches Urteil
Gloss: A judgment in which subject and predicate have nothing to do with each other, as "the mind is not an elephant"; or one that denies a whole kind of a thing, as crime denies right.
HGloss: The positive infinite judgment, the singular is singular, and the negative, in which subject and predicate do not fit at all: crime.

N:
  infinite judgment; nonsense judgment, absurd predication, category mistake, category error, incongruity; negatively infinite judgment, positively infinite judgment; total negation, radical negation; crime as negation of right, negation of the genus; meaninglessness, senselessness, absurdity; spirit is a bone &c H0511
  the nonsensical judgment, the identical judgment, total incompatibility, crime as infinite judgment

V:
  misapply, misattribute, confound categories, talk nonsense; negate wholly, deny utterly
  disjoin wholly, sever

Adj:
  infinite, absurd, incongruous, nonsensical, senseless, meaningless, category-mistaken, wildly inapplicable; radical, total, sweeping, macaronic†
  unmeaning
  preposterous
  negative
  incommensurable, tautologous

Adv:
  absurdly, nonsensically
  infinitely, wholly, without relation

F:
  Latin: iudicium infinitum, non sequitur, reductio ad absurdum, toto caelo

Phr:
  the mind is not an elephant
  a lion is not a table
  colourless green ideas sleep furiously [Chomsky]
  neither here nor there
