# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Dissemblance is what the moral worldview shows itself to be when its postulates are examined: a nest of thoughtless contradictions. Consciousness fixes one moment, passes at once into its opposite and cancels the first, and as soon as it has set up the second it shifts its ground again, making the contrary into the essence. Knowing both its contradiction and its shifting, it confesses that it is in earnest about neither side.
