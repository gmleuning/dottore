G: Die Kirchenväter
Gloss: The early Christian thinkers who gave the faith a doctrinal form, using Greek philosophy as their tool.
HGloss: The Church Fathers, who worked out the faith into doctrine.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Church Fathers were philosophically cultivated men who introduced philosophy, particularly Neoplatonic philosophy, into the church. They built the philosophical idea into the Christian principle and so developed a system of doctrine, dealing with the nature of God, human freedom, and the origin of evil. Hegel defends this development against those who would return to the letter, since the Spirit gives life.
