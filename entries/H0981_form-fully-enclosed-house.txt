# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The fully enclosed house is the basic form of the Gothic church: as the Christian spirit concentrates itself in the inner life, the building becomes a place shut in on every side for the congregation and its thoughts. Yet worship rises above the finite, so that churches grow out of the ground toward the sky, while light is excluded or glimmers dimly through stained glass.
