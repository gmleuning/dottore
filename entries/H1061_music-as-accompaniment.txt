# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Music as accompaniment is bound more strictly to a specific subject than independent music, since the words give definite ideas from the start. Yet it must not sink into servitude and become an intellectual trick of indicating a ready-made content, nor cast off the text; it must be filled with the sense of words and situation and from that inner animation develop a soulful musical expression.
