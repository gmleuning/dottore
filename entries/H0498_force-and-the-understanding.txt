# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The understanding takes as its object the unconditioned universal, which has returned into itself from the one-sided concepts of perception. Behind the appearance that is the play of forces it looks into the inner of things, a supersensible world that it fills with the calm realm of law, and then with the inversion of that world. In explaining, it posits differences that are no differences and sublates them again. What it experiences in the inner is in truth only itself, and so it passes over into self-consciousness.
