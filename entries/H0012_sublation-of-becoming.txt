# 12 Sublation of Becoming
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being › c. Becoming
G: Aufhebung
Gloss: The coming to rest of change in a stable result, a vanishedness that is not nothing. To sublate is at once to cancel and to preserve: being and nothing are cancelled as separate, yet kept as aspects of what results.
HGloss: The vanishing of the vanishing itself: becoming collapses into a quiescent unity, a vanishedness that is not nothing but the unity of being and nothing become quiescent simplicity; to sublate (aufheben) is at once to negate and to preserve.
