G: Funktionen des Organismus
Gloss: The three functions of the animal body: sensibility, the capacity to feel; irritability, the capacity to respond; and reproduction, the capacity to renew itself.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The functions of the organism are three: sensibility, the subject's simple, universal being within itself, in which any particular is immediately taken up; irritability, its capacity to be stimulated from outside and to react; and reproduction, their unity, the return from outward relation by which it produces itself as individual, the basis of the other two.
