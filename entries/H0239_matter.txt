# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Matter is the immediately identical, existing unity of space and time into which motion collapses, the substrate of place and motion. The transition from the ideality of space and time to this reality is incomprehensible to the understanding, which takes them as empty forms filled from outside and treats matter as externally in them, though matter is essentially spatial and temporal.
