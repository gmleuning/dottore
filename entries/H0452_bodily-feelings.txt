# 452 Bodily Feelings
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › i. Particular Feelings
G: Leibliche Empfindungen
Gloss: Feelings of the body itself, such as pleasure and pain, appetite and desire.
HGloss: Feelings whose content is the body's own state, pleasure and pain, the pleasant and the unpleasant, felt where they arise.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first and most superficial practical feeling is that of the pleasant and the unpleasant. Practical feeling involves an ought, a self-determination related to an individuality that counts only in so far as it is adequate to it. The will compares how it is immediately determined from outside with what its own nature determines it to be: agreement is the pleasant, disagreement the unpleasant. Since that inner measure is still only felt and belongs to natural individuality, the judgement is entirely contingent, and in important matters it is a matter of indifference whether something pleases.
