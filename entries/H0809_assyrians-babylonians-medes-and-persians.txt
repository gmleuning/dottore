# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The peoples in whom the other elements of the Persian empire appear beside the higher spiritual element of the Zend race. Assyria and Babylonia contribute external wealth, luxury and commerce, and great cities raised for settled trade and for protection against roving peoples; the Medes, a warlike mountain people, pass on the Zend religion; and the Persians at last embrace all Anterior Asia in their rule.
