# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Rhyme belongs to romantic poetry, which, growing more inward, withdraws from the natural length of syllables and plays with the sounds of words for their own sake. It is no accidental invention but necessary: the soul's need to apprehend itself is satisfied by returning like sounds, indifferent to regulated time, which bring us back to ourselves and bring verse near to music.
