# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The frenzy of self-conceit is what the concern for the welfare of mankind becomes when the realized law of the heart turns against the one who authored it. Others do not find their own hearts' law in it and resist it. To save itself from destruction, consciousness casts out of itself the inversion that it is, and rages against the universal order as a fabrication of fanatical priests and gluttonous despots. In truth it is the singular heart, wanting to be immediately universal, that is mad and inverted.
