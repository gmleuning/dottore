# 564 The Frenzy of Self-Conceit
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › a. The Certainty and Truth of Reason › iii. Self-Actualization of Reason › 2. The Law of the Heart and the Frenzy of Self-Conceit
G: Der Wahnsinn des Eigendünkels
Gloss: The rage of the heart that finds its law perverted in the world and blames the world's order for its own madness.
HGloss: Der Wahnsinn des Eigendünkels: the heart, seeing its law turned into a universal order alien to it, declares that order a perversion devised by fanatical priests and despots.
