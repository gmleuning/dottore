# 468 Self-Consciousness
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness)
G: Selbstbewusstsein
Gloss: Awareness that has itself as its object; the self knowing itself.
HGloss: The truth of consciousness: the I that is its own object, I = I, whose first form is desire.

N:
  self-consciousness; self-awareness, self-knowledge, reflexivity, self-reflection, self-recognition, self-relation, the I that is a we, apperception
  the I, I am I, selfhood, the native realm of truth

V:
  know oneself, be self-aware, reflect upon oneself, recognize oneself
  be aware of oneself, reflect, apperceive

Adj:
  self-conscious, self-aware, self-knowing, reflexive, reflective, introspective
  meditative, contemplative
  apperceptive

Adv:
  self-consciously, reflexively

F:
  German: Selbstbewußtsein
  Arabic: shuʿūr bi-l-dhāt [self-awareness]
  Sanskrit: ātma-jñāna [self-knowledge], sva-saṃvedana [self-awareness]
  Chinese: ziwo yishi [self-consciousness]
  Japanese: jiko ishiki
  Korean: jagi uisik [self-consciousness]

Phr:
  know thyself, gnōthi seauton [Gr.]
  I that is We, and We that is I [Hegel]
