# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Cambium is the third sap, the neutral substance in which the plant's vital sap becomes a product. Following Schultz, it is a delicate soft substance between bark and wood that appears with the leaf, does not circulate, and is the embryonic shape of the whole plant, from which wood, bark and cellular tissue develop.
