# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea is the adequate concept, the objectively true or the true as such: whatever has truth has it by virtue of its idea. Hegel keeps Kant's reclaiming of the word for the concept of reason, the unconditioned, but rejects the view that an idea, because nothing in the senses is congruent with it, lacks actuality or truth. It is the unity of concept and objectivity, appearing first as life, then as cognition, then as the absolute idea.
