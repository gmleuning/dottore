# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Hegel illustrates the less developed forms of magic from travellers' and older reports, among the Eskimos and in Africa. There is no representation of a universal being; magicians or conjurers claim to command rain, gales and animals, often by putting themselves into a wild state of ecstasy. Where a state exists the king may stand at their head, and the people may reward or ill-treat their magicians.
