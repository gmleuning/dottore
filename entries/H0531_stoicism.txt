# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Stoicism is the freedom of self-consciousness as it first appeared in the history of spirit. Its principle is that consciousness is the thinking essence, and that something is essential, true or good for it only in so far as it behaves in it as a thinking being. It stands neither as master nor as servant, but is free within all the dependences of individual existence, on the throne or in fetters, withdrawing from movement into the simple essentiality of thought. Such abstract freedom is only an incomplete negation of otherness.
