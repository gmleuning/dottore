# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The shaping of the divine world, in which the Greek idea becomes objective as religion. The gods are beautiful individuals: spirit is their essence and the natural only their point of departure, transformed into spiritual power. Their human form is their truth, yet they are not absolute free Spirit, since each is a particular character bound by human limitation.
