# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Appearance proper is concrete existence posited as what it is: essence in its concrete existence, whose immediacy is reflected and has negativity within it. To call something appearance is not to set it below some truer immediate thing, for appearance is the higher truth, concrete existence as essential. The reflection that makes it appearance is its own and not an external comparison. It unfolds as the law of appearance, the opposition of an apparent world and a world in itself, and the dissolution of that opposition into essential relation.
