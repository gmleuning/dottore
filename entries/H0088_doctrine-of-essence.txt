G: Lehre vom Wesen

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Essence is the truth of being. Knowledge does not stop at what is immediate but penetrates behind it, and this is no activity of cognition external to being: it is the movement of being itself, which recollects itself and by this inwardness becomes essence, being that is past, but timelessly past. Essence is not what abstraction leaves over when it has removed all determinacy. It first shines within itself as reflection, then determines itself in the essentialities, and as ground passes into concrete existence and appearance.
