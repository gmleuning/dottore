# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Dramatic poetry splits into genres because an action made real on stage must be wholly determined in conception and treatment, and the types come from the different ways characters, aims, conflicts and outcome are presented, with opposition or reconciliation. The chief ones are tragedy and comedy and their conciliation, considered in principle, in ancient and modern forms, and in history.
