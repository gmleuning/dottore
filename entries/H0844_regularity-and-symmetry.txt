# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Regularity is sameness in something external, the repetition of one and the same shape as the determining unity of objects; being abstract, its beauty is that of the abstract understanding. Symmetry adds unlikeness, connecting a form with another of its kind that differs from it, so that a new and richer unity arises. Both are chiefly determinations of quantity, external to what makes a thing what it is.
