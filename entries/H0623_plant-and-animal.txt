# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Plant and animal religion is the religion of spiritual perception, in which the shapeless essence falls apart into countless weaker and stronger spirits. The innocent religion of flowers, a representation of the self without a self, passes into the seriousness of warring life and the guilt of animal religion: scattered peoples take determinate animal shapes as their essence and fight one another to the death. In this hatred pure negativity wears itself out.
