# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Kepler's laws are the laws of absolutely free motion, discovered by Kepler with immortal fame and proved in the sense that Kepler gave the data their general expression. Hegel denies that Newton first proved them: the Newtonian formulae can be deduced from them, the idea of the planets' immanent connection with the sun was already Kepler's, and Newton did not prove the ellipse.
