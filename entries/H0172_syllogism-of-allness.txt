# 172 Syllogism of Allness
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › b. Syllogism of Reflection
G: Schluss der Allheit
Gloss: Inference from what holds of all members of a class to what holds of one.

N:
  syllogism of allness; universal instantiation; deduction from all, application of a general rule to a case; question-begging generalization, circular deduction
  the syllogism of totality, the inference from all, universal inference, the all as middle

V:
  apply, instantiate, deduce from the general, bring under the rule
  infer from all, apply to each, subsume

Adj:
  deductive, universal, general, instantiating, circular
  generic, collective
  all-inclusive, distributive

Adv:
  by universal instantiation
  universally, from all to one

F:
  Latin: dictum de omni, dictum de omni et nullo, a fortiori

Phr:
  what holds of all holds of each
  dictum de omni et nullo [Lat.]
