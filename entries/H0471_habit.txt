# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Habit is the soul's making itself into an abstract universal being and reducing the particular feelings to a determination in it that simply is. The soul possesses the content and moves in it without sensation or consciousness, neither distinguishing itself from it nor absorbed in it, and so is free of it and open to other activity. Produced by repetition or practice, it is the mechanism of self-feeling, a second nature, immediate yet posited by the soul. By it one becomes free, and yet habit also makes one its slave.
