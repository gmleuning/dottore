# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The individual epic action proceeds on the ground of a world open to conflicts between whole nations: from a national undertaking a particular aim arises, interwoven with the life of the whole people and revealing every side of its character, faith and action. Individually definite, it takes the form of an event, in which outer reality has as full a right as inner will.
