# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The effect of music springs from its working directly on the heart, the simple concentrated centre of human life, without intellectual considerations. Unlike a statue or picture, which remain outside as objects we can view freely, a piece of music vanishes as it sounds, penetrates the secret of all movements of the soul and captivates a consciousness no longer facing an object.
