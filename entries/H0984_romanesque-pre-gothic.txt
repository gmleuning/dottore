Def: Romanesque architecture, distinct from the Gothic, developed from the Roman. The oldest Christian churches are basilicas, taken from huge oblong imperial halls with timber roofs, whose tribune may have given rise to the chancel. From Rome, especially in the west, came columns with round arches, domes and decoration, which Ostrogothic and Lombard buildings largely retained.
