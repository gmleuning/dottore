# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the great systems of Plato and Aristotle Greek science takes shape, objective thought forming itself into a whole. Plato's philosophy is oriented toward the intellectual, supersensible world, the domain that Christianity later organized. What is true and universal, the Idea of the good, the beautiful, and the true, resides beforehand in spirit and develops from it.
