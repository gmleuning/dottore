# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Parable, proverb and apologue take up events from ordinary life and give them a deeper, more general meaning. Unlike the fable they look not to nature and animals but to familiar human doings. The parable enlarges a seemingly trivial case to suggest a deeper meaning; the proverb states the universal immediately in a particular case; the apologue brings out in the example itself the maxim it contains.
