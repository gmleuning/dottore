# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Formal reason, the inferring intellect, is the third moment of thinking, which sublates the difference of form and posits the identity of the differences, determining the content from itself. Here the universal is recognized as particularizing itself and gathering itself into individuality, producing content from its own self as the self-developing concept of the thing. Thinking then has no content but its own determinations and finds in the object only itself.
