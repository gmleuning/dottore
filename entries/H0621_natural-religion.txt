# 621 Natural Religion
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › c. Religion as Spirit's Self-Consciousness
G: Natürliche Religion
Gloss: Religion that finds the divine in natural forms.
HGloss: Spirit knowing itself in natural shapes: light, plant and animal, and the artificer's work.

N:
  natural religion; nature worship, religion of nature, animism, fetishism, totemism, pantheism, light worship, Zoroastrianism, Mazdaism, Egyptian religion, Indian religion

Adv:

Phr:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Natural religion is the first shape in which spirit knows itself, in the form of immediate being. As self-conscious essence it is at first only its concept, the night from which its shapes are born, and it intuits itself in the manner that corresponds to immediate consciousness. It passes from the shapeless light, through the religion of plants and animals, to the artisan, who brings forth spirit as an object by an instinctive kind of work.
