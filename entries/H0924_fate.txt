# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fate is the abstract and shapeless necessity that stands higher than the plural gods, overpowering gods and men while remaining incomprehensible. Thought dissolves the gods' plurality into one divinity under whose might they strive. The tranquil mourning that marks the sculpted god in blessed repose already constitutes its fate, showing that something higher stands above and that a passage to universal unity is needed.
