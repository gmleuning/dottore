# 9 Moments of Becoming
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being › c. Becoming
G: Die Momente des Werdens
Gloss: The two directions of becoming, coming-to-be and ceasing-to-be, each passing into the other.
HGloss: The moments of becoming: coming-to-be, where nothing passes into being, and ceasing-to-be, where being passes into nothing; each sublates itself in itself.
