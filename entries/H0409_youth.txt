# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Youth is the stage of developed opposition. As the life of the genus stirs, the young person turns to the substantial universal, conceived as an ideal still more or less subjective, of love and friendship or of a better state of the world. To themself they ascribe the true and the good, and to the existing world what is contingent. This opposition carries the urge to remove it by realizing the ideal, so that youth feels called to transform the world and breaks the peace the child had with it. In trying to actualize the ideal, the youth becomes an adult.
