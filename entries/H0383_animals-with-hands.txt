# 383 Animals with Hands
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › γ. The Animal's Genus Process › a. Genus and Species (Zoology) › iii. Vertebrata › 4. Mammals
G: Tiere mit Händen
Gloss: Primates: mammals with grasping hands.
HGloss: Mammals whose feet are hands: man and the monkey, which is a satire on man.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mammals are classed by how, as individuals, they oppose other animals, through teeth, feet, claws and beaks. The first class are animals whose feet are hands, the human being and the monkey, which Hegel calls a satire on the human, one it must amuse us to see if we can laugh at ourselves.
