# 504 Subjectivity (Transition to Self-Consciousness)
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › α. Consciousness as Such › c. Force and the Understanding
G: Subjektivität
Gloss: Awareness discovering that what it found behind appearance is its own activity; awareness turning back on itself.
HGloss: Consciousness discovering that in the inner it has itself before it, and so becoming self-consciousness.

N:
  subjectivity; self-discovery, the turn to the subject, reflexive turn, recognition that the inner is oneself, infinity, the concept of life, curtain drawn back; behind the curtain there is nothing to see unless we go behind it ourselves [Hegel]
  reflection into self, the turn to the self, the inner as one's own, the curtain drawn away, the understanding finding itself

V:
  turn inward, discover oneself, recognize oneself, see through
  turn back, reflect into itself, find itself, discover

  reflexive, self-discovering, inward-turning, subjective
  self-returning

  inwardly, reflexively, back upon oneself

  to find oneself

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In infinity, the absolute restlessness in which every determinacy is the opposite of itself, consciousness at last has as its object a difference that is immediately sublated: it distinguishes what is not distinct, and so is self-consciousness. The explanation in which the understanding enjoys only itself already describes this. The curtain that seemed to hide the inner is lifted, and the inner gazes into the inner. Self-consciousness is shown to be the truth of the shapes of consciousness, though at first only for us.
