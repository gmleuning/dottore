# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The syllogism is the restoration of the concept within judgement, and so the unity and truth of both. In it the determinations of the concept stand as extremes, as in judgement, and at the same time their determinate unity is posited in a middle. It is therefore the completely posited concept and the rational: not only is the syllogism rational, but everything rational is a syllogism. It is no mere subjective form set apart from the reason that deals with content.
