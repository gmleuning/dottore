# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the stratified formations the moments of primitive rock are separated out almost as pure earths, in layers of sandstone, clay, coal, peat, rock-salt, limestone, gypsum and marl. Organic shapes abound here, yet Hegel denies that these forms, with some exceptions, once lived and died: they are stillborn, produced by nature in dead shape.
