# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The natural qualities are the determinations the soul has in itself by nature, not as objects over against it. They descend from the most universal to the most singular: the soul shares in the planetary life; this life breaks up into the natural minds of the world regions and of nations; and it is finally differentiated into the individual subject, with temperament, talent and character.
