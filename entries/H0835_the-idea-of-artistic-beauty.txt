# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea of artistic beauty, or the ideal, treats beauty as no abstraction of the understanding but the inherently concrete absolute concept, the absolute idea appearing in a way adequate to itself. That idea in its genuine actuality is universal, infinite and absolute spirit, which determines out of itself what is truly the true. The inquiry places art among the realms of reality and in relation to religion and philosophy.
