# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea of the true is the theoretical idea, in which the subjective idea is first the impulse to sublate its subjectivity by filling itself with the content of a presupposed world, and to find its identity with itself in the object. The concept is certain of itself, while the world counts as inessential. Truth here means truth for the subjective concept, in knowledge; this cognition attains only a neutral synthesis of what stays separate.
