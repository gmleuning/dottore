# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Divining is the first appearance of immediate awareness in the magnetic states: people who, fully awake and without the mediation of sight, detect metal or water lying underground, and some who sense salt as well. They may use a forked hazel twig as a divining rod, but its movement has no ground in the wood; it is determined solely by the individual's sensation.
