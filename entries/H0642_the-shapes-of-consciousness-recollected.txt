# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The shapes of consciousness are recollected so as to show how consciousness knows its object as itself. The object is in part immediate being, answering to immediate consciousness; in part relation and determinateness, answering to perception; and in part essence or universal, answering to understanding. Absolute knowing does not at first appear as pure comprehension of the whole: it gathers the earlier shapes, in which the totality of the object's moments and of consciousness's conduct can be shown only as distributed.
