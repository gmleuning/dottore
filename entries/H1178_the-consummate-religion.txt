# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The consummate religion is religion that has become objective to itself, the revelatory religion, in which consciousness knows the absolute essence as its own and spirit is the object of spirit. God is himself consciousness, differentiating himself and giving himself as object. This religion is revealed, in that God has given himself to be known, and also positive, in that it has come to humanity from without.
