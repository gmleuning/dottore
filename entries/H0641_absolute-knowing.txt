# 641 Absolute Knowing
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason
G: Das absolute Wissen
Gloss: Knowledge in which the mind fully understands itself, with nothing left opaque or outside it.
HGloss: Spirit that knows itself as spirit, in the form of the concept rather than representation: science.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute knowing is spirit knowing itself in the shape of spirit, comprehending knowing, in which what religion held as content in the form of representation receives the form of the self. Truth is here not only the same as certainty but has the shape of self-certainty. The form of objectivity that remained is overcome, since consciousness has related to its object according to all its determinations and found in each a determination of the self. Spirit appearing in this element is science.
