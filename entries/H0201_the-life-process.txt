# 201 The Life Process
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › III. Idea › α. Life
G: Der Lebensprozess
Gloss: The activity by which a living being maintains itself through exchange with its surroundings.
HGloss: The living individual against an inorganic nature that it assimilates, so that it sets itself up as itself.
