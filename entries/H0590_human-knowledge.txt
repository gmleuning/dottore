G: Das menschliche Wissen
Gloss: The knowledge that belongs to the human law: what the community openly knows and decrees.
HGloss: Das menschliche Wissen: the self-conscious knowledge of the human law, manifest in the light of day, which in acting knows only one side of the ethical substance.

N:
  human knowledge
  Creon

V:

Adj:
  civic, political, public, manifest

Adv:
  by the law of the land

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Human knowledge here is the knowing of the consciousness that belongs to the human law. Since ethical consciousness has decided for one power, it sees right only on its own side. The commands of government have a universal and public meaning that lies open in the light of day, and to the consciousness holding to them the other law appears as the obstinacy and disobedience of an inward being-for-itself, and as sacrilege where it contradicts them.
