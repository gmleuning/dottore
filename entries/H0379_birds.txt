# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Birds belong to the air: plumage is the vegetative principle developing on their skin, their chest system is specially developed, and bones and lungs are connected with air-pockets. They feel for their young and build nests, and many sing, continuing their cry as an ideal enjoyment of themselves in their element.
