# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ancient epigram follows from the one-sidedness of didactic and descriptive poetry, which art cannot retain without cancelling itself, so that external reality is again brought into relation with inwardly grasped meaning. It is an inscription on a thing that states what the object is, in a sentence about its meaning, joining abstract universal and concrete appearance more deeply than their mere juxtaposition.
