G: Bloße Manier

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mere manner, or mannerism, must be essentially distinguished from originality: it concerns the particular and accidental idiosyncrasies of the artist, which come forward in place of the topic and its ideal representation. So understood it is the worst thing to which an artist can submit, since in it one indulges only personal, restricted whims, whereas art demands that such accidental peculiarity be extinguished.
