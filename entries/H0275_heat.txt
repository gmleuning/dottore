# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Heat is matter's self-restoration to formlessness and fluidity, the triumph of its abstract homogeneity over specific determinatenesses, an existing activity of dissolution which appears formally as expansion. Unlike passive breaking, it belongs to qualitative cohesion: while repelling force the body inwardly yields, and this inner melting is the birthplace of heat, in which sound perishes.
