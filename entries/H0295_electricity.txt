# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Electricity is the relation of shaped bodies as physical individualities which, not yet in chemical process, preserve themselves in indifference but, in reciprocal tension, manifest their abstractly real selfhood as a light pregnant with difference. Like sound, it needs two bodies, but here both are differently disposed to each other, so that the exciting body too enters into the difference.
