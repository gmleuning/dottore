# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The recent philosophy begins from the thesis that universality and necessity have a source other than perception, the I in its self-consciousness. Kant's philosophy is called critical because its first aim is a critique of knowing: before getting at the truth, we are to examine the instrument, to see whether it can accomplish what is required of it. Fichte, Jacobi, and Schelling follow.
