# 949 National Architecture
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › a. Architecture › i. Symbolic Architecture
G: Bauwerke zur Vereinigung der Völker

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: National architecture is building whose content is the holy understood, with Goethe, as what links many souls together. In the story of the tower in the plains of the Euphrates, all the peoples laboured in common, and the aim of the work was the community of its builders, a new and wider union beyond family ties. Herodotus' account of the tower of Bel at Babylon, rising in eight stages, gives a historically surer instance.
