# 825 Roman World (Abstract Legal Personhood)
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › γ. The State › c. World History
G: Die römische Welt
Gloss: The world of Rome, in which, as in Greece, only some are free, and the individual counts as an abstract legal person under an impersonal state.
HGloss: The Roman world of abstract universality, where the person counts as a holder of rights and the state is a hard, alien power.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third stage of world history, in which an abstract universality of power binds individuals and sacrifices their ethical life to the aim of the state. Over against this stands the abstract personality of the individual, the ground of legal right, which shows itself chiefly in property. The end pursued with heartless severity is mere dominion, and from the resulting unhappiness the free Spirit of Christianity arises.
