# 271 Quantitative Coherence
# Nature › 2. Physics › II. Physics of Particular Individuality › β. Cohesion › b. Coherence
G: Quantitative Kohärenz
Gloss: Cohesion measured as resistance to force, as in tensile, breaking or crushing strength.
HGloss: Coherence as resistance in a determinate direction, as tensile, breaking or crushing strength.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Quantitative coherence is ordinary cohesion, the first and merely quantitative coherence of matter with itself: the strength with which it holds together in resistance to weight. The amount of weight decides whether a mass retains its coherence or gives it up, so that glass or wood bears a certain load before breaking; this order does not follow that of specific gravity.
