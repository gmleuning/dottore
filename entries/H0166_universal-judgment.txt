# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The universal judgement makes its subject all: the external universality of reflection, the all of all the singulars, in which each singular remains unchanged. This universality is only a commonality of self-standing singulars, an association made by comparison, which is what representation first takes universality to be, as what fits many. Its truth lies beyond mere allness, in the objective universality of the genus, and with it the judgement of necessity.
