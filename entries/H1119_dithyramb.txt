G: Die Dithyrambe
Gloss: The ecstatic choral song of Dionysus, in which the singer is carried out of himself.
HGloss: Die Dithyrambe: subjective elevation in worship, the poet shaken by the power of the god and borne beyond himself in rapture.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dithyramb is subjective elevation in worship: the poet, enraptured and stunned in the inmost heart, in a mood wholly without definition, is absorbed directly into the absolute and sings in jubilation the infinity and its splendid manifestations. The Greeks soon interrupted such outbursts with mythical narratives, which grew into drama with the chorus.
