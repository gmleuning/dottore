# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The forms of art are the actualizations of the idea of the beautiful, which as a totality of essential differences must issue in them: not species brought in from outside but the ideal's own more concrete determinations, spread out by its concept. Through them the idea presses on to representation, and each stage of its content has its own shape, so that defects of artistic form prove to be defects of the idea.
