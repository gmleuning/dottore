# 990 Beautiful Sculptural Form

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The beautiful sculptural form takes the human figure as its given fundamental type, a product of nature and not devised by art, much as architecture takes the house. Unlike the animal body, the human form is the body not only of soul but of spirit, soul being the simple self-awareness of the body and spirit the self-awareness of conscious life with all its feelings, ideas and aims.
