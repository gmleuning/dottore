# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Classical architecture is subservient to an end and meaning not immanent in itself: an inorganic surrounding structure ordered by gravity and severe regular forms. Its beauty lies in appropriateness to purpose, freed from confusion with the organic and symbolic, a totality that lets its single purpose shine through all its forms and in the music of its proportions reshapes the useful into beauty.
