# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Christian mysticism affords pantheistic unity as felt by the subject, who senses God present in consciousness and its unity with God; it developed this more subjective way within Christianity. Hegel cites Angelus Silesius, who expressed with the greatest audacity and depth of intuition the substantial existence of God in things and the union of self and God. Eastern pantheism stresses instead contemplating the one substance in all phenomena.
