# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The disappearance of the symbolic form comes when meaning and shape are posited as wholly external to each other. In symbolic art they never fully interpenetrated, though some kinship held them; since in comparison only caprice joins them, the link becomes mere adornment, leaving on one side a ready-made meaning and on the other bare externality, in subordinate forms that show the symbolic destroying itself.
