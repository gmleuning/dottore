# 671 Ethical Life
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit
G: Sittlichkeit
Gloss: Freedom made real in the shared customs and institutions of a living community.

N:
  ethical life; ethical community, ethical order, social ethics, customary morality, second nature, the living good, family, civil society, and state
  ethical substance, the ethical world, custom, the institutions

V:
  live ethically, belong, participate, abide by custom
  inhabit, conform, realize freedom

Adj:
  ethical, customary, communal, social, civic
  conventional &c H0427, well-bred, well behaved
  national, public
  substantial, institutional

Adv:
  ethically, in community
  by custom, in a living community

F:
  German: Sitte, Sittlichkeit
  Latin: mores
  Greek: ethos, ēthos
  French: comme il faut
  Arabic: adab [right conduct], akhlāq
  Sanskrit: dharma
  Chinese: lunli [ethics], li [ritual propriety]
  Japanese: rinri

Phr:
  the ethical is the idea of freedom as the living good [Hegel, paraphrased]
