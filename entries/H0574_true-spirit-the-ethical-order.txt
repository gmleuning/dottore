# ---- Hegel ----
Def: True spirit, ethical life, is spirit in its immediacy, in which the simple ethical substance divides into a differentiated essence, a human and a divine law. Self-consciousness, facing it, assigns itself to one of these powers, knowing it and ignoring the other, and so is a deceived knowing. In acting it experiences the contradiction of the two powers, their mutual destruction and its own downfall. Through this movement the substance becomes actual self-consciousness, and just therein ethical life perishes.
