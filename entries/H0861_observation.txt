# 861 Observation
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › a. Fancy
G: Beobachtung
Gloss: The artist's gift for seeing the shapes of the world and keeping them in memory.
HGloss: The first side of Phantasie: the gift and sense for grasping actuality, which sees much, attends closely and holds the many images of the world in a retentive memory.

N:
  observation; perception, a keen eye, an eye for detail, attentiveness, noticing, the observing eye, retentive memory, a store of images, knowledge of the world, study from life, life study, sketchbook, field notes

V:
  observe, notice, take in, study, watch, sketch from life, take notes

Adj:
  observant, perceptive, keen-eyed, sharp-eyed, attentive, watchful

Adv:
  observantly, from life

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Observation is the first requirement of the artist: the gift and sense for grasping actual reality and its shapes, attentively heard and seen, together with a retentive memory of them. The artist creates from the abundance of life, not of abstract generalities, and so must have seen, heard and retained much, knowing both outward forms and the passions and aims of the human soul.
