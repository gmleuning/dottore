# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The classical temple as a whole unites its parts by juxtaposition and thorough eurhythmy, and is more than satisfying to behold. It does not strive upward but spreads in breadth, so that the eye is drawn across it, unlike Gothic building that struggles toward the sky; its ornament never impairs simplicity, and the Greeks kept a beautiful mean with well-proportioned variety.
