# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The ethical world as a self-complete totality is a peaceful equilibrium of all its parts, each a local spirit that seeks no satisfaction beyond itself because it is in balance with the whole. The balance is living: inequality arises and is brought back to equality by justice, the government of the people and the avenging spirit of the family. Undivided and unpolluted, the ethical realm moves by one of its powers peacefully becoming the other, and the union of man and woman is the middle that joins them.
