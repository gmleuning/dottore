# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Cyrenaics, headed by Aristippus, held that the human vocation, what is supreme and essential, is to seek pleasure, pleasant sensation. The principle at first appears trivial, since pleasure may come from the most diverse circumstances. It belongs to the time when individuals no longer find their place in the customary ethos of their people and begin to ask on their own account what they are to do.
