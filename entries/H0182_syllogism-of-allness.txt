# 182 Syllogism of Allness
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › b. Syllogism of Reflection

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The syllogism of allness is the syllogism of the understanding in its perfection, but no more. Its middle contains all singulars, so that only a predicate belonging concretely to the subject can be attached to it. Yet allness gathers the singular into universality only externally. Its major premise already presupposes the conclusion, since one can assert of all only if the singular case is already included, and so it points to induction.
