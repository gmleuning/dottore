# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External vision is the clairvoyant awareness of an external circumstance removed from the senses by distance in space or in time. The soul is immersed in the vision of a single outward event, as when someone sees an absent relative far away. Such seeing is possible because the soul is not subject to the externality of space, but it is no higher knowledge: it may be more obscure or clearer, may mix what is correct with error, and belonged more to times of a more soulful life than to the modern independence of the intellect.
