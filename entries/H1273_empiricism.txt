# 1273 Empiricism

G: Empirismus
Gloss: Philosophy that takes experience through the senses as the source of knowledge.
HGloss: Empiricism, which takes experience as the source of knowledge.

Phr:
  nihil est in intellectu quod non prius fuerit in sensu [Lat.]
  nothing in the intellect that was not first in the senses
  experience is the teacher

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The philosophy that rests knowledge on experience, still that of the English and the French, holds that what is true, what we know, rests upon observation and its analysis according to general characteristics drawn from it. This is a metaphysical empiricism, the procedure of the ordinary sciences, opposed in method to Spinoza, who begins from definitions.
