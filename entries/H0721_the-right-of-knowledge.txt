# 721 The Right of Knowledge
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality › α. Purpose and Responsibility
G: Das Recht des Wissens
Gloss: The will's right to own as its deed only what it knew of the circumstances in which it acted.
HGloss: Das Recht des Wissens: the will has the right to recognize as its action only what it knew of the presupposed circumstances; Oedipus, who killed his father unknowing, cannot be charged with parricide (§117).
