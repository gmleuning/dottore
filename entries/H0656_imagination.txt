# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Imagination is the second stage of representation, where intelligence works freely on the images it possesses and the opposition arises between the represented and the intuited content. It first reproduces images from its inwardness, then associates them and raises them to universal representations, and finally, as phantasy, gives its universals a pictorial reality in symbol and sign, so forming the transition to memory.
