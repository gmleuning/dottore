# 118 Dissolution of Appearance
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › β. Appearance Proper
G: Auflösung der Erscheinung
Gloss: The collapse of the contrast between the apparent and the real world into a relation between them.
HGloss: The two worlds each proving to be the essence of the other, so that appearance passes into essential relation.

  the collapse of appearance, the sublation of the two worlds, the passage into relation, the identity of the opposed worlds
