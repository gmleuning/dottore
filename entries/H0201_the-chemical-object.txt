# 201 The Chemical Object
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › β. Chemism
G: Das chemische Objekt
Gloss: A thing whose nature lies in its affinity for another.
HGloss: An object which is, in its own being, the contradiction of its one-sidedness with its whole nature, and strives to sublate it.

  the chemically tensed object, the biased object, the non-indifferent object, the affine, the one-sided object
  oxygen, hydrogen, nitrogen, carbon, carbon dioxide, dioxide, sodium, calcium, potassium, aluminium, aluminum, platinum, chrome, chromium, silicon, nickel, uranium, plutonium, mercury, plastic, polymer, molecule, compound, Amberlite†, cauterizer†, apozem†, moxa†, catheretic†
  silver, gold, copper, iron, lead, tin, zinc
  reactivity

  tend, strive toward, attract, seek its other

  chemical, affine, tensed, biased, non-indifferent, disquiparant†
