# 201 The Chemical Object
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity › β. Chemism
G: Das chemische Objekt
Gloss: A thing whose nature lies in its affinity for another.
HGloss: An object which is, in its own being, the contradiction of its one-sidedness with its whole nature, and strives to sublate it.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The chemical object differs from the mechanical in that its determinateness, and with it its relation to another and the manner of that relation, belong to its nature. Implicitly the whole concept, yet existing one-sidedly, it cannot be understood from itself, since the being of one such object is the being of another. It is thus a contradiction between its immediate existence and its concept, which gives it the necessity and drive to sublate its one-sidedness in a real whole.
