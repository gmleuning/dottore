# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Observing reason is consciousness that again takes up meaning something and perceiving, but now with the certainty that it is itself the other. It makes its own observations and sets out to know the truth, seeking to find in what is a thing a concept, and so to have in thinghood only the consciousness of itself. It therefore takes a universal interest in the world, certain that the present is rational. It turns first to nature, then to self-consciousness, and then to the relation of self-consciousness to its immediate actuality.
