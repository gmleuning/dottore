# 1236 The Early Stoa
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 1. Dogmatism › a. Stoic Philosophy
G: Die ältere Stoa
Gloss: The first Stoics at Athens, Zeno, Cleanthes and Chrysippus, who built the system of logic, physics and ethics.
HGloss: The older Stoa of Zeno, Cleanthes and Chrysippus, whose criterion is the grasping representation and whose physics is the rational world-fire.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The founder of the Stoic school is Zeno of Citium, whose philosophy takes its name from the portico where he taught, who sought to unify philosophy into a whole, and who was known for the strictness of his ethics. Cleanthes was his successor in the Stoa, and Chrysippus, distinguished for logic and dialectic, spread the Stoic philosophy widely.
