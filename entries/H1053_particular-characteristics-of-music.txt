# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The particular characteristics of music concern the means by which notes become not a natural shriek of feeling but its developed artistic expression. A single note is independent and complete, gaining its own definiteness only through relation to others, a relation external to it, made by a composer for a hearer. These means are the measure of time, harmony and melody.
