# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conscience, the beautiful soul, evil and its forgiveness form the last shape of spirit certain of itself. The self of conscience, immediately certain of itself as truth and being, no longer places moral validity in another being but knows its own conviction as duty and has its content within its certainty. Its freedom splits into the beautiful soul that will not act and the acting conscience that the judging consciousness calls evil, until confession and forgiveness let each recognize itself in the other, which is absolute spirit.
