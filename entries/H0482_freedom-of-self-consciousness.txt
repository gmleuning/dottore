# 482 Freedom of Self-Consciousness
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › β. Self-Consciousness › b. Recognitive Self-Consciousness
G: Freiheit des Selbstbewusstseins
Gloss: Self-awareness that finds freedom in thought, by withdrawing inward from the world.
HGloss: Self-consciousness free in thought, withdrawn from the world into itself: stoicism, skepticism, the unhappy consciousness.
