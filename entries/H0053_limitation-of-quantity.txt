# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The limitation of quantity arises in discrete magnitude, which has the one for its principle, is a plurality of ones, and is essentially continuous. Posited as one magnitude, it has the one as its determinateness, which excludes and so limits. This limit encloses the many ones and also refers to itself. Yet the being so limited is continuity, which passes beyond the limit and is indifferent to it. Real quantity as an existence and a something is therefore quantum, and in this the difference between continuous and discrete magnitude no longer matters.
