# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Wood is one of the oldest materials for images of the gods, which began as a post with a head set on it, and it was still used in the time of Phidias, gilded or joined with marble. On the whole, unless gilded or overlaid, its grain makes it unsuited to grand works and better for small ones, as in the Middle Ages.
