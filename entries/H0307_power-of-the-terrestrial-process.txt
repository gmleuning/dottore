# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The powers behind the earth's process of formation, which nature leaves behind as self-subsistences beyond the earth, are its connection with the solar system and position within it, its solar, lunar and cometary life, the inclination of its axis, and the magnetic axis. Unlike an animal, whose powers lie within it, the earth merely occupies its place.
