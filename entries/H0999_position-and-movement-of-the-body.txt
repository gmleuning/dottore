# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Position and movement of the body make trunk and limbs, beautiful sensuously but not directly expressive like the face, give a glimpse of spirit, so that spirit appears poured over the whole figure. It appears through position freely determined from within, movement or repose, and the situation shown. Upright posture, an act of will by which a person tears loose from the ground, already expresses spirit.
