# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Breathing is a spontaneously interrupted continuity: exhaling and inhaling volatilize the blood, a transition into air begun and drawn back. The blood is an absolute thirst, an unrest within and against itself that craves to be ignited; air is the fiery, negative element in which it satisfies itself and becomes for itself.
