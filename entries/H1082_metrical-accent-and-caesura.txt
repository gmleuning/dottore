# 1082 Metrical Accent and Caesura
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse › 2. Accent
G: Versakzent und Zäsur
Gloss: The stress that the metre itself places, and the pause that divides the line.
HGloss: The verse-accent on particular syllables of the foot, and the caesura, which cuts across the feet and keeps the line from falling apart into its units.

N:
  metrical accent and caesura; verse accent, metrical stress, diaeresis, pause, break, masculine caesura, feminine caesura
  caesura, ictus, arsis, thesis

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Metrical accent and caesura enliven verse where feet must not coincide with words: a word running past the end of a foot links rhythms, and the pause at its end with the verse accent becomes a deliberate interval. The caesura, required in every line, breaks the cold monotony and brings connection and life into the flow.
