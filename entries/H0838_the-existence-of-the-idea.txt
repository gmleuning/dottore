# 838 The Existence of the Idea

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The idea in existence means that everything existent has truth only as an existence of the idea, which alone is genuinely actual. A thing is not true merely because it exists inwardly or outwardly, but because its reality corresponds to the concept. Truth here is not agreement between an object and my representations, but the objective realization of the concept in a self, object, action or situation; otherwise the existent is mere appearance.
