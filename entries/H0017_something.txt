# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Something is existence that has sublated the distinction of reality and negation and so is again equal to itself, no longer immediately but through that sublating. It is the first negation of the negation, simple existent self-reference. It therefore mediates itself with itself, though only abstractly, and is the beginning of the subject, which determines itself further as being-for-itself and finally as concept. As becoming that has become concrete, something is a transition between somethings, and so it alters.
