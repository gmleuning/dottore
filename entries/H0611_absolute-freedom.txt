G: Die absolute Freiheit
Gloss: The freedom of the universal will that recognizes no institution or estate outside itself.
HGloss: Die absolute Freiheit: self-consciousness that knows itself as the universal will, for which the world is simply its will, and which tolerates no particular sphere of work.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute freedom is the self-consciousness whose certainty of itself is the essence of the whole real and supersensible world. Its will is no empty idea of tacit or represented consent but the real universal will of all singular individuals, so that each, undivided from the whole, always does everything. All separate social estates are erased, and because it will not let anything become a free-standing object, it can arrive at no positive work, law or institution; where the self is only represented, it is not actual.
