G: Die absolute Freiheit
Gloss: The freedom of the universal will that recognizes no institution or estate outside itself.
HGloss: Die absolute Freiheit: self-consciousness that knows itself as the universal will, for which the world is simply its will, and which tolerates no particular sphere of work.
