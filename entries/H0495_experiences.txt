# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Experiences are what the single sensory observations from which perception starts become when they are considered in their connection, reflected upon, and turned into something necessary and universal according to definite categories. Perception does not stop at seeing and hearing but relates what is sensed to a universal that is not immediately observable. Yet its demonstrations rest on presuppositions that themselves need demonstrating, so that experience runs on to infinity; philosophy must rise to proof of the necessity of things.
