G: Sokrates
Gloss: The thinker who made inner reflection the judge of truth and goodness, seeking through questioning the general definitions behind moral words; the turn from received custom to examined conviction.
HGloss: Socrates, whose principle is that the self must find truth by its own thinking; the good as the end.
