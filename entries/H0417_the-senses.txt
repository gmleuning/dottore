# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The senses form the simple system of the specific bodily functions through which the sentient soul is determined from outside. Each is not tied to some single item but is a universal possibility of being determined, a whole circle of sensations. The five reduce to three classes answering to the moments of the concept: sight and hearing, the senses of physical ideality; smell and taste, the senses of real difference; and feeling, the sense of earthly totality. Around the centre of the sentient individual they arrange the distinctions of natural corporeality more simply.
