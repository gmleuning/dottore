# 880 Authenticity
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 3. Authorship › a. Mere Manner
G: Die echtere Manier
Gloss: A manner that has widened beyond private habit to keep to the nature of the matter, like Goethe's or Horace's light turn at the end of a serious poem.
HGloss: Die ächtere Manier: a manner that lifts itself out of narrow particularity and holds to the nature of the matter in a more general way, as Goethe and Horace end serious beginnings with a cheerful turn.

N:
  authenticity; the truer manner, a personal yet general manner, the light touch, the deft turn, a cheerful ending, conversational ease
  authenticity

Adj:
  authentic, genuine, natural, unforced

Phr:
  die ächtere Manier [Ger.] [Hegel, Aesthetics]

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Authentic or more genuine manner frees itself from restricted idiosyncrasy and broadens so that special modes of treatment cannot harden into habit, the artist holding in a general way to the nature of the things treated. Goethe's knack of ending even serious pieces with a happy turn, and Horace's way in the Epistles, are manner of this wider kind, which paves the way to style.
