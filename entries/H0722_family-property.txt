# 722 Family Property
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › a. The System of Needs › iii. Division of the General Resources › 1. The Substantial (Agricultural) Estate
G: Familienvermögen
Gloss: Land held by a family across generations: the entailed estate, primogeniture, feudal tenure and the landed nobility.
HGloss: The estate that cannot be sold or divided, bound to the family by entail (Majorat); it frees the landed nobility for political life and sends it to the upper house (§306-307).
