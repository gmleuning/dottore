# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Real measure is measure determined as a connection of the measures that make up the quality of distinct self-subsisting somethings, or things: no longer abstract qualities such as space and time, but specific gravity and chemical properties. Such an independent measure relates to others and specifies them, forming a series of measures. Some of these relations are exclusive elective affinities, which form a nodal line of self-subsisting things, and out of the progression there emerges the measureless, in which anything self-subsisting stands in negative relation to itself.
