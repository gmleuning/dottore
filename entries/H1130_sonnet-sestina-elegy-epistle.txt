G: Sonett, Sestine, Elegie, Epistel
Gloss: The reflective song forms: sonnet, sestina, elegy, epistle.
HGloss: Forms in which reflection enters the song: the sonnet, sestina, elegy and verse epistle.

N:
  sonnet, sestina, elegy, epistle, villanelle, epigram, epithalamium, madrigal, idyl, eclogue, pastoral, romance, occasional poem

Adj:
  epigrammatic

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sonnets, sestinas, elegies and epistles form a group that already rises above song: immediacy of feeling is raised to the communication of reflection and meditation that looks about in every direction and gathers particular insights under general categories. Knowledge and culture prevail, and although the individual still joins universal and particular, a wider view is taken.
