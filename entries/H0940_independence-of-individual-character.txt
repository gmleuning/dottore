# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The independence of individual character is the particular subject, a whole and individual person, whose world is restricted and accidental and who rests not on a substantial, justified pathos but formally on mere subjectivity. It appears in two forms: energetic firmness that puts the whole power of a one-sided individuality into specific ends, and an inward totality that cannot unfold and express itself.
