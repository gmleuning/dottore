# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Insects are invertebrates developed in the opposite direction from molluscs: their organs of movement are far superior, with feet and wings and head, thorax and abdomen quite distinct, while the inner structure is correspondingly less developed, respiration pervading the body and the blood system hardly distinguishable from the digestive.
