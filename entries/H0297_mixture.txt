# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Mixture is the merely formal process, to be set aside first: a combination of different but unopposed bodies which need no middle term, since their common nature determines their relation. Their combining or separating is immediate and their properties are preserved, as in alloys of metals or mixtures of acids, or of alcohol with water.
