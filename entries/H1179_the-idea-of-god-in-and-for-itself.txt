# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The first element considers God in the eternal idea, in and for himself, apart from the creation of the world, within the element of thought. This idea is expressed as the holy Trinity. Spirit is a life of self-differentiation in which the one distinguished from God is yet God's entire idea, so that the distinction is sublated and the one remains present to itself in the other. In sensible terms this is eternal love.
