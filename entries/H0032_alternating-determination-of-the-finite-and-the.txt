# 32 Alternating Determination of the Finite and the Infinite

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Taken immediately, as the non-being of an other, the infinite falls back into something determinate standing over against the finite. This is the bad infinite of the understanding, a beyond, empty and unattainable, which is limited by the finite and so itself finite. Each posits the other outside itself and each emerges only in the other. Their alternating determination runs on as the infinite progress: a limit is crossed, a new one arises, and so without end. It is the perpetual ought, a contradiction never resolved but only pronounced.

  This bad infinite is in itself the same as the perpetual ought; it is indeed the negation of the finite, but in truth it is unable to free itself from it; the finite constantly resurfaces in it as its other, since this infinite only is with reference to the finite, which is its other. The progress to infinity is therefore only repetitious monotony, the one and the same tedious alternation of this finite and infinite.
