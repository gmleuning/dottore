# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Conviction is the principle that whatever the subject holds to be right is right, so that its own conviction decides the ethical character of an action. Since the good willed is still without content, this makes the subject solely responsible for subsuming an action under the good, and abolishes all objective right and duty.
