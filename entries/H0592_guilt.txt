# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Guilt arises because the ethical essence is the unity of both laws while the deed carries out only one. In fulfilling one law, action calls up the other as a violated and hostile essence demanding revenge. Only one side of the decision lies in the light; actuality keeps hidden the other, which comes forth after the deed has been done. The agent can deny neither the crime nor the guilt, for the deed joins the conscious to the unconscious and one's own to what is alien.
