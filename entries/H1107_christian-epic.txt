G: Das christliche Epos
Gloss: The epic of Christian faith, above all Dante's Divine Comedy.
HGloss: The Christian epic, whose world is the eternal action of divine justice and love, its great example Dante's Divine Comedy.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Christian epic has its richest work, the greatest of the Catholic Middle Ages, in Dante's Divine Comedy. Instead of a particular event its subject is the eternal action, the love of God, set in Hell, Purgatory and Paradise, into whose changeless existence the living world of human deeds and fates is plunged and judged, individuals standing solidified for ever like images of bronze.
