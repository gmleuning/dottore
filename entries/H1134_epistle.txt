G: Die Epistel
Gloss: The poem in the form of a letter, addressed to a friend and free to reflect and advise.
HGloss: Die Epistel: the verse letter, a lyric form in which reflection and instruction mingle with personal address.

N:
  epistle
  verse epistle
  letter in verse, Horace's Epistles, epistolary poem, heroic epistle

Adj:
  epistolary

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The epistle belongs to the group of reflective lyric forms with sonnet, sestina and elegy, in which the immediacy of song is raised to reflective communication. The individual still dominates in linking universal and particular but takes a more general and wider view than in the song proper, with knowledge and culture prevailing.
