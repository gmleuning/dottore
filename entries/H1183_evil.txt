# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Evil belongs to the natural human being, for natural being without consciousness and will of its own is what ought not to be, so that even so-called innocence is declared evil before the pure unity known as absolute truth. Evil taken by itself is an abstraction: it exists only in contrast with the good. It becomes infinite only where the good is known as the one, purely spiritual God.
