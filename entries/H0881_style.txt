# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Style, beyond the idiosyncrasy expressed in turns of phrase, means a mode of representation that complies with the conditions of its material and with the demands and laws arising from the nature of a given kind of art, as church music differs from opera. A defect of style is either inability to make such a necessary mode one's own or caprice that puts a bad mannerism in its place.
