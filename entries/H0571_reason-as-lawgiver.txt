# 571 Reason as Lawgiver

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reason as lawgiver is the consciousness for which the matter in hand has become ethical substance, valid in and for itself, and which supposes that it knows immediately what is right and good and can state it in commands, such as that one should love one's neighbour as oneself. On examination such laws have no universal content: whether intelligent beneficence can happen or does good is contingent. They remain at the ought without actuality; they are not laws but only commands.
