# ---- Hegel ----
Def: At this second stage of the ethnic religions the spiritual raises itself above the natural, so that the simple blending of the two ceases. Free subjectivity attains lordship over the finite, and the natural serves only to manifest spirit. In the religion of beauty the natural is transfigured into a sign of spirit; in the religion of sublimity it is not so taken up.
