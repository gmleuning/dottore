G: Edelsteine und Glas
Gloss: Carving in gems and the shaping of glass.
HGloss: Gems and glass, sculpture on the smallest scale, cut stones and cameos.

Adv:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Precious stones and glass complete the materials. Greek gems, cameos and pastes are beyond price, repeating in the smallest compass the whole range of sculpture in supreme perfection. Their topics were drawn not from the artists' own invention but, apart from Bacchic scenes and dances, from myths and traditions; allegory belongs only to later art.
