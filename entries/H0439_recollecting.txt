# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Recollecting is the first content of clairvoyant awareness: the soul becomes aware of a content it had long forgotten and can no longer bring into consciousness when awake. In illness or in the magnetic state people speak a language learned in early youth and since lost, or recite fluently something read long before and never learned by rote. The soul enclosed in its inwardness thus only makes present to itself again what already belongs to it.
