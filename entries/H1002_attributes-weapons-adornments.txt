# 1002 Attributes, Weapons, Adornments
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › ii. The Ideal of Sculpture › 3. Individuality of the Ideal Sculptured Figure
G: Attribute, Waffen, Schmuck
Gloss: The emblems and ornaments by which a sculpted figure is identified.
HGloss: The attributes, weapons and adornments by which the individual gods are known.

F:
  French: bijouterie

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Attributes, weapons and adornment are external accessories that great sculpture keeps simple, moderate and limited, so that nothing is there beyond what lets the work be understood, since the figure and its expression provide the spiritual meaning. Such marks are nonetheless needed to recognize gods, whose common divinity makes them closely akin.
