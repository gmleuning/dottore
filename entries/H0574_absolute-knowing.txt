# 574 Absolute Knowing
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason [Tetrad]
G: Das absolute Wissen
Gloss: Knowledge in which the mind fully understands itself, with nothing left opaque or outside it.

N:
  absolute knowing; absolute knowledge, comprehension, philosophical science, self-knowing spirit, the end of the journey, the standpoint of science, total self-transparency, wisdom
  complete self-transparency, the unity of substance and subject, the final shape

V:
  know absolutely, comprehend, grasp completely, arrive, conclude
  know itself, comprehend itself, see through itself, close the circle

Adj:
  absolute, complete, self-transparent, comprehensive, final
  self-knowing

Adv:
  absolutely, completely, at last
  transparently, without remainder

F:
  German: das absolute Wissen

  Arabic: ʿilm al-yaqīn [knowledge of certainty], ḥaqq al-yaqīn [truth of certainty]
