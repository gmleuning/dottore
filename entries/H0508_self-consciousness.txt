# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Self-consciousness is the certainty that is equal to its truth: consciousness is its own object, and the otherness it distinguishes is for it no difference at all. With it we enter the native realm of truth. Yet it is not the motionless tautology of "I am I", for it is a return out of otherness, preserving the sensuous world as appearance whose essence is its own unity with itself. This unity must become essential to it, so it is desire; it finds satisfaction only in another self-consciousness, and so the concept of spirit appears.
