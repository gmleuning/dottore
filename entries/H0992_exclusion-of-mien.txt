# 992 Exclusion of Mien
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › i. Principle of Sculpture › 2. Beautiful Sculptural Form
G: Ausschluss des Mienenspiels
Gloss: The leaving aside of fleeting facial expression in sculpture.
HGloss: The exclusion of fleeting expression; the sculptural figure has no glance, no momentary mien.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Exclusion of mien keeps sculpture free of the accidents of personality: the artist may not present facial expression that shows the subject's own inner life and momentary feeling, such as pride, envy or scorn, nor the fleeting smile or flash of an eye. Those changes suit painting; sculpture must reproduce the permanent features of spiritual expression in face and posture.
