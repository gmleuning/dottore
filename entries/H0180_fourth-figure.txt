# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The fourth, or mathematical, figure is the syllogism that results when all quality is abstracted from the terms: if two things are equal to a third, they are equal to each other. Here there is no longer any relation of inherence or subsumption, and each term can stand as middle. Its evidence rests on this poverty of thought. Its positive result is that mediation must rest on reflection, that is, on a concreteness of the terms.
