# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Virtue and the way of the world is the shape in which consciousness takes the law to be essential and individuality to be what must be sacrificed, both in itself, by discipline, and in the way of the world, which makes individuality the essence and subordinates the good to itself. Virtue wants to bring the good into existence by giving up individuality, but individuality is the very activity that makes the in-itself actual. The activity of individuality proves an end in itself.
