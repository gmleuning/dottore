# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The end is the result in which the method returns to its beginning, restoring the first immediacy through sublated determinateness, as a system of totality. Each result is a new beginning, so that cognition rolls on from content to content, growing richer and more concrete. By the nature of the method science is a circle of circles, whose links are the single sciences; and logic has returned in the absolute idea to the simple unity of being with which it began.
