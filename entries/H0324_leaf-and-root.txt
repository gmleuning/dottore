# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Leaf and root are the first division in the plant's formation, a simultaneous outward separation directly related to its process with the outer world, the root in reciprocal relation with the earth and the leaf with air and light. A second, more intimate division is the plant's own inner separation into woody fibres and vessels.
