# 188 Disjunctive Syllogism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › c. Syllogism of Necessity
G: Disjunktiver Schluss
Gloss: Inference through a complete set of alternatives: A is B, C or D; it is neither B nor C; therefore D.
HGloss: The middle is the genus as the totality of its exclusive species, the mediating and the mediated in one: the syllogism completed.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The disjunctive syllogism has as middle a universality full of form, the genus sorted out into its species, which includes the particular and excludes the species from one another: A is either B or C or D; but A is B; therefore it is neither C nor D. Mediator and mediated are the same A, so that it is the truth of the hypothetical syllogism and no longer a syllogism at all. With it the formalism and subjectivity of inference sublate themselves.
