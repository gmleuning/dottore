# ---- Hegel ----
Def: External reflection is reflection that presupposes itself as sublated, and is therefore doubled: on one side an immediate that is presupposed, on the other a reflection referring negatively to it. It finds its presupposition already there, starts from it and returns into itself by negating it, and the determinations it posits in the immediate remain external to it. On closer view, however, in negating this immediate it also posits it, so that the immediate is not only in itself but posited as the same as reflection.
