# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Finitude as such is the quality of something whose limit is immanent in it, so that its negation constitutes its very being-in-itself. To call things finite is to say not only that they are determinate and limited, but that non-being constitutes their nature. They are, but the truth of this being is their end. Finitude is qualitative negation driven to the extreme and fixed in itself, the most obstinate category of the understanding, which makes the transitoriness of things imperishable and absolute and will not let the finite pass into its affirmative, the infinite.
