# 229 Matter (Substrate of Place and Motion)
# Nature › 1. Mechanics › I. Mathematical Mechanics (Space and Time) › γ. Place and Motion (Union of Space and Time)
G: Materie
Gloss: The stable stuff that fills a place and moves; space and time united and made real, and by its nature heavy.
HGloss: The identity of space and time, of place and motion, as an immediate existent unity: matter, which has the concept outside it, in gravity.
