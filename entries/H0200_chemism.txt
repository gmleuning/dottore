# 200 Chemism
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › II. Objectivity
G: Chemismus
Gloss: The relation of things defined by their attraction to one another, which combine and change in combining, like chemical substances.
HGloss: Objects whose determinateness is their relation to their other, which they strive to complete.
