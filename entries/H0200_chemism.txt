G: Chemismus

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Chemism is the moment of judgement in objectivity, of difference become objective, and of process. Unlike mechanism it begins from objects whose determinateness and relation to others belong to their very nature. The name is not confined to chemistry proper: meteorological processes fall under it, as do the sex relation among living things and, as their formal basis, the spiritual relations of love and friendship.
