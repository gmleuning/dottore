# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Positive retention of negated features means that despite the victory of the new gods the old ones remain in classical art, partly as before and partly transformed and still venerated. Unlike the jealous national God of the Jews, the Greek gods are particular divinities amid a group of others. The Mysteries were the first form in which the Greeks preserved the old gods, though no deep wisdom seems hidden in them.
