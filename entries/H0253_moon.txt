# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The moon is the body of rigidity, subservient to the planet whose satellite it is and in which it has its axis. It lacks atmosphere, water and meteorological process, showing only conical mountains and craters and the combustion of its own rigidity. Hegel calls it the waterless crystal, the earth's hard interior, which strives as it were to unite with our sea and so produces the tides.
