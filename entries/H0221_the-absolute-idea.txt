# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The absolute idea is the identity of the theoretical and the practical idea: the rational concept that in its reality rejoins only itself, free subjective concept with personality that is at the same time universality and cognition, with its other as its object. It alone is being, imperishable life and self-knowing truth. It is the sole content of philosophy, which shares that content with art and religion but apprehends it in the highest way, through the concept.
