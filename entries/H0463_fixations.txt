# 463 Fixations
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology) › β. The Feeling Soul › b. Self-Feeling › ii. Derangement › 2. Madness Proper
G: Die fixe Vorstellung
Gloss: Madness fixed on one idea, which comes either from a particular passion or from something contingent.
HGloss: Madness with an individualized content, where the fixed idea is drawn either from a particular passion (to be God or king) or from something contingent (to be a barleycorn or a dog).

N:
  fixations; fixed ideas, idées fixes, individualized delusions, delusional content
  fixed idea, monomania, obsession, fixation

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Fixations are the forms of madness with an individualized content, which, unlike the indeterminate disgust that kills all vitality, are coupled with lively interest and even passion. The content depends partly on the particular passion from which the madness sprang, as with those who take themselves to be God or a king, and partly on something contingent, as with those who suppose themselves a barleycorn or a dog. In either case the sufferer has no determinate awareness of the contradiction between the fixed idea and what is objective.
