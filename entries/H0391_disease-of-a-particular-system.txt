# 391 Disease of a Particular System
# Nature › 3. Organics › III. Animal Nature › γ. The Animal's Genus Process › c. Genus and Individual (Disease and Death) › i. Nosology
G: Krankheit durch besondere äußere Schädlichkeiten
Gloss: Disease that starts in one organ or system and sets it against the rest of the body.
HGloss: The second kind of disease, where a particular system (the lungs, the liver, the heart, the gut) isolates itself and asserts its own activity against the whole; the disease is the organism divided against itself.
