# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Subjective spirit is mind in the form of relation to itself, having the ideal totality of the idea arise within it, still in its undeveloped concept. It is mind as cognitive, developing in three forms: immediate, as the soul or natural mind treated by anthropology; for itself or mediated, as consciousness, treated by phenomenology; and determining itself within itself, as a subject for itself, treated by psychology.
