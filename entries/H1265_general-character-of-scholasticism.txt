# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In its general character Scholasticism is the philosophy of the European Middle Ages, arising among new peoples who built on the ruins of an older world and received from outside an alien spiritual principle. The age is marked by duality and antithesis, which it must take into itself and overcome, as spirit makes the natural into its presupposition and works upon it.
