# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A fable, in Aesop's original form, represents a relation or occurrence in nature, especially among animals, taken as it truly is and not invented at whim, so told that a general lesson for human life, chiefly its practical side, can be drawn from it. It is no longer a religious reading of nature as revealing the divine will but an everyday event yielding a maxim, warning or prudential rule.
