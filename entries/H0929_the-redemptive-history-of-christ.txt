# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The redemptive history of Christ presents spirit's reconciliation with itself as God's appearance in the world: an individual man is God and God an individual man, so that in Christ every individual beholds their own reconciliation with God as actual. Since this unity is no immediate coalescence, the process of spirit must appear as this man's history: suffering, death, resurrection through death's grief, and presence as spirit in the church.
