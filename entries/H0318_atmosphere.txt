# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The atmosphere is the first determinate life of the organic earth, air as the universal element generated in the earth's own process with the sea. It is the earth in dissolution and pure tension, the relation of gravity and heat, running through yearly, monthly and daily periods; it vivifies the earth without being its life-process.
