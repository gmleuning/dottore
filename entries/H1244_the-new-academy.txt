# 1244 The New Academy
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › ii. Dogmatism and Skepticism › 2. Skepticism
G: Die neuere Akademie
Gloss: The Platonic Academy under Arcesilaus and Carneades, which denied certain knowledge and lived by the probable.
HGloss: Die neuere Akademie: Arcesilaus and Carneades, who oppose the Stoic criterion and allow only the probable, a subjective conviction.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The New Academy, a continuation of the Platonic Academy opposed to dogmatism, confronted Stoicism and Epicureanism and stands very close to Skepticism; Arcesilaus begins the Middle Academy and Carneades the New. Carneades holds that criteria for the conduct of life are to be sought, but that they are only convincing representations, something subjective.
