# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The coastal part of the empire, with rich trading cities such as Tyre and Sidon, whose Phoenician seafarers introduce a new principle: industry, in which human will, courage and intelligence work upon nature in place of dependence on its bounty. Beside the worship of Hercules stands that of Adonis, a funeral festival lamenting the departed god.
