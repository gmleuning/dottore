# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Intuition is the first stage of intelligence, in which mind finds its material immediately within itself, attends to it and puts it outside itself in space and time. It runs from feeling through attention to intuition proper. Unlike sensory consciousness, which clings to the immediate single object broken into many aspects, intuition is a consciousness filled with the certainty of reason, whose object is a totality, a coherent fullness of determinations.
