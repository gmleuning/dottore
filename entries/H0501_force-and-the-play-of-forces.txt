# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Force is the movement in which self-sufficient matters spread out into their being, which is its expression, and are driven back again into a unity, which is force proper. These are not two separate things: the force driven back must express itself, and in expressing it remains within itself. First only a concept of the understanding, force becomes actual by doubling into two forces that solicit and are solicited, each of which is only through the other. So the realization of force is at once the loss of its reality: what proves to be its essence is a universal, the inner of things.
