# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Quantity is sublated being-for-itself: the one that behaved only negatively toward the others has gone over into connection with them, and its absolute hardness has melted into a unity that still contains repulsion. As a determinateness indifferent to being, it is first pure quantity, with no determinateness in it; then quantum, determinacy that is only external and passes into infinite progress; and last quantitative ratio. Its moments are continuity and discreteness, and quantity is their unity.
