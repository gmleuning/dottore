# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Irony is the supreme form of this subjectivity: it knows itself as the master of every ethical content, which it may accept or set aside at will. It acknowledges law and duty, yet holds itself above them as the one who decides. The name comes from Plato, but Socrates used irony against conceit, not against the Idea.
