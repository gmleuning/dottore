# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Arabs soon turned to the sciences and became acquainted with Greek philosophy chiefly through the Syrians. Their philosophy constitutes no proper principle or stage of its own in the development of philosophy; they chiefly translated Aristotle and commented on him. Historically it is important as a main channel through which Aristotle became known in the West.
