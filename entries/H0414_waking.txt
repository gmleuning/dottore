# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Waking is the soul's being-for-self set against its mere being: the individual soul's own act of distinguishing itself from its still undifferentiated universality. It includes in general all self-conscious and rational activity of mind in its distinguishing. Taken concretely, to be awake is to behave as an I and as intellect, before which intuition stands as a concrete whole of determinations, each holding its place by and with all the others, so that every part of the content is confirmed by its interconnection with the rest.
