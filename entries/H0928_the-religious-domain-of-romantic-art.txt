# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The religious domain of romantic art has for its substantial content the reconciliation of God with the world and so with himself, spirit's union with its essence and the satisfaction of the heart, so that the ideal seems at home again, though in a form quite unlike the classical, which could not admit self-division, grief, sacrifice and renunciation, nor a world of evil to be reconciled.
