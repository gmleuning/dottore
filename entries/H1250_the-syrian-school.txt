# 1250 The Syrian School
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy › b. Greek Philosophy › iii. Neoplatonism
G: Die syrische Schule
Gloss: The school of Iamblichus in Syria, which joined Neoplatonism to theurgy and the rites of the old gods.
HGloss: The Syrian school of Iamblichus, in which Neoplatonism turns to theurgy and multiplies the orders of the divine.

N:
  the Syrian school
  Iamblichus, theurgy
  Apamea, On the Mysteries, ritual, the emperor Julian

Adj:
  Iamblichean

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Iamblichus is mentioned only in passing in these lectures, among those whose biographies mingle the details of their lives with a host of miracles. Theurgy, which crops up frequently in the Neoplatonists, means making a god: certain symbols, principally names and images, are used for the sake of illumination.
