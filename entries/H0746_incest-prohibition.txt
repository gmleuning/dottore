# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The prohibition of incest follows from marriage as the free surrender of two personalities unique in each. Marriage ought not to be concluded within the naturally identical circle of kin, who have no distinct personality in relation to one another; since marriage is a free ethical act, the marriage of blood relations runs counter to its concept.
