# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Taste is the second of the senses of real difference, the sense of the concrete process. It relates to the real dissolution of a body and to the chemical determinacies that emerge in that process: the sweet, the bitter, the alkaline, the acid and the salt. It therefore requires direct contact with its object.
