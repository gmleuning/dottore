G: Epoptische Farbe
Gloss: Color produced at the surface of a thin film or plate, as on a soap bubble.
HGloss: Colour formed on the surface of thin plates and films, by their mutual pressure.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Epoptic colours, allied to the entoptic, have a mechanical origin: when a lens is pressed on a glass plate the point of contact is first black and then spreads into rings of different colours. Here merely mechanical pressure produces the colour, and pressure is nothing but an alteration of cohesion in the nearest parts.
