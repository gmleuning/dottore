# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Determinate religions are the particular and therefore finite religions, in which the concept of God first takes on determinations. As God is defined, so human beings define themselves. They are definite stages of the consciousness of spirit and necessary conditions for the emergence of the true religion. For that reason they also exist historically, successively in time and side by side in space.
