# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The methods of magnetizing vary. The magnetizer usually works by contact, and must have an uncompromising will to communicate this power and to join the two spheres of animal life into one. Most often the operation is by stroking, with or without actual touch, from the head toward the pit of the stomach and the extremities, but laying on the hand, a pressure, even a glance and the invitation to sleep may suffice. Vessels with iron rods and other material means are also used.
