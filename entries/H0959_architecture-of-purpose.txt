# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Architecture of purpose is building that serves an end outside itself, the form proper to classical art. It may start from symbolic works or from need: need supplies purely purposeful forms of straight line, right angle and level surface, and above all demands an enclosure for the image of a god, whose surroundings must likewise be produced by art. These useful forms must then rise to beauty.
