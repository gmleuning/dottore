# 421 Smell and Taste

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Smell and taste are the senses of real difference, the second class. They enter into relation with real corporeality, not yet as it persists on its own and resists, but only as it is dissolving and entering into process, the process by which bodies consume themselves by their own nature. They differ in that smell receives a body in the abstract, simple and indeterminate process of dissipation or evaporation, whereas taste is related to its real, concrete process and the chemical qualities that emerge in it.
