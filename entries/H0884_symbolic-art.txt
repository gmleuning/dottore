# 884 Symbolic Art
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art
G: Die symbolische Kunstform
Gloss: Art in which meaning and shape do not yet correspond; art that hints rather than shows.
HGloss: Art in which the idea is still seeking its true expression, so that shape and meaning do not correspond; the symbol.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Symbolic art is the beginning of art, in nature and history, only its threshold, belonging especially to the East. Its symbol is an external existence given to contemplation which is to be understood in a wider and more universal sense, so that meaning and expression are distinct. Since the idea is still indeterminate and measureless it finds no wholly corresponding form and transcends its appearance, which gives it the character of sublimity.
