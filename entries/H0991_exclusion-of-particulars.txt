# 991 Exclusion of Particulars
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › b. Sculpture › i. Principle of Sculpture › 2. Beautiful Sculptural Form
G: Ausschluss des Partikularen
Gloss: The leaving aside of accidental detail in sculpture.
HGloss: The exclusion of the particular, the contingent and the merely natural from the sculptural figure.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Exclusion of particulars follows from sculpture's content: it investigates only how spirit, substance and individual at once, takes on a body, so that accidental particularity of bodily or spiritual appearance is excluded. It presents only the permanent, universal and regular in the human form, even while this universal must be individualized into a figure closely fused with it, not mere abstract regularity.
