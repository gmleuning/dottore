# 200 The Living Individual
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › III. Idea › α. Life
G: Das lebendige Individuum
Gloss: A single living creature, whose parts are organs serving the whole.
HGloss: The organism, a subject which is its own end and means, whose members are only moments of the whole.
