# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dramatic work of art must be more tightly concentrated than epic, its characters entering such opposition that their interconnection is their existence in the play. Of the unities of place, time and action only that of action is essential. Its division into acts and scenes, its scope and progress differ from epic, and the inner spirit finds expression chiefly in poetic diction.
