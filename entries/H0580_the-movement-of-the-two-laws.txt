# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The movement of the two laws unfolds the differences within each, through which they engage one another and pass into each other. The polity has its liveliness in government, which lets the whole spread into self-sufficient parts and gathers them again, and has the proof of its power in the divine law and the netherworld. The divine law, within the family, moves through the relations of husband and wife, parents and children, and brother and sister, from which the individual passes over into the human law.
