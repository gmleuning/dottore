# 199 Life
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › III. Idea
G: Leben
Gloss: Thought present as a living being, in which an organizing principle is at work in every part of a body.

N:
  life; vitality, liveliness, animation, vigour, vivacity, spirit, vital force, life-force, life principle, entelechy; organism, organization, living being, creature; self-organization, autopoiesis, self-maintenance
  viability, vital spark, vital flame, soul
  protoplasm
  activity, energy
  the immediate idea

V:
  live, be alive, breathe, exist, subsist, survive, thrive, flourish, animate, enliven, vivify, quicken, invigorate
  respire, subsist &c H0004, walk the earth, strut and fret one's hour upon the stage [Macbeth], be spared

Adj:
  living, alive, live, animate, vital, vivacious, lively, organic, biological, animated, quick, sentient, breathing, self-organizing
  in life, in the land of the living, on this side of the grave, above ground, animative†, lively &c H0375, all alive and kicking, tenacious of life, full of life, yeasty
  active

Adv:
  alive and well, alive and kicking, vitally, organically, in the flesh

F:
  German: Leben
  Latin: vita, vis vitalis, anima
  Greek: bios, zōē, psychē [principle of life]

  grey is all theory, and green the golden tree of life [Goethe]
