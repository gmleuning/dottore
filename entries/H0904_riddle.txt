# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A riddle belongs to conscious symbolism: its inventor knows the meaning completely and deliberately chooses a veiling shape through which it is to be guessed. Unlike the symbol proper, which stays an unsolved problem, it is wholly soluble. It joins scattered traits in a striking and apparently senseless way that points to a unity, the solving word, putting ingenuity to the test, and belongs especially to the art of speech.
