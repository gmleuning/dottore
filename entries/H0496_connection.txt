# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Connection is what perception establishes: it takes the object not as merely immediate but as mediated, reflected into itself and universal, a combination of sensory determinations with extended thought determinations of concrete relations and connections. It seeks to know each individual as an internally coherent whole and the mediations between individual things, showing that when certain circumstances are present certain consequences follow. Consciousness is thereby no longer abstract certainty but a determinate awareness.
