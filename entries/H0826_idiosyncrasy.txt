# 826 Idiosyncrasy
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 3. Authorship › a. Mere Manner
G: Zufällige Eigentümlichkeit
Gloss: A merely accidental peculiarity of the artist, which belongs to them and not to the matter.
HGloss: Manier as the artist's contingent peculiarity (zufällige Eigentümlichkeit), concerning not the general nature of the subject but only the particular artist.
