Gloss: The various feelings in which self-feeling can be caught.
HGloss: The particular feelings in which the self finds itself, its self-feeling caught in a particular sensation. Hegel sorts them by their content: feelings of the body, feelings with an outside content, and feelings whose content is a thought.
