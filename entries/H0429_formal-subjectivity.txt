# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Formal subjectivity is the first form of the soul's magical relationship, in which the soul feels the totality of its individual life without claiming what belongs to objective consciousness, being itself a moment of objective life. It is therefore no illness but to be expected in a healthy human being. Its simplicity is undivided, so that there is as yet no relation between two self-subsisting persons and no opposition of subjective to objective consciousness. Its three states are natural dreaming, the life of the child in the womb, and the individual's relation to its genius.
