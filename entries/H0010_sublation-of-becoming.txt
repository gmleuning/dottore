# 10 Sublation of Becoming
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › α. Being › c. Becoming
G: Aufhebung
Gloss: The coming to rest of change in a stable result. To sublate is at once to cancel and to preserve: being and nothing are cancelled as separate, yet kept as aspects of what results.
HGloss: The vanishing of becoming into a quiescent result; to sublate (aufheben) is at once to negate and to preserve, as in determinate being.
