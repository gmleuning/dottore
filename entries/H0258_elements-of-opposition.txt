# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The elements of opposition are fire and water. Fire is being-for-self posited as a moment, the unrest of individuality, materialized time that consumes other things and itself and so passes into neutrality. Water is the neutral element, opposition returned into itself, without rigidity or shape of its own, taking limits from outside and only the possibility of process, solubility.
