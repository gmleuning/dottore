# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Sound arises when the inner form of a body, its specific simplicity in density and cohesion, becomes free from submergence in material externality. In the vibration of the body, through the momentary negation of its parts and the negation of that negation, this ideality gains independent existence and mechanically soul-like manifestation: materialized space passes into materialized time.
