# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reason as testing laws no longer gives laws but compares a given content only with itself, to see whether it is a tautology and free of contradiction. Such a formal standard takes in a content as readily as its opposite, so that property and community of goods can each pass or fail. To test what is right is already to have started down an unethical path: the ethical disposition holds unwaveringly to what is right because it is right, and so reason passes into spirit.
