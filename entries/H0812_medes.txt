G: Die Meder
Gloss: The Medes, a warlike mountain people of Iran, whose priestly caste were the Magi.
HGloss: Die Meder, the warlike people whose Magi kept the Zend religion, from whom rule passed to the Persians.

N:
  the Medes
  Media, Ecbatana, the Magi
  Deioces, Cyaxares, Astyages

Adj:
  Median

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A mountain people living south of the Caspian, fierce and warlike, who freed themselves from Assyrian supremacy and gathered their tribes under a king at Ecbatana. The Magi, one of their tribes, preserved and spread the Zend religion, though with great modifications, so that the Medes carried it on to other peoples before sovereignty passed to the Persians.
