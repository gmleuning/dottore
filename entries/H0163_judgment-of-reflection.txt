# 163 Judgment of Reflection
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment
G: Urteil der Reflexion
Gloss: A judgment that ascribes a relational or practical property, such as "useful" or "dangerous."
HGloss: The predicate is an essential universality of relation, by which the subject is related to other things.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The judgement of reflection is that in which the subject is a singular as such and the predicate no longer an abstract quality but a universality gathered through the relation of different terms, as in saying that humans are mortal or that something is useful. Such predicates express an essentiality which is relational. The movement falls on the side of the subject, which is extended from this one through some to all.
