# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third stage of Greek philosophy follows on the return of self-consciousness into itself, an infinite subjectivity without objectivity. What is now required is that spirit which has so internalized itself become objective again, in an intellectual world born of spirit: both the return to God and God's manifestation to human beings as God is in truth, for spirit.
