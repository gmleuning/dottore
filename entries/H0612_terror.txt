# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Terror is what absolute freedom does when only negative doing remains to it. To act at all, the universal will must gather itself into one individual at the head, excluding all others from the deed, so that it is no deed of universal self-consciousness. Its sole work is a death with no inner extent or fulfilment, the negation of the empty point of the free self, the coldest and emptiest of deaths. In this meaningless negativity the terror turns suddenly into the positive knowledge of universal will.
