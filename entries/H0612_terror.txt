G: Der Schrecken
Gloss: The destruction into which absolute freedom turns, when it can do nothing positive and suspicion takes the place of guilt.
HGloss: Der Schrecken: the fury of destruction, whose sole work is death, the coldest and meanest, in which being suspected takes the place of being guilty.
