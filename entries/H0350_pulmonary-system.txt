# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the pulmonary circulation the blood is a purely negative, immaterial life for which nature is air, and over which it wins a sheer victory. The infant's first breath is its own individual life: having floated in lymph and fed like a plant, it now relates to nature as air, in an interrupted rhythm in which blood proves itself fire.
