# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reconciliation is the consciousness that the antithesis between the finite subject and God is nullified, and that unity is attained through its negation. The subject cannot bring this about by its own activity alone: the antithesis must already be implicitly sublated in the eternal divine life. For this to become certain to humanity, the unity of divine and human nature must appear in sensible, external existence, as something seen and experienced.
