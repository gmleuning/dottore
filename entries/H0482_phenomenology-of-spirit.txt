# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The phenomenology of spirit is the science of the experience of consciousness. Consciousness knows an object and carries its own standard within itself, so that examining knowledge is its comparing itself with itself; when knowing and object fail to correspond, both alter, and a new object arises. This movement is experience. Driven by its necessity through a series of shapes, consciousness comprehends the whole system of consciousness, until it sets aside the semblance of being burdened with something alien, appearance becomes equal to essence, and it reaches absolute knowing.
