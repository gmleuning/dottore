# 75 Relation of the Two Sides as Qualities
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › α. Specific Quantity › b. Specifying Measure

Gloss: The two sides of a measure, such as distance and time in a law of motion, each a distinct quality with its own amount.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the relation of the two sides as qualities, the external quantum that was merely specified is shown to be the quantum of a something with its own quality, so that both sides are qualitative existences for themselves, each with its specific magnitude. Their relation is determined by an exponent of measure, and the quantities are so interwoven that the qualities are determined only through them, as indivisible. Measure is here the immanent quantitative relation of two qualities to each other, as of space and time in the motion of bodies.
