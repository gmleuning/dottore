# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absent-mindedness is a modification of the first form of derangement, an unawareness of the immediate present, which often begins madness, though there is also a lofty absent-mindedness in a mind drawn by deep meditation away from everything unimportant. Absent-mindedness proper is a submergence in wholly abstract self-feeling, a suspension of sober objective consciousness. In it the subject takes a false situation for the true one and grasps outer circumstances one-sidedly, outside the totality of their relations.
