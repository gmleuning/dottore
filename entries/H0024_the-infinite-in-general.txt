# 24 The Infinite in General
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › c. Infinity
G: Das Unendliche überhaupt
Gloss: The unlimited in its first, simple sense: what the limited passes into when it goes beyond itself; the negation of the finite, taken as what truly is.
HGloss: The infinite in its simple concept: the affirmation that results as the negation of the finite.

  the infinite as such, the unlimited, the non-finite
  boundlessness, endlessness, interminability, agelessness†
