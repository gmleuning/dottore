# 281 Properties of Bodies (Specification of the Individual Body)
# Nature › 2. Physics › III. Physics of Total Individuality
G: Eigenschaften der Körper
Gloss: The qualities by which one body is distinguished from others.
HGloss: The individual body specified in its relation to the elements: to light, to air and water, and to fire as electricity.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The properties of bodies arise as the process of shaping passes into physical specification. The individual body, in itself the physical totality, is to have difference posited in it, held within individuality; these determinations are its properties or predicates, yet also its relations to the free universal elements, with which they form processes: light, air and water, and electricity.
