# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The beauty of art, the ideal, is beauty in which the true unfolds into external reality yet holds its separate parts in unity, so that every one of them lets the soul, the whole, appear. Art has to turn every point of its visible surface into an eye in which spirit is seen. It is considered as the ideal as such, as the ideal's determinacy in the work, and as the creative subjectivity of the artist.
