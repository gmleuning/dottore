# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Civil society is the sphere of difference between family and state, in which the ethical unity is lost in its extremes. The concrete person, a totality of needs, is its own end, yet attains satisfaction only through others, so that a system of all-round interdependence arises. It comprises the system of needs, the administration of justice, and the public authority and corporation.
