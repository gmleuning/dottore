G: Der Psalm
Gloss: The Hebrew song of praise and lament before the one God.
HGloss: Der Psalm: the Hebrew lyric that exalts the Lord's power and glory and cries out of the depths, the soul raised in longing toward God.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The psalm, in the sublime Old Testament Psalms, is the soul's jubilant flight of praise as it lifts its eyes to heaven and cries to the one God in whom it finds its end and the source of all might and truth. Its ecstasy swells into a vague enthusiasm that cannot put into words what it feels, juxtaposing images in disorder with irregular rhythm.
