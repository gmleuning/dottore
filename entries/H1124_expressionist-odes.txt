G: Die horazische Ode
Gloss: The Horatian ode, which gives the poet's own reflective mood.
HGloss: The Horatian ode, where the subject is mastered less than the poet's own mood and reflection is expressed.

N:
  expressionist odes; Horatian ode, Horace, the ode of reflection, carmen, Klopstock's odes

Adj:
  Horatian

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In odes of this second kind the subject need not be weighty: the poet becomes so important to himself that, taking even slight things, trifling occasions and accidents, as matter, he confers on them dignity and nobility or higher interest, with no struggle against the topic's importance. Much in Horace's odes is of this sort, as in Klopstock.
