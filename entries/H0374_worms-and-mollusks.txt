# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Worms and molluscs belong to the invertebrates, in which organic and animal life stand opposed, one developed at the other's expense. In them the organism is inwardly more developed than outwardly: they have brain, heart and vessels, but a formless exterior, with hardly any external limbs and few muscles of movement.
