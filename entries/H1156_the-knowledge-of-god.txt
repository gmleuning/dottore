# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The knowledge of God is the second moment of religion, in which a distinction arises and God becomes the object of consciousness. Seen from God's side, this is God's self-manifestation, the creating of a subjective spirit for which God is. God can therefore be known, since it is God's nature to be revealed. The forms of this knowing are immediate certainty, feeling, representation and thought.
