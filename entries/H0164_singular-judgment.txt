# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The singular judgement is the immediate judgement of reflection, in which the singular is universal, or this is an essential universal. But a this is not an essential universal, so the judgement must be taken negatively. Since the predicate is the implicit being of the subject, the negation falls not on the predicate but on the subject, which needs further determination: not merely a this. So the singular judgement has its truth in the particular judgement.
