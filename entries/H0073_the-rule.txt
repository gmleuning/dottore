# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The rule, or standard, is measure as it first specifies: a magnitude determinate in itself serving as a unit for a quantum belonging to some other concrete existence, which it measures by fixing it as an amount of that unit. The comparison is an external act, and the unit itself is an arbitrary magnitude which can in turn be treated as an amount, as a foot is an amount of inches. Yet measure is not only an external rule, for as specific measure it relates by its own nature to its other.
