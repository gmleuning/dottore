# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Representation is the recollected intuition, the mean between intelligence's immediately finding itself determined and its freedom in thinking. It is intelligence's own possession, still one-sidedly subjective and conditioned by immediacy, and its path is to make the immediate inward and then to divest that inwardness of its subjectivity. Its stages, recollection, imagination and memory, are not independent faculties but an organic development of intelligence.
