# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The point is the first negation of space, since space is immediate self-externality without difference. Yet this negation is spatial negation, so that the point, essentially a relation that sublates itself, passes into the line. It is the being-for-self that negates space within space; line does not consist of points, being rather the point existing outside itself.
