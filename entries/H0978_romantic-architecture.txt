G: Romantische Architektur
Gloss: Architecture in which the building encloses a space for inward devotion.
HGloss: Building whose purpose is to enclose a space for the inward gathering of the congregation: the house of God shut off from nature.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Romantic architecture, whose real centre is the Gothic of the Middle Ages, long thought crude and barbaric until Goethe led its rehabilitation, unites independent architecture with that serving a purpose. Enclosure is its basic type more than in the Greek temple, yet it transcends mere utility: the house of God stands freely on its own account, fixed and eternal, answering to Christian worship and spirit.
