# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Magnetism is the principle of formation in its developed but still abstract determinateness: the point becomes a line whose form posits itself as opposed extremes, which exist only in the relation that is their middle term and point of indifference. The poles have an ideal, not mechanical, reality and are inseparable, so that the magnet naively exhibits the concept as syllogism.
