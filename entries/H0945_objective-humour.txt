# 945 Objective Humour
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › c. Romantic Art › iii. The Formal Independence of Individual Characteristics › 3. The Dissolution of Romantic Art
G: Der objektive Humor
Gloss: Humour that stays with its object, joining the artist's feeling to the thing with warmth and depth.
HGloss: Der objektive Humor: an inward humour of feeling that does not destroy the object but dwells in it intimately, as in Goethe's West-Eastern Divan, the close of romantic art.

N:
  objective humour
  Goethe's West-Eastern Divan, humour of the heart, the deep wit of feeling, intimate humour

Adj:
  genial, heartfelt, warm-hearted

Adv:
  with heartfelt humour
