G: Fixe Ideen
Gloss: Madness fixed on one idea, which comes either from a particular passion or from something contingent.
HGloss: Madness with an individualized content, where the fixed idea is drawn either from a particular passion (to be God or king) or from something contingent (to be a barleycorn or a dog).

N:
