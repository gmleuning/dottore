G: Descartes
Gloss: The thinker who began philosophy anew from the doubting, thinking self, making the certainty of one's own thought the foundation of all knowledge and dividing reality into mind and extended matter.
HGloss: Descartes, who began from thought: I think, therefore I am, the beginning of modern philosophy.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Descartes starts from the I think, which is utterly certain. Everything may be doubted, God, corporeal things, the world, except that we are; being is immediately bound up with the I. In the famous cogito ergo sum thinking and being are inseparably bound together, though they remain distinct.
