# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Phallic columns arose from the eastern worship of the universal force of life in nature, the productive energy of procreation, represented in the shape of the generative organs. Chiefly in India it produced enormous stone columns, broad at the base like towers, at first ends in themselves and objects of veneration, only later hollowed to hold images of gods, and becoming pagodas.
