# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Coming-to-be is that direction of becoming in which nothing is the immediate and refers to being: the determination begins with nothing and passes over into being. Being here is what nothing goes over into. Coming-to-be is not a process standing apart from ceasing-to-be, for being equally sublates itself and passes back into nothing. Each direction sublates itself in itself and carries its opposite within it.
