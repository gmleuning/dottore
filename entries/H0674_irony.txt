# 674 Irony
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality › γ. The Good and Conscience › e. Evil and Its Forms
G: Ironie
Gloss: A stance that treats all moral content as dependent on the self's whim.
HGloss: Irony, the supreme form of subjectivity, which knows itself as master of all ethical content.

  ridicule, derision, banter, raillery, badinage
  absolute subjectivity, subjective vanity, the self as master of all content, self-enjoyment, frivolity

  play with, toy, dissolve
