# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the Egyptian religion the principal figure, Osiris, takes the negation into himself: he is killed, is perpetually restored, and rules the realm of the dead, belonging to the realm of representation and spirit rather than to nature. The stage as a whole is that of the enigma: an inner meaning strives to become outwardly visible but has not yet achieved it. In Greek religion the riddle is solved.
