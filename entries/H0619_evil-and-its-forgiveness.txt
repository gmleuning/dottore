# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Evil and its forgiveness is the movement between the acting conscience, which gives duty a content from its own particular self, and the universal consciousness that judges it as evil. The agent confesses, and the judge, whose hard heart must also break, renounces its one-sided judgement and forgives. The wounds of spirit heal and leave no scars. The word of reconciliation is a reciprocal recognition in which each knows itself in its opposite, and this is absolute spirit.
