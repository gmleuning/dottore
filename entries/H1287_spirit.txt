# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The philosophy of spirit is the third part of the Encyclopaedia, the science of the idea that returns into itself from its otherness in nature. It treats spirit as subjective, objective and absolute. Its knowledge is the most concrete and therefore the highest and most difficult, answering the command to know oneself, understood as knowledge of what is genuinely human, since all activity of spirit is an apprehension of itself.
