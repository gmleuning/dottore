# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The individuality of the state is its existence as an exclusive being-for-self, a relation to other states, each independent of the others. In this independence the actual spirit has its existence, and it is the primary freedom of a people; individuals have the substantial duty to preserve it, even at the sacrifice of property and life.
