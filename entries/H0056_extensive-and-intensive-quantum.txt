# 56 Extensive and Intensive Quantum (Degree)
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum
G: Extensives und intensives Quantum
Gloss: Two forms of definite amount: extensive, a size made up of parts side by side, such as length or number; and intensive, a degree that is simple and undivided, such as temperature or brightness.
HGloss: Quantum as extensive magnitude, a sum of units outside one another, and as intensive magnitude, degree, a simple determinateness.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Extensive and intensive quantum are the two determinations of the quantitative limit itself. A quantum whose limit is a plurality is extensive magnitude; when that self-external plurality collapses into simple determinateness, it is intensive magnitude, or degree. Extensive magnitude has its amount within it, and degree has the amount outside it, but they are one and the same determinateness of quantum, each passing into the other. With this identity something qualitative comes on the scene, indifferent to the quantum it has.
