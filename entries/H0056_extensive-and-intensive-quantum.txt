# 56 Extensive and Intensive Quantum (Degree)
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › II. Magnitude › β. Quantum
G: Extensives und intensives Quantum
Gloss: Two forms of definite amount: extensive, a size made up of parts side by side, such as length or number; and intensive, a degree that is simple and undivided, such as temperature or brightness.
HGloss: Quantum as extensive magnitude, a sum of units outside one another, and as intensive magnitude, degree, a simple determinateness.
