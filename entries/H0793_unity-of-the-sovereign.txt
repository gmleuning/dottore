# 793 Unity of the Sovereign
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › γ. The State › a. Constitutional Law › i. Internal Constitution › 1. The Sovereign Power
G: Einheit der Souveränität
Gloss: The sovereign as the single point of the state's unity.
HGloss: The sovereignty of the state, the one in which it has its actuality.

N:
  unity of the sovereign; unity of the state, sovereignty, oneness of the state, indivisible sovereignty, the single will
  the apex, the summit, the pinnacle, the single point, the one, ideality, the ideal unity of the powers

V:
  unify, embody
  crown, concentrate, gather, culminate

Adj:
  unified, indivisible, sovereign
  unitary, single, one, supreme, ideal

Adv:
  as one
  at the summit, indivisibly

Phr:
  e pluribus unum [Lat.]
  one nation, indivisible
  the crown

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The unity of the sovereign is the sovereignty of the state: its particular functions and powers are not self-subsistent, either on their own or in the particular will of individuals, but are rooted ultimately in the unity of the state as its simple self.
