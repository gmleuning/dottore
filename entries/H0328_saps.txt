# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The saps carry the plant's inner formation. Where the spiral vessels first absorb moisture just as it is, the second function is to elaborate the sap into organic form, which the plant, having no stomach, does immediately. The sap circulates through the entire plant, a quivering of vitality within itself that combines its many individuals into one.
