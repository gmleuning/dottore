# 955 Indian and Egyptian Subterranean Buildings
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › a. Architecture › i. Symbolic Architecture › 3. Transition to Classical Architecture
G: Indische und ägyptische unterirdische Bauten
Gloss: Chambers cut into rock or built underground, especially for the dead.
HGloss: Subterranean buildings of India and Egypt, the houses of the dead.

N:
  Indian and Egyptian subterranean buildings
  mining, digging

Adj:
  subterranean, underground, dissocial†, inconversable†
  deep, profound, subterraneous, subterrene†

Adv:
  underground, in the bowels of the earth

F:
  Latin: hypogeum

Phr:
  underground chambers

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The subterranean buildings of India and Egypt differ from mere cave dwellings: they served as places of assembly, subterranean cathedrals for religious wonder, with symbolic arrangements, colonnades, sphinxes, Memnons, elephants and colossal idols hewn from the rock and left growing out of the unworked stone. Within them something like a temple or house appears in its most natural form; the Mithras caves belong here too.
