# ---- Hegel ----
Def: World history is the court of judgement of the world, in which the universal spirit exercises its right over the finite spirits of peoples. It is the necessary development, from the concept of spirit's freedom, of the moments of reason and so of spirit's self-consciousness; peoples rise and pass as the bearers of its successive principles.
Texts:
  The Philosophy of Right
    §§341–360
Citations:
