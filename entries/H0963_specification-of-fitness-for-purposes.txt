# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A building's fitness for its purpose is the first question: its function and the circumstances of its erection, including climate, position and landscape, which the architect must satisfy while producing a freely coordinated whole. The Greeks built chiefly public works and the Romans luxurious private ones, but where utility prevails beauty enters only as decoration; the freest purpose is a temple for the god's statue.
