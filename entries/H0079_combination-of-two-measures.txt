# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The combination of two measures is the union of two things of different inner measure, as of two metals of different specific gravity, each determined by the ratio of its quantitative sides. Each preserves itself in the alteration, but this self-preservation specifies the other and alters its own measure. The weight of the compound is the mere sum of the weights, but the volume is not, so that alteration falls in the exponents. Measure and the quality that rests on it prove to be nothing fixed, having their determinateness in other relations of measure.
