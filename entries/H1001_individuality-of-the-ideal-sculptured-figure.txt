# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Individuality of the ideal figures means that sculptures must show substantial individuals who are also particular persons, the group of Greek gods. A single supreme beauty in one statue is foolish, since the ideal's beauty lies in individuality and character, broadening abstract beauty into an ensemble of figures, though Christian virtues resting on the breach of body and spirit have no place among them.
