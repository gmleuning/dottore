# 682 The Good and Conscience
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › II. Morality
G: Das Gute und das Gewissen
Gloss: The universal good, and the inner certainty that knows it.
HGloss: The good, the idea as the unity of the will's concept and the particular will, and conscience, the subjectivity which determines it.

N:
  the good and conscience; the good, conscience, moral reflection, subjective certainty of the good
  the idea of the good, the universal good, moral conscience, inner certainty, the good as end, the subjective knowing of the good

V:
  aim at the good, follow one's conscience
  will the good, know the good, determine

Adj:
  good, conscientious, moral
  universal, inward

Adv:
  in good conscience
  for the good, in conscience

Phr:
  the good and conscience
  to do the right thing
