# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Form and essence is the first distinction within ground. The determinations that return into ground have their subsistence in it as sublated reflection, and because this subsistence is posited and determinate, they are distinguished from the simple identity of essence and make up form over against it. Essence has a form and determinations of this form. The distinction is not one between two separate things, since form is the reflection by which essence is mediated with itself.
