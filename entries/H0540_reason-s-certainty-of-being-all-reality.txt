# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reason's certainty of being all reality is idealism as consciousness first expresses it: I am I, an object with the consciousness that nothing other has being. Self-consciousness, whose singular individuality has proved to be in itself the absolute essence, now discovers the world as its own and takes an interest in its continuing existence. It must be this reality not only for itself but in itself, by proving it. At first the certainty has forgotten the path that justifies it, and its reality is only a pure abstraction, the simple category.
