# 26 Restriction and the Ought
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude › iii. Finitude as Such
G: Die Schranke und das Sollen
Gloss: The limit felt as a barrier to be overcome, and the demand to overcome it. What a thing ought to be stands against what it is.
HGloss: The limit as a restriction (Schranke) within something, and the ought (Sollen) that is to go beyond it; each implies the other.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Restriction and the ought are the two moments of the finite. Something's own limit, posited by it as a negative that is at the same time essential, is a restriction; and its being-in-itself, as negative reference to that limit as to its restriction, is the ought. For a limit to be a restriction, something must at the same time transcend it. Each is only with the other: something is raised above its restriction as ought, yet has its restriction only as ought. What ought to be both is and is not.
