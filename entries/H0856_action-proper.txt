# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Action proper is the third stage after the state of the world and the situation, presupposing circumstances that lead to collisions, to action and reaction. Art does not begin with the external first origin of an action but takes the listener into the midst of things, since a person's proper nature, the true core of their capacity, is revealed in a single great situation and action. It treats the universal powers, the individuals and their character.
