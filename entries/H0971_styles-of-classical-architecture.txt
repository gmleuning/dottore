Def: The styles of classical architecture differ most strikingly in their columns: the Greek Doric, Ionic and Corinthian orders, than which nothing more beautiful or fitting was found, the Tuscan belonging to simple timber work and the so-called Roman being only richer Corinthian. Rome added the arch and vault and a general character of its own.
