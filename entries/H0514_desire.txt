# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Desire is self-consciousness in its first form, as the negative relation to an independent object. It is certain of itself only through sublating that other, which shows itself as self-sufficient life. In satisfying itself it learns that the object is independent and that both its desire and the certainty gained from it are conditioned by that object. There is therefore something other than self-consciousness that is the essence of desire.
