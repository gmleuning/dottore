# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Discrete magnitude is quantity posited in the determinateness immanent to it, the one: the outsideness of one another as discontinuous, as broken off. Discreteness is a moment of quantity and for that very reason the whole of it, so it never separates from continuity. The broken-off ones do not restore the relation of atom and void, since their discreteness is itself continuous, consisting in the ones being the same as one another and having one unity. It is the many of a unity, not just many ones.
