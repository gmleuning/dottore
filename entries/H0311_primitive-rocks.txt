# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Primitive rock is the first forming of the earth's physical organization, with granite as its innermost, mean and basis, to whose traits the other formations attach themselves in two directions. Granite, though primary, has three constituents, quartz, mica and felspar, forming a single hard mass, a simple earthy triad that develops further; its antithesis is primitive limestone.
