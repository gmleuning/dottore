# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The argillaceous moment and its cognate forms stand between the siliceous and the calcareous. In granite its place is taken by mica, the surface developed to the stage of opposition and the moment of combustibility containing the germ of every abstract form. Argillaceous earth and coal formations finally pass into peat, where mineral and vegetable are indistinguishable.
