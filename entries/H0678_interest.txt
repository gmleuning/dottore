# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Interest is the moment of subjective individuality and its activity contained in what has been brought about, when the content of the urge is distinguished as what matters. Nothing comes about without it, even the most unselfish act, in which the individual must find satisfaction. It must not be confused with selfishness, which prefers its particular content to the objective one.
