# 865 Talent
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › α. The Idea of Artistic Beauty › c. The Beauty of Art, or the Ideal › iii. The Artist › 1. Inspiration › b. Genius
G: Talent
Gloss: A particular aptitude for one art or skill, as distinct from genius.
HGloss: Talent, the specific capacity for a particular art, which reaches full life only with genius; one may have talent and not genius.

N:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Talent is the particular capacity that a particular art demands, such as for violin playing or singing. It is not immediately identical with genius, though perfect creation requires their identity: mere talent can excel only in one separate side of art and still needs the general capacity and inspiration that genius alone confers. Talent without genius gets little beyond external skill.
