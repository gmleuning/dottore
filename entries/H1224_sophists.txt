# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Sophists were the teachers of Greece, through whom general culture came into existence. Calling themselves teachers of wisdom, they traveled from city to city, and their art was to make someone a powerful speaker, turning things around and viewing them from many sides. The bad sense of the name, as refuting or proving anything on false grounds, must be set aside.
