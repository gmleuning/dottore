G: Stil
Gloss: The way of presentation that the medium and the laws of an art demand, as distinct from an artist's manner.
HGloss: Stil, the manner of presentation that follows the conditions of the material and the demands of the art and its kinds, as in the severe, the ideal and the pleasing style.
