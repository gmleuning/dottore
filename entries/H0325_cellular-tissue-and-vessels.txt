# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The plant's inner structure is quite simple, consisting of cells, spiral vessels and tubes. Its general texture is cellular tissue, which separates into woody fibres or spiral vessels on one side and the vessels that carry the plant's circulation on the other. This separation is itself an immediate production, generally a mere multiplication.
