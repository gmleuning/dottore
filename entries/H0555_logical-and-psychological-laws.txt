# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Logical and psychological laws are what observation finds when it turns from nature back to self-consciousness. First it finds the laws of thought, taken as formal and lacking reality, though as laws of pure thinking they are inseparably essentialities of both form and things. Then it turns to psychology, seeking a law relating individuality to the actuality acting on it. Since individuals can let that influence have its way or turn it on its head, the supposed psychological necessity is an empty phrase.
