# 179 Third Figure
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › a. Syllogism of Existence (Qualitative Syllogism)
G: Dritte Figur
Gloss: The form of inference whose connecting term is a general idea.
HGloss: Singular and particular joined through the universal: S–U–P, the transition to the mathematical figure.

N:
  third figure; S–U–P, singular–universal–particular; universal as middle term
  P-U-S, the universal as middle, the mean as universal, the figure of the abstract universal

V:
  infer through the universal
  mediate, conclude

Adj:
  tertiary, particular-concluding
  third-figure, universal, mediating

Adv:
  in the third figure
  through the universal

F:
  Latin: Darapti, Disamis, Datisi, Felapton, Bocardo, Ferison, figura tertia

Phr:
  all men are mortal
  all men are rational
  therefore some rational beings are mortal
  as a rule
