# 179 Third Figure
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › γ. Syllogism › a. Syllogism of Existence (Qualitative Syllogism)
G: Dritte Figur
Gloss: The form of inference whose connecting term is a general idea.
HGloss: Singular and particular joined through the universal: S–U–P, the transition to the mathematical figure.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third figure, singular, universal, particular, has no immediate premise left, both having been mediated by the other two figures, so that it presupposes them as each presupposes the others. Its middle is the abstract, indeterminate universal, in which the extremes are contained only according to their universality and not their essential determinateness. It is the truth of the formal syllogism and shows its formalism.
