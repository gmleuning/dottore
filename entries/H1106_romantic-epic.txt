# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Romantic epic enters through the faith, deeds and fates of new peoples, Germanic and Latin: the remains of their pagan days, such as the Ossian poems and the Edda, and the medieval epics of national heroes, like the Cid and the Nibelungenlied, of religion, and of chivalry, until the renaissance turned to new matter and forms.
