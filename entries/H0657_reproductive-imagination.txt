# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Reproductive imagination is intelligence calling images forth from the inwardness of the I, which from now on is the power over them. It reproduces images voluntarily and without the help of a present intuition, which distinguishes it from mere recollection. In the process the immediate spatial and temporal concretion of the intuition is dissolved, and a universal representation becomes the associating link among the images.
