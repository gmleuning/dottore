G: Die Parabel
Gloss: A story of everyday life that points to a higher, general meaning.
HGloss: Die Parabel: an occurrence from ordinary life given a higher significance, so that the meaning is grasped through the familiar happening, as in the Gospel parables.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A parable takes events from everyday human life and gives them a deeper and more general meaning, so as to make that meaning intelligible and perceptible through the occurrence. Unlike the fable it draws on human action, enlarging a case that seems trivial into something of general interest. Cyrus' two days of labour and feasting and the Gospel parable of the sower are examples.
