# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The dissolution of classical art springs from the germ of decline the classical gods carry within them. Spiritual individuality, adequate to immediate bodily existence, broke up into a group of gods whose particular character is not absolutely necessary and so is surrendered to contingency, where the gods find the source of their dissolution, in fate, in anthropomorphism and at last in satire.
