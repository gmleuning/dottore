# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Beauty as abstract unity of the sensuous material concerns not shape but the stuff perceived, whose only unity is undifferentiated concord, purity of shape, colour or tone. Smooth surfaces, a clear sky, a mirror-like lake, a pure voice and unmixed colours please by their homogeneity. Like abstract form, this purity is lifeless and affords no truly actual unity, which requires ideal subjectivity.
