# 1026 Neoplatonism
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy [Tetrad] › b. Greek Philosophy
G: Neuplatonismus
Gloss: The late Greek philosophy in which all things flow from a single, ineffable One and the soul seeks to return to it; reason turned inward toward the spiritual.
