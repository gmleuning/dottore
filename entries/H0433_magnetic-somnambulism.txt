# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Magnetic somnambulism and states like it arise when the life of feeling becomes a form of the self-conscious, educated, sober human being, and this is a disease. The individual stands in unmediated relation to the concrete content of its own self, and its sober awareness of itself and of an intelligibly ordered world becomes a state separate from this feeling life. Only the individually determined world of particular interests enters it, so that to expect revelations of universal truth from it is foolish.
