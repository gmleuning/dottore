# ---- Hegel ----
Def: In the chemical process whole particular bodies, each an individual totality, are related only as different. This relation, the identity of non-identical independent bodies, is a contradiction and therefore essentially process, whose function is to posit the differentiated as identical and to differentiate the identical. It is the third stage in the process of shape, after magnetism and electricity.
