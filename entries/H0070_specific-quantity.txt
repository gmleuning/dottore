# 70 Specific Quantity
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure
G: Spezifische Quantität
Gloss: An amount that carries a quality, as a certain temperature is the boiling point of water.
HGloss: Measure as immediate: a quantum to which a quality is bound.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Specific quantity is the first form of measure, qualitative quantity. It is first an immediate, specific quantum. Second, in relating to another it becomes a specifying of the indifferent quantum, a rule containing the two moments of measure as different, the quantitative determinateness and the external quantum. Third, these two sides themselves become qualities, and the rule becomes a relation of the two, one measure that specifies itself further into a difference of measures.
