# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The third period, that of Spirit conscious that it is free in willing what is universal in and for itself. The Reformation establishes that human beings are by their nature destined to freedom; its consequences unfold in states; and the Enlightenment makes the verdict of Spirit itself the criterion of belief and right, until in the French Revolution thought is held to govern spiritual reality.
