# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Adventures are the fundamental form of romantic events and actions: spirit withdrawn into itself treats the external world as a place released from it, a scene of complications and endless contingency, and goes into it with contingent ends it does not weave into a consistent whole. Its collisions are determined from outside and by accident, so that what matters is asserting oneself more than accomplishing a lasting work.
