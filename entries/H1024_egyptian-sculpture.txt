# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Egyptian sculpture is a starting point and source for Greek forms, an imperfect art that classical art had to negate. Its content begins to meet the ideal but cannot be ideally treated because it still belongs to a symbolic outlook that cannot fuse universal meaning with individual visible shape; despite technical perfection it lacks inner creative freedom.
