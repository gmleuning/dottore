# 211 The Absolute Idea
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › III. Idea
G: Die absolute Idee
Gloss: Thought that grasps itself as the whole of its own development; the method of reason, complete.
HGloss: The idea that knows itself, the concept of the idea as its own object: pure form, which is the method.

N:
  the absolute idea; method, dialectical method, speculative method; the whole, totality, system, completion, consummation, self-knowledge of reason, thought thinking itself; the logical idea, the Logos; truth in and for itself; personality, pure personality
  fulfillment, culmination
  arrangement
  all, sum total
  the absolute method, the self-knowing idea, the idea thinking itself, the unity of the theoretical and practical idea, the speculative idea

V:
  complete, consummate, close, round off, comprehend the whole, know itself
  accomplish, perfect
  grasp itself, comprehend itself, think itself

Adj:
  absolute, total, complete, consummate, self-knowing, self-comprehending, methodical, systematic
  entire, whole &c H0120

Adv:
  absolutely, in and for itself, completely, as a whole
  methodically

F:
