# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Animals with fin-like limbs, the Cetacea, are a class of mammals in which the land animal falls back again into the water. Following Lamarck, they have only two short, flat limbs, no pelvis or hind legs, swallow food unchewed, and usually live in water while surfacing to breathe air: the least highly organized mammals.
