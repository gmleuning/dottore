# 1255 The Apologists

G: Die Apologeten
Gloss: The second-century writers who defended Christianity against pagans and heretics: Justin, Irenaeus and Tertullian.
HGloss: The Apologists, who defended the faith before the pagan world and against the Gnostics, giving it its first doctrinal statement.
