# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Representation is consciousness of something that one has before oneself as an object, and it is chiefly in this form that God is for human beings. It uses sensible images and figures, such as the begetting of a son, which are known to mean something other than what they immediately say, and developed allegories and stories. Its content is the same truth that philosophy puts into concepts.
