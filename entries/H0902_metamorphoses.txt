# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Metamorphoses are symbolic and mythological tales that oppose natural to spiritual, giving a rock, animal, flower or spring the meaning of a degradation and punishment of a spiritual being who falls into guilt or endless grief, as Niobe weeps as stone. By distinguishing natural and spiritual they form the transition from symbolic mythology to mythology proper, in which natural content becomes gods of human shape.
