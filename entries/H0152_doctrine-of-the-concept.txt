# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The doctrine of the concept is the subjective logic, which treats the concept as the third to being and essence, to immediacy and reflection. Being and essence are the moments of its becoming, but the concept is their foundation and truth, the identity into which they have sunk. The objective logic is its genesis: substance is implicitly what the concept is explicitly, and the movement of substance through causality and reciprocity brings the concept forth.
