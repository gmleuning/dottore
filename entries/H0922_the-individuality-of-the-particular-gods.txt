# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The individuality of the particular gods goes beyond abstract particularity of character, since the classical ideal must set individuality in motion, relating it to an opposite, so that each god enters further particular traits. These bring contingency, as traits no longer led back to the substantial meaning surround the gods as arbitrary external accessories, and so raise the question where the material of their individuality comes from.
