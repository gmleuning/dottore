# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Flemish painting begins in the mastery of the van Eyck brothers, inventors or perfecters of oil painting, whose figures delight by innocence and piety though lacking Italian freedom of form. After the Reformation, the Dutch burgher people painted its neat homes, festivals and peasant life with perfect care and the magic of colour; a naive cheerfulness, the Sunday of life, takes away all evil.
