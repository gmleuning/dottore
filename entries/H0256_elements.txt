# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The physical elements are the determinations of elemental totality, which existed as free heavenly bodies, now contained in the planet's individuality as subordinate moments. Hegel rejects the chemist's definition by simplicity: a physical element is a real matter not dissipated into chemical abstraction. There are four, air, fire and water as the elements of opposition, and earth, and light kindles their process.
