# 20 The Immediacy of Finitude
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude › iii. Finitude as Such
G: Unmittelbarkeit der Endlichkeit
Gloss: The limited thing as simply given: something whose very being is to perish.
HGloss: The finite in its immediacy, determined only to perish, its passing away fixed as its unalterable being.

N:
  immediacy of finitude; the merely finite, sheer finitude, bare mortality; fixed finitude, hardened finitude, the finite as absolute; despair of the finite, melancholy, sadness, mourning; perishing as essence; fatalism, resignation, acceptance of mortality; nothingness of the finite, nullity, vanity &c H0019
  immediate finitude, the perishable, perishing, the hour of birth as the hour of death, the given finite
  perishability, transience, caducity

V:
  be merely finite, be nothing but mortal; perish, waste away; insist on finitude, hold fast to the finite, cling to the finite
  pass away, cease

Adj:
  merely finite, purely mortal, irredeemably finite; ephemeral, fleeting; melancholy, mournful, elegiac, resigned, fatalistic
  transient, transitory, passing
  perishable, immediate

Adv:
  merely, only, no more than; in the end; irrevocably, without remedy
  transiently, perishably

Phr:
  all is vanity and vexation of spirit [Bible]
  out, out, brief candle [Macbeth]
