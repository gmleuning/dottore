# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Recollection proper is the relation of an abstractly stored image to a present intuition with the same content: the intuition is subsumed under the representation, and intelligence recognizes both as already its own. By repeated summoning the image gains such vitality that no external intuition is needed, so that intelligence can externalize its own property. The more cultivated a person is, the more their intuitions are also recollections.
