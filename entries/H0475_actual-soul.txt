# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The actual soul is the third and last section of Anthropology. When its bodiliness has been thoroughly trained and made its own, the soul becomes an individual subject for itself, and the body is an externality that represents not itself but the soul, of which it is the sign. In this identity of inner and outer, with the outer subdued to the inner, the soul is actual: in its body it has its free shape, in which it feels itself and makes itself felt, and which, as its work of art, bears a human expression.
