# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The kinship group is the wider circle of clan or house from which the partners of a new family come. The new family is self-subsistent and based on ethical love, while its tie to the clan rests only on consanguinity; an individual's property is therefore essentially connected with the marriage and only remotely with the clan.

  A marriage brings into being a new family which is self-subsistent and independent of the clans or ‘houses’ from which its members have been drawn. The tie between these and the new family has a natural basis—consanguinity—but the new family is based on ethical love.
