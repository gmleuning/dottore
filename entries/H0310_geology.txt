# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Geology and oryctognosy concern the earth's mineral formations: the former the rock species, the latter lodes and veins. The earth's physical organization begins not with a simple germ but divided into a granitic and a calcareous principle, whose moments develop through a series of formations into metals, fossils and finally mechanical stratifications lacking formative process.
