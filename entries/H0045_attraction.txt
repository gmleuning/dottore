# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The one one of attraction is the ideality toward which repulsion is only the ought, realized as the many ones pass over into one. Attraction presupposes repulsion, which supplies its material, and could not be completed without becoming an inert one that no longer attracts. It is the positing of a one distinct from the others, mediated through their negation. This one does not swallow the attracted ones abstractly into a point; containing repulsion in its determination, it keeps them as many within itself, so that in it repulsion and attraction are united.
