# 134 Infinity of Force
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › γ. Essential Relation › b. Relation of Force and Its Expression
G: Unendlichkeit der Kraft
Gloss: A force understood as identical with its effects, so that its outward expression is its own self-expression.
HGloss: Force expressing itself only to return into itself: its expression is the expression of the force itself.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The infinity of force overcomes the finitude it has so long as its presupposing and its self-relation fall apart, so that one force appears as external and the other as passive. What force expresses is that its relation to another is its relation to itself, that its passivity consists in its activity: the stimulus that solicits it is its own soliciting. In short, its externality is identical with its inwardness, and so the relation passes into that of outer and inner.
