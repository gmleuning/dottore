# 134 Infinity of Force
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › γ. Essential Relation › b. Relation of Force and Its Expression
G: Unendlichkeit der Kraft
Gloss: A force understood as identical with its effects, so that its outward expression is its own self-expression.
HGloss: Force expressing itself only to return into itself: its expression is the expression of the force itself.

N:

  the infinitude of force, force as self-expression, the identity of force and expression, the return of force into itself

  express itself, manifest itself, solicit itself, return into itself, sustain itself

  self-expressing

Adv:

  infinitely, in its expression
