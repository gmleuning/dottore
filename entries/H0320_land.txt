# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The land is the giant corpse of the life once immanent in it, a solid concretion that has freed itself from the sea's neutrality. It shows an infinite fecundity like water's, but whereas the sea brings forth mainly animal life, the earth tends to vegetable life, covering itself everywhere with indeterminate green growth such as lichen and moss.
