# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Feet are the time relations of syllables into which poetry binds long and short. The simplest are relations of equals, such as the dactyl and anapaest, where two shorts may be gathered into a long as a spondee; a long beside a short makes a more marked difference, as in iambus and trochee; more complex are the cretic and bacchius.
