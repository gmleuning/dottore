# 160 Positive Judgment
# Logic › 3. Doctrine of the Concept › I. Subjectivity › β. Judgment › a. Judgment of Existence (Qualitative Judgment)
G: Positives Urteil
Gloss: A judgment that affirms a quality of a thing, as "the rose is red."
HGloss: The singular is a universal: the rose is red; the subject is not wholly its predicate, so the judgment is untrue.
