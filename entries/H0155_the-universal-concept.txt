G: Der allgemeine Begriff

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The universal concept is the pure concept, absolutely infinite, unconditioned and free: absolute self-identity by being first the negation of negation, the infinite unity of negativity with itself. It is not the abstract universal that leaves determinateness outside it. It is a simple that contains the richest difference and determinateness within itself, so that in determining itself it remains with itself and pervades its other without violence, as free power.
