# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The people named after the language of the Zend books, the canonical writings of the religion of Zoroaster. In that religion the one universal being is worshipped in the form of light, which is no particular natural thing but sensuous universality itself, and which signifies the good and the true. It stands opposed to darkness as Ormuzd to Ahriman.
