# 591 Divine Knowledge

G: Das göttliche Wissen
Gloss: The knowledge that belongs to the divine law: the unwritten, eternal law of the family and the dead.
HGloss: Das göttliche Wissen: the knowledge of the divine law, unwritten and eternal, of which no one knows whence it came; kept by woman and the family.

N:
  divine knowledge
  Antigone

Adj:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Divine knowledge is the knowing of the consciousness that belongs to the divine law, which sees in the law of the state merely human, contingent violence, while its own law has the meaning of the netherworld, sealed up in inwardness. With ethical knowing there arises the opposition of the known and the not known, as in substance there was one of the conscious and the unconscious, so that the absolute right of ethical self-consciousness comes into conflict with the divine right of essence.
