# 591 Divine Knowledge

G: Das göttliche Wissen
Gloss: The knowledge that belongs to the divine law: the unwritten, eternal law of the family and the dead.
HGloss: Das göttliche Wissen: the knowledge of the divine law, unwritten and eternal, of which no one knows whence it came; kept by woman and the family.

N:
  divine knowledge
  Antigone
  the unwritten law, the law of the gods, the law of the nether world, the knowledge of the family

V:
  obey the gods, keep the unwritten law

Adj:
  unwritten, eternal, hidden

Adv:
  by the law of heaven

Phr:
  nicht etwa jetzt und gestern, sondern immerdar lebt es, und keiner weiß, von wannen es erschien [Ger.] [Sophocles, quoted by Hegel, Phenomenology]
