G: Alliteration
Gloss: The rhyme of initial sounds, as in Germanic verse.
HGloss: Alliteration (Stabreim), the earliest and most abstract echo, the binding of words by their initial sounds.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Alliteration, developed most thoroughly in old Scandinavian poetry, repeats a single letter, the initial one, without requiring whole syllables to recur. It is the most imperfect kind of rhyme, so weak that the words must already carry accent on their first syllable and stand close together, and meaningful words are chiefly chosen for it.
