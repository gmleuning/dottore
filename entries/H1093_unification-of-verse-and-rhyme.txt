# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The union of rhythmic versification and rhyme is considered chiefly in modern languages, which have partly restored rhythm or joined it with rhyme. Lacking the plasticity of ancient quantity, they compensate with metrical accent and caesura, which now coincide with word accent; and the combination is allowed only within limits, since the two systems rest on opposed principles.
