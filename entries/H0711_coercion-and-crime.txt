# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Coercion and crime are wrong that violates the existence of freedom by force. Because I place my will in external things, my will can be seized in them and compelled, either subjected to force or made to sacrifice something to keep its possessions. Crime is coercion that infringes right as right.
