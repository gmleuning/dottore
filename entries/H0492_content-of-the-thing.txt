# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The content of the thing is the particular sensory filling, smell, taste, colour and the rest, which as such belongs to sensation. It is the material of consciousness, the substantial and qualitative element that the soul is and finds within itself. What marks sensory consciousness is not that this content comes through the senses but that the object receives no thought determination beyond being and independence, into which the manifold content is concentrated as one unit.
