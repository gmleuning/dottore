# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Romantic art arises because there is something higher than the beautiful appearance of spirit in sensuous shape: spirit is pushed back from reconciliation in the body to reconciliation within itself. The ideal's solid totality falls apart into subjective being and external appearance, so that spirit may reach deeper reconciliation in its own inwardness, where outward shape is no longer adequate, though classical art attained the height of beauty.
