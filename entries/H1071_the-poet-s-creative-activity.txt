# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The poet's creative activity is less tied than that of other artists to a perceptible material, so that poetic talent is more general and independent, needing only a gift for rich imaginative formulation. The task is both easier, with fewer technical hurdles, and harder, since depth of imagination must replace visible form while the poet reaches all depths of spirit through words.
