# 1187 Death of Christ
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › II. Religion › γ. The Consummate Religion › b. Representation and Appearance (the Kingdom of the Son) › iii. Reconciliation
G: Der Tod Christi
Gloss: The death of Christ on the Cross, in which God himself dies, the highest love.
HGloss: Der Tod Christi: God himself is dead; the death of the God-man is the highest finitude and at the same time its sublation, the infinite love.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The death of Christ is the point where one passes into the religious sphere. It is the violent death of a human being, but comprehended spiritually it becomes the means of salvation. Natural death is the utmost fragility of humanity, and that God himself is dead means that the finite and negative are a moment of the divine nature and do not hinder unity with God. Christ's death belongs to the eternal divine history.
