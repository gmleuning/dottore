# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The historical development of epic matters more than that of sculpture, since epic displays the substantial kernel of a nation's life and belongs to every people, though epic proper reached perfection in Greece beside sculpture. Hegel distinguishes the oriental epic centred in the symbolic, the classical epic of the Greeks and its Roman imitation, and the romantic epics of Christian peoples.
