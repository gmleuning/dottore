# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Intuition proper is intelligence as the concrete unity of attention and the casting of its material into space and time, immediately recollected into itself in this external being and immersed in its own self-externality. The object is both detached from me and mine, but what is mine is present only implicitly, and the objective side predominates. Genuine intuition apprehends the solid substance of its object. Posited as the mind's own, it is raised to representation.
