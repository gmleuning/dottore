# 1085 Sound of Syllables
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 2. Poetic Expression › c. Versification › i. Rhythmic Verse
G: Der Klang der Silben
Gloss: The sound of syllables and words in themselves, which comes forward as rhythm recedes.
HGloss: The sound of the syllables, which gains weight as the rhythmic element recedes.

N:
  sound of syllables; sound, euphony, tone, verbal music, vowel music, word music
  cadence

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The sound of syllables is the third element of rhythmic verse, which is not exhausted by the shaping of time but extends to the sound of words. Here there is an essential difference between classical languages, which keep rhythm as the chief thing, and modern ones, which are chiefly suited to rhyme.
