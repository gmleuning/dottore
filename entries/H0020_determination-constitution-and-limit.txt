# 20 Determination, Constitution, and Limit
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude
G: Bestimmung, Beschaffenheit und Grenze
Gloss: Three ways a thing is defined: its determination, what it is essentially; its constitution, what it happens to be through outside circumstance; and its limit, the boundary where it stops and something else begins.
HGloss: Determination, what something is in itself; constitution, what it is through its relation to other; limit, the non-being of each in the other.
