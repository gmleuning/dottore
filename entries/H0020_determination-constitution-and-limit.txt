# 20 Determination, Constitution, and Limit
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being › b. Finitude
G: Bestimmung, Beschaffenheit und Grenze
Gloss: Three ways a thing is defined: its determination, what it is essentially; its constitution, what it happens to be through outside circumstance; and its limit, the boundary where it stops and something else begins.
HGloss: Determination, what something is in itself; constitution, what it is through its relation to other; limit, the non-being of each in the other.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Determination, constitution and limit are the ways in which something, having taken its being-for-other back into its being-in-itself, holds its otherness within itself. Determination is the in-itself by which something preserves itself while involved with an other; constitution is what in it is exposed to external influence and relation. Each passes over into the other. Since the otherness is now posited within something as its own moment, there are two somethings that negate and join each other through one determinateness, and this is limit.
