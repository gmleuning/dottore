# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Poetic treatment takes as its proper matter spiritual interests, the whole realm of human ideas, deeds and fates, with external nature entering only as human environment. Poetry is older than elaborated prose, the original presentation of truth, which does not yet separate the universal from its living existence or oppose law to appearance and end to means, as the understanding does.
