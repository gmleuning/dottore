# 1126 Song Proper
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › c. Song
G: Das eigentliche Lied
Gloss: The simple song proper, of the people, of company and of the church.
HGloss: The song proper, in its simplest forms, rooted in a people, a company or a congregation.

N:
  song proper; art song, simple song

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The song proper, meant for singing to oneself or in company, needs little substance or lofty grandeur, which would hinder direct self-expression; whatever joy or grief is deeply felt finds its place there. Fleeting and ever renewed, it must still have universal validity, arousing the same feeling so that it passes from mouth to mouth.
