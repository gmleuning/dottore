# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The epic world-situation is a state of life already present to individuals yet closely bound to them by primitive common life: the relations of family and people developed, but not yet fixed in institutions and laws valid apart from living individuals, resting on custom, justice and character. So heroes share an objective community while their action appears to spring freely from their own will.
