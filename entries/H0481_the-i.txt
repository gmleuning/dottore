# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The I arises when the soul, which has set its bodily being over against itself and made it its own, completes its reflection into itself. This being-for-itself of free universality is the soul's higher awakening, to abstract universality that is for abstract universality, which thinks and is a subject for itself. Judging, the I excludes from itself the natural totality of its determinations as an object, a world external to it, and relates itself to that world so that in it it is immediately reflected into itself. This is consciousness.
