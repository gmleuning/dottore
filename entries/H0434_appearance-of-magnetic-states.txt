# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The magnetic states appear when the two sides of soul life, the unmediated relation of the feeling soul to its individual world and the mediated relation to an objectively connected world, come apart and break loose from each other. This divergence is an illness, in which the soul side, grown independent of mental consciousness, usurps its function. It shows itself in sleepwalking, catalepsy and other conditions, and especially in animal magnetism, whether it arises of itself in an individual or is produced in one individual by another.
