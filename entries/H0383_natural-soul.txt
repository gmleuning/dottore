# 383 Natural Soul
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › I. Soul (Anthropology)
G: Natürliche Seele
Gloss: The soul as wholly immersed in its natural surroundings and bodily life.
HGloss: The soul in its immediate natural determinacy, which merely is and shares the life of nature.

  natural soul; nature-bound soul, unreflective life, natural mind, the natural man, natural self, instinct, temperament &c H0389, disposition, natural endowment, bodily soul, vegetative and animal soul, physis of the soul
  the natural psyche, the immediate soul, the soul in nature, the sleep of spirit, the merely being soul
  animism

V:
  be natural, follow nature, live by instinct
  slumber, be immersed, live naturally

Adj:
  natural, natal, inborn, innate, instinctive, unreflective, primitive, spontaneous, elemental
  implanted, inbred
  impulsive, unpremeditated
  incipient, inchoate, embryonic, rudimental
  immediate, immersed, unawakened

Adv:
  naturally, instinctively, by nature, spontaneously
  immediately, in nature

F:
  Arabic: fiṭra [inborn nature]
  Chinese: tianxing [heaven-given nature]

Phr:
