# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Among the principal moments of Scholasticism, Anselm sought to treat church doctrine philosophically and to give proofs of it, and is famous for the ontological proof of God's existence, which embodies the antithesis of thinking and being. With Peter Lombard, Thomas Aquinas was foremost in making theology philosophical and systematic.
