# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Manifest or revealed religion is the religion in which spirit has passed from the form of substance into that of subject. Its content is that the divine essence essentially and immediately has the shape of self-consciousness, and is known as spirit. Nothing in it remains secret, for what is an object to consciousness is its own self: the divine nature is the same as the human, and this unity is intuited. It is represented as essence, as creation and its other, and as the community.
