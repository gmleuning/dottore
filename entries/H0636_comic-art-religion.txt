# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Comedy is the shape in which the actual self-consciousness presents itself as the fate of the gods. The universal powers have only an imagined individuality, and the subject, raised above them, wears the mask ironically and lets it drop, appearing in its ordinariness as no different from actor or spectator. The self is the negative power in which the gods vanish while it remains with itself, without fear of anything alien, in a well-being found nowhere outside this comedy.
