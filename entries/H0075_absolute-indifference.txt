# 75 Absolute Indifference
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › III. Measure › γ. The Becoming of Essence
G: Absolute Indifferenz
Gloss: A single underlying stuff that remains the same while its qualities and amounts change.
HGloss: The substrate become indifferent to all determinations of quality and quantity, which are now only states of it.
