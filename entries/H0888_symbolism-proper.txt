# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Symbolism proper, in Egypt, requires that meaning become explicitly free from the immediate sensuous shape, the natural being grasped as negative and to be superseded, and this negative accepted as a moment within the divine itself. Egypt is the country of symbols, which sets spirit the task of deciphering itself without attaining it; its works are riddles, and the sphinx is the symbol of the symbolic itself.
