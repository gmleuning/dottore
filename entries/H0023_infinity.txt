# 23 Infinity
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › β. Determinate Being
G: Unendlichkeit
Gloss: That which is not limited by anything outside it; boundlessness.
HGloss: The negation of the negation: the finite's own going-beyond itself, which first appears as the infinite set over against it.

N:
  infinity, infinitude, the infinite; boundlessness, limitlessness, endlessness, illimitability, immeasurability, immensity, vastness, unboundedness, eternity &c; the unbounded, the unlimited, the endless; absolute, perfection, plenitude
  infiniteness, perpetuity &c H0222
  infinity &c H0053
  unlimitedness

V:
  be infinite, have no limit, know no bounds, be without end, stretch to infinity, extend indefinitely
  know no limits, have no limits, have no bounds, go on for ever
  transcend, exceed all bounds

Adj:
  infinite, boundless, limitless, endless, unbounded, unlimited, illimitable, immeasurable, measureless, incalculable, innumerable, numberless, countless, inexhaustible, interminable, unending, never-ending, everlasting, eternal, perpetual; vast, immense, unfathomable, fathomless, bottomless; absolute, unconditioned
  uncircumscribed, boundless &c H0053
  inconceivable

Adv:
  infinitely, endlessly &c adj.; world without end, without limit, without end
  boundlessly

F:
  German: das Unendliche
  Latin: infinitum, ad infinitum, in infinitum
  Greek: apeiron, plērōma

  Sanskrit: ananta [endless]
