# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The product of the mechanical process is, formally, the object as a determinate thing, the process dying out in rest. But the determinateness also withdraws into itself, so that the object becomes an objective singular and the semblance of a self-standing singularity set against universal substance is sublated. This reflected oneness is an individual self-standing unity, the centre; and the universality that particularizes itself from within, a rational fate, is law.
