# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Catalepsy and sleepwalking are states of immediate feeling awareness in which an object is sensed without its specific sense, which is replaced by the common sense active chiefly in the pit of the stomach or by touch. In catalepsy the organs are paralysed. Sleepwalking is a kind of cataleptic state in which dreaming expresses itself not only in speech but in walking and other actions, guided by an often accurate, though by no means infallible, feeling of surrounding things. The eye is fixed, and with it the distance that consciousness requires.
