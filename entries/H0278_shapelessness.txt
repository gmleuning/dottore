# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Shapelessness is immediate shape, posited as formless within itself, in two extremes: the point-like brittleness of the granular, pulverizable body, and the spherical shape taken by liquids. Sphericity is a self-rounding fluidity that effaces every dimension, so that determinateness remains undeveloped; fluid matter, inwardly indeterminate, assumes it under equal pressure on all sides.
