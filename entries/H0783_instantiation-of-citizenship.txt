# 783 Instantiation of Citizenship
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit › III. Ethical Life › β. Civil Society › c. Public Authority and Corporation › i. Public Authority (Police) › 2. Common Utility
G: Das Individuum als Sohn der bürgerlichen Gesellschaft
Gloss: Civil society's claims on the individual and its duties to them, as their second family.
HGloss: Civil society tears the individual out of the family and becomes a universal family to them, with claims on them as they have rights against it (§238-239).

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Instantiation of citizenship names civil society's relation to the individual as a universal family. Civil society tears the individual from family ties, estranges family members, and recognizes them as self-subsistent persons; it puts its own soil in place of the external inorganic nature and paternal soil, and so has claims on the individual as they have rights against it.
