# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Judgement is the determinateness of the concept posited in the concept itself, the concept's own dividing and setting its determinations against each other. It is the other function of the concept beside conceiving, its first realization. It contains two self-standing sides, subject and predicate, which are determined only through the judgement itself, and their connection is the unity of the concept. Its development into a series of forms is the progressive determination of the concept.
