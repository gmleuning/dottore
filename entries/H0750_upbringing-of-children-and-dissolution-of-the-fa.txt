# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The upbringing of children and the dissolution of the family is the third moment of the family. In children the unity of the marriage first exists objectively. The family dissolves ethically when the children, educated to free personality, come of age and found families of their own, and naturally through the death of the parents, with inheritance.
