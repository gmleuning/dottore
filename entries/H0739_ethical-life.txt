# 739 Ethical Life
# Spirit › 2. Objective Spirit
G: Sittlichkeit
Gloss: Freedom made real in the shared customs and institutions of a living community.
HGloss: The idea of freedom as the living good, which has its knowledge and will in self-consciousness and its actuality in the ethical world.

N:
  ethical life; ethical community, ethical order, social ethics, customary morality, second nature, the living good, family, civil society, and state
  ethical substance, the ethical world, custom, the institutions

V:
  live ethically, belong, participate, abide by custom
  inhabit, conform, realize freedom

Adj:
  ethical, customary, communal, social, civic
  conventional &c H0471, well-bred, well behaved
  national, public
  substantial, institutional

Adv:
  ethically, in community
  by custom, in a living community

F:
  German: Sitte, Sitten, Moralität
  Latin: mores, moralitas
  Greek: ethos, ēthos
  French: comme il faut, morale, éthique
  Arabic: adab [right conduct]
  Sanskrit: dharma
  Chinese: li [ritual propriety]
  Korean: illyun [ethical life]

Phr:
  die Sittlichkeit ist die Idee der Freiheit als das lebendige Gute [Ger.] [Hegel, Philosophy of Right §142]
  the done thing
  the moral fabric
