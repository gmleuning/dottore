# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Air is the element of undifferentiated simplicity, no longer light's positive self-identity but a negative universality, reduced to a moment and therefore heavy. Harmless in appearance, it is an insidious power consuming what is individual and organic; toward light it is passive and transparent, outwardly an elastic fluid pervading everything. It is the universal posited as passive, indeterminate but absolutely determinable.
