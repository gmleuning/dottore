# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Chivalry is the secular sphere of romantic art. The heart, perfected in simple religious bliss, must leave the heavenly kingdom, look into itself and win a worldly content proper to the individual subject, so that religious inwardness becomes worldly. The infinitude of the person then fills mundane life in honour, love and fidelity, feelings in which one sees oneself affirmed directly in others and not only through a third thing.
