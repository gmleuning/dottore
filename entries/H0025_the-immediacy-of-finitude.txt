# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The immediacy of the finite is finitude taken in its simplest form, as qualitative negation returned to the abstract opposition of perishing and nothing to being. Things are left with no affirmative being apart from their determination to ruin. The finite may be put into flux, for that it should end is its only determination, but it refuses to be brought affirmatively to its affirmative, the infinite. So held, its finitude is eternal. The development of finitude shows, however, that the finite collapses within itself as this contradiction, and that perishing itself perishes.
