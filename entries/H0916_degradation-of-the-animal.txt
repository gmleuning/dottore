# 916 Degradation of the Animal
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › β. The Forms of Art › b. Classical Art › i. The Process of Formation of Classical Art
G: Die Herabsetzung des Tierischen
Gloss: The lowering of the animal from divine status.
HGloss: The animal degraded from the divine: animal sacrifice, hunts, metamorphoses.

N:

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The degradation of the animal marks the passage to classical art: whereas Indians and Egyptians revered animals as visible presences of the divine, spirit's self-consciousness dispels respect for the dull inwardness of animal life, and this lowering of the animal world itself becomes the content of religious ideas and art, in animal sacrifice, hunts and metamorphoses.
