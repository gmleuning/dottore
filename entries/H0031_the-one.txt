# 31 The One
# Logic › 1. Doctrine of Being › I. Determinateness (Quality) › γ. Being-for-Self › a. Being-for-Self as Such
G: Das Eins
Gloss: A self-contained unit; the simplest notion of an individual that excludes everything else from itself.
