# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The judgement of necessity is that in which universality exists in and for itself, the objective universality which in the sphere of essence corresponds to substance, but which here has its differences immanent within it. It is posited with a determinateness essential to it and with a particularity diverse from it, so that the universal is determined as genus and species. It unfolds as the categorical, the hypothetical and the disjunctive judgement.
