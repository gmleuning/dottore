# 1128 Convivial Song
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › I. Art › γ. The System of the Individual Arts › c. Romantic Arts › iii. Poetry › 3. Different Genres › b. Lyric Poetry › ii. Particular Aspects of Lyric › xxx. Kinds of Lyric Proper › c. Song › i. Song Proper
G: Gesellschaftslied
Gloss: Songs for company: drinking songs, table songs, rounds.
HGloss: The convivial song, which expresses the shared pleasure of company.

N:
  convivial song; drinking song, table song, glee, catch, round, skolion, carousal song

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Convivial songs belong to an enriched culture that delights in cheerful society, full of wit, graceful turns and gallantry, or else turns sentimentally to nature and narrow situations. Mere description soon becomes trivial; the poet must rise above immediate desire through free contemplation, as in many songs of Anacreon, Hafiz and Goethe's Divan.
