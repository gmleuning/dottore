# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The corporation is the association of members of the business estate according to their particular skill, a second family for its members. It assures their livelihood and recognizes their capability, so that their particular interest becomes a common, ethical end. As the family is the first, the corporation is the second ethical root of the state.
