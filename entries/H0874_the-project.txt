# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Inspiration proper is nothing but being completely filled with the theme, wholly present in it, and not resting until it is stamped and polished into artistic form. Having made the matter their own, artists must forget their personality and its accidents and become only the form in which the theme takes shape; an inspiration in which the subject parades itself is a poor one.
