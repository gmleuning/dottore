# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Concrete alteration is the coming and going of the sensory object in consciousness. Known immediately and singly, the object enters my consciousness by chance and disappears from it again, as something given whose existence and constitution I cannot account for. Sensory consciousness is therefore a wholly inadequate form for any content that is universal in and for itself, since it gives what is necessary and eternal a finite, singular, self-external shape.
