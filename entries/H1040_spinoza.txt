# 1040 Spinoza
# Spirit › 3. Absolute Spirit › III. Philosophy › β. History of Philosophy [Tetrad] › d. Modern Philosophy › i. Rationalism
G: Spinoza
Gloss: The thinker who overcame the split between mind and matter by making both aspects of a single infinite substance, God or nature, of which all things are passing modes.

N:
  Spinoza; the one substance, God or nature, mode, affect, affection, attribute, adequate ideas, monism, pantheism, parallelism, ratio, conatus, determinism, the Ethics, tractatus, blessedness; Spinozism, acosmism

V:
  unify, determine, see under the aspect of eternity

Adj:
  Spinozist, monist, pantheist, determinist

Adv:
  under the aspect of eternity, necessarily

F:
  Latin: affectus, scientia intuitiva, natura naturans, natura naturata, beatitudo, imaginatio

Phr:
  Deus sive Natura, sub specie aeternitatis, amor dei intellectualis, determinatio negatio est, conatus sese conservandi [Lat]. Worship likeness and images, that is paper and ink [Spinoza], You are either a Spinozist or not a philosopher at all [Hegel, paraphrased]
  omnis determinatio est negatio [Hegel, Lat.], der gottbetrunkene Mensch [Novalis, Ger.], le prince des philosophes [Deleuze, Fr.]
