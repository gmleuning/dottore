# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Madness proper is the second main form of derangement. The closure of the natural mind within itself acquires a determinate content, which becomes a fixed idea because the mind, not yet in full control of itself, is absorbed in it. The mind sticks fast in a merely subjective representation and takes it for something objective. Unlike imbecility and rambling, madness holds to something definite and so shows itself still to be consciousness, so that beside the deranged point the mad keep a coherent grasp of things and can act intelligently.
