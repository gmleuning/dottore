G: Das Sprichwort
Gloss: A short common saying, drawn from a single case, that holds good generally.
HGloss: Das Sprichwort: a single case taken from ordinary life and so stated that it carries a general meaning.

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: A proverb is a middle form that can be expanded into fable or apologue. It presents a single case, mostly from daily human life, which is to be taken in a universal meaning, as that one hand washes the other. Here universal and concrete appearance do not stand apart as in comparison, the one being immediately expressed with the other. Goethe composed many such aphorisms.
