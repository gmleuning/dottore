# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The interfusion of the two laws is the content of the ethical world in which each is realized in the other's element. The ethical essences are substance as universal and as singular consciousness, with the people and the family as their actuality and man and woman as their natural self and activating individuality. Here the purposes of earlier shapes of reason are achieved. Justice, as human law, brings what strays from the balance back into the universal, and, as the spirit of one who has suffered wrong, avenges from the netherworld.
