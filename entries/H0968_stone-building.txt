# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Building in stone starts from a shapeless mass that must be split and worked before pieces can be piled and joined, and huge blocks invite excavation, serving symbolic and romantic architecture. Classical architecture nonetheless builds in stone when it becomes beautiful, preserving the original principle of timber in its forms while introducing characteristics that wood does not contain.
