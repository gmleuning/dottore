# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spiritual certainty is spirit's knowledge that it is in itself the totality that constitutes reason, so that what appears to it as an object is no object such as consciousness has but a rational one. Spirit therefore proceeds freely out of itself, neither dependent on an other as consciousness is nor shut up in an abstract identity with itself as self-consciousness is. It is free in the world, having the certainty of rationality.
