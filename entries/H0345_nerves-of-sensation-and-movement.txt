# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The nerves of sensation and of movement make up the outward-directed side of sensibility, sensation as determined either from outside or by self-determination. The motor nerves, mostly from the spinal cord, are the nervous system in its practical function; the sensory nerves, from the brain, are it as receptive, and to these the sense organs belong.
