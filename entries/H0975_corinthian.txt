# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The Corinthian style keeps basically to the Ionic and its slenderness but develops it into tasteful brilliance with the final wealth of ornament, no longer letting the origin in wood glint through. Its column is no loftier but appears more slender and richer through a higher capital adorned with acanthus leaves, of which the Greeks told a charming story of a girl's grave.
