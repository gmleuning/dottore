# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The magnetizer is the subject whose power brings another into the magnetic state and who becomes that person's current subjective soul, their genius, able even to fill them with content. In the magical relationship that results, one subject acts on an individual inferior in freedom and independence of will. The patient, a formal self without actuality of its own, has the sensations and ideas of the magnetizer, seeing, tasting and hearing in that other, so that there is only one subjectivity of consciousness between them.
