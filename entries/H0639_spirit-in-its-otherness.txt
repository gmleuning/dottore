# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Spirit in its otherness is the eternal, abstract spirit becoming other to itself and entering immediate existence: it creates a world, which is representation's word for the movement of the concept. Spirit as being for an other is the subsistence of the moments formerly enclosed in pure thought, split into their particularity. In this element arise the opposition of good and evil and the reconciliation of the two, represented as separate and self-standing beings.
