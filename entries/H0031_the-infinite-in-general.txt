# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The infinite in general is the simple concept of the infinite: the negation of negation, the affirmative, being reinstated out of restrictedness. It is in a more intense sense than immediate being; it is true being, elevation above restriction, in which spirit is at home. It does not stand fixed above the finite, for it arises out of the finite's own nature, which is to refer to itself as restriction, to pass beyond it and so to become infinite. The finite has thus vanished into it, and what is, is only the infinite.
