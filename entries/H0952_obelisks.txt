# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Obelisks are Egyptian works between architecture and sculpture whose form is not taken from living nature but is purely regular, without yet serving as house or temple. They stand freely as symbols meaning the sun's rays, dedicated to the sun god, whose rays they were to catch. Hegel sets beside them the colossal seated Memnon figures and the sphinxes.
