# ---- Hegel ----
Def: The public authority and the corporation are the third moment of civil society, in which the welfare of individuals, left contingent in the system of needs, is secured. The administration of justice annuls offences against property and personality; beyond this the right of particular welfare must be actualized, first externally by the public authority, then from within by the corporation.
