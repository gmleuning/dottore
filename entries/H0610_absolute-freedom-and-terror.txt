# 610 Absolute Freedom and Terror
# Spirit › 1. Subjective Spirit › II. Phenomenology of Spirit (Consciousness) › γ. Reason › b. Spirit as a World › ii. Self-Alienated Spirit: Culture
G: Die absolute Freiheit und der Schrecken
Gloss: Freedom that refuses all limits and institutions and so turns to destruction, as in the Terror of the French Revolution.
HGloss: The absolute freedom of the universal will, which destroys every institution and, having nothing else to negate, ends in the terror.

N:
  absolute freedom and terror; the French Revolution, Jacobinism, absolute freedom

F:
  German: Furie des Verschwindens, Gleichschaltung [forced coordination]
  French: la Terreur, volonté générale, à la lanterne, sansculottes

# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Absolute freedom and terror is spirit grasping its certainty of itself as the essence of all reality, so that the world is simply its will, the universal will of all individuals as such. No power can resist it; the social estates collapse, and each undertakes only the total work. But it can produce neither a positive work nor deed, only negative doing, and its one work is death. This extreme negation turns into the pure knowing and willing of the moral spirit.
