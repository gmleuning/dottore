# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Comparing music with the other arts shows an affinity with architecture, since both follow laws of quantitative proportion drawn from the spirit's invention and not from existing things. Yet they stand in opposite realms: architecture raises enduring heavy masses to be seen from outside, whereas the swiftly vanishing world of sound passes through the ear into the heart.
