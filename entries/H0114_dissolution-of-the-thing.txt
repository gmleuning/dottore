# 114 Dissolution of the Thing
# Logic › 2. Doctrine of Essence › II. Appearance › α. Existence
G: Auflösung des Dinges
Gloss: The breaking down of the thing into the stuffs of which it is composed.
HGloss: The thing as the mere also of its matters, porous to them, its unity a contradiction that dissolves it into appearance.
