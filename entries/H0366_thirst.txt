# ---- Hegel ----
Def: Thirst is the process with water, the demand for the neutral element, on one side to counteract the abstract heat within, on the other to remove a specific taste. An urge is instinct only when related to an individualized object, and the satisfied need returns, whereas spirit finds satisfaction in knowing universal truths.
